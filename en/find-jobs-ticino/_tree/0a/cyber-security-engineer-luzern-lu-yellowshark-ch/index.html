01

Future employer

For our client, an established IT service provider (Managed Services Provider) with prominent corporate customers in Central Switzerland, we are looking for additional support. The company plans, builds and operates IT infrastructure solutions ranging from basic infrastructure through networking and security to the modern workplace, on-premises, in the Microsoft Cloud or in its own data center.

This job advertisement is aimed at professionals who work or have worked for an IT service provider, systems house or MSP. You know the day-to-day reality of customers, changing environments and working in projects and operations.

02

Your responsibilities

  • Analyze and resolve security incidents

Alerts from the SOC end up with you. You check whether a real attack is behind an anomaly, involve the affected accounts and systems, and help contain and properly remediate an incident. You record your findings in writing and explain to customers in clear language what happened and what comes next.

  • Help shape security projects for customers

You accompany initiatives from clarifying requirements to handover: build protection mechanisms and systems, test them in practice, document them and sign them off together with the customer. The environment ranges from traditional server environments through hybrid setups to pure cloud.

  • Manage vulnerabilities

Scan results interest you not merely as a list. You classify them, decide together with customers what should be addressed first, and ensure that gaps, weak configurations and excessive access rights actually disappear. Afterwards, you check whether the measure is effective.

  • Manage the security platform

You keep our own security platform running. If data is missing or an analysis seems implausible, you look for the cause and pass ideas for improvement on to development.

  • Continuously refine detection

Together with the team, you build and test detection rules, clean up false alarms, write investigation steps into the playbooks and replace manual work with scripts and automation.

Your profile

Professional

  • You have an IT qualification or equivalent knowledge. A part-time degree program that you are currently completing is not an obstacle.
  • You have already worked for an IT service provider in System Engineering or Cyber Security and are familiar with working for multiple clients in different environments.
  • Windows servers and clients, Active Directory, Entra ID and Microsoft 365 are familiar to you, and you know how identities, systems and networks interact.
  • You have already gained some hands-on security experience, for example in hardening systems, working with endpoint protection, evaluating alerts, or securing accounts and permissions.
  • A plus: Microsoft Defender XDR, Sentinel, PowerShell, KQL.

Personal

  • You explain technology in a way that customers can understand and enjoy interacting with them directly.
  • You work carefully and reliably and handle sensitive data responsibly.
  • Even under time pressure, you keep an overview, set priorities and speak openly when you do not know something.
