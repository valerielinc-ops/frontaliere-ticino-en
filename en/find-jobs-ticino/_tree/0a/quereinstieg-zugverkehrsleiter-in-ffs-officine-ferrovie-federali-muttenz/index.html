Cross-entry train manager: in 100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Cross-entry train traffic manager:in Muttenz, by agreement, 100% Apply now

You can look forward to that.

As a train transport manager, you keep an eye on the route network assigned to you in the Basel marshalling station, steer and monitor the trains at your workplace with around six to eight screens. In addition, you set the various shunting and train journeys via a push-button switch. In normal operation, you observe the traffic, control the processes and ensure a smooth process. In the event of disruptions, you take over the operational management: you analyze the situation, initiate measures and correct the disruption so that the normal state is restored as quickly as possible. In this way, you share responsibility for Switzerland’s mobility. Without you, the net stands still. The job is system-relevant and immediately effective: You coordinate people and technology, make quick decisions and make a direct contribution to the safety and reliability of rail transport. We offer you a paid second training to train manager, which takes eight months. After a successful final exam, you will work at the Basel marshalling yard and help 2000 freight trains reach their destination safely and on time every day. Find out more about the job profile at the next info event. You see irregular working hours and shift work as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. Night work and Sunday work are paid in addition, e.g. with an hourly wage supplement of 16 francs for Sunday work. For night assignments, you get time surcharges of 10 to 30%, which allows you more free time. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad – also for the family – can be found on our website under Benefits.

You can move that.

  • As a train traffic manager: in the Basel marshalling station you keep an eye on the route network assigned to you, steer trains safely and ecologically and monitor the traffic.
  • In regular operation, you ensure that the international transits of freight trains (Germany – France) are carried out smoothly.
  • In the event of disturbances, take measures to restore the normal state.
  • You will coordinate and communicate with teammates, locomotive staff and construction and shunting staff in Switzerland. In addition, you coordinate with the train managers of cross-border freight traffic.
  • You are responsible for customer information at railway stations and ensure clear, timely communications.
  • Thanks to your efforts, passenger and freight trains are safe and on time around the clock.
