Head: in Logistics – Transportation \& Disposition (m/f/d)

The position is ideal for a person who likes to take responsibility, is close to employees and customers and keeps a cool even if the daily schedule no longer fits the actual daily business in the short term.

Their tasks

  • Overall responsibility for the operational organisation and management of transport logistics
  • Leadership of a small team in disposition and a larger number of chauffeurs
  • Planning and coordination of an extensive own commercial vehicle fleet as well as on-demand use of external transport capacities
  • Ensuring efficient and customer-oriented disposition
  • Coordination of different transport orders in regional and interregional local transport
  • Active support for scheduling in bottlenecks, failures and challenging situations
  • Leadership, support and operational planning of the chauffeurs
  • Organisation of holidays, absences, replacement and special missions
  • Coordination of vehicle availability, repairs, maintenance and MFRP appointments
  • Ensuring compliance with legal working, driving and rest periods
  • Responsibility for the economic use of personnel, vehicles and external transport partners
  • Direct contact with internal and external customers as well as pragmatic solution of customer concerns
  • Rapid organisation of solutions in the event of vehicle failures, staff shortages, accidents or short-term changes
  • Active further development of processes and processes in transport and disposition

Your profile

  • Several years of experience in transport logistics with disposition
  • Sound knowledge of road transport and its operational challenges
  • Leadership experience in transport, logistics or comparable environment
  • Experience in the operational planning of chauffeurs and commercial vehicles
  • Good knowledge of the relevant legal regulations, in particular ARV as well as working, driving and rest times
  • Technical understanding of commercial vehicles and enjoyment of a diverse vehicle park
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and high cost awareness
  • High service and customer orientation
  • Decision-making strength and the ability to find pragmatic solutions even under time pressure
  • Down-to-earth, clear and appreciative leader
  • High flexibility and willingness to be able to reach operationally important events outside of classic office hours
  • Place of residence at a reasonable distance from the place of work or willingness to choose it accordingly

What to expect

They assume a responsible key position with a great deal of room for manoeuvre and a direct influence on day-to-day business. Instead of corporate structures, you expect short paths, direct decisions and an environment in which own initiative is expressly desired.

The mandate is exclusively managed by swisselect ag. For further information, you are happy to personally and confidentially under.
