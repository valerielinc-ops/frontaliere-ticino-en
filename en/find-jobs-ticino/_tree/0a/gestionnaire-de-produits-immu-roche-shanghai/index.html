The Position Position Summary We are seeking a proactive and execution-oriented Product Manager to join our commercial team for the launch of Gazyva® (Obinutuzumab). In this role, you will be responsible for executing brand tactical plans, creating high-impact educational and marketing materials, and coordinating multi-channel campaigns for HCP engagement. You will collaborate closely with field sales, medical affairs, and external partners to drive brand awareness and adoption across target accounts. Key Responsibilities

  • Tactical Execution: Translate brand strategy into actionable marketing tactics, promotional materials, and regional event plans tailored to nephrology stakeholders.
  • Campaign & Content Creation: Develop compliant promotional content, digital assets, and educational toolkits for sales representatives to drive effective customer interaction.
  • Field Support & Alignment: Work closely with the sales team to gather field feedback, provide regular product training, and ensure message consistency.
  • Event Management: Plan and execute regional academic symposiums, advisory boards, and medical conference participation in alignment with brand objectives.
  • Performance Tracking: Monitor campaign execution metrics, track promotional budget usage, and analyze sales feedback to optimize marketing activities.

Qualifications & Experience

  • Bachelor’s degree or above in Life Sciences, Pharmacy, Medicine, or Business.
  • 2–4 years of pharmaceutical product management or strong sales/medical experience in specialty care (Nephrology, Immunology, or Oncology experience preferred).
  • Solid project management, communication, and execution skills.
  • Fluency in Mandarin and professional proficiency in English.
