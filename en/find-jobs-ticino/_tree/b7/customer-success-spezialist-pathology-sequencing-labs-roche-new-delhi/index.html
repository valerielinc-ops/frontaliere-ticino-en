The Position The Opportunity

The Customer Success Specialist – Pathology Lab is a field-based position that is responsible for providing pre-sales support, installation services, technical expertise, and training/education to new and existing customers (Pathology Labs, Sequencing Labs, Commercial sequencing providers). The primary role of this position is to collaborate with sales, support teams, and serve as the scientific/technical expert to help drive sales and provide support for customers. While some work may be performed remotely, this position requires approximately 50-70% travel (including overnight travel) to customer sites across India.

Who You Are

You are a mid-career IHC and NGS technical professional who is enthusiastic, customer-focused, and people-oriented. You look forward to traveling and serving in a customer-facing role.
