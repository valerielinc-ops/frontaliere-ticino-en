Cross-Border Officer (80-100%)

We are looking for a motivated and analytical professional with a strong interest in regulatory and cross-border matters. The successful candidate will support the ongoing development of the Bank's Cross-Border Risk Management Framework while acting as a key point of contact for Front Office and internal stakeholders on day-to-day cross-border matters.

YOUR ROLE

  • Support the interpretation and implementation of Cross-Border rules, country manuals and regulatory requirements within the Bank's processes and workflows.
  • Prepare analyses, monitoring reports and governance materials related to the Cross-Border Risk Management Framework for CP COO and relevant committees.
  • Support internal and external audits, regulatory reviews and remediation activities related to Cross-Border matters, ensuring appropriate follow-up of action items.
  • Provide day-to-day guidance to Front Office and internal stakeholders on Cross-Border requirements and related procedures.
  • Maintain and update the Cross-Border training framework, training materials and awareness initiatives.
  • Contribute to strategic, regulatory and business projects by conducting impact assessments and supporting the implementation of Cross-Border requirements.
  • Review and update Cross-Border policies, procedures, country manuals and related governance documentation.
  • Assist with the organization of Cross-Border governance committees, including agenda preparation, action tracking, reporting and drafting of meeting minutes.

YOUR PROFILE

  • University degree in Law, Business Administration, Compliance, Risk Management or equivalent.
  • 3 to 5 years of experience in Private Banking, Compliance, Risk Management, Legal or a Cross-Border function.
  • Good understanding of banking regulations and cross-border risks.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Ability to work autonomously while collaborating effectively across departments.
  • Proactive, organized and solution-oriented mindset.
  • Swiss resident.
