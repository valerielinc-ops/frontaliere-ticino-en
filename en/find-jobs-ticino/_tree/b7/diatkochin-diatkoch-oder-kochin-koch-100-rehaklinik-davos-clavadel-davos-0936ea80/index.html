About us *

With 13 locations and nine specialist areas, the Valens Clinical Group is the largest rehabilitation provider in Switzerland. As carriers of the labels “Friendly Work Space” and “Best Employers Switzerland”, we support our 2300 employees in their work\-life\ balance and cultivate a culture of cooperation.

The Davos Clavadel rehabilitation clinic is a centre for musculoskeletal, internistic oncology and pulmonary rehabilitation as well as psychosomatics. It has about 106 beds and employs about 220 people.
