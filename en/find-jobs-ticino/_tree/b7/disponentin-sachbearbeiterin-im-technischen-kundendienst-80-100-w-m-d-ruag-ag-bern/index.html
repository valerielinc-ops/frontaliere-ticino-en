Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

Are you an organizational talent with technical understanding and enjoy coordinating customer concerns and providing optimal support to service technicians? Then a versatile turntable function awaits you with us, in which you ensure that our service missions run smoothly.

Your tasks

  • You support internal and external clients in all scheduling requests relating to our multimedia and security services.
  • You triage and coordinate incoming customer requests and orders.
  • You will be responsible for efficient operational planning and work preparation for our service technicians operating throughout Switzerland, including material provision and ordering.
  • You support the service technicians during their assignments in maintenance, corrective service work, conversions and installations.
  • You will complete the necessary service administration tasks, such as the preparation of repair offers, the control and processing of reports and the creation of acceptance protocols.
  • You support the organization of the general workshop as well as the materials management including rental warehouse.
  • You provide sporadic piketting service and, if necessary, assist in booking the team's production hours as well as directly in the service.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed basic technical training, ideally supplemented by further training, for example as a technical merchant / technical merchant (TK).
  • You will advantageously bring experience in the disposition of service technicians in technical customer service.
  • You are an initiative, structured and extremely service-oriented personality with a strong organizational talent.
  • You are resilient, appreciate versatile tasks and enjoy the regular exchange with different internal and external contacts.
  • You communicate stylishly in German and have a good knowledge of French and English.

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
