MAIN TASKS

In this exciting role, you have the opportunity to further deepen your know-how and set new standards together with a dynamic team. Do you feel at home in the mobile apps architecture on iOS and Android, love challenges and want to develop your skills? Then become part of an extraordinary team:

  • Strategic responsibility for the architecture of several Geberit Mobile Apps (Control, Home, Service, Pro)
  • Definition and further development of the technology roadmap
  • Design and implementation of architecture standards for Microsoft .NET MAUI,

Android and iOS, C#

  • Integration of Azure services and development of CI/CD pipelines

PROFILE

For this challenging function, you will bring a completed degree in computer science or software development and several years of professional experience. Further, the following points distinguish you:

  • In-depth knowledge of C#, Azure Cloud Services, CI/CD processes and App Store Deployment
  • Experience in development for Android and iOS
  • Understanding Clean Architecture and Modern Software Design Principles
  • Enjoying technical challenges as well as a good feeling for clean, maintainable code
  • Self-responsible, solution-oriented and structured working method
  • Communication strength and ability to collaborate in interdisciplinary teams
  • Very good German and English skills in word and writing (min. level B2)
