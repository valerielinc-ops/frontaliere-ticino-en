Full description

Pensum: 80–100% I Baden and factory location

As Head of Purchasing Nuclear, you assume overall responsibility for the strategic and overarching procurement activities of the Core Division. You ensure a secure, economical and compliant supply of nuclear-specific goods and services and use synergies between the Beznau (KKB), Leibstadt (KKL) nuclear power plants and Group Purchasing.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Overall responsibility for the procurement activities of the Kern division taking into account the Group-wide purchasing strategy as well as active representation of divisional interests in Group-wide purchasing committees and participation in strategic decisions
  • Ensuring the long-term availability of nuclear-related goods, services and safety-related components
  • Functional management and coordination of purchasing activities between KKB, KKL and the central purchasing group
  • Building, maintaining and strategically developing key suppliers to strengthen competition and minimise procurement risks
  • Observation and analysis of relevant procurement markets, early identification of trends and derivation of strategic recommendations for action for division management
  • Management of purchasing projects and initiatives to increase cost and process efficiency as well as support in contract and claim management
  • Close cooperation with internal stakeholders, nuclear power plants and external partners along the nuclear value chain, especially in the front-end and back-end sectors

Profile and Requirements

  • Completed technical and/or business studies
  • Several years of experience in strategic purchasing in a senior role, ideally in an industrial and regulated environment
  • Very good technical and commercial know-how, ideally related to nuclear technology
  • Leadership skills, an independent, analytical and structured working style and strong communication skills
  • High orientation towards safety, quality and compliance as well as an analytical, structured working style
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Group experience and international environment are a plus

About the team

You will become part of a dedicated team that actively shapes procurement processes in the nuclear sector. We count on cooperation, personal responsibility and mutual appreciation – regardless of origin, gender or life path.
