Full description

A contributor to the domestic economy (on call hours) - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite Ticino hospital is present with its institutes on the entire cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of more than 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of more than 41,000 patients per year and providing more than 600,000 outpatients. To complete our team of the Home Economy service at the EOC Rehabilitation Clinic (CREOC), headquarters of Novaggio we look for a(a) Collaborator/trice for the domestic economy (-%) Profile: The future/cooperator/professional feels comfortable in a multicultural context, manages to manage interpersonal relationships with patients, manages to ensure quality while respecting the established time and procedures and enjoys collaborative work and teamwork. In possession of functional and technical capabilities he manages important workloads and is able to meet the expectations and demands of internal and external customers with a great spirit of collaboration.
