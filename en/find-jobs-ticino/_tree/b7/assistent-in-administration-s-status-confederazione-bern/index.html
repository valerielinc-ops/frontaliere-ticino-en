Put to the Point

As an assistant in the Administration S-Status department, you and your colleagues are responsible for the administrative handling of the S-Procedure in Switzerland.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: 420-21028

You can make this contribution

  • Complete administrative tasks in connection with the S-application dossiers from registration until and upon withdrawal from the BAZ
  • Register protection seekers in the applications MIDES and ZEMIS, create S-Dossier in the e-Asylum (eGov) and check information in the relevant personal data systems
  • Create positive decisions on the S status using standard letters on behalf of the responsible specialists and prepare the necessary documents
  • Perform simple termination procedures of the S status (expiration and revocation)
  • Process standard cantonal change requests including the corresponding correspondence

This makes them unique.

  • Commercial or equivalent education, editorial skills
  • Rapid comprehension and networked thinking in a dynamic environment
  • High personal responsibility, reliability and goal- and result-oriented working methods
  • High performance and resilience as well as flexibility, teamwork and a safe presence
  • Knowledge of a second official language, very good IT skills

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Migration moves

The temporary Section S status is responsible for the implementation of the general order on granting temporary protection in connection with the situation in Ukraine adopted by the Federal Council in March 2022. In doing so, it is responsible for the registration process of the applicants for protection, their assignment to the cantons and the decision on the granting of protection status. In addition, it is responsible for the operational execution of expiry and revocation of the S status.

As of November 2023, the S-procedure was centralized in Bern. For this purpose, two new departments of Administration S-Status were created. The Accommodation, Security and Partners section in Bern is responsible for coordinating the accommodation.
