Full description

Tasks: Our pulmonology and sleep medicine offers outpatient assessments, therapies and special consultation hours, provides a consultancy service for institutions in the region and is constantly evolving. With us you get to know a wide range of investigations.

  • Care: You care for pneumological patients.
  • Lead station: You lead the pneumological bed station under cadreic supervision.
  • Perform visits: You carry out visits independently and create therapy plans.
  • Examine: You will perform load tests, lung functions, punctures, ECG and night screenings, independently or under supervision, depending on your training level.
  • Findings: You find chest X-rays, simple sonography and point-of-care laboratory tests. | Requirements: You bring that – and what you can not yet, you learn with us.
  • Medical Diploma: You have the Federal Medical Diploma or a diploma recognised by MEBEKO.
  • Language: You speak and write German very well [level C1].
  • Design pleasure: You want to actively develop pneumology and sleep medicine.
  • Working method: You work independently and reliably
