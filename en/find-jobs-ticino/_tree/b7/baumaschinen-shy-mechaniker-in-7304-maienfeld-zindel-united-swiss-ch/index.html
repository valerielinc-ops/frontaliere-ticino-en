Construction machinery mechanic:in About the profession As a construction machine mechanic, you maintain and repair machines, vehicles and equipment used in civil engineering, special civil engineering, material processing and recycling. Often these are complex high-tech devices with electronic control elements. You perform service work, convert machines and produce spare parts. You work in our modernly furnished workshop, where machine saws, drilling and grinding machines, welding equipment, lathes, lifting platforms and special tools as well as various measuring and control equipment are available. Often you are on the road to resolve problems directly on the construction site or at the customer. Activities Repair work Construction machinery mechanics: inside perform this work on motors and transmissions of construction machinery and construction equipment. To test the electronic, electrical, mechanical and hydraulic systems, they use notebooks with fault diagnostic software or electronic measuring instruments. Service work In order for the construction machines to function as well as possible during the construction season, they check the construction machine mechanics: inside in the quieter winter time and do the service work: control of steering, brakes, ignition, tyres, oil changes, etc. Conversions Often there are special applications, for example with drilling dredgers, dismantling machines, attachments or special construction site solutions. So that these perfectly correspond to the use, there are always conversions or constructions. Construction machine mechanics: insides produce custom-fitting individual parts for this. Further work In order for our construction companies to be able to use a new machine, construction mechanics prepare for commissioning. You bring that with you Construction machinery mechanics: inside have craftsmanship and great interest in motorized equipment and machines.
