Location: Hirslanden Clinic St. Anna | Lucerne

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 71093

St. Anna Clinic is the most important private acute care clinic in Central Switzerland. Classified by the federal government as a center hospital and equipped with a public service mandate, the clinic forms an essential part of regional health care.

The Department of IVF/Reproductive Medicine offers a wide range of diagnostics and therapies related to unwanted childlessness. In our fertility centre we advise and look after our couples professionally and in the best possible way.

ITS TASKS

  • You are independently responsible for the outpatient examinations and treatments in the entire spectrum of gynecological endocrinology and reproductive medicine
  • Together with the center management and the team of the fertility clinic, they strive for a high medical quality standard with effective treatments and a high service culture.
  • Together with the management, you will be responsible for and lead the future orientation of the fertility centre at Hirslanden Klinik St. Anna.

YOUR PROFILE

  • They have the specialist title Gynecology and Obstetrics FMH as well as the Swiss recognized priority title for Gynecological Endocrinology and Reproductive Medicine
  • They have a high level of social and professional competence, maintain a team-oriented working style and appreciate the exchange in networks.
  • Their strengths include a high degree of responsibility and empathy.
  • They are innovative and purposeful
  • Flexibility and willingness to perform are among your characteristics (weekend and holiday services)

Our offer

  • In the summer of 2027 we will move to a new, highly modern IVF center in the new building Eichhof-West, Kriens
  • Family environment with proximity to employees and patients
  • Diverse training opportunities
  • Good social benefits
  • Further employment conditions and employee offers can be found here

For additional information, Dr. med. Sabine Steimann, Head of the fertility centre, is available under T.
