The Position The team In the modern laboratory of the group, 11 colleagues await you, who work in close exchange with each other and other quality control groups. At your side are laboratory assistants who, as the “right hand” of the group leader, can plan the laboratory operation and answer questions about the analytics. Beyond the groups, we are closely networked with the interfaces of pattern drawing, planning and production. A working environment awaits you that will make it easy for you to gain a foothold. We have a good mix of long-time experienced and young employees. The good and trusting cooperation in the team is an important component of our success.

Your tasks

  • You carry out independent analysis of input materials and kits with a focus on cell-based methods (FACS etc.) but also in the field of immunology and molecular biology (MagNA Pure, Cobas, MGPs etc.)
  • You select analytics results independently and document them in accordance with DIN ISO / GMP. Applicable work instructions
  • You recognize, document and edit deviations
  • You independently carry out test measurements as part of validations and optimizations of analytical test methods
  • Complete additional tasks and special analyses You are self-employed after instruction by your manager

The readiness for use in a special biological laboratory and there the handling of infectious material is desired for the position.

Your profile

Bring for this position You have the following requirements

  • You have a completed degree (Dipl., Master, Bachelor) in the field of biology, biochemistry or a comparable discipline. Alternatively, you bring a scientific education (ideally in biology) with several years of professional experience and a further education as a technician.
  • You have several years of experience in cell-based and molecular biological analysis
  • Laboratory experience in working according to GMP or in the area of quality control is an advantage
  • You master the common IT applications (e.g. MS-Office, Google Tools, SAP)
  • With your team spirit and commitment, you drive topics forward and make a significant contribution to achieving the goals of the team. You are a true team player, even in stressful situations You have the overview and do not forget the fun at work.
  • Independent work, coordination, care and reliability are among your personal strengths. You are committed and flexible, very structured, open to new things and ready to take on responsibility.

This item is limited to 24 months.
