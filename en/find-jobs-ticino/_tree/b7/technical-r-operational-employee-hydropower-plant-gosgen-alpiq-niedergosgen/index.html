Technical Operations Employee at Gösgen Hydroelectric Power Plant

Niedergösgen, CH - 100% | Full time

About the Role

Employment type: Permanent ¦ Location: Niedergösgen

Our company

Alpiq is a leading Swiss electricity producer and energy service provider with a presence throughout Europe. With a strong portfolio comprising hydropower, nuclear energy and renewable energies, we make an active contribution to climate protection and security of supply.

Our employees from various nations work together toward one goal: Together for a better climate and an improved security of supply.

Our values shape our daily actions

  • We are ALP1Q – teamwork is at the heart of everything we do
  • We learn and grow together
  • We take responsibility
  • We focus on impact

Mission

Shape the future of energy with us! With its three run-of-river power plants, Alpiq Hydro Aare AG ensures sustainable electricity production. For our facilities in Niedergösgen, we are looking for a committed personality who enjoys technology and would like to take on responsibility.

Your main responsibilities

  • Support the management and organisation of the operations team to ensure safe, economical and legally compliant operations.
  • Independently inspect and maintain the facilities and carry out repairs; execute switching orders; analyse and rectify faults.
  • Act safely and thoughtfully in the event of operational faults and carry out minor repairs.
  • Carry out electrical, mechanical and hydraulic inspections and maintenance work, outdoor work on the trash rack, boat transfers and minor work on the surrounding grounds.
  • Lead small teams, supervise fitters and technicians, and ensure occupational safety.
  • Maintain inspection and maintenance plans, document activities, and handle small projects and optimisations.

Your Impact

The extensive, modern power plant facilities offer you exciting and challenging tasks in which you can actively and energetically contribute after thorough familiarisation. As a plant specialist, you can expect a well-coordinated and highly experienced operations team as well as a collegial working atmosphere in a promising, local and meaningful energy production environment.

Your profile

  • Basic training in the electrical or mechanical field with further technical education (e.g. HF technician).
  • Professional experience in the power plant sector or comparable industry, with practical maintenance experience being an advantage.
  • Willingness to work on-call duty every 5–6 weeks (Niedergösgen and Winznau should be reachable from your home within 30 minutes).
  • Expertise in mechanics, hydraulics, electrical engineering and automation; organisation and documentation of processes.
  • Independent, resilient, team-oriented and flexible.
  • Fluent in German
  • English and French are an advantage.
  • Category B driving licence.
