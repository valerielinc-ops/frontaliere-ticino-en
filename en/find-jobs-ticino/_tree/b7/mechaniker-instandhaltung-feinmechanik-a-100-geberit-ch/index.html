Full description

MAIN TASKS

In your role, you are responsible for the maintenance, repair and maintenance of the raw material supply as well as the production facilities and machines. You carry out preventive maintenance measures to ensure plant availability. This includes in particular the following activities:

  • Fault analysis and troubleshooting of mechanical, hydraulic and pneumatic systems
  • Documentation of the work carried out and maintenance of the maintenance database
  • Cooperation in the optimization and improvement of production processes
  • Support for the installation and commissioning of new plants

PROFILE

You bring a degree as a polymechanic EFZ or a comparable education and have several years of professional experience in the areas of maintenance. In addition, the following characteristics and abilities characterize you:

  • In-depth knowledge in mechanics, hydraulics and pneumatics, as well as expertise in manufacturing and production processes
  • Analytical skills and structured approach
  • Experienced handling of MS-Office programs and good SAP knowledge
  • Self-employment, a high degree of personal responsibility and commitment
  • Team spirit and reliability

ADDITIONAL NOTES

For this position, we only consider direct applications.
