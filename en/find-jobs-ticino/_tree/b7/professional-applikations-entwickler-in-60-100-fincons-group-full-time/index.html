Full description

The Fincons Group is an IT consulting company that has been shaping the digital future of leading companies in international markets for 40 years. The Fincons Group has made a name for itself by predicting and successfully interpreting new business models and the rapid development of IT systems. She has built a complete range of services: from research and consulting to design and development, from system integration of leading software solutions to application management, supporting her customers step by step on their digital transformation journey. Fincons is a multinational company with more than 2,600 employees and several offices worldwide (in Italy, Switzerland, Germany, France, the United Kingdom, Belgium and the United States), but above all a family business where the founders play a strategic role with commitment and passion, building the company on the same principles of a united and caring family. Tasks:

  • Participation in the conception and further development of application-specific UX/UI concepts and design principles taking into account defined design and usability standards.
  • Development, further development and maintenance of the front end of a central application in the area of vulnerability management.
  • Professional sparring partner for architectural topics as well as active support in the evaluation and further development of technical solutions.
  • Development and provision of APIs with PHP as well as integration and connection of backend interfaces in Angular applications.
  • Ensuring high software quality through compliance with coding, architecture and design standards as well as continuous optimization of the source code.

Requirements

  • In-depth knowledge of Angular, HTML, CSS and JavaScript as well as experience in UX/UI design and API development with PHP.
  • Strong teamwork and communication skills, as well as the ability to work effectively with diverse stakeholders and interdisciplinary teams.
  • Independent, structured and responsible working method with a high level of initiative.
  • High willingness to learn and quick comprehension in order to familiarize oneself with complex professional and technical contexts.
  • Close cooperation with various specialist and project roles to ensure a consistent and user-centred user experience.
  • Very good knowledge of German (C1/C2) and good knowledge of English.

Why Fincons?

  • Career & further development: Benefit from individual development opportunities.
