Full description

Start on 1 August 2027 with the three-year apprenticeship specialist health 100 - 100%

Be there as one of the 20 learning professional / health professional! In this profession, you accompany, care for and care for people who are dependent on support – and thus do meaningful things every day. We offer you an exciting and practical education in Chur, in which you can grow both professionally and personally.

Exactly your thing? Perfect, then apply now and start your future in care!

These are your tasks

  • You care for patients in the stationary area and support them in everyday life
  • You perform medical-technical tasks such as blood sampling, medication delivery or blood pressure measurements carefully and professionally
  • You promote the physical, social and psychological well-being of patients and always align your actions according to their individual needs
  • You take on administrative and logistical tasks that are important for a smooth working day

You bring that with you

  • You have completed compulsory schooling and are ready to enter the professional world
  • You support people in need of care and help and actively shape their everyday life
  • You are responsible, flexible, team capable and resilient – both physically and psychologically
  • You have a good power of observation and perception and can quickly assess situations

Can you do something that others can’t? You make the difference? Then you belong to us!

And that's what we offer you.

  • Career and family: As a flexible employer, we promote different working models so that you can optimally combine work and family for yourself. We also offer you and your family a variety of support services.
  • Holidays / loyalty leave: The annual holiday entitlement with us is at least 25 days. Long-term employees: insiders are rewarded with an additional loyalty leave.
  • Flexible working hours / Home Office / Part-time work: If the company allows it, working hours and expenses can be flexibly tailored to your current living situation.
  • Gastronomic offer: All four locations of the cantonal hospital Graubünden offer a wide range of catering options. With us you can eat varied, balanced and healthy.
  • Health: The health of our employees: inside is important to us: The cantonal hospital Graubünden was awarded the quality label Friendy Work Space in November 2022.
  • Accommodation for staff: For our employees, interns and apprentices, we provide staff rooms and apartments at a discount as far as possible.
  • Social benefits: In addition to the legally prescribed benefits, we offer you supplementary and generous non-compulsory benefits (such as 100% wage continuation, waiver of coordination deduction at PK).
  • Further education and personal development: We continuously invest in the education, training and further education of our employees. With us you will be supported, motivated, challenged and promoted.
  • Our values: The excellent quality of our services and the health and satisfaction of our employees are equally our focus. An open, appreciative corporate culture, diversity, tolerance and sustainability are just as much an integral part of our philosophy as progressive employment conditions and diverse development opportunities.
