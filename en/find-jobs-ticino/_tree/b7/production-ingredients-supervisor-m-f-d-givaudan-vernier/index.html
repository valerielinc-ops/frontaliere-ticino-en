Production supervisor ingredients (h/f/d) - Your next job?

As Production Supervisor Ingredients (h/f/d)in Vernier, you are attached to our Ingredient Productionin Sector 190 and you report to our Production Chemistry.

You lead a production team and coordinate the industry's activities to ensure that intermediate or finished products are delivered in accordance with established production specifications, while respecting safety, quality, cost and service level standards.

You are your sector's focal point for issues related to production activity, human resources management and security.

You ensure that employees in your sector have the knowledge, training and experience to effectively fill their positions. You also make sure that they continuously develop the skills required to perform in their position, in line with the technical and technological evolution of the company.

You're interested in this job? This could be the perfect opportunity if, as for us, your goal is to always meet the needs of customers and touch the emotions of the world around you.

Your Main Mission

Sector management

  • You ensure the smooth running of your sector's production activities and ensure the achievement of safety, quality, cost and timeliness objectives;
  • You plan, coordinate and optimize the production plan by ensuring the availability of the necessary human and technical resources;
  • You ensure compliance with the manufacturing program, processes, work instructions and operational standards;
  • You identify performance gaps, analyze their causes and implement appropriate corrective actions with the teams involved;
  • You contribute to the resolution of operational problems in collaboration with support functions in order to guarantee lasting solutions;
  • You define the production and performance objectives of your sector and follow the associated key indicators;
  • You ensure the application of EHS requirements, participate in risk analyses and promote an exemplary safety culture within your team;
  • You guarantee the proper use of production facilities, prioritise maintenance needs and contribute to projects to improve and develop equipment;
  • Finally, you drive the continuous improvement process by encouraging problem solving, maintaining 5S standards and optimizing your industry's performance.

Team Management

  • You frame, accompany and develop a production team to ensure efficient operation and optimal use of the means of production;
  • You are involved in recruiting, integrating and increasing the skills of employees, both permanent and temporary;
  • You organize staffing and planning to meet operational needs while ensuring efficient resource management;
  • You identify training needs, develop individual development plans and ensure that your team maintains its skills;
  • You support team leaders in the development of their collaborators and support the integration paths of newcomers;
  • Community leader, you drive, motivate and unite your teams around the sector's objectives and accompany change with benevolence and demand;
  • You ensure respect for HR processes and manage individual situations related to performance, behaviour or labour relations by fostering constructive dialogue and lasting solutions;
  • Finally, you are creating a collaborative, accountable and focused working environment for continuous improvement, security and operational excellence.

  • You benefit from a Technologist CFC in chemical and pharmaceutical production or from training deemed equivalent. Yellow Belt certification is an asset;
  • You have 8 to 10 years experience in chemical production ideally in a similar function;
  • You have 2 to 5 years experience in team management;
  • You have a perfect command of French and a good knowledge of technical English (understanding documents, training, interaction in simple discussions, ...);
  • You have a good command of SAP and you have very good knowledge of computer tools such as Microsoft Office;
  • You have a solid understanding of chemical processes.
  • You have strong knowledge of chemistry-related industrial techniques and infrastructure.
  • With a strong sense of responsibility, you are able to evolve effectively in a demanding environment while bringing together teams
  • You demonstrate a proactive attitude to changes and ongoing improvements to the site.
