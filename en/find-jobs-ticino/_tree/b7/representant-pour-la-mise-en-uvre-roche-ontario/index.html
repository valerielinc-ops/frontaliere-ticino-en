The Opportunity

As the Field Support Implementation Representative, you are part of a team of experts who play a crucial role in supporting key hospitals and laboratories with the installation and smooth functioning of their clinical chemistry, immunoassay, molecular, pathology, urinalysis, and automation solutions.

You are an effective problem solver passionate about the healthcare environment and understand the importance of our solutions on the customer experience and patient care. Your passion for problem resolution and customer satisfaction is supported by your technical knowledge to deliver a stellar customer experience. You will provide timely, accurate and consistent support to our customers to minimize the disruption of their diagnostic service delivery.

This role is installations and modifications focused but does not exclude maintenance and repair support, as required.

  • Customer-Centric Focus & Experience: Use a customer-centric focus to deliver a flawless and positive customer experience, complying with administrative and customer experience standards with each service interaction.
  • Communication & Collaboration: Communicate regularly with national and regional squad members and the National Implementation Manager to gain or share perspective on the customer environment, their expectations, and their experience. Participate in service, sales and customer meetings, where required.
  • Implementation & Deployment: Act as an implementation crew team member or lead to i nstall and configure instrument software and hardware components and maximize efficient completion of installations (including pre-site inspections and technical installation, upgrades, and issue resolution, where required).
  • Team Support, Accountability & Coverage: Back up other Support Representatives for vacation and holiday rotation coverage, where required.
  • Administrative management and documentation: Document all service related activities (inventory, service activity cases, expense reports, etc.), facilitating smooth hand-off to regional teams. Manage day-to-day administrative activities.
  • Maintenance & Technical Support: Perform preventative maintenance and technical service bulletin resolution to support regional teams, and work with local teams to install and configure instrument software and hardware components, as needed.
  • IT Systems Integration: Work with local RIS application specialist and technical specialist teams to install, configure, and support IT software and hardware components, as needed.
