For more than 170 years we have embraced a bold, pioneering spirit that continues to inspire our teams across all Professions from our boutiques to our workshops and corporate offices. Our 10,000+ colleagues of more than 105 nationalities are united by a shared independent spirit and commitment to excellence, striking to continuously rich our Maison

YOUR MISSION

Within our Maison des Métiers d'art, you ensure the enamelling of the watch elements by applying the operating ranges using equipment, machinery or tools in accordance with the requirements of quality, delay and safety.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT?

Ensures the enamelling of the parts in accordance with the operating ranges •

Powers its workstation into components according to the order of manufacturing orders

Proposes enamels in correlation with the design of Creation

Apply enamelling techniques

Document the work done (written, photos)

Provides repairs and restorations on enamelled parts

Sets requirements to repair and/or restore enamelled parts

Participates in continuous improvement of working environment

Following technological developments in the field of enamelling

Participates in the capitalization of know-how

Is a proposal force for Creation and to improve productivity

Checks manufacturing conformity

Participates in training activities in the context of developments in the manufacturing process

HOW ARE YOU TO BE US?

E-mail in the field of watchmaking / jewellery with certificate from the Swiss watchmaking industry employers' convention or equivalent

At least 3 years of experience in a similar position

Good knowledge of materials (emel, paint)

Self-employed, meticulous and who likes to work as a team

A plus

Ability to reproduce art drawings: for grey

Attraction for innovation

Knowledge of jewellery
