Full description

Background As part of the modernization of its energy trading information system, our customer is looking for a Technical Analyst specializing in ETRM platforms. You will be responsible for the design, development and maintenance of the platform's technical components, as well as their integration into the existing IT ecosystem. You will help ensure the performance, reliability and scalability of solutions supporting trading, risk management and settlement processes.

Responsibilities

Development and integration

  • Develop new features and customize the ETRM platform using editor APIs and internal frameworks.
  • Design and develop application components in C#, .NET and Python.
  • Develop and maintain user interfaces with Angular, KendoUI and AG Grid.
  • Design and maintain interfaces between the ETRM platform and peripheral systems: EDM, Risk Engines, Schedule, Settlement and Finance.
  • Ensure data integrity and real-time flow performance.

Tests and deployment

  • Prepare and perform unit, integration and non-regression tests.
  • Participate in UAT phases and performance tests.
  • Manage deployments on different environments.
  • Collaborate with DevOps teams on CI/CD pipelines.
  • Monitor and optimize application performance, including complex valuation and P&L calculations.

Support and collaboration

  • Collaborate with Business Analysts, Product Owners and technical teams of publishers.
  • Translate functional requirements into robust technical solutions.
  • Provide Level 3 (L3) support for critical incidents.
  • Participate in root cause analyses and implement ongoing corrections.
  • Document technical developments and developments.
  • Minimum 10 years of development experience in trading or financial environments.
  • Master's degree in Orchestra.
  • Excellent mastery of C#, .NET Core and Python.
  • Good knowledge of REST APIs, SQL and Open APIs.
  • Experience with Docker and Master of Kubernetes (OpenShift is a plus).
  • Knowledge of Angular, KendoUI and AG Grid.
  • Experience in AWS and On-Premise environments.
  • Good understanding of energy trading processes, Risk Management and P&L.
  • Mastery of DevOps, CI/CD and Agile/Scrum practices.
  • Advanced computer or engineering training.
  • Common English, French appreciated.
