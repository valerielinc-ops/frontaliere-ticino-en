Full description

Caritas St.Gallen-Appenzell is the charity of the Diocese of St.Gallen. We work with concrete offers and social commitment for people affected by poverty, excluded and in need. Do you speak Church and Caritas? The Church and Caritas share a common mission: to support people, assume social responsibility and live solidarity in concrete ways. Different experiences, perspectives and working methods meet. This is precisely where there are opportunities to learn from each other and make a difference together. We are looking for a personality who knows these worlds, creates connections and shapes the diaconal mission with conviction and joy. .

What you do with us

  • You maintain and shape the cooperation between Caritas St.Gallen-Appenzell, the Diocese of St.Gallen and other church partners.
  • You bring social experiences and perspectives to church committees and development processes and promote the dialogue between church and Caritas.
  • You connect theological, social and socio-political questions and contribute to the strategic development of our organization.
  • You are involved in regional and national church and social networks.
  • You lead the Diakonieanimation division with four employees and help shape its educational and project work.
  • You are a member of the management team and bring your professional perspective there.

What you bring

  • A theological education, in-depth knowledge of the Catholic Church and ideally a good network in the diocese of St.Gallen.
  • A strong understanding of social and socio-political issues as well as the realities of life of disadvantaged people.
  • The joy of dialogue, the ability to bring together different perspectives and a good sense of institutional connections.
  • Strategic thinking, initiative and the willingness to take responsibility for a small team. Leadership experience is welcome, but not a requirement.
  • Communication strength, openness, patience and a healthy dose of humor.
