Full description

Our physiotherapy services, located in Delémont and Porrentruy (Jura / Switzerland), offer varied and quality services, adapted to the needs of all ages. Our teams work on a wide variety of pathologies, linked to injuries, chronic diseases or post-operative suites, in a professional and welcoming environment.

Responsibilities

  • · Ensure patient care according to a specific treatment plan, based on medical prescription, assessment and diagnosis.
  • · Conduct functional evaluations and implement treatments tailored to the needs and capacities of patients.
  • · Work closely with the interdisciplinary team to ensure comprehensive and coherent management.
