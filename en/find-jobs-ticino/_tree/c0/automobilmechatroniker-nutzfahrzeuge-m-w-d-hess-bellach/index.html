Automotive mechatronics engineer for commercial vehicles (m/f/d | 100%)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 600 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our modular system “Co-Bolt”. We are the only Swiss bus manufacturer to go far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The culture of innovation is the fuel of our company.

To strengthen our team in the assembly of bus production in the operational area, we are looking for motivated specialists with know-how in vehicle mechanics for the PDI work at the end of the manufacturing process of our electric buses.

As a PDI specialist for our innovative electric buses, you play a crucial role in the production chain. Your job is to ensure that every bus that leaves our production halls is in perfect condition and meets the highest safety standards. If you want to take responsibility, you've come to the right place! Your tasks

  • Carrying out all “pre-delivery inspection” work on our electric buses (as specified by the development department)
  • Commissioning of tachographs and the RAG
  • Testing and commissioning of the brakes according to WABCO system specifications including creation of test reports for official bodies
  • Measurement and adjustment of the complete steering geometry and the drive motors
  • Taking turning circle measurements
  • Calibration of the bus joints and the steering angle sensors
  • Planning of the work to be carried out in consultation with the assembly management
  • Design and implementation of sensible time management for the implementation of PDI appointments
  • Driving articulated and double-articulated buses during vehicle moves and shifting
  • Helping to optimize work processes and workplaces

Our requirements

  • Completed apprenticeship as an automotive mechatronics engineer specializing in commercial vehicles, agricultural machinery mechanics or construction machinery mechanics
  • Authorization to drive heavy commercial vehicles (category C) or willingness to take the driving test (costs covered by the employer)
  • Strong can-do mentality
  • Working independently and dealing with sensitive facilities
  • Flexibility and reliability

We offer you

  • Varied, interesting and long-term work at the cutting edge of electromobility
  • Permanent position in a successful, traditional company that places great value on quality
  • Flexible working hours
  • Good pay
  • Opportunity for further training and development

If you feel addressed, send us your complete application documents including a letter of motivation via link (Apply now).
