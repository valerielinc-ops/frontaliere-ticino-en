Full description

To strengthen our team at AMEOS Spital Einsiedeln search by 1. December 2026 a committed personality as

Operational buyer (m/f/d) — temporary for 6 months, 40 %

Their advantages

  • An interesting, versatile and demanding activity
  • Strong and motivated team
  • Modern employment conditions and a good working atmosphere
  • Interesting benefits
  • Attractive offer for further education and training, which we support generously

Their tasks

  • Processing of special requirements in the field of medicine, care and administration
  • Ensuring needs-based and cost-efficient procurement
  • Maintaining master data in the material management system
  • Maintaining and managing supplier relationships as well as price and contract negotiations
  • Audit and release of invoices in close cooperation with accounting
  • Support for local/regional purchasing issues
  • Organization and information sharing in the event of product changes, recalls or the like in cooperation with strategic purchasing
  • Support if necessary. Participation in the respective meetings (e.g. purchasing group, procurement commission meeting, etc.)

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training in the commercial or health sector
  • Relevant professional experience in purchasing desirable, preferably in the healthcare industry or a comparable sector
  • Commitment and assertiveness in the design and support of work and change processes
  • In-depth knowledge of a material management program desirable
  • Professional handling of MS Office products
  • Goal-oriented and independent work as well as good organizational ability
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
