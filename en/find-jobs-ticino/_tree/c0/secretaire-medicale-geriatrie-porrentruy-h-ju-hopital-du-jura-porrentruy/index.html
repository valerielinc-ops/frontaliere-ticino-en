Full description

Our geriatric units, located on the Porrentruy site, welcome elderly patients requiring specific medical, nursing and psychosocial care. We support our patients with an interdisciplinary approach which aims to preserve their autonomy, improve their quality of life and support their loved ones in a caring and secure environment.

We are looking for an immediate entry into office

Activity rate: 80% for a period of 6 months Specificities: Possibility of changing the position to a permanent contract of 40-50%.

Responsibilities

  • Management of discharge letters (corrections, geriatric standards to be completed, follow-up).
  • Scanning and recording of documents for patient records (SIC).
  • Telephone switchboard.
  • Generate summaries of multidisciplinary conferences.
  • Organize family conferences.
  • Establishment of prescriptions and certificates.
  • Management of post-graduate training for doctors in training (invitation-program-contacts).
  • Reception of internal doctors and CDC and preparation – transmission of the kit (envelope).
  • Updating the space reserved for doctors from the different departments on the Extranet for training and documentation relating to the service.

Qualifications

  • Medical secretary diploma or title deemed equivalent.
  • A few years' experience as a medical secretary in the field of geriatrics would be an asset.
  • Mastery of the French language.
  • Good knowledge of medical terminology essential.
  • Mastery of computer tools (Word, Excel, Powerpoint, Outlook).
  • Knowledge of SIC software (an asset).
  • Excellent organizational skills, sense of priorities and initiative.
  • Dynamic personality able to work independently.
  • Flexibility and availability to replace colleagues in the event of absence.

Benefits

  • Fast decision-making pathways with a positive work culture promoting open and respectful communication.
  • Stimulating work environment within a dynamic and committed team.
  • A cafeteria restaurant with preferential rates.
