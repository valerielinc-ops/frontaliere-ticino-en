SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. Become part of the “Service Desk External” division, which has been growing continuously since October 2021, within sourcing, recruiting and talents in SBB’s HR and shape the further development of our service for Switzerland with personal responsibility and commitment. As a competence center for external personnel, we advise and support the specialist areas of SBB professionally and uniformly – from demand assessment to exit. In order to successfully cope with the planned increase in the areas under our supervision, we need several team players: inside to ensure quality, integration and support. This post shall be limited in time until 31 December 2027. You can move that.

  • You take on the administrative responsibility for the onboarding of new areas (including data collection and analysis) and participate in the central management of external staff.
  • The internal and external contacts (hiring companies, executives, external colleagues, purchasing, finance, etc.) will accompany you professionally throughout the entire onboarding process and ensure partnership-based solutions.
  • You support the entire procurement process (requirements analysis, tendering, pre-selection, conclusion of contracts) of the external staff in the personnel rental, with the aim of attracting the best temporary staff for SBB.
  • Your responsibility lies in exciting projects for process optimization, quality assurance and implementation of new tools as well as the opportunity to actively contribute to the expansion of the External Service Desk.

You bring that with you.

  • Every day we are faced with many challenges. Therefore, we are looking for several solution-oriented and customer-focused personalities who convince with their winning appearance and always keep a cool head.
  • We evolve every day and want to be among the best. Therefore, you are committed and challenges spur you. You actively share your knowledge and you are motivated to further your teammates.
  • In our continuously growing field, an excellent organization and in-depth IT knowledge (such as SAP HR, MS Office, SAP Fieldglass) is an advantage. We are looking for several colleagues who already have professional experience in the commercial sector and are just as convinced as we that the direction of human resources reflects their passion. Experience in personnel rental is a big plus – but not a must.
  • We work for the whole of Switzerland, so different language skills are of great importance to us. In German, you are confident in negotiations and have a very good knowledge of French (level B2-C1). Italian would be the icing on the i.
