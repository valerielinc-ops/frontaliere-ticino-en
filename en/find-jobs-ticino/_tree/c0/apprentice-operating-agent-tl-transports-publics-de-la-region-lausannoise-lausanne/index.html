In the event of a change of course, we are committed to supporting you throughout your training to develop the knowledge necessary for your professional success.

Are you looking for a place to learn in a company that offers its apprentices the best conditions for training? Are you motivated, curious and willing to invest in your future? Then join us!

On 1 August 2027 we are looking for a

Apprentices operating agents Your mission

As an apprentice operating agent, you are responsible for the maintenance and proper functioning of our external infrastructure and spaces.

  • Maintenance of buildings, infrastructure and green spaces, using professional cleaning tools and products
  • Small mechanical repairs and monitoring of technical installations
  • Support for technicians responsible for maintenance during operations or breakdowns
  • Maintenance of indoor plants, lawns, floors and windows (with strict compliance with environmental protection requirements)

Your profile

  • Aged between 15 and 20 years, to benefit from the compulsory school leaving certificate
  • High interest in manual work
  • Sense of order and cleanliness
  • Logic and methodical
  • Want to learn, learn, engage in learning
  • Service and sociality

Further information

What we offer you

  • 13 months' salary
  • 6 weeks of vacation up to 20 years
  • Annual exit of apprentices
  • A half-yearly bonus based on results and benefits
  • Work hons provided and maintained by the company
  • Recreational activity and fitness room at preferential prices
  • Business restaurant at headquarters with preferential prices for apprentices
  • Free of charge for transport on the network or acquisition of a general subscription at preferential prices
  • Structured coaching to help you achieve success
  • The opportunity for some apprentices to continue with us after training

Applications must contain

  • Letter of motivation explaining your willingness to join the tl
  • curriculum vitae
  • Copy of school bulletins for the last three years
  • Work experience or internships
  • Copy of the document, identity and residence permit (if you are not Swiss)

In the tl, talent has no gender: we accept with the same interest women and men candidates.
