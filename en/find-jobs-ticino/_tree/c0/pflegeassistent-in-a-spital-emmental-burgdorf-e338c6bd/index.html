Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

Work when others still dream of work? Not with us. Do you want to work in health care and still have your evenings, weekends and holidays for you? Then we have good news: You work with us from Monday to Friday – without any weekend, piquet or night services. Best of all, you’ll be part of a warm team in a growing gastroenterological department where coherence, appreciation and humor are just as important as professionalism. For our subsidiary Gastroenterologie am Spital Emmental AG we search for the site in Burgdorf immediately or by agreement:

Your tasks

  • Processing, cleaning and storage of endoscopes in accordance with applicable quality and hygiene standards
  • Provision and withdrawal of endoscopes for examinations
  • Documentation of preparation processes
  • Support for material management and organisation of study rooms
  • Cooperation in the recovery room, preparation and follow-up of patients
  • Administrative and logistical activities
  • Co-creation of a positive and appreciative working environment

Your profile

  • Completed training as a nursing assistant or comparable qualification
  • Readiness to complete the H+ certificate in endoscopy preparation
  • Interest in gastroenterology, endoscopy and hygienic treatment processes
  • Experience in dealing with medical equipment, in particular endoscopes, is advantageous
  • Enjoying precise, responsible and careful work
  • Technical understanding and interest in medical processes
  • Teamwork, communication strength and reliability
  • Flexibility, responsibility and high quality awareness
  • Motivation for continuous further education and personal development
