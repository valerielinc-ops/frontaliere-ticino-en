Full description

Where power meets ability. ####

With a leading Swiss provider of sustainable construction solutions, you move as a construction machine operator: in tons of excavators through the quarry and ensure with precision and safety awareness that the extraction of raw materials runs smoothly.

The main tasks ####

  • Operation and maintenance of construction machinery
  • Compliance with safety, quality and environmental standards
  • Assistance in troubleshooting installations
  • Cleanliness of area and roads
  • Support for preparations for raw material mining

The requirement profile ####

  • Vocational mechanical training or several years of experience in the construction sector
  • Experience in operating large construction machines, especially excavators from 30 tons
  • Knowledge of maintenance and maintenance of construction machinery
  • Driving licence category B and training of construction machinery drivers
  • Experience in quarrying or civil engineering, tunnelling and earth construction is advantageous
  • Good knowledge of German, English is advantageous
