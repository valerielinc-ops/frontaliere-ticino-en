Reference code: JR134319

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

Operational Quality Manager (M/F/X) - HOM 2 years Post based in Buttes

HOW DO YOU HAVE AN IMPACT?

In this function, you ensure the conformity of products in production through the deployment and monitoring of quality methods and tools, from development to serial life. You are the reference quality of a Autonomous Entity of Production (EAP) and you are in charge of in particular to animate problem solving with multidisciplinary teams, in order to eradicate the causes of appearance.

You will be responsible for

  • Actively participate in the deployment of quality methods and tools in the Manufacture according to current standards.
  • Pilot and animate problem solving at the PAE level.
  • Ensure that internal and external non-compliances are addressed to ensure follow-up and treatment.
  • Initiate stock and stock security, ensuring the quarantine and traceability of non-compliant parts.
  • Piloting non-quality costs in partnership with production.
  • Be the reference quality associated with new projects, in pairs with a product project manager.
  • Pilot transversal product reliability projects and support production teams in the quality control of manufacturing processes.
  • Trigger and conduct client audits, suppliers.

HOW ARE YOU GONNA USE US?

  • To the benefit of an engineering training (HES, EPFL or equivalent), a Master's in Quality Management or a diploma deemed equivalent
  • You have at least 5 years of experience in the field of quality, in an industrial sector.
  • You master the quality methods and tools, the means of routine control, the reading of the plan and the statistical analysis. The knowledge of watchmaking is a plus
  • You master common IT tools as well as standard quality and statistical analysis software.
  • At home in a dynamic environment, you have a good analytical and synthesis capacity and perform your work rigorously and accurately (sense of detail).
  • You also have a good ability to work as a team and you are comfortable with the practice of transversal management and communication.

WHAT DISTINGUES OUR GROUP?

Our true strength lies not in our similarities, but in the rich diversity of our arts, cultures and human skills, as well as in our specific ability to encourage untapped potentials.

  • We value freedom, collegiality, loyalty and solidarity.
  • We encourage empathy, curiosity, courage, humility and integrity.
  • We take care of the world we live in.

YOUR TRAVEL WITH US

Join a stimulating work environment where the legacy of luxury watchmaking meets innovation.
