Full description

This is what awaits you

As part of Mobi24 AG, a company belonging to the Mobiliar Group, you will support and advise our customers in exceptional situations and emergencies.

This is what you do with us

You answer calls from our customers outside the opening hours of the Mobiliar general agencies.

You simply provide our customers with information, carry out address changes and forward callback requests to the general agencies.

You record claims electronically and take on further administrative tasks.

This is what you bring with you

  • Ongoing studies or completed teaching
  • Enjoyment of customer contact and strong communication skills
  • Team skills and independent, customer-oriented working methods
  • Flexibility and high load capacity
  • Readiness for irregular and sometimes short work assignments (e.g. morning, noon, evening until 20:30 as well as on weekends and public holidays)
  • Fluent knowledge of German and good knowledge of Italian. Additional solid knowledge of French is beneficial

Working with us

Your assignment starts from February 2027 or by agreement and is limited to one year, with the possibility of extension. The workload is 30%. During the summer months, a higher workload is also possible by arrangement.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
