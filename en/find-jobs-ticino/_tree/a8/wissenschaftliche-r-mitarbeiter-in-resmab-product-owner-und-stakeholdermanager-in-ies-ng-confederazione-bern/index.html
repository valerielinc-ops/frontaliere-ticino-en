Put to the Point

Promote the protection of the population and crisis management in the health sector – we offer you this exciting and versatile task. As a product owner and stakeholder manager in the National Emergency Operation Center (NEOC) division, you play a central role in the further development and introduction of the new IES NG deployment system. In the event of an incident, you are part of the “Operational Organization National Alert Centre” (EO NAZ) and support emergency and crisis management in the healthcare sector.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20959
  • Location: Guisanplatz 1b, 3014 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Responsible for system management, system administration and user management of the information and deployment system New Generation (IES NG).
  • Maintaining and expanding stakeholder relationships through regular feedback and optimization of communication in order to integrate the needs of stakeholders in the national and international context into product development.
  • Plan and control the releases of the IES NG application, including testing, acceptance and release.
  • develop conceptual bases and prepare documentation for training and training, ensure the implementation of training and training.
  • Responsible for test management in connection with updates and changes to the infrastructure (concept, implementation, logging and acceptance).

This makes them unique.

  • Completed university of applied sciences or equivalent education or several years of proven equivalent professional experience.
  • Strong negotiating skills, persuasion and assertiveness.
  • Experience in release, change and lifecycle management in complex systems.
  • Experience in the administration and user management of critical mission applications or systems.
  • Experience and knowledge of the health service, in particular in the ambulance service.
  • Active knowledge of two official languages, passive knowledge and active English language skills in words and writing.

The Federal Office for Civil Protection BABS is the nationally recognized coordination office for civil protection between federal, cantonal and third parties. It provides fundamentals and coordination, particularly in the areas of risk analysis, warning and alerting, training, telematics and protective infrastructure, as well as disaster medicine, ABC protection and disarmament. Together with the cantons and other national and international partners, it has the capabilities, current technologies and infrastructure for dealing with disasters and emergencies.

Women and people from the French and Italian linguistic communities are still underrepresented in our administrative unit. Your applications are therefore particularly welcome.
