Full description

You want to solve complex insurance cases and accompany the insured with your expertise back to work? Then you are right with us. What you do

  • As part of an ongoing project, you will work on complex insurance cases with accident and illness consequences according to the Military Insurance Act (MVG) until mid-2029.
  • You check performance and liability claims and charge daily allowances.
  • You clarify insurance and medical issues in cooperation with insurance physicians: internal and external partners.
  • You advise and accompany the insured competently and support them on their way back to working life.
  • You check medical expense bills and make sure that the applicable tariffs and legal bases are applied correctly. What you bring
  • You have a completed commercial or paramedical training.
  • Ideally, you will bring experience in the insurance industry or social security.
  • A professional card in the field of social insurance is a plus.
  • You work independently, structured and with a lot of initiative and communicate safely with insured persons, service providers, employers and other stakeholders.
  • You speak fluent German. Further language skills are advantageous.
