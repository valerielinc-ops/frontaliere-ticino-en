Shift manager: cleaning 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Shift manager: cleaning Rapperswil SG, September 1st, 2026, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. As a cleaning shift manager, you and your team will ensure that our customers travel on clean trains. You lead the shift, help clean and ensure the quality of cleaning. You coordinate human resources according to requirements and ordered services and are the contact person for your team and for communication with stakeholders. You see irregular working hours and shift work as an advantage in order to enjoy your free time while others are working. We pay additional wages for night and weekend work, for example with an additional hourly wage of 16 francs for Sunday work. For night shifts, you receive a time allowance of 10 to 30%, which gives you more free time. You can find benefits such as free GA and discounted travel abroad

  • You have a good knowledge of German (level B1); Knowledge at level B2 is an advantage.
