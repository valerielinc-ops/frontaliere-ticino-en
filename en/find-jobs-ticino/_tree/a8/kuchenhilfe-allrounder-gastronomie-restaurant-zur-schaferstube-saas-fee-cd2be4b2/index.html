Full description

Our profile

Since June 2019 we have been able to run the Schäferstube and inspire our guests with great pleasure. On the forest edge of Saas-Fee, directly accessible by the skis (SKI IN SKI OUT), the Schäferstube with its unique sun terrace is definitely worth a visit. As young hosts of the region, we revive the gastronomy of Saas-Fee. Both of us were able to work as management positions in upscale restaurants and hotels and acquire in-depth expertise there.

Honest craftsmanship mixed with creativity, precision and a lot of love for domestic, high-quality products set us apart.

Your profile

  • You are motivated, flexible, resilient and open to new things
  • You enjoy working in a small, dynamic team where everyone supports each other
  • You can make it possible to work quickly when washing dishes
  • You are happy to support the kitchen team
  • You already have some experience in the kitchen/with kitchen work
  • You are also ready to take on tasks outside
  • Honesty and reliability are a matter of course for you
  • You want to develop yourself personally and professionally

Look forward to a great winter in the mountains of Saas-Fee and send us your application.
