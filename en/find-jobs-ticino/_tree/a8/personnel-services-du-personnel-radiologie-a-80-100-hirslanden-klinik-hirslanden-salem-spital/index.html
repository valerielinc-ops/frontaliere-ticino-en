Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Bern Occupation by: Immediately or by appointment

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

With us at the Department of Radiology Services, you will find a dedicated, experienced team of 22 motivated colleagues who work with enthusiasm and passion. Our department is a place where innovation, team spirit and humanity are lived every day – Together we shape the future of radiology.

According to our guiding principle “I, You, We are Hirslanden Bern” we cultivate a respectful, open and supportive approach – within the team as well as interdisciplinary. Trust, appreciation and a positive attitude are the basis of our cooperation. We believe that every individual counts and actively promote individual strengths and development opportunities.

You can rejoice.

  • Central workplace in the heart of the city of Bern – with the best accessibility
  • Varied everyday work through rotation at six modern locations in Bern, Bümpliz
  • Dynamic environment: New encounters, new tasks and changing teams every day
  • Scheduled working hours: Monday to Friday
  • Structured familiarization – step by step into the fascinating world of radiology

YOUR TASKS

  • You are the friendly face at the reception – for patients and doctors in our radiology locations: Salem Hospital, Medical Center Wankdorf, Klinik Beau-Site, Klinik Permanence
  • In the call center you coordinate appointments, plan radiological examinations and are the central contact for our customers.
  • You inform, accompany and prepare patients for radiological examinations
  • You take care of administrative tasks, such as sending out findings and documents or obtaining medical records
  • As a hub in everyday practice, you keep an overview and act confidently at the interface between medicine, administration and patient service.

Your profile

  • You have completed basic training in a service-oriented industry
  • You are known as a communication strong, flexible and socially competent personality
  • A natural, heartfelt appearance with a lot of empathy is important to you
  • Your PC skills are very good and you are confident in using digital tools
  • You have very good knowledge of German and French, other languages are a plus

A variety of benefits and attractive employment conditions can be found under: Team diagnosis - What we lack is you.

For additional information, Kathrin Stettler, Head of Radiology Services, is available at T or kathrin.stettler@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact and application.
