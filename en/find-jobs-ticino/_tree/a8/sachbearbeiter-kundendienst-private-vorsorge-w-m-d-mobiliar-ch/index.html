Full description

This is what awaits you

In this role, you complement our team of 18 people and are the first point of contact for our customers and our general agencies around the topic of private provision.

This is what you do with us

Your main task is to answer questions by phone and email professionally and competently.

Thanks to your social competence and your strong sense of service, you provide our customers with all the necessary information about our private pension products.

In collaboration with various departments, you coordinate complex requests and ensure that they are handled at a high level.

This is what you bring with you

  • Basic commercial education or equivalent
  • Experience in customer contact and customer-oriented thinking
  • Preventive knowledge is beneficial, but lateral beginners: inside are welcome
  • Communication-capable, team-oriented and dynamic personality as well as independent working methods
  • Fluent knowledge of German, French is a plus

Working with us

You are waiting for a team that enjoys the constant development and is motivated. Together we live an open and friendly working atmosphere.

Your assignment starts by appointment and your place of work is in Nyon.

More about our commitment as an employer can be found below.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
