Short overview position 550 employees, who are responsible for the production of high-quality infant food for Germany and abroad, shape the dynamic, highly automated working environment of the Konolfingen factory. This places high demands on our team every day. Compliance with hygiene regulations and occupational safety. Would you also like to be part of our team?

To strengthen our Special Products (SP) department, we are looking for a communicative and responsible personality as a crew leader.

A Day in the Life of a Crew Leader

  • Leadership and coordination of a team of 6 employees
  • Daily presence on the production line
  • Coaching and leadership in the areas of safety, quality, compliance, line performance and competitiveness to ensure the achievement of KPIs and the implementation of the Nestlé philosophy in close cooperation with the Line Performance Manager PEP
  • Conducting employee promotion discussions, agreeing on goals as well as tracking and determining the degree of goal achievement with the support of the Special Products Manager and the trainer
  • Assistance in planning the required staff and ensuring the holiday and break changes within the shift
  • Management and securing of proper shift transfers (staff, processes, facilities)
  • Assist in the preparation of the weekly production plan in cooperation with Supply Chain and Line Performance Manager PEP and monitor the timely implementation of the production program in compliance with all SOPs, GMP guidelines and safety regulations
  • Management and implementation of TPM processes
  • Management of GSTD projects in cooperation with other departments
  • Acquisition of production feedback in SAP, troubleshooting in DMO and verification of time recording in Pro WFM
  • Management of SAP orders
  • Management and verification of production reports
  • Ensuring responsible use of raw materials, energy, packaging materials and cleaning products

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
