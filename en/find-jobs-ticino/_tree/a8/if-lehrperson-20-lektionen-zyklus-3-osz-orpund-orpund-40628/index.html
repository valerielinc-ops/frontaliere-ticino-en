Full description

Portrait In the upper school center of the municipality association Education Gottstatt, about 220 pupils from the association communities Orpund, Safnern, Meinisberg and Scheuren are taught in eleven school classes (7th - 9th class). Our specialised teachers are proven specialist and accompanying personalities who shape and shape the culture and pedagogical development of the school.

We search from February 1, 2027

Tasks

As a curative pedagogue you are together with another curative pedagogue for integrative promotion in cycle 3. They accompany the students integratively in the class association, in small groups or individually. Successful cooperation and communication with the teachers - with the focus on the development potential of the learners - is central for you.

Requirements

  • They are trained in school curative education, strive for it or have professional experience.
  • You are a reliable, communicative and initiative personality.
  • They have experience in individualizing teaching.
  • The regular exchange with the special teaching team is part of your way of working.

We offer

  • Sound introduction and close accompaniment by the school management and the second curative teacher.
  • Professional exchange opportunities and further training within the special teaching team of the Association Gottstatt.
  • Motivated team of teachers who are well mixed and helpful.
  • Institutionalized comprehensive school events, as well as events and projects in the year teams.
  • Association that values the school and provides the necessary infrastructure.
  • School social work is established and on site.
