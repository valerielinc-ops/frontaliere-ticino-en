Full description

Introduction

You love precision, like working on modern CNC systems and want parts to fit right away? For our customer we are looking for you as a CNC mechanic / in EFZ. You take over from setup to programming to quality assurance - and ensure that customer requirements are met on time and in top quality.

Tasks

  • Setting up and operating CNC milling and/or lathes
  • Programming and adapting directly on the machine
  • Optimize programs: tool and cut data, zero points, corrections, strategies
  • Reduce set-up times, increase service life
  • Drawing: Safely adhere to ISO tolerances, surfaces and fits
  • Quality assurance: initial and self-testing with caliper, bracket measuring screw, altimeter
  • Maintenance & 5S: Maintaining plants, fixing small faults

Profile

  • Completed training as CNC mechanic or polymechanic EFZ
  • Multi-year practice in CNC manufacturing (milling and/or turning): setup, programming, optimization
  • Precise, independent and structured working methods
  • Team-ready and quality-conscious
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Additional language skills are beneficial

Advantages

  • Modern machinery park in a clean, well-organized workshop
  • Collegial environment and short decision-making paths
  • Development opportunities and training support
