Full description

Located in Château-d’Œx at 1000 meters altitude, the Pôle Santé Pays-d’Enhaut (PSPE) offers a wide range of hospital services, accommodation, support for the elderly, as well as home care. According to the so-called integrated care approach, this innovative organization presents an opportunity for healthcare professionals to work within small multidisciplinary teams, ensuring better continuity of care and responding to the health needs of the Pays-d’Enhaut population. In the heart of a privileged natural environment, less than an hour from Lausanne or Bern, Pays-d’Enhaut is renowned for the quality of its living environment and offers a very broad range of sports, leisure, and cultural activities to enjoy both summer and winter! For its hospital sector, the Pôle Santé of Pays-d’Enhaut is looking for: Position Head Nurse of Care Unit (ICUS) Full-time position, immediate start or to be agreed Mission

  • Manage a unit of 8 to 16 beds for polyvalent hospitalization (Medicine, Rehabilitation, Intermediate Palliative Care) and an outpatient sector (Emergencies, Polyclinic, Dialysis, Outpatient Oncology)
  • Supervise a team of 18 staff members
  • Ensure proper care practices and the application of procedures according to the missions
  • Monitor staff competencies and evaluations
  • Propose and develop continuous improvement projects for care
  • Profile
  • Holder of a HES nursing diploma or equivalent, recognized by the Swiss Red Cross
  • Training in local management or equivalent qualification
  • Minimum of 5 years of professional experience, including 3 in a hospital environment
  • Successful experience in team leadership
  • Clinical nurse or specialization training, an advantage
  • Leadership, organizational skills, and communication skills
  • Ability to lead change
  • Ease in communication and negotiation
  • Good knowledge of Soarian
  • Proficiency in Polypoint, an advantage What We Offer

  • Deployment of full nursing practice within small interdisciplinary teams working collaboratively with the care network
