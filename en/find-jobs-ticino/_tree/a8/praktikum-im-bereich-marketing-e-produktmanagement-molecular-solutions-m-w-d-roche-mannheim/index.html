About us

Our Marketing Molecular Solutions department at the Mannheim site (Roche Diagnostics Division) is responsible for marketing planning, strategy development and product management in the area of tissue diagnostics and women's health in Germany. For this permanently advertised position, we are always looking for motivated talents who want to shape the future of diagnostics together with us.

Your tasks

During your internship, you will take on a variety of tasks and make a significant contribution to supporting marketing and product management:

  • You will be responsible for carrying out (sub)projects to support product management, including optimizing processes and reporting.
  • You carry out market and potential analyzes to support strategic decisions and help with the planning and implementation of multi-channel marketing campaigns
  • You are actively involved in the conception and implementation of marketing campaigns in order to successfully position our products on the market.
  • In collaboration with external agencies, you develop creative means of communication (e.g. advertising brochures, advertisements) or implement your own ideas when creating marketing materials.
  • You prepare scientific and product-related information and support our sales team in using it in a targeted and effective manner.
  • You support the planning and implementation of training courses and events

Who you are

  • Study status: You are a registered student of natural sciences or economics, such as biology, business administration and health management and are already in an advanced bachelor's or master's degree. An internship during your gap year is also possible with us.
  • Experience: Initial practical experience in marketing and sales is an advantage.
  • Way of working: You work proactively, independently and with high motivation. Your quick comprehension helps you to quickly understand and implement complex issues.
  • Interests: You are enthusiastic about biology, complex diseases and challenges in the healthcare market.
  • Technical skills: Our work is mainly based on Google tools such as G-Sheets and G-Slides. Previous knowledge is therefore a plus, but with Excel or PowerPoint experience you will quickly find your feet. Very good German (C level) and good English skills (B level) round off your profile.
