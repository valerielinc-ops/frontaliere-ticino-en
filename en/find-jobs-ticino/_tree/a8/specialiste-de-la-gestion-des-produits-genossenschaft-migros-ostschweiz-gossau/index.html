Full description

Become part of our motivated team and support the product management of the Backwaren & Convenience department in the successful implementation of the goals in the areas of Food at Home and Food to Go.

Mansioni

  • Ensuring the operational implementation of national and regional product range specifications in the area of Food at Home and Food to go
  • Ensuring correct flows of goods, information and data in the area of responsibility
  • Ensuring company objectives, among other things, by analyzing sales and availability data and deriving optimization measures
  • Detailed maintenance and management of master data in SAP
  • Planning and control of quantities taking into account influencing factors
  • Support of stores with assortments and orders
  • Collaboration in projects and introduction of new products and concepts

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in the retail trade or comparable industries
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): in the field of business administration, product management, controlling or comparable is required
  • Safe handling of Microsoft Office, in particular Excel is required
  • Strong numerical affinity and analytical skills and networked thinking are required
  • Very good SAP knowledge, especially in the area of article and master data management
  • Experience in goods management, volume planning and assortment implementation is advantageous
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • L-GAV: Attractive Migros Land collective employment contract
  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Insurance: Benefit Conditions at Various Insurance Companies
  • Mobile subscription: Highly discounted conditions for private mobile subscription also for family and friends
