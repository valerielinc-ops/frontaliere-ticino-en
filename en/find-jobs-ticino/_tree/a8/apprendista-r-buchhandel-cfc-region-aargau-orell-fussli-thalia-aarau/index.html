Full description

Share your passion for books Do you like reading for your life? Do you like to dive into incredible books? And you love sharing your passion for books with other reading enthusiasts? Then apply for an apprenticeship as a bookseller at Orell Füssli. We love books as much as you do. In addition, Orell Füssli is a place where employees feel comfortable, where they enjoy working well together and achieve more together. During your apprenticeship, you get to know the book trade from the ground up. We look forward to welcoming you on 1 August 2027 in one of our branches in the Aargau region (Aarau, Baden).

Your main tasks

  • Competent advice to customers
  • Recommendation of books and new publications
  • Receiving orders and handling them
  • Various work on the computer and in our goods management system
  • Presentation of books and non-books

This is what you bring with you

  • Good secondary education
  • High service orientation, sociability, attention and curiosity
  • Excellent knowledge of German and enjoyment of foreign languages
  • Passionate about books and reading
  • Passion for media and current affairs

This is what awaits you

  • Varied training in different departments
  • Collaborative and appreciative corporate culture
  • Generous holiday regime
  • Versatile discount offer
  • Central location with good public transport connections

We look forward to your online application. Please send us this including motivation letter, CV, complete school reports and a short text about your favorite book. See you soon.
