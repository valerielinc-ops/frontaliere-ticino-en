Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. Active in vocational training, it promotes the development of young talent, with every year more than 120 learners trained in our departments. In order to strengthen our Risks, Actuarial and Reinsurance Department, we are looking for a Master's intern for a period of 6 months from February 2027 in Lausanne. The missions that await you:

  • Taking charge of the Databricks environment (Python/Spark Notebooks, SQL) and code management tools (Git) for actuarial requirements
  • Participate in the development of data flows and quality controls necessary for actuarial processes
  • Contribute to the migration of existing actuarial processes to Databricks through the development of Python solutions
  • Study and propose improvements to the definition of actuarial assumptions
  • Test, validate and document the new processes developed Your profile:
  • You are in the course of academic studies (Master University or High School) in the field of actuarial, quantitative finance, data science or equivalent training
  • You have development experience in Python and SQL
  • You have knowledge of advanced mathematics and database management
  • Experience with a cloud data platform such as Databricks is an asset
  • Professional experience in insurance or finance is an asset
  • You are fluent in French and English
  • Proactive/ve and rigorous/se, you are rigorous in your work You are also self-sustaining, with a critical sense developed and appreciate teamwork. We offer you :
  • A healthy working environment in a cooperative company certified "Great Place to Work"
  • A varied and rewarding work experience
  • A possible replacement opportunity at the end of the internship/trainingChoose an element. The HR team is looking forward to receiving your complete application package (CV, cover letter and 3 final transcripts).
