Within the Digital Transformation team, you ensure and develop the support of information systems in SAP streams, "Manufacturing Execution System (MES)" and "Logistic Execution System (LES). You also help monitor the performance of our information systems.

HOW DO YOU HAVE A IMPACT? · Provide daily support to employees

o By supporting employees, through your presence in the field, in the use of different information systems

o By relaying between technical providers and users in the defined level of service · You handle incidents related to production and logistics management flows

o Providing technical and professional expertise

o By following up globally from detection, creation and resolution · You raise users' needs through system development requests, in direct connection with the Product Owners of each solution and with the Group's SAP Experts

o By developing your field expertise from the various production and logistics professions

o By analysing the causes and roots and developing an action plan to ensure the continuous improvement of our systems · You support the deployment of new features

o Monitoring progress in IT developments and business projects related to SAP (PP, WM, MM) and MES

o By participating in testing new features and non-regression · You manage the security of access to information systems

o By securing access to data according to procedures in information systems

o Ensuring the full life cycle of our employees' IT/System accesses

WHAT ARE THE CLES OF YOUR SURVEY IN OUR HOUSE?

To benefit from HES engineering training in production/logistics management or equivalent, you justify a proven experience of 3 to 5 years in the in-depth use of SAP (PP, WM, MM, EWM) and in an MES, in an industrial environment (knowledge of the watchmaking sector is an asset).

With good relational ease, you are recognized for your excellent communication and listening skills. You control information systems while having a strong appetite for field work. Autonomous, organized and with a common sense of priorities, you have a strong analytical capacity to identify problems and find solutions. You are a dynamic, curious, rigorous and methodical person. You stand out for your sense of service and good team spirit.
