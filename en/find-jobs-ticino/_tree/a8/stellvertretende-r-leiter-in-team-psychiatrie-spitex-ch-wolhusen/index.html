Full description

The Spitex Region Entlebuch supports and enables with its services living and living at home for people of all ages who need care, care or help. Our comprehensive range of services includes somatic and psychiatric care, palliative plus and domestic services for eight communities in the region.

Deputy Head of Team Psychiatry Your task

  • Care and support of adult clients with mental impairments with the aim of enabling them to live a self-determined everyday life at home
  • Participation in securing management tasks in close cooperation with the team leader
  • Responsibility for the organisation and coordination of the care process and assumption of case responsibility
  • Close networking and cooperation with the other teams of our core services
  • Active cooperation with external services, professionals and agencies

Your profile

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH psychiatry or as a dipl. Nurse HF/FH with further training in the field of psychiatry (e.g. CAS ambulatory psychiatric care)
  • Mind. two years of professional experience 100% in psychiatry for the implementation of needs assessments
  • Joy of self-responsible and independent work
  • Strong communication skills as well as an appreciative and targeted working method
  • Valid driving licence of the Cat. B and preferably own vehicle

Our offer

  • Demanding, interesting and versatile activity
  • Schedulable working hours: Fixed working days without weekend and evening services
  • Careful introduction and support during the training period
  • A motivated, collegial and supportive team
  • Individual training and development opportunities
  • Progressive and attractive employment conditions

Are you interested? For further information, please contact Madeleine Zihlmann, Head of Core Services, Tel. 041 484 28 00. Please send the complete application documents to bewerbung@spitexregiontlebuch.ch .
