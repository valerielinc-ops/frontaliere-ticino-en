# Scientific collaborator / Scientific collaborator in the field of automation and collaborative robotics applied to human-centred production systems”

The Professional University School of Italian Switzerland (SUPSI) puts in competition a position as Scientific collaborator / Scientific collaborator in the field of automation and collaborative robotics applied to human-centred production systems” , at the

Institute for Systems and Technologies for Sustainable Production (ISTePS)

of the Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI) with headquarters Lugano. Employment rate of 100%. Entry into service 1 December 2026 or date to be agreed.

Responsibility and activity

  • Dedicate a significant part of the activity (indicatively 50%) to the development of DemoStART, SPS laboratory platform dedicated to collaborative robotics and human-centred production systems, currently under development and also employed in the Bachelor in Management Engineering. The activity includes design, realization, updating and maintenance of the demonstrator, as well as demonstration activities towards industrial, academic and institutional partners.
  • To carry out applied research, experimental development and demonstrators in the field of collaborative robotics and automation for human-centred production systems, with particular reference to man-robot collaboration (HRC), human-robot interaction (HRI) and the digital twins of production systems, including prototyping, conducting experiments and collecting and analysis of data, starting from objectives defined with the team.
  • Contribute to the technical development of European and national research projects, particularly in software development and system integration, in collaboration with team members and under their supervision.
  • Treat the integration of sensors, actuators and communication systems between devices.
  • Develop software for the integration, programming and control of robotic and automated systems, especially in Python and/or C/C++, also using programming and simulation environments such as RoboDK.
  • Contribute to the experimentation of vision, data acquisition and sensory systems, as well as the use of tools for the integration and orchestration of systems (e.g. Node-RED or equivalent) and middleware for data transmission (e.g. MQTT).
  • Explore the use of artificial intelligence and human-machine interfaces applied to collaborative robotics and human-centred production systems.
  • Support the dissemination of results by writing scientific articles, presentations at conferences and technology transfer activities to local companies.
  • Occasionally engage in teaching and student supervision ( Bachelor/Master courses), consistent with the needs of the laboratory.

Requirements

  • University education (Master or equivalent) in robotics, mechatronics, automation, electronics, computer science, mechanical engineering, management engineering or related disciplines.
  • Experience in research activities (also matured during the Master thesis), preferably matured within a university research group or a research and development centre.
  • Scientific publications and/or lectures in areas such as robotics, automation, mechatronics, production systems or related disciplines are preferential.
  • Good programming skills in Python and/or C/C++ and ability to develop software for the integration and control of robotic and automated systems.
  • Experience or strong interest in the programming of industrial and/or collaborative robots and related programming and simulation environments, such as RoboDK or equivalent tools.
  • The familiarity with some of the technologies behind the activities of the laboratory is a preferential title: automation and integration of systems (sensors, actuators, communication between devices), vision and data acquisition systems, orchestration tools and middleware for data transmission (e.g. Node-RED, MQTT), digital twin (demonstrator and robotics activities); machine learning, deep learning, generative AI and applied systems (research) Java, containerization (Docker), relational database or NoSQL (development software and activities in projects).
  • Interest in research in collaborative robotics, HRC, HRI, robot programming and human-centred production systems.
  • Excited autonomy in learning and using new technologies, also not previously known, and ability to independently achieve the entire experimental cycle: from design and simulation to realisation, programming, integration and validation of a system.
  • Ability to work effectively in restricted teams and in multicultural contexts, completing tasks assigned without continuous supervision. Ability to create experimental prototypes, with readiness to use (also learning it quickly) CAD software for the design of basic supports and mechanical interfaces, and 3D printing.
  • Great knowledge of English and mastery of a national language. Knowledge of a second national language is a preferential title.

What we offer

  • A fixed-term contract of up to 2 years, with possibility of renewal.
  • A stimulating working environment in an applied research team active in national and European projects, with access to experimental infrastructure and industrial use cases.
  • The possibility of contributing to the development of new lines of research and technological transfer to local companies in the context of Industry 4.0/5.0 and Circular Economy.
  • Professional growth opportunities through progressive responsibility on project activities, publications and acquisition of competitive funding.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
