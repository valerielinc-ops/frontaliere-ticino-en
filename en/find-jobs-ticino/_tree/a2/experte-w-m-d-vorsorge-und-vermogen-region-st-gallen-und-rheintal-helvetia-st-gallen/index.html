Customer Consulting – Sales

Workload: 50-100% Workplace: Experience level: With professional experience FlexOffice: on-site

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means much more than simply completing tasks. Here, you work with people who take responsibility, have high standards, and bring the courage to break new ground. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand, and where your ideas, your initiative, and your contribution count. This gives you the opportunity to truly make a difference for our customers, your team, and your personal development.

What you achieve

  • Professional expertise: As an experienced insurance consultant with a focus on the pension and financial business, you know the industry and its products. You advise our customers, develop financial and retirement plans, support and empower our customer advisors, provide training, and act as the primary contact for pension-related questions
  • Market analysis: You conduct market analyses and develop market penetration strategies to identify and seize potentials and opportunities in the areas of pensions and finance
  • Expansion of pensions and finance: You are involved from the very beginning and play a key role in building and co-shaping the area surrounding insurance and banking solutions (e.g., asset management, funds) in pensions and wealth. Planning marketing measures, implementation, and controlling are also your responsibility
  • Building customer relationships: You maintain close relationships with the customer advisors you support, assist them on their path to success, and actively expand your network within Helvetia and in the market.
  • Teamwork: As a member of our Pensions and Wealth expert community, you make a significant contribution to the professional and sales-oriented further development of these topics at Helvetia

What you bring

  • Expertise: You have completed a sound education or degree and have several years of professional experience in the financial services industry
  • Entrepreneurial spirit: You are enthusiastic and inspiring, enjoy development work, and like collaborating with various stakeholders
  • Strong communication skills: You are an excellent communicator and can convey complex matters in an understandable way
  • Analytical thinking: You have a flair for numbers, think in an interconnected way, and possess strong organizational and planning skills
  • Customer orientation: The satisfaction of your customers is close to your heart, and you do everything you can to meet their needs in the best possible way
