Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow – networked and efficient. Start with us into the future of logistics and assume responsibility in the function as a dispatcher*in.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Logistics, Migros Verteilbetrieb AG, Wynenfeld, 5034 Suhr

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Independent disposition of transports with fresh and food products to the migrolino shops
  • Planning of empty goods returns and classification of chauffeurs and vehicles
  • Contact person in the area of transport for the migrolino shops, Migros stores and other clients
  • Key role between customers and transport partners
  • Transport controlling activities
  • Assistance in various projects
  • Picquet service (1x per month) / Saturday use (1-2x per month) Working hours: Monday - Saturday, early shift: 03:30 to 12:00 / Middle shift: 07:00 to 15:00 / Late shift: 08:00 to 17:00

Requirements

  • 3-5 years: Transportdispo
  • Completed initial or further training: disposition/transport
  • Vocational apprenticeship (EFZ) in transport or logistics
  • Further training in the field of disposition and/or experience in truck disposition in national transport
  • Organizational talent with good geographical knowledge of Switzerland and high resilience
  • Stylistic German in word and writing / French is advantageous
  • C/CE driving licence, advantageous
  • Good IT knowledge (office/disposition software)
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • French (basic knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central location: Our locations are directly connected to the motorway or public transport
  • Modern infrastructure: Our logistics work with fully automated, state-of-the-art facilities. Office workplaces meet the latest standards
  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: We grant our employees 18 weeks of paid maternity leave resp. 4 weeks paid paternity leave, flexible subscription options
  • Fair wages: We pay a fair and fair wage. The basis is our personnel management and compensation system
  • Occupational pension provision: Migros Pension Fund benefits are clearly above the law. All are retired at age 65
  • Food: In our staff restaurants, employees can cater at preferential prices
  • Employee offers: We offer our employees many special services and discounts (e.g. fuel, mobile subscriptions)
  • Cumulus Points: Employees of MVB AG can collect additional Cumulus Points for purchases in Migros stores and specialty markets
  • Social and personnel consultancy services: We offer our employees contact persons for private and professional challenges
  • Healthy work: We operate an active, operational health management and invest in prevention measures
