Transport logistics operator: in 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Transport logistics operator:in Bern, 01.04.2027 or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

Do you love having free time during the week and making your free time flexible? Then we have the perfect job for you! With us as a transport logistics specialist: you not only have the opportunity to actively shape your everyday life, but also to prove your physical robustness and endurance. Our physically demanding tasks challenge and promote you – Boredom has no place here! A versatile working environment awaits you, in which you can use your logistics experience profitably. Together with your teammates, you will experience a varied everyday life. You work as a team at the station, but you are also on your own with the delivery truck. We support further development requests and have a variety of internal further education opportunities for your personal career at SBB. Our other benefits such as child allowances, free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As a logistician, you sort your luggage, packages and letters for our customers.
  • You convert these shipments into trains and vans.
  • With our road vehicles you transport the shipments to the destination.
  • As a service, you transport the luggage of our customers from door to door.
  • You place advertising posters on our trains.

You bring that with you.

  • You are characterized by reliability, flexibility and a solution- and goal-oriented working method. Your commitment and open-minded personality round off your profile perfectly.
  • A strong awareness of work safety is a matter of course for you, and you always work customer- and quality-oriented.
  • In order to perform this job optimally, you bring a completed basic training as a logistics specialist: in EFZ or chauffeur.
  • You have the driving license of category B as well as a forklift card.
  • You master the German language well in word and writing (at least level B2).
  • You are ready to work in shifts and on weekends (Monday to Sunday between 5 a.m. and 11 p.m.).

Your reward and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. For this reason, further and detailed information on the wage system can be viewed transparently at SBB. This position is in requirement level D, regional allowance level 1.
