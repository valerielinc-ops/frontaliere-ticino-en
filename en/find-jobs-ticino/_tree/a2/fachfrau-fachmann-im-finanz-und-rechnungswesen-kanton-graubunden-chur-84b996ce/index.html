Professional in finance and accounting

Financial management / DFG \| 80\-100% \| Chur

Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. Graubünden is the largest canton in Switzerland with 7106 km2 and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days per year. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the Cantonal Administration Graubünden together with us.

For team reinforcement in financial management, we are looking for 1\. February 2027 or by arrangement a specialist in finance and accounting.

Your responsibilities

  • Advising and supporting the cantonal services in matters of financial and operational accounting
  • Responsibility for VAT issues, advising services and drawing up VAT accounts
  • IT and logistics responsibility in financial management with user management
  • Ensuring 2nd level support for users of the central accounting system
  • Management and participation in cross-cutting projects

That is what we want from you.

  • Further education in finance and accounting or trust, preferably with a Swiss certificate
  • In-depth professional experience in finance and accounting
  • High self-employment, reliability and high quality awareness as well as a strong service and service orientation
  • Experienced handling of IT\ systems, financial applications and sound Excel\ knowledge for evaluations and analyses
  • Analytical thinking, rapid comprehension and pronounced understanding of numbers.
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Good knowledge of several cantonal languages is advantageous

Our advantages

Flexible working time models and where possible incl. Home office and mobile work\-life\-balance with mind. 5 weeks holidays and service leave Special social allowance in addition to child allowance and support for third-party childcare Attractive wage and social benefits Various training and further education opportunities to shape the future of the canton

Place of work

Financial management Quarry road 18 7001 Chur
