Full description

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. Our culture encourages personal expression, open dialogue, and genuine connections, where you are valued, accepted and respected for who you are, allowing you to thrive both personally and professionally. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters.

The Position The Country Medical Director is a senior leader who easily navigates between science, data and business in service of our vision at Roche. You are an experienced professional with a solid scientific background. You are confident in playing a key role in developing the local medical strategy to enable future success of the organization and you have experience in transforming complex healthcare challenges into clear and compelling actions that benefit patients.

Building relationships and scientific partnerships with our stakeholders is your second nature. Considering the nature of Bosnia and Herzegovina healthcare system, the Medical Director will bring understanding of the operating environment (or comparable market) and its challenges. You come with a strong vision on evidence generation and data enabled healthcare. You are passionate about innovation and can envision how healthcare data and science can have a sustainable impact on the healthcare system.

As a member of the Roche Bosnia and Herzegovina Enabling/Leadership Team, you will be responsible for setting the strategic direction and achieving company objectives within the Affiliate and Medical Chapter, including strategic decision making and empowering teams. You will serve as a role model to the entire organization, lead the functional chapter, and focus on upskilling and coaching team members, while also co-creating organizational strategies and fostering agility and cultural change. Additionally, you will collaborate with other Country Medical Directors, Chapter Heads and Squad Leads to allocate resources and support professional development within the Network, Affiliate and Chapter.

Who are we looking for?

Beyond strong focus on external engagement, strategic partnerships, data and science you have the following responsibilities:

  • Shape and develop the overall affiliate medical strategy based on external insights and Roche’s product portfolio developments
  • Drive above-brand medical strategy and alignment
  • Represents Roche medical authority with key external stakeholders
  • As a member of the Enabling /Leadership Team, develop and implement the vision, mission and overarching strategy for Roche Bosnia and Herzegovina and ensure it adheres to the highest scientific and ethical standards and fulfills all regulatory and compliance requirements.
  • Ensure excellence in scientific consulting with stakeholders, patient safety, quality, compliance, regulatory, evidence generation and medical information
  • Establish and maintain local medical and scientific independence and assurance of medical compliance acting as the local delegate of authority of Roche`s Pharma CMO ensuring business integrity and company reputation.
  • Drive early health data gap identification, prioritization and integrated evidence planning inclusion by being actively involved and by collaborating with strong medical enablers
  • Bring clinical practice perspective and act as a sparring partner to decide how (and which) data should be used / generated to build the medical narrative in the respective disease areas
  • Work closely with local, cross-country and global compliance networks, strong cross-functional partnership with other affiliate (and network) functions and actively engage with Roche global functions and group members.
  • Being a servant leader to the entire affiliate and network acting with courage and integrity. Role model creative leadership and network mindset supporting transparency across the organization.
