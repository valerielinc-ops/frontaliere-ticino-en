Full description

In order to support its team of the Hospital Unit of Psychiatry of the Elderly , the Nant Foundation is putting in the competition a position of

80% Community Care and Health Assistant

MISSION The Community Care and Health Assistants (CHS) of the Seniors Hospital Unit are responsible for providing the required care for each patient in close collaboration with an interdisciplinary team.

WE OFFER

The psychiatry and psychotherapy service for the elderly is one of the four services of the Nant Foundation. Its mission is to provide care for people over the age of 65 in the Vaud area. The hospital unit of this service, Molaine, is intended to provide urgent, semi-urgent or scheduled hospitalizations for people over 65 years of age. Being an ASSC in this service means serving patients over 65 years of age in an interdisciplinary team.

WE SEEK A person holding an SWC in community care and health (or title deemed equivalent), ideally with experience in age psychiatry. Moreover, having the capacity to be at the service of the patient , his colleagues and the institution is important for working as an ASSC on the Molaine team. Finally we wish to hire a person motivated by the mission of this unit and loving the work as an interdisciplinary team.
