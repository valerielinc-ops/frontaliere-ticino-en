Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to discover the highlights of 2025 by clicking here . The Department of Medicine (D-MED) is composed of 14 medical-care units (12 medical services, 1 Unit for Investigation and Short Treatment (UITB) and the Blood Transfusion Centre (CTS)), with the particular feature of having in its organization technical trays (including interventional cardiology and digestive and pulmonary l 3 rehabilitation and rehabilitation programmes. In addition, the medical-care activity is highly dedicated to patients and stationary patients (9 care units, 160 beds) with internal consultants and consultants for the benefit of the patient care provided by other medical departments.

Description of post

As part of your function, you ensure the proper functioning of the Department of Medicine's computer systems. You manage and track applications as well as track the renewal of the Department of Medicine's IT fleet in connection with the DTN-IA. You coordinate with the team of computer correspondents maintenances, updates, upgrades of versions, migrations of the department's software with external and internal publishers including the application references of the services, the DTN-IA and the biomedical service. You provide assistance to the users of the department. You are involved in the development of statistics.

Qualifications

You hold the Bachelor of Computer Science and you have knowledge of Windows 11, infrastructure and network. You have knowledge of office hardware and software (PCs, printers and accessories, barcode readers, office automation...). You have knowledge of SQL programming tools. Optional: You have knowledge of institutional tools (DPI, DPA, Qualic, GRECO, VNA). You know the Inlog software. You have knowledge of medical imaging (DICOM files, ultrasound...). You are a person determined to deepen your knowledge through specific training. You have perfect command of the French language as well as good editorial capacity and good knowledge of the English, spoken and written language.

Further information

You have a regular schedule, while being available off schedule including night, weekend and holidays during software upgrades and emergency interventions.
