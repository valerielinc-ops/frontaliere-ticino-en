Stv. Team Leader: incoming goods

Coop Cooperative — Oberbuchsiten, SO, Svizzera

  • Job opening: immediately or by agreement
  • Field of expertise: Logistics / Transport
  • Location: Coop, Industriestrasse 9, 4625 Oberbuchsiten
  • Pensum: 100%

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the timely and correct processing of the goods after receipt as well as for the control of the receipt of goods according to internal specifications and also unload at the container
  • You represent the team management goods receipt in case of absences and support in the daily leadership
  • You plan the day in the team and coordinate internal offices and external service providers:
  • You check the incoming goods according to specifications, check quantities/quality and document deviations and process disruptions
  • You take on administrative tasks (e.g. truck notification, container unloading planning, e-mail inbox, master data maintenance)

Requirements

  • You have completed vocational training as a logistician: in EFZ
  • You communicate stylishly in German
  • You bring experience with SAP and WAMAS and work safely with email and Excel
  • You have a valid SUVA-approved truck ID and are physically fit
  • You think connected and are flexible and reliable
  • Perks: cheaper mobile and prepaid subscriptions on Coop Mobile, Reka Card: perks on purchase, perks on GA subscription, perks for leisure activities
  • Conditions of Employment: Pension Fund: 2/3 is paid by Coop, Attractive Conditions of Employment and Certified Equal Pay, Total Employment Contract (GAV), 18 weeks of paid maternity leave and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave
  • Working hours: 5 weeks holidays
  • Training: Over 600 internal training courses, support for external education and training
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people and animals and nature with over 400 actions
  • Communication: internal app for employees
