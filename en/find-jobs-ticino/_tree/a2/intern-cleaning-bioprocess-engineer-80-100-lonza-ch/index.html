Full description

The actual location of this position is in Visp, Switzerland. Relocation support is available to eligible applicants and their families if needed.

As a Bioprocess Engineer, you will be part of the production team and perform the duties of a bioprocess engineer in the area of purification and day-to-day project support.

Within the Microbial Large Scale production facility, both initial engineering campaigns and continuously running commercial processes are carried out.

This position is a fully site-based role. Close personal collaboration on site enables direct coordination in real time and supports the technical precision required to manufacture medicines according to the highest quality and safety standards.

What awaits you

  • An agile career and a dynamic work culture.
  • An inclusive and ethical working environment.
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance.
  • The opportunity to actively shape and optimize complex GMP bioprocesses.
  • Collaboration with interdisciplinary teams in an international environment.
  • Diverse development and training opportunities.
  • You can find the complete list of our global benefits here: https://www.lonza.com/careers/benefits

Your responsibilities

  • Collaboration with facility and project teams to ensure an efficient and cross-functional operational workflow.
  • Creation, editing, and maintenance of SOPs, cleaning reports, and other work documents in the area of equipment cleaning.
  • Independent management and monitoring of ongoing cleaning and production processes in compliance with high quality and compliance standards.
  • Handling of projects as well as continuous optimization and further development of existing processes.
  • Representing the production team during customer and regulatory authority audits and inspections, with a focus on equipment cleaning.
  • Supporting project teams in the preparation, documentation, and handling of deviations, CAPAs, and investigations, as well as with technical questions regarding cleaning processes.
  • Performing tasks in a GMP-regulated environment in compliance with applicable guidelines and quality requirements.

What we are looking for

  • MSc, BSc in Chemistry/Chemical Engineering/Biotechnology from a university or university of applied sciences, or comparable qualification with several years of professional experience
  • Experience in pharmaceutical production and in a GMP environment is desirable
  • Experience with TrackWise and knowledge of equipment cleaning are an advantage.
  • German and English, both written and spoken
  • Solution-oriented and independent working style with a high degree of initiative and sense of responsibility.
  • Openness to continuous improvement processes
  • Willingness to participate in on-call duty
