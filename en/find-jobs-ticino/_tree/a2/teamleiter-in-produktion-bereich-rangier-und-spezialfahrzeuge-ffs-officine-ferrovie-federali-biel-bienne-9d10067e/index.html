Team leader: in production, shunting and special vehicles 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Team leader: in production, shunting and special vehicles Biel/Bienne, from now on or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

Are you looking for a new, real challenge in a dynamic environment? Do you want to take responsibility and actively shape the future of maintenance? Then you are exactly right with us. With us, you contribute every day to ensuring the stability of the Swiss infrastructure – in a technologically versatile environment that thrives on innovation and tangible technology. Together with your team for shunting and special vehicles, you will develop pragmatic solutions for current challenges and actively implement them. Your commitment has a visible impact: safe and efficient operations, satisfied employees and customers and a strong network across departmental boundaries. We promote your further development in a targeted manner: a wide range of internal training opportunities support your personal career at SBB. We also offer attractive working conditions, including a free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad - also for your family. On our website you will find more information about our benefits and how we make work worth living with us. Interested in learning more about our professions? We look forward to seeing you from 22 to 24. 2026 in our SBB Berufswelten Café at Zurich Central Station. All information can be found at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. You can move that.

  • You lead a high-performing, expert maintenance team (approximately 15 people) and create a technically demanding, varied working environment where reliability and efficiency come together.
  • Together with your team, you will ensure that our shunting and special vehicles and their components remain safe, high-quality and operational. You take on the professional and personnel responsibility and are the driving force for smooth processes.
  • You actively develop processes: Optimizing workflows, optimizing collaboration at interfaces with other areas and introducing pragmatic improvements are part of your daily activities.
  • You promote the professional development of your employees, systematically expand your skills and plan for future successions – ensuring long-term know-how and team strength.
  • You take responsibility for productivity and costs and bring economic thinking to operational leadership without losing sight of quality and safety.

You bring that with you.

  • You are an independent, resilient leader with a high level of initiative, flexibility and customer-oriented action.
  • Structured, systematic working methods, strong social skills, good communication behaviour and assertiveness.
  • Strong quality awareness and high customer orientation.
  • You bring several years of professional and managerial experience in a comparable position as well as railway know-how are beneficial.
  • A completed vocational apprenticeship EFZ (mechanical or electrical) plus further training technician:in HF / industrial master:in or similar as well as very good knowledge of German (C1) and good to very good (min. B1) knowledge of French are prerequisites for this position.
