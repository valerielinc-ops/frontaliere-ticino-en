This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

GIG Europe SA, a company of the Tarchini Group, which deals with the planning, development and management of “green” energy plants, is looking for a technical-commercial specialist for the management of relationships and the negotiation of land agreements for the realization of BESS plants within the framework of the Swiss Energy Program with a view to expanding its own activities at national level in the field of energy storage through the realization of BESS plants.

The selected person will identify real estate, get in touch with the owners of the land, as well as with the operators of the power grids and conclude agreements on building rights for the installation of BESS facilities.

Requirements

• Age between 30 and 40 years • Swiss citizenship or residence in Switzerland • very good knowledge of Italian, German, Swiss, English and French • Experience in areas such as real estate, energy, infrastructure or related areas • Willingness to travel frequently throughout Switzerland • good knowledge of the Swiss environment and sensitivity to local dynamics • excellent communication, negotiation and representation skills • self-employed persons • basic technical training or at least a good familiarity with topics related to energy, plant technology, infrastructure and land issues. A purely technical specialisation is not required

  • What is important, however, is the ability to understand the project and communicate it clearly and credibly to external contacts.

We offer

• Contract of indefinite duration • Employment level 100% • Location: Manno – German-speaking Switzerland • Job opening: from now on • Willingness to travel within Switzerland
