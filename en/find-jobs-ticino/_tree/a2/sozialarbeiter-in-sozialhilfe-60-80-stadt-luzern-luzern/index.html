Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Do you want to bring your optimistic and open way to work with people? Do they feel empathetic?

Your tasks

  • You are responsible for the advice, support and financial support of individuals and families within the framework of statutory social assistance.
  • You accompany people in different situations on their way to social and professional integration.
  • You work with internal and external specialists, social insurance companies and authorities.
  • You will be administratively supported by specialists, can draw on the knowledge of internal specialist departments (legal service, employment and education, integration and living) and work together with your colleagues in a team.

Your profile

  • You bring a university degree in social work or an equivalent education, ideally supplemented with subject-specific further education.
  • You have already gained your first professional experience in economic welfare.
  • - You meet all people with appreciation and can deal with different expectations.
  • You are experienced in administrative activities and in dealing with numbers.
  • You are accustomed to working independently, are an organizational talent and willing to make decisions.

In the social services, around 170 employees work for residents in emergencies. We advise and support them in the areas of livelihood security, adult protection, support and work integration.
