Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Service in the lodge (security center) of the central prison, supervision and operation of all security facilities and telephone control center
  • Management of the Detention Coordination Office of the Central Prison and all District Prisons of the Canton of Aargau
  • General supervision, supervision and control activities in the department (including assistance in other departments)
  • Completion of various administrative tasks

What you bring

  • Completed vocational training with eidg. Certificate of Competence preferably in the commercial area or in the area of disposition
  • Additional qualification as a specialist for the administration of justice with federal law. Professional card or willingness to complete training
  • Reliable and independent working methods, high social skills, emotional stability and a non-prejudicial attitude
  • Willingness to provide irregular services (including 2 - 6 night services and 2 weekends per month)
  • Minimum age: 28 years, impeccable reputation (current penal and enforcement register extract), leadership card Cat. B

Your working environment

The Lenzburg Prison provides care for persons who have been sentenced to imprisonment or to a custodial measure. The offer includes 380 prison places for closed prison, pre-trial detention and short sentences.
