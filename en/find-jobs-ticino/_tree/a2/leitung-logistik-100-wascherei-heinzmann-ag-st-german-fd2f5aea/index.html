Head of Logistics 100%

As one of the leading laundry companies in Switzerland and a traditional family business, we have stood for reliability, discretion and personal support for decades. We specialize in the needs of exclusive homes and demanding hosts. Our services impress with the highest quality standards, uncompromising care and absolute reliability – even with the most sensitive textiles and in compliance with the strictest hygiene, safety and sustainability requirements.

Our customers include renowned companies from the luxury hotel industry, which depend on immaculate cleanliness, perfect presentation and smooth processes.

Our success is based on competence, commitment and innovation. That is why we are looking for people who not only work, but also think \ - personalities who actively contribute their ideas and their energy and want to shape the future together with us.

Tasks

  • Development of the newly created management function "Management of Logistics"
  • Creating a logistics concept
  • Assignment planning as well as technical and personnel management of the logistics department (functions of dispatcher, logistics operator, forwarder)
  • Ensuring occupational safety and quality
  • Route optimisation

Qualifications

In order to be successful in this, you ideally bring the following qualifications:

  • have successfully completed vocational training as a specialist/logistics specialist
  • have completed leadership training
  • You can at least. 3 years of experience in a comparable function
  • are a motivated, flexible person and can assert themselves well
  • have good knowledge of German and German and can ideally also communicate in Italian
  • have good computer knowledge as well as good knowledge of the Office programs (Word, Excel) and have the driver's license
  • Work in an international environment
  • remain calm and solution-oriented in hectic situations

We offer

  • A varied activity with a lot of personal responsibility and scope for action
  • A collaborative and motivated team

Are you ready for a new challenge and want to become part of our success story? We look forward to your application!
