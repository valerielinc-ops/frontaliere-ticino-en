Move Switzerland with us. Specialist / Project Leader under the BZU+ programme (LHand implementation) (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, immediate start or suitable, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

In accordance with the requirements of the Law on Equality for the Disabled (LHand) and the requirements relating to the maintenance of the substance of the railway infrastructure, 170 stations will be transformed throughout Switzerland in the next few years under the "BZU+" programme, for an investment of approximately 1.5 billion francs. You are in a collegial work environment where you share your know-how and enjoy interesting development opportunities. An overview of current construction projects can be found here: Construction Projects To find out about the benefits, such as free AG and low-cost travel abroad (for family as well), visit our website under Benefits. What you can move.

  • You support the management of the program in project management. As a (m/f/d) specialist, you are assisting in the quality control of multidisciplinary and complex railway station processing projects mainly located in French-speaking Switzerland. You are responsible for program objectives, financial management, timelines, quality and search for cost-effective solutions.
  • The support and technical support of general and specialized project managers throughout all phases of the project (of the study, then when the mandate is given and the execution) is part of your remit. In addition, you are involved in coordination between the FTO, the management of the SBB program and the specialized units.
  • You provide training for general and specialized project managers on the priority themes of the programme.
  • Adapting your communication to the programme and project level, you are also in charge of promoting innovation and implementing optimization processes.

What you bring.

  • You have experience working with stakeholders and you know how to work with them by being oriented towards solutions. You are also distinguished by your conceptual and entrepreneurial skills. In addition, you are excited about complex projects. You have a high degree of autonomy and initiative, are resistant to stress and are willing to commit to designing processes.
  • You know the SBBs as well as the fundamental steps in its planning and process in the area of SBB Infrastructure. In addition, you are able to take a critical look at the mandates and content of projects, based on your experience in projects and implementation.
  • For this function, a diploma from a high school or specialized high school of civil engineering, architect or related discipline is indispensable. With several years of professional experience in project management, you are looking for a position where you can capitalize on your previous experience and skills.
  • You have at least the B2 level in French and German. Special discussions in both national languages are not an obstacle for you.

Your salary and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive salary system and we are actively committed to equal pay. This is why additional and detailed information on the SBB salary system can be consulted in full transparency. This position falls within the K level of requirements and is eligible for a level 1 regional allowance. Learn more about salary and benefits.

The fact that you want to move Switzerland with us is the biggest motivation for us. We therefore waive our request for a cover letter for this position. If you wish, you can write to us in our application tool.
