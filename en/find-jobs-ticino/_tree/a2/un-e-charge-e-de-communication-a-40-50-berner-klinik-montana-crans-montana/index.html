Full description

Our rehabilitation clinic located in Crans-Montana, in the idyllic setting of the Valais Alps, researches as part of the creation of a position attached to the Directorate and transversal between the two clinics, one. 40-50% communication

Your missions

  • Participate in the design of internal and external communication
  • Maintain links with senders and ensure CRM update
  • Organize events (congresses, conferences, etc.) of Bernese and Lucernoese clinics
  • Develop and manage the presence of clinics on social networks
  • Participate in the design of brochures, flyers, posters
  • Participate in updating the website and intranet
  • Write press releases on an ad hoc basis

Your profile

  • Federal certificate of communication specialist or training/experience deemed equivalent
  • Strong experience in a similar position in the health or insurance sectors.
  • Digital mastery (web and social networks)
  • Writing skills (perfect spelling and ability to adapt tone to target audience)
  • French mother tongue, with professional ease in German (oral and written)

For this position, we want to recruit a collegial, innovative and agile personality, able to carry out various front-line files, while respecting deadlines and budgets. For our part, we offer

  • An activity within an agile company in its decisions and with understandable and harmonious management
  • A developing institution with major projects
  • Flexible organization of working time
  • Opportunities for internal and external training
  • High-level social benefits
  • A pleasant place to work

For any information or first contact Isabelle Gessler, Director of Operations, Tel. +41 (0)27 485 51 21
