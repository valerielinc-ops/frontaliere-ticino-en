Full description

Team leader railroads 60-100% - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Head of team railway tracks Renens (VD), 01.01.2027 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

Join an open-minded, committed and action-oriented team. You will have the opportunity to develop while contributing to the daily railway operation in the heart of the western region. Together, you will imagine and implement innovative solutions to meet the challenges of the network. In return, we offer a stimulating work environment and many attractive benefits. What you can move.

  • Support a team of about 28 people: support them in their professional and technical development and succession management of the SBB infrastructure.
  • Adopt motivating and professional leadership: you build confidence and foster collective performance.
  • Effectively manage track maintenance and sub-projects in accordance with SBB safety, economic and quality requirements.
  • Ensure the availability of the human and material resources necessary for the construction, maintenance and resolution of incidents on our railway infrastructure.
  • Monitor and analyze financial and operational indicators and be responsible for your team's performance.
  • Detect and conduct optimizations: you propose improvements, implement them and mobilize your team around new ideas.

What you bring.

  • You are comfortable with people of diverse cultures and contribute to an inclusive environment.
  • You have a global vision of the company and strong relational and technical skills: you can inspire, coach and guide your team towards the goals set.
  • You promote transparency on a daily basis: you communicate with insurance and presence.
  • You have a CFC or confirmed experience in a railway technical field. Staff management training (e.g., recognized federal, technical commercial, public transportation) is an asset - or you are prepared to do so within the next two years.
  • You have good computer skills (Office 365, SAP) and an indispensable category B driving licence.

Your salary and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive salary system and we are actively committed to equal pay. This is why additional and detailed information on the SBB salary system can be consulted in full transparency. This item falls within the level of requirements I and gives entitlement to a level 1 regional allowance. Learn more about salary and benefits .
