Full description

For our international architectural office in Haldenstein near Chur, we are looking for an employee immediately or by appointment: in Administration & Assistant Management (40-100%, job sharing welcome). In this varied role, you are part of our administration team and support the management in organizational and administrative matters. You enjoy a versatile secretarial activity, work carefully and independently and keep an overview even in a lively office day. They are culturally interested, articulate and appreciate an international working environment. Their tasks

  • Completion of all general secretarial and administrative tasks
  • Receiving, processing and triage of incoming requests by telephone, e-mail and post
  • Professional and stylish correspondence in German and English
  • Management scheduling and coordination
  • Organisational and administrative support of management
  • Organisation of business trips and internal and external events
  • Management of our company-owned holiday homes, in particular booking processing and communication with our guests
  • Supporting the office infrastructure and ensuring a smooth office routine

This is what they bring with them.

  • Commercial or other appropriate training and/or experience in a comparable capacity
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • A stylish, well-kept and situational communication
  • Strong organizational skills and a structured, independent working method
  • Discretion, reliability and responsibility
  • A service-oriented, friendly and winning personality
  • Enjoy thinking along, setting priorities and keeping track
  • Good user knowledge of common office programs

We offer

  • A culturally diverse and inspiring working environment
  • A varied administrative activity in an unconventional company
  • A beautiful workplace
  • A pleasant working atmosphere in a highly motivated and international team
  • A total expense of 100%, which can also be filled in job sharing with individual expenses from 40%
