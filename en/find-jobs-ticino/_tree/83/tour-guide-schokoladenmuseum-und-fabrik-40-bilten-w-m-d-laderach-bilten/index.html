Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, innovative ideas in agile teams. The “Retail” division comprises more than 250 chocolateries in more than 30 countries. Here we offer our chocolate specialties and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. Do you also want to participate? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • You will independently lead groups of up to 15 people on approximately 90-minute guided tours of our museum and our chocolate production – in German, English and, if necessary, in other languages you master.
  • As a passionate host, you will inspire our international audience – from families and tourists to Läderach’s business customers and partners.
  • You represent Läderach with charm and professionalism and convey interesting facts about our chocolate and its production in a lively and interactive way
  • You support with chocolate tastings, special events as well as on request at the welcome desk, reservations and ticketing
  • You take on various preparatory, final and background work around the guided tours and work closely with the live production, the welcome desk and the café
  • You will receive a comprehensive training and get to know everything important about Läderach, our chocolate and your tasks.

You bring that with you

  • You love standing in front of people, speaking freely and ripping groups with your enthusiasm
  • You communicate fluently and safely in German and English and present confidently in both languages
  • You enjoy contact with guests and openly and attentively approach people of different cultures, age groups and interests
  • You are enthusiastic about chocolate and want to learn more about Läderach and our chocolate world
  • You convince with a sympathetic and well-kept appearance and distinguish yourself through team spirit, reliability and initiative
  • You are also ready to work on weekends and during school holidays and ideally live in Glarnerland or the surrounding area
  • You are also welcome as a lateral beginner – Experience as a tour guide or expertise in chocolate is not a prerequisite

This is what we offer you

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The opportunity to bring in fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Attractive sales premium for joint achievement of goals
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Chocolate in the workplace
  • Good accessibility of locations

Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh.

Sounds interesting? Then apply to us. Josiane Widmer, Team Leader Events, +41 55 645 99 67, is at your disposal for any questions.

We ask for your understanding that we do not consider external recruitment agencies when filling this position.
