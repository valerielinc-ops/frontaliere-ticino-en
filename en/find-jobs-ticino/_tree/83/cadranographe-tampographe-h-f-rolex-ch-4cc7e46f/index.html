Full description

Introduction

For our Production division, we are looking for a (M/F) Dial/Tampograph whose main mission will be to perform decal operations on dial plates by tampography.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct decal operations on dial plates and discs (days, calendars);
  • Preparation of decal varnishes and machine settings;
  • Quality control;
  • Participation in daily meetings;
  • Activities 5S.

Profile

  • Ideally, watchmaking training (observer in watchmaking, dialograph (AFP/CFC), training in stamping or printing);
  • Mastery of stamping operations, preparation of varnishes and adjustment of means;
  • Excellent visual acuity;
  • Rigor, accuracy and thoroughness;
  • versatility and autonomy;
  • Quiet and respectful nature;
  • Good relationship skills and team spirit.

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.
