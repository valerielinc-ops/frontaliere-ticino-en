Back to Overview Polymechanics / Electricians for assembly (m/f/d) Job opening Immediately or by agreement Employment level 100% Permanent Place of work/region SO-solothurn For our customer we are looking for a polymechanic / electrician for assembly (m/f/d) - Greater Niedergösgen area

Task area

In this function, you play a central role in the assembly and commissioning of plants and machines. They work independently on site at the customer or in the company and ensure that all installations are implemented professionally, efficiently and on time.

Their tasks include

  • Assembly, installation and entry into service of equipment, machinery or technical systems
  • Carrying out maintenance and service work on existing facilities
  • Collaborate with internal teams and external customers to ensure smooth operations
  • Documentation of work and preparation of protocols
  • Participation in the optimisation of assembly processes and improvement of processes

Your profile

  • Completed training as a polymechanic, electrician or equivalent technical training
  • Practical experience in assembly, installation or service work
  • Independent, careful and structured working methods
  • Teamwork, solution-oriented thinking and customer-oriented appearance
  • Willingness to travel on business or work with the customer on site
