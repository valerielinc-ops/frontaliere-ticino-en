Full description

Welcome to Medbase Wattwil

Our modern practice with our own X-ray and ultrasound is located in the village center of Wattwil. The team takes care of the medical care of the population. Our specialists from the fields of general internal medicine and orthopaedics offer family consultation hours and special medical examinations. After consultation, we also carry out home visits. The MPA team ensures an excellent practice organization. Active participation and a good work-life balance are of great importance to us.

As an Advanced Practice Nurse, you take on an important role in our outpatient primary care. You take care of patients independently within the framework of your competence profile and work closely with our doctors and the MPA team. With us you can contribute your clinical expertise and at the same time actively shape and develop the role at Medbase.

You can move that

  • Independent care of patients – from acute complaints to the support of chronically and multimorbidly ill people
  • Conduct of structured history and clinical examinations
  • Urgency assessment, clinical reasoning and differential diagnostics
  • Initiation and interpretation of diagnostic examinations such as laboratory, ECG or X-ray in the defined competence framework
  • Planning and coordination of treatment processes in close cooperation with our doctors
  • Depending on the expertise involved in Acute and/or Chronic Care Management
  • Active co-creation and further development of the Nurse Practitioner role within Medbase

What you bring

  • Master of Science in Nursing (MScN) with clinical focus
  • Advanced clinical knowledge and competencies
  • Joy of self-responsible, patient-oriented work
  • Strong skills in clinical reasoning
  • Enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation
  • Initiative and motivation to play a future-oriented role in outpatient primary care
  • Registration as a nursing expert APN-CH of advantage

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton St. Gallen's migration office after the contract is signed.
