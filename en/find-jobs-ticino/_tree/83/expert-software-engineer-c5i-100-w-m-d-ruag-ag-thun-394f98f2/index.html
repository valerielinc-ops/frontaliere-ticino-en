Full description

Around 3000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to Switzerland’s security every day. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Development across different application levels – from backend and web applications to building a new mobile app
  • End-to-end responsibility for assigned software solutions from conception through development to productive operation
  • Development of secure messaging solutions with a focus on end-to-end encryption, data protection and reliable, scalable communication
  • Participate in the selection and evaluation of suitable technologies, frameworks and development tools
  • Close cooperation with departments and stakeholders to develop a user-centred solution
  • Design, implementation and optimization of new app features along the entire software lifecycle
  • Implementation of modern CI/CD pipelines as well as automation of build, test and release processes
  • Ensuring high quality, safety and performance standards

You bring that with you

  • Studies in computer science, vocational teaching as a computer scientist or comparable qualification as a lateral entrant as well as several years of professional experience in software development
  • In-depth knowledge in the conception and development of multi-tenant and scalable app architectures
  • Experience in cross-platform development of apps with established and modern frameworks
  • Competence in technical decisions, ensuring high software quality and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Good knowledge of virtualization and container technologies, especially Kubernetes, is an advantage
  • Team player with a high level of initiative, conscientious working methods and pleasure in helping to shape
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • English language skills (level B2) are beneficial
  • Military career as a senior non-commissioned officer or officer of advantage

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.

Over the area

The Business Area C5I of RUAG MRO Holding AG supports the Swiss Army in the realization of ICT projects with a specific focus on the Cyber Command. This is your chance to contribute to highly secure and sovereign IT systems. Over the past eight years, we have grown from a small “start-up” with zero employees to more than 260 employees and offer a unique, dynamic environment. Here you can work at the forefront of the digitization of the Swiss Army. Do you want to contribute to the security environment? Then you are exactly right with us. We work agile and consistently follow the DevSecOps model. With the C5I campus, we have created an innovation space for highly secure IT systems and offer the most attractive workplaces.
