Full description

Our customer is a construction company with around 20 employees in the Chur region. The focus is on smaller conversions and renovations directly at the customer – for the most part in the interior.

Their tasks

  • Independent execution of customer orders, depending on the size of the order, with the assistance of an auxiliary
  • Minor remodeling and refurbishment works, e.g. window installation, plaster, masonry, plate-laying and concrete works
  • Advice and contact with local customers
  • Clean work in inhabited spaces
  • Management of working papers and reports

Your profile

  • Good professional experience in customer service or in conversions and renovations
  • Completed training as a mason or in the main building industry
  • Versatile, independent and clean working method
  • Good physical condition and resilience
  • Good knowledge of German for customer contact and working papers
  • Cat B driving licence mandatory
  • Mobile or resident in the Chur region

We offer

  • Varied customer orders with a lot of personal responsibility
  • Mainly indoor work
  • Manageable team in a regionally anchored company
  • Possibility of permanent employment after 3 months of temporary employment
