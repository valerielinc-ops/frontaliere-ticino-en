Full description

With a draughtsman apprenticeship at AFRY Schweiz AG, you will set the course for your professional future. We offer you a varied and challenging apprenticeship in the exciting world of an engineering company. Over a period of four years, you will gain interesting insights into various specialist areas and conclude your apprenticeship with a federal certificate of competence at the end. As of August 2027, we have a vacant apprenticeship position at our branch in Chur: APPRENTICESHIP AS A DRAUGHTSMAN WITH A SPECIALIZATION IN CIVIL ENGINEERING EFZ - CHUR During the apprenticeship, you will learn the following activities, among others:

  • Preparation of plans in civil engineering (e.g. public buildings, residential buildings)
  • Preparation of plans in civil engineering (including sewer systems, bridges, roads)
  • Collaborate with civil engineers and assist with construction inspections
  • Construction site internship of at least 2 weeks, at the earliest in the 4th semester

For the apprenticeship, you preferably have the following profile:

  • Completed elementary school (secondary school or very good secondary school)
  • Interests / strengths in geometry, mathematics and physics
  • Enjoyment of technical drawing and precise work
  • Good spatial awareness

What we offer

  • Young, collegial and helpful team
  • Modern employment conditions
  • Training location in a very easily accessible location
  • Exciting and versatile apprenticeship

We look forward to receiving your online application (preferably with Multicheck)!
