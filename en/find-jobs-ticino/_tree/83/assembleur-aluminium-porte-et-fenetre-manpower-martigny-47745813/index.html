Aluminium assembly, door and window - Martigny, Switzerland

Our company, a leader in the manufacturing and installation of aluminium structures, is recognized for its commitment to quality, innovation and customer satisfaction. By joining our team, you will join a dynamic company, caring for its employees and offering many opportunities for professional development in a stimulating and growing environment

Key responsibilities

  • Assemble and assemble aluminum doors and windows according to technical drawings and specifications
  • Check conformity of parts and assemblies before installation
  • Use manual and electrical tools to perform assembly operations
  • Comply with existing workplace safety standards
  • Work with the team to ensure efficient and quality production flow
  • Participate in the maintenance and maintenance of assembly equipment

Skills and qualifications required

  • Prior experience in mounting or assembling aluminium structures, doors and windows
  • Knowledge of industry-specific assembly techniques and tools
  • Ability to read and interpret technical plans
  • Strict compliance with safety and quality standards
  • Good teamwork and communication skills
  • Valid driving licence is an asset
  • Vocational training in construction, carpentry or metallurgy
