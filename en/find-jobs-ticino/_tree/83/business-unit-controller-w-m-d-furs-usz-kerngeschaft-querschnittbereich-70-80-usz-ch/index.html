USZ Campus

immediately or by agreement

In this function, you advise and support the decentralized executives as a business partner: in, actively shape change processes and, as an information specialist: in and business conscience of the clinics and institutes, ensure a sound basis for decision-making. They work closely with the departmental management, the decentralized business experts and interprofessional stakeholder groups and form the interface between the units under management, the department and the finances.

Their main tasks

  • Advice and support in all business administration or Controlling relevant requests and concerns
  • Support in financial processes such as planning (budget and multiannual planning) and reporting as well as in the creation/plausibility checking of business cases
  • Assistance in creating transparency about the course of business and developing the basis for decision-making for management and other stakeholders
  • Assistance in the preparation of proposals for measures and quantification of the impact of possible alternative courses of action to improve the financial result
  • Support for the active management and monitoring of budget targets and action plans
  • Participation in sectoral and cross-sectoral projects and representation of financial concerns in commissions and committees of the units to be supervised
  • Improvement of operational interdisciplinary processes and interfaces for integrated controlling
  • Conceptual development of business management tools
  • Analysis and assessment of economic accounts and investment applications

Your profile

  • Business degree or equivalent qualification with specialisation in finance and controlling
  • Several years of experience in controlling in a medium or large company, preferably in the healthcare sector
  • Deep understanding of financing healthcare providers
  • Strong analytical and conceptual thinking skills
  • Structured and precise working methods as well as a high quality understanding
  • Independent and reliable work with a lot of commitment
  • Recipient-oriented information presentation and strong communication skills required
  • Flexible, resilient and team-capable personality
  • Very good knowledge with MS Office, especially Excel and PowerPoint
  • Very good SAP knowledge required
