Full description

Our Medical-Social Centres (CMS) offer the Lausanne population quality multidisciplinary services and their interventions are based on values of humanity and fairness. By joining the Lausanne Care Foundation, you are committed in a concrete way to promoting health and well-being in the canton of Vaud, within expert, motivated and enthusiastic teams, in a varied activity rich in human contacts. In the field, you intervene at the place of life or activity of the beneficiaries. You define with customers the solutions best suited to their situations and thus develop a fine knowledge and a privileged relationship of trust with them. As part of a team that is welded and supported, the exercise of these professions allows for a great autonomy but requires great responsibility and flexibility on the part of our employees. In order to complete the team of the CMS of the Ancien-Stand, La Fondation Soins Lausanne researches de suite or to agree a.e.:

RAI Nurse - HC (M/F)

Participation rate between 80% Fixed contract with undetermined duration

Your Mission

  • Conduct assessments and reassessments of all client situations at home using the Resident Assessment Instrument (RAI-HC) method
  • Assess situations and identify needs
  • Plan, coordinate, provide and monitor care
  • Advise and inform cared persons and their surroundings
  • Contribute to health maintenance and disease prevention through an interdisciplinary approach
  • Strengthening personal autonomy and coping skills
  • Developing the quality of care and adapting services to new knowledge

Your profile and skills

  • Bachelor's degree in nursing or equivalent (foreign diploma recognized by the CRS)
  • RAI-HC Evaluator Training an asset
  • 2 years of proven experience in the medical and social field, in interdisciplinary collaboration and networks
  • A sense of responsibility, ability to organize independently and set priorities
  • Ability to collaborate in networking and multidisciplinarity

We offer

  • A working environment that favours human contact and exchange
  • A stimulating and rewarding activity within a dynamic multidisciplinary team
  • Offers of rich and varied training
  • Conditions of work and remuneration under the Collective Labour Convention (CCT) in the public health sector in Vaud

Benefits of joining us

  • Plan for integration by occupation, offers of rich and varied continuing training, opportunities for vocational development
  • Gender pay equity (fair-on-pay certification)
  • Remuneration according to the Collective Labour Convention (CCT) of the public health sector of voodoo
  • Favourable offers with preferential rates in service companies around sport, health and welfare and leisure
  • Soft mobility policy and participation in public transport costs
  • Five weeks of vacation per year and six weeks from 50 years
