Full description

Are you an organizational talent, reliable and enduring? Do you enjoy technical equipment, craftsmanship and a high sense of responsibility? If this is true and you bring appealing school grades from the Real or Secondary School, an apprenticeship as a logistician might be the right thing for you! As a logistics specialist EFZ, a 3-year apprenticeship awaits you. With us you can work in different storage areas and get to know the work of warehouse logistics in all facets.

  • In the beginning, you will be introduced by experienced logisticians and perform various, more manually oriented activities.
  • These tasks are primarily concerned with the picking and provision of smaller goods, promotional products and the like.
  • Once you have completed the course for the use of electric trucks, you are allowed to carry out the first internal mechanical movements of goods
  • In addition to the lessons at the vocational school in Olten, you will learn about the correct handling of goods receipt and delivery in practice.
  • If you have successfully completed the intercompany forklift course, you can store and remove palletized goods independently
  • Later you will learn to use a hall crane to store, remove or relocate long goods of all kinds professionally.
  • In addition to the efficient loading of goods into/from trucks, you will get to know the most important points of a correct load securing

We expect

  • Practical understanding
  • Craftsmanship
  • Enjoyment of technical equipment
  • High sense of responsibility
  • Reliability and endurance
  • Self-employment
  • Appealing real or secondary school grades

We offer

  • Interesting activities in various camps
  • Varied and practical training
  • Lively working environment
  • Top infrastructure (32 tons of hall crane system, large forklifts, etc.)
  • Collegial working environment
