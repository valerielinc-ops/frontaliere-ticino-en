Full description

1st employer of the canton, the Hospital of Valais employs 6’200 collaborators. Each year, 41’500 patients are cared for and nearly 652’700 outpatient visits are provided. The Hospital of Valais is seeking for the Hospital Center of the Valais Romand Nurses in CDD and CDI between 60% and 90% for the geriatric pole of the Hospital of Martigny The Geriatric Pole of the Valais Romand groups several establishments specialized in the care of the elderly. It offers a continuity of care adapted to the specific needs of the patients, guaranteeing a medical, caring, medico-therapeutic, social and human support of quality. In Martigny, you will work in a dynamic clinical environment, welcoming acute and complex situations, at the heart of a global and interdisciplinary care. ## Mission

  • Ensure the care of patients in the service according to the strategy of the HVS and the Professional Practice Model
  • Actively contribute to a person-centered care, taking into account their life path, resources and specific needs
  • Ensure the good functioning of the service, the quality and safety of care, the integration of the patient and their relatives as partners in care
  • Promote network work, within an interdisciplinary team ## Profile
  • Diploma of nurse from HES or equivalent (validation from Swiss Red Cross mandatory)
  • Practical experience in a service of acute somatic care desired. Experience in geriatrics, an asset
  • Interest in clinical complexity and interdisciplinary work
  • Ability to promote and develop quality care
  • Relational qualities, sense of listening and communication
  • Mastery of computer tools and general office work
  • Dynamism, great flexibility and openness of mind ## Offer
  • A dynamic and benevolent working environment within a cohesive and committed interdisciplinary team
  • A rich and stimulating care context, favoring the development of clinical reasoning and professional autonomy
  • Continuous professional development opportunities
  • An attractive remuneration and advantageous working conditions Find here all the benefits for our collaborators.
