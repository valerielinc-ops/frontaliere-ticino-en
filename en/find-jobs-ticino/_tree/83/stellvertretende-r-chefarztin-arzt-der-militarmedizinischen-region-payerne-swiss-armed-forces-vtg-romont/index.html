Put to the Point

Are you a medical professional looking for a versatile working environment with high relevance, development opportunities and a balanced work-life balance? The role as deputy chief physician of a military medical region offers you the opportunity to take direct responsibility for the medical care of the members of the army. The complex questions in the interdisciplinary environment of medicine and psychology will inspire you! Military prior knowledge and experience are not mandatory.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-18165
  • Location: Caserne Drognens, 1680 Romont

You can make this contribution

  • Represent the Chief Physician in the Military Medical Region (MMR) technically and personnel
  • Ensure medical care for ambulatory and inpatient patients in the various medical services infrastructures and ensure coordination for further treatment
  • Assess the capacity of members of the army and carry out or order medical and special investigations
  • The military doctors support, coach and monitor their treatments
  • Maintain contacts with military partners and civilian service providers and actively participate in medical service projects

This makes them unique.

  • Eidg. Diploma as a doctor or equivalent with MEBEKO recognition, specialist title and clinical experience
  • Interest in contact with members of the army and in military and military medical topics
  • Flexibility in terms of time (within regular working hours, no regular night or weekend assignments)
  • Good active knowledge of at least one second official language and, if possible, passive knowledge of a third official language

We offer this

  • We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the material, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3,500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
