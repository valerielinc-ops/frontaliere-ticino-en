Full description

The Office for Industry, Trade and Labour (KIGA) currently employs around 120 staff members in six regions in the Canton of Graubünden. In addition to the public employment service (RAV), we organise labour market measures for jobseekers and manage the cantonal unemployment fund. We also perform various tasks in the areas of the labour market and occupational safety.

We are looking for an employment adviser (80-100%) for the Employment Services department at RAV Chur as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Responsibilities

  • Advising and placing jobseekers
  • Supporting jobseekers with their positioning and reintegration into the labour market
  • Developing and agreeing on appropriate reintegration strategies and qualification measures with jobseekers
  • Working with various partner institutions (Social Services Office, IV, etc.)
  • Maintaining contact with employers in the region
  • Acquiring job vacancies

Requirements

  • Vocational qualification and several years of professional experience
  • Swiss federal certificate as an HR specialist or willingness to complete this further training (alternatively, a certificate in social insurance)
  • Knowledge of the local labor market
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented way of working
  • You are resilient and have strong social skills
  • You have empathetic skills and are able to respond to people in difficult circumstances
  • You have excellent communication skills and are a team player
  • Good German and Italian language skills as well as good IT skills are required
  • Willingness to work at other RAV locations in the canton of GR as well
