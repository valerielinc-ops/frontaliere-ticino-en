Full description

The CNP is looking for an assistant medical officer in Psychology. Join a dynamic team and participate in patient rehabilitation.

Tasks

  • Develop and implement programs for the department.
  • Ensure the proper functioning of clinical activities as a team.
  • Provide primary care expertise and assistance.

Competences

  • Recognized Swiss diploma and 2 years experience in psychiatry.
  • Interdisciplinary work and teamwork.
  • Leadership and Planning.

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients suffering from mental disorders by adopting an integrated and community psychiatry model; implements public policies to promote and prevent mental health

  • It acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research.

The department of advanced age is looking for an outpatient unit:

Medical Officer, Assistant Clinical Officer, Psychology - 80-100% s/ref: R2026011

You have a Swiss diploma or are recognised by MEBEKO. You have obtained the first part of the title FMH, and would like to join a recognized institution specializing in mental health? We welcome your application and treat it confidentially.

As part of your function

o You are involved in the development and implementation of the guidelines and programs necessary for the department's objectives. o You ensure the proper functioning of clinical activities in a spirit of multidisciplinary collaboration and within a mobile team. o You provide expertise and assistance to front-line medicine, home care teams or EMS, as well as in collaboration with somatic care. o You are able to provide specialized care and adequate support outside the psychiatric hospital setting. o You are involved in the ongoing training of teams to ensure a high level of skills, and contribute to the development and improvement of diagnostic treatment methods.

The competencies required for this position are

o have a minimum of 2 years of hospital and/or outpatient psychiatry activity o have a spirit of interdisciplinary teamwork and networking o demonstrate leadership and planning o be able to make decisions o driver's license and personal car, to travel to the healthcare facility: EMS and other partners. Entry into office: 1 May 2026 Place of work : Vieux-Châtel 18-20, 2000 Neuchâtel (mobility on the canton) Type of contract: Indeterminate contract CDI Information: Marie Leclerc, Recruitment Manager, and Dresse Liana Sutu, Acting Department Physician Full applications (motivation letter, CV, diplomas and work certificates) should be submitted by online application.
