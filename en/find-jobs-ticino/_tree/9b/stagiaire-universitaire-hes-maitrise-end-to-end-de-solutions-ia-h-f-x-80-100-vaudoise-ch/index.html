Full description

In order to strengthen our Digital Transformation & Information Systems Department as part of the evolution of an artificial intelligence platform on the Azure cloud, we are looking for a Master's intern for a period of 6 months from February 2027 in Lausanne. Vaudoise Assurance has set up an artificial intelligence platform (Agentic AI) on the cloud Azure. One of the challenges around such a solution is the mastery end to end when executing usage cases. During this internship, the student will join Vaudoise Assurance's Artificial Intelligence team to review the existing comprehensive monitoring, performance and study industry best practices, provide recommendations and then implement the solution. During this internship, the student will have the opportunity to work on a platform among industry standards and to participate in the development of a critical component for artificial intelligence, namely the measurement of performance monitoring (inference, scoring, data drift). The missions that await you:

  • Review and understanding of an existing Agentic AI solution
  • Analysis of industry good practices
  • Recommendations adapted to the context of Vaudoise Assurance
  • Implementation of the recommended and selected solution Your profile:
  • You are in the course of academic studies (Master University or High School), Computer Science or Data Science
  • You master the end-to-end principles of Data Science and have strong knowledge of Python
  • You are familiar with the development and industrialization of data models; an experience with MLflow is an asset
  • You have a lot of ease to fit into a team.
  • You know how to adapt in a rapidly changing technical environment We offer you :
  • A healthy working environment in a cooperative company certified "Great Place to Work"
  • A varied and rewarding work experience
  • A possible opportunity for placement at the end of the internship/training The HR team is looking forward to receiving your complete application package (CV, cover letter and 3 final transcripts).
