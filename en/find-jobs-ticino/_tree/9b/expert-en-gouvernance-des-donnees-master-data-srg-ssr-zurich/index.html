Full description

How does SRG ensure that its most important data – across all regions, languages and business units – is consistent, reliable and usable? As part of the transformation, we are building company-wide data governance that answers this question. In the Data Governance team, you are responsible for master data and contribute significantly to ensuring that SRG operates on a uniform, high-quality database.

This is your contribution.

  • Develop, maintain and update SRG-wide master data standards and policies
  • You define and maintain business rules for critical master data objects
  • They coordinate the cross-domain integration of master data and ensure consistency across systems and domains.
  • They monitor master data quality, identify deviations and initiate measures in cooperation with data stewards
  • You support data stewards and departments with your technical expertise in dealing with master data tooling
  • You contribute to the Community of Practice Master Data and take over their operational moderation if necessary

They inspire us.

  • University degree or equivalent education and deep experience in metadata management
  • Good understanding of data architectures, processes and models as well as business rules and their practical application
  • Experience in establishing standards, processes or governance structures as well as master data management tools
  • Knowledge of DAMA-DMBOK knowledge areas, in particular Reference & Master Data Management; CDMP certification is an advantage
  • Strong communication and moderation competence in interdisciplinary and cross-functional settings to advise specialist areas in the use, structuring and quality assurance of data
  • Very good language skills at level C1 in a national language as well as German and French at least at level B1, English at level B1

So we inspire you.

  • Meaningful operational key role in building enterprise-wide data governance with direct impact on SRG data quality
  • Close cooperation with editorial, business, technology and governance in an interdisciplinary environment
  • Active participation in data governance, data quality and data-driven working methods within the SRG
  • Space for personal responsibility, professional impact and continuous learning
  • Collaboration with an interdisciplinary team across all language regions in Switzerland
  • Hybrid working model with flexible forms of work and home office possibilities

Please submit your application documents (motivation letter and CV) via the “Apply now” button. The application period ends on 6 November 2026.

The position focuses on Master Data Management. Depending on the profile and organizational needs, it is possible to take on selected tasks in the field of meta data management.
