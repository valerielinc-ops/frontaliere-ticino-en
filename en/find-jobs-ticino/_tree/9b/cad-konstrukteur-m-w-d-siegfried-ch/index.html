Your Role

As a CAD constructor, you are responsible for the creation, maintenance and further development of the technical plant documentation at the Zofingen site. They support investment projects by creating CAD documentation and 3D models and thus make an important contribution to ensuring up-to-date and GMP-compliant plant documentation.

Your Profiles

Your role

  • Creation and maintenance of pipeline and instrument flow diagrams (R&I) for existing plants and investment projects in the CAD system
  • Creating and updating 3D models for new and renovation projects
  • Responsible for versioning and managing CAD master documents
  • Creating and maintaining building plans, floor plans, cuts and views for the Zofingen location
  • Maintenance and updating of site plans and supply and disposal network plans (e.g. water, steam, nitrogen and sewage networks)
  • Creating and updating clean room, ex-zone, fire protection and escape route plans
  • Creation and maintenance of apparatus and plant layouts
  • Supporting investment projects by creating technical project documentation
  • Ensuring a current, complete and comprehensible plant documentation
  • Cooperation with internal departments and external partners to ensure correct documentation

Your profile Specialised knowledge

  • Completed training as a CAD designer, draftsman and experience in the chemical-process engineering environment
  • Several years of experience in the preparation of technical documentation and CAD plans, ideally in the industrial environment
  • Good knowledge in CAD systems as well as in dealing with technical documentation as well as sound knowledge in AutoCad ideally with Cadison
  • Understanding process plants, pipeline planning and technical infrastructures is imperative
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

Personal strengths

  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • High quality and responsibility awareness
  • Analytical thinking and technical understanding
  • Teamwork and strong communication skills
  • High service orientation and flexibility

Founded in 1873 in Zofingen, Switzerland, Siegfried has grown into a global network with 16 locations on three continents. With a team of over 4,200 highly qualified professionals, we bring the innovations of our pharmaceutical customers to industrial production and manufacture safe medicines for patients worldwide. As a fully integrated CDMO, Siegfried is one of the few companies that can now combine the development and production of active ingredients (APIs) and finished dosage forms under one roof. This unique combination of know-how and experience makes us the most trusted partner of the pharmaceutical industry.

Siegfried is a leading CDMO (Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization) headquartered in Zofingen, Switzerland. The company operates manufacturing sites in Evionnaz (Valais), Zofingen (Aargau), and Schlieren (Zurich).
