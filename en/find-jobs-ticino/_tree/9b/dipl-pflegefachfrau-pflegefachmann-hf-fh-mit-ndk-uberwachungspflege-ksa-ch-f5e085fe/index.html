Full description

Tasks

  • As a certified nurse / certified nurse HF/FH with NDK monitoring care care and accompany our patients with epathic and competencies.
  • Under appropriate guidance and supervision you acquire the skills to care for the critically ill patient with appropriate severity in the intensive care area.
  • In addition to your care competence, you also bring interest and enthusiasm for the care of patients in complex situations and are ready to engage in new activities.
  • Learning and developing is supported by professional trainers and the whole team.

Requirements

  • The handling of fast changing, complex situations is a welcome challenge for you.
