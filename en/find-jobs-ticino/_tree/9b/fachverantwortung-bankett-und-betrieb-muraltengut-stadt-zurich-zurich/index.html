Full description

To complement our team, we are looking for 1. February 2027 or by agreement a service-oriented, resilient and committed personality.

  • Together with your teammate, you organize all, mostly city council events in the Muraltengut from preparation to implementation and post-processing, ensuring compliance with the respective budget and a high representative standard.
  • If necessary, they support the organisation of external events on site.
  • They ensure the maintenance of the house and the care of the high-quality interior.
  • They are under the co-management of the Office for Representation and Events and are in close contact with it.
  • You have a degree from a hotel management school or equivalent and have some years of professional experience in the event sector and/or in the upscale catering or hotel industry
  • You are an organizational talent with a very high level of quality awareness, work independently and goal-oriented, have a healthy assertiveness, are reliable in negotiations and are flexible to integrate into existing structures and adhere to service routes.
  • Good IT knowledge (MS-Office) as well as correct and stylish German in word and writing we require
  • They convince with a winning personality, a well-groomed appearance and pronounced manners, look after guests discreetly and attentively and keep calm and overview even in hectic situations.
  • They have craftsmanship, can independently monitor, control and control technical equipment and are ready to perform high attendance hours as well as work on occasions in the evening and on about two Saturdays per year.

A versatile and interesting task in a challenging and dynamic environment as well as a motivated team and progressive working conditions. The workplace is the Muraltengut in Zürich Enge, which you share with your teammate. The Muraltengut is a former country house and today the representative house of the City Council of Zurich.

The Presidential Department of the City of Zurich comprises the departments of Culture, Population Office, Museum Rietberg, City Archives, Statistics City of Zurich, Urban Development, Office for Gender Equality and the Department Secretariat. The Departmental Secretariat, to which the Office for Representation is assigned, sees itself as an internal service provider and supports the City Council, the President and the Department. Equality, diversity and inclusion are important to us.
