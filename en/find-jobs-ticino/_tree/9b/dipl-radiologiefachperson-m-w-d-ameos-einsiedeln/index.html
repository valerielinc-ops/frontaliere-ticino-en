Full description

To strengthen our team at AMEOS Spital Einsiedeln we are looking for a

Dipl. Radiology specialist (m/f/d) – 20–40 %

Their advantages

  • 20-40% workload without night services – with versatile and demanding tasks
  • Modern devices for your daily work in radiology
  • An established, motivated team and a family working atmosphere
  • Contemporary employment conditions with numerous benefits
  • Attractive training opportunities

Their tasks

  • Independent planning and implementation of our radiological investigation spectrum
  • Conventional X-ray, fluoroscopy and computed tomography, use in MRI optional
  • Appointment, reception and administration
  • Care of patients before, during and after the examination
  • Assistance in minimally invasive procedures
  • Collaboration in shift operations, as well as on weekends and public holidays. No night service

Your profile Completed training for the dipl. Radiology specialist HF, also professional or re-entry. They meet patients empathetically and friendly, like to work interdisciplinary and are service-oriented in contact with our referrals. They bring team spirit, motivation and a sense of responsibility and enjoy working with modern technology. They have technical understanding and savvy PC application knowledge.
