Your new role Senior Process Science Engineer - Laser Technologies (m/f/d) You play a highly specialized role in our organization and, through a deep understanding of cold ablation and extremely precise material removal rates, ensure the successful development and transfer into the series production of laser beam microstructuring processes (especially with UKP lasers, i.e. ultrashort pulse). Your main tasks are:

  • Conduct feasibility studies and develop robust processes for laser ablation, cutting and drilling at the micron scale.
  • Systematic process window determination based on the application of scientific methods (e.g. DoE – Design of Experiments) to define stable process parameters (pulse energy, repetition rate, scanning speed).
  • Selection and qualification of laser sources - Evaluation and selection of suitable beam sources (e.g. picosecond or femtosecond lasers) and optical components for specific material requirements.
  • Analysis of removal results using high-resolution measurement techniques (e.g. light microscopy, white light interferometry, REM) to ensure the quality of microstructuring.
  • Optimisation of beam guidance and shaping - adaptation and adjustment of optical systems (scanners, objectives, beam expanders) to achieve optimum focal diameters and beam profiles.
  • Transfer to series production - transfer of the developed processes from the laboratory status to industrial series maturity, including the preparation of the necessary documentation
