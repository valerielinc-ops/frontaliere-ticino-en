Full description

The cantonal police and its approximately 4,000 employees assume responsibility for security in the canton. Our vehicle workshop in Urdorf is responsible for maintenance/repair work, 24-hour piquetting service and police-specific equipment of the service vehicles as well as trailers. For this demanding and responsible function, we are looking for an expert, independent and committed automotive mechatronics specialist / - in 100% tasks

  • Performing diagnostic, repair and maintenance work on emergency vehicles of the Cantonal Police of Zurich
  • Cooperation in the procurement and management of spare parts
  • Support for special operations (e.g. driving courses)
  • Piquet service providers
  • Participation in training and mentoring of the learner profile
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as automotive mechatronics specialist EFZ
  • Additional training as an automotive diagnostician
  • Several years of professional experience desired
  • Brand knowledge BMW, Mercedes-Benz and/or VW is an advantage
  • Good IT user knowledge (Word, Excel)
  • Reliable, accurate operation and high load capacity
  • Flexibility, dedication, teamwork and confidentiality
  • Cat B driving licence (A and C required)
  • Correct reputation and offer
  • We offer you a versatile and meaningful activity in a dynamic environment
