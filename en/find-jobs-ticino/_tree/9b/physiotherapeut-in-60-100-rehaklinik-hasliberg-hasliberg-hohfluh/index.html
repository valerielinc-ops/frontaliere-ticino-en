Full description

Hasliberg Rehabilitation Clinic is a renowned Swiss clinic in the field of psychosomatic, internistic-oncological and musculoskeletal rehabilitation. The clinic has 83 beds and is on the hospital lists of the cantons of Bern, Lucerne, Solothurn, Nidwalden and Uri. The ISO-certified company works process-oriented and interdisciplinary, based on the mission statement of “REHA competence” with the characteristics: respect, efficiency, warmth, attention and competence.

Their tasks

  • Individual and group treatments of inpatient patients in the fields of musculoskeletal, psychosomatic and internistic-oncological rehabilitation
  • Treatment of outpatient patients with different clinical pictures
  • Implementation and recording of clinic-internal assessments
  • Electronic documentation of treatments and treatment courses
  • Creating Individual Home Programs
  • Active cooperation in the interdisciplinary treatment team

Our expectations

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Physical therapist: in FH or an equivalent education recognised in Switzerland
  • Enjoy working with different patient groups
  • Self-employed, responsible and flexible working methods
  • Empathy and an appreciative treatment of patients and employees
  • Enjoying teamwork and interdisciplinary exchange
  • Motivation for professional and personal development

Our offer

  • A motivated and collegial physiotherapy team with different specialist focuses
  • A varied and independent activity with inpatient and outpatient patients
  • A careful and well-founded introduction to your new task area
  • Plenty of space for independent work and professional exchange
  • Regular internal training
  • Support and promotion of external training
  • Regular team meetings and joint team events
  • Shared use of our swimming pool and fitness area
  • A job in one of the most beautiful regions of Switzerland – where others go on holiday
  • Whether hiking, biking, skiing or simply enjoying nature – the region offers an exceptional quality of life and leisure
