Become part of our team!

Together for our region and our corporate customers. At Raiffeisen See-Lac, we combine cooperative values with entrepreneurial thinking. Trust, closeness and collaborative cooperation shape our everyday life. For the support and further development of our company customers, we are looking for a committed personality who lives our values and wants to actively shape the future of the regional economy together with us. Venue: Murten with missions at further locations of Raiffeisen See-Lac

What do you expect?

  • Holistic advice and support of corporate clients with focus on financing solutions
  • Analysis and structuring of credit operations and preparation of credit applications
  • Expansion and maintenance of its own customer portfolio as well as targeted acquisition of new corporate customers
  • Identifying and exploiting customer potential through needs-oriented consulting and networking
  • Representation of the bank at customer events and participation in market development and network activities

What do you bring?

  • Several years of experience in corporate banking and in-depth knowledge of finance
  • In-depth knowledge in the analysis and support of credit transactions, ideally in the area of commercial and return objects
  • SAQ certification as an SME customer advisor or willingness to acquire it
  • Customer-oriented personality with pleasure in the active further development of customer relationships and market potential
  • Good knowledge of French

Would you like to accompany companies in our region and make a difference together with a committed team? Then we look forward to meeting you and learning more about your motivation for corporate client consulting with us. Roman Bachmann Member of the bank management Why Raiffeisen?

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.
