Intern Office of Studies (m/f) - 6 months 100%

As part of the development of our activities, we are looking for an Intern in charge of CVC Studies to gain practical experience in the design office and to contribute to various projects in the fields of heating, ventilation, air conditioning and technical installations of the building.

In our house, you can move things.

Your missions will be as follows

  • Participate in the analysis of project files and technical needs.
  • Conduct on-site technical surveys.
  • Contribute to the calculation and dimensioning of HVAC facilities.
  • Perform flow, power, load loss and other technical calculations.
  • Participate in the selection and benchmarking of technical equipment.
  • Complete and update plans and outline principles.
  • Establish metres and quantitatives.
  • Consult with suppliers and analyse technical proposals received.
  • Participate in the encryption and development of offers.
  • Prepare calculation and analysis tables under Excel.
  • Attend technical meetings and participate in project monitoring.
  • Support study staff in the preparation of technical documentation.
  • Contribute to the creation and improvement of tools for calculating, quantifying and monitoring projects.

Which is what you do.

  • Current training in climate engineering, energy, CCV, building technology or equivalent.
  • Basic knowledge in thermal, hydraulic and aeraulic.
  • Good control of Microsoft Office 365 tools, including Excel.
  • Knowledge of AutoCAD and/or Revit is an asset.

Interest in Swiss building standards and regulations.

  • Use with digital tools and controlled use of artificial intelligence tools.

Here's what we offer you.

An immersion in a team of passionate experts. The discovery of various and stimulating technical projects. A proximity accompaniment that promotes your professional development. The opportunity to put your academic knowledge into practice on concrete projects. A modern, collaborative and innovative working environment.
