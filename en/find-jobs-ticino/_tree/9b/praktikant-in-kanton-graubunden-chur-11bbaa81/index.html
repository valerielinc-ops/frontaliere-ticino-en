Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. Graubünden is the largest canton in Switzerland with 7106 km2 and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days per year. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the cantonal administration Graubünden together with us.

The Road Traffic Office is responsible for the traffic approval of vehicles, drivers and ships, the periodic inspection of vehicles and ships, the enactment of administrative measures against fallible vehicle drivers as well as for the assessment and collection of traffic taxes.

We are looking for an intern for the Road Traffic Office / Department of Justice, Security and Health as of 01\.03\.2027 or by appointment in the area of administrative measures (warnings, driver's license revocation, etc.).

This is what we offer

  • Insight into the work of a cantonal administration
  • Collegial and supportive working environment
  • Assistance from experienced employees
  • Opportunity for participation and active participation

Your responsibilities

  • Support for the case-in-law officers
  • Completion of various administrative activities
  • Telephone and written information
  • Customer switch operation
  • Management of matters in the field of administrative measures

That is what we want from you.

  • Ongoing or completed education/vocational training
  • Interest in administrative procedures and public service
  • Basic knowledge of MS Office (Word, Excel, Outlook)
  • Self-employed and careful working methods
  • Discretion in dealing with confidential information
  • Teamwork and reliability

Our advantages

Flexible working time models and where possible incl. Home office and mobile work\-life\-balance with mind. 5 weeks holidays and service leave Special social allowance in addition to child allowance and support for third-party childcare Attractive wage and social benefits Various training and further education opportunities to shape the future of the canton
