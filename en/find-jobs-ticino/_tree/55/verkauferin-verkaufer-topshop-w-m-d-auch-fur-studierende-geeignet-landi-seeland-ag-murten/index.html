The LANDI cooperatives support Swiss farmers in the economic development of their businesses. Together with the Volg, TopShop and AGROLA brands, the LANDI cooperatives also supply the population in rural Switzerland with food, everyday goods and energy.We are looking for a motivated and independent person for our TopShop in Murten, with immediate effect or by agreement.

These tasks will inspire you

  • Expert advice and support for our customers, as well as active sales
  • Operating the scanning checkout
  • Inventory management and restocking shelves and coolers, including freshness checks
  • Baking bread and preparing lunch offerings
  • Ensuring order and cleanliness in the shop, warehouse and at the petrol station
  • Contributing to a positive working atmosphere

This is the profile we are looking for

  • Outgoing and team-oriented personality
  • Motivated and dynamic approach to work
  • Flexibility and resilience are among your strengths
  • Willingness to learn as well as interest in new tasks
  • Availability to work on weekends, public holidays, as well as early and late shifts
  • Good German language skills, both spoken and written, as well as French language skills

Discover your benefits

  • Training and continuing education: We invest in the development of our employees. A broad range of internal educational opportunities is open to everyone, and we support external continuing education.
  • Attractive holiday policy: Our holiday policy guarantees all employees at least 5 weeks of holiday. As of 50. Year of age, it is 6 weeks, and from the 60. Year of age, even 7 weeks. In addition, it is possible to purchase additional vacation days.
  • Family time: Mothers receive 100 percent of their insured salary during the 18 weeks of maternity leave. The other parent enjoys 3 weeks of family time at full pay.
  • Insurance benefits: We provide our employees with above-average social security and pension benefits and offer special conditions on supplementary insurance with various health insurance providers for their entire families.
  • Discounts and rebates: Our employees benefit, among other things, from discounts when shopping and refuelling, REKA money credits, and discounted mobile subscriptions for themselves and their families.
