Full description

You want to not only shape care strategically, but also be present where care takes place daily? You enjoy working closely with managers and teams, tackling operational issues yourself and at the same time keeping an eye on the big picture? Then we look forward to meeting you. For our Nursing Directorate, we are looking for an experienced and committed leader who supports our Nursing Director and at the same time actively participates in the operative day-to-day care of our clinic. Your tasks

  • You support the nursing director in the operational and strategic management of the nursing area.
  • You are regularly present on the wards and in the areas and know the challenges of everyday clinical life.
  • You support our station and division management in personnel, organizational and professional issues.
  • You actively help ensure smooth day-to-day business and short-term operational challenges.
  • Together with the management teams, you ensure a high quality of care, patient safety and efficient processes.
  • You accompany change, quality and digitalization projects and put them into practice together with the teams.
  • You promote an open, appreciative and binding cooperation between nursing, the medical profession and other professional groups.
  • In the absence of the nursing director, you assume their deputy and the corresponding management responsibility.
  • Completed training in nursing (HF/FH) with further qualification in management (e.g. MAS, CAS leadership in healthcare)
  • Several years of leadership experience in the acute field, ideally in a comparable position
  • Enjoyment of an operative leadership function close to everyday care
  • In-depth knowledge in Swiss healthcare as well as in quality and process management
  • High social skills, assertiveness and communication
  • Strategic thinking and strong organizational skills
  • Structured, solution-oriented and resilient personality
  • Language skills: French, Italian and English

You are part of the operational process, you are involved in shaping and you can have an immediate impact. We offer you a responsible leadership position with short decision-making paths, plenty of room for manoeuvre and close cooperation with the care directorate as well as our interprofessional teams. Interested?

Treatment at the highest level, high-class medical care and care, competent care in a more familiar, private atmosphere with hotel comfort, dedication, conscientiousness and absolute discretion – this is what the private clinic Bethanien stands for. Around 340 medical doctors from different disciplines provide high-quality, integrated medical care. Among the most important areas of expertise are orthopaedics, visceral and thoracic surgery, urology, gynecology and birth, gastroenterology, cardiology, pneumology and internal medicine incl. Oncology. In the birth department about 500 children are born each year. Founded in 1912, the private clinic Bethanien is part of the Swiss Medical Network.
