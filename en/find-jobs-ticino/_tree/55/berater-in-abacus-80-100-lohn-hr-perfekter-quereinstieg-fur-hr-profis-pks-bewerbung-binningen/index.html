ID: Category: HRM Region: Basel Reference: Consultant:in ABACUS 80 - 100% (Wage/HR) Our customer is a successful, ISO-9001 certified general contractor specializing in high-quality software and hardware solutions. Sales, implementation, training and support of ERP software (Abacus) are among the central competencies of our client. Thanks to a consistent focus on high consulting and service quality, the company has developed a strong market position and currently employs around 30 people. In order to strengthen the team, we are looking for a customer- and service-oriented personality as Consultant:in ABACUS 80 - 100% (Wage/HR) - perfect lateral entry for HR professionals

Their tasks

  • Expert advice for customers on the introduction of Abacus as well as on the optimization and digitalization of processes, with a focus on human resources (e.g. employee interviews, certificates, application and competence management, ESS and MSS) as well as payroll
  • Creation of offers, concepts, documentation and training materials
  • Recording and specification of customer requirements as well as supporting implementation through parameterization and customization
  • Conduct of workshops and training for users
  • Supporting customers through support and hotline
  • Responsibility for project implementation in terms of timetable, budget and quality

Your profile

  • Several years of professional experience in payroll/HR administration
  • Practical experience in holistic project management is advantageous
  • You are an established and awakened personality who reacts flexibly and situationally to people (customers) and situations.
  • A networked way of thinking and a structured, independent and exact way of working
  • A flair for IT, digitalization and the SME environment
  • High service mentality and enthusiasm
  • stylish knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Knowledge of French and/or English is advantageous

What to expect

  • A comprehensive internal and external induction program over approx. 2 years
  • A dynamic and harmonious team with an excellent working atmosphere
  • Modern offices
  • Very easy access to public transport (5 minutes walk from Basel city centre)
  • Advanced employment conditions, including 5 weeks holidays
  • An environment in which employees are promoted and challenged
