Full description

At Micarna SA in Bazenheid, you take on a central role in production planning and ensure that everything along the entire pork value chain meshes smoothly – from slaughter to finished goods availability. Look forward to a dynamic environment, a lot of responsibility and close cooperation across different areas.

Mansioni

  • You create and maintain short- and medium-term production plans.
  • You plan the slaughter and dissection process in the pig area.
  • You coordinate capacities with the plants and adapt plans flexibly in the event of deviations or disruptions.
  • You optimize stock levels and clarify risks in the flow of goods.
  • You simulate alternative scenarios, such as delayed delivery, machine failure or changes in demand.
  • You work closely with production, sales, purchasing and forwarding and actively participate in defined sessions.

Requirements

  • You have several years of experience in production or supply planning, ideally in the meat or fresh produce industry.: You will bring practical knowledge of SAP IBP as well as SAP R/3 or S/4HANA.
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): You have an apprenticeship as a butcher:in or food technology:in.
  • You think analytically and networked and also keep an overview in a dynamic environment.
  • You are open to change, take your own initiative and take responsibility.
  • You communicate safely in German as well as in French and/or English and enjoy working with different teams.
  • in German (negotiation certain)

  • English (advantageous)
