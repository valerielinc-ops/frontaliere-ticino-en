Full description

Mission You guarantee the regular maintenance of EPFL's consolidated accounts, the integrity and compliance of financial reporting according to IPSAS, the requirements of the EPF/CEPF Domain and the requirements of the internal control system. You lead a team of 25 employees, develop the vision of service evolution and ensure digital transformation, especially thanks to the opportunities offered by artificial intelligence. You work closely with internal and external stakeholders and play an active role in institutional projects within your perimeter. Main tasks and responsibilities Financial statements, closure and reporting

  • Ensure implementation of IPSAS
  • Oversee monthly, quarterly and annual closures.
  • Supervising the consolidation of EPFL's equity financial statements
  • Supervising the maintenance and quality of Real Estate's accounting
  • Produce and comment on EPFL's consolidated financial statements in accordance with IPSAS.
  • Ensure the quality of ancillary accounts and reconciliations in accordance with the closing schedule.
  • Ensure consistency of the data published in the annual report Information systems (SAP S4 HANA) & data
  • Pilot the evolution of the financial ERP with the VPF Project Management Office.
  • Ensure digital transformation by encouraging and integrating the use of AI and BI tools within the service.
  • Monitor user data to develop tools and processes. Leadership & Management
  • Lead a team of about 25 people, including 5 in direct management.
  • Define organization, roles and priorities; planning the succession; conduct annual interviews.
  • Recruit, train, develop and support employees.
  • Manage performance situations or inappropriate behaviour.
  • Actively contribute to talent management efforts.
  • Promote a culture of service, reliability and demand. Internal control, audit & quality
  • Pilot and improve the internal control system (ICS) of the consolidation perimeter.
  • To be the lead contact for internal and external audits of EPFL's consolidated financial statements.
  • Propose and conduct projects for continuous improvement of internal control activities (standardization, digitalisation, automation) Accounting governance & compliance
  • Contribute to updating the accounting rules of the FPS domain in line with IPSAS developments
  • Implement domain rules within EPFL.
  • Ensure proper application of VAT rules related to training/research activities.
  • Provide technical expertise on accounting positions.
  • Supervising the quality of accounting documentation (KB) for the EPFL community Interface & Representation
  • Participate in the cross-cutting projects of the Vice-Presidency for Finance and internal meetings (meetings of heads of departments, working group of finance managers of faculties and vice-presidency, working group with the administrative body).
  • Represent EPFL in the EPF domain for accounting.
  • Maintain regular relations with internal financial managers and partner entities. Profile
  • Master in finance, accounting, economics or title deemed equivalent.
  • Control of IPSAS (or IFRS).
  • Excellent knowledge of analytical and budgetary accounting.
  • 10+ years of experience in accounting in a complex environment (public, parapublic, NGO or similar) with a good understanding of public financing rules
  • Strong experience in management and organizational transformation.
  • Mastering SAP, AI and Business Intelligence tools.
