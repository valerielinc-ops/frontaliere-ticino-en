Head of financial accounting (m/f/d) 60-100% | FFS

Move Switzerland with us. Head of financial accounting (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, 01.03.2027 or date to be convented, 60-100% Apply here

What we offer.

A role of responsibility awaits you in managing a team, with a direct impact on transparency, quality and reliability of financial data. Together with a motivated team, you will develop modern accounting processes, promote the digitization of financial accounting and ensure efficient processes, from daily activities to the closure of accounts. You will have space to introduce improvements, develop your team's professional and methodological skills and collaborate with Controlling, Taxation, Review and IT departments. On our website, in the " Benefits" section, you will find advantages such as free general subscription and discounts on travel abroad, even for your family. What you can move.

  • You will lead the Financial Accounting team on a professional and personal level and will accompany the growth of the seven people of the team, defining clear goals and skills.
  • You will manage the monthly and annual closures by meeting deadlines and ensuring high quality, consistent processes and reliable data.
  • As head of the internal control system (SCI) for the Shared Service organization, you will promote optimizations and ensure compliance with accounting standards, directives and requirements.
  • You will coordinate collaboration with internal and external audit, specialist departments and service units and you will be the reference person for accounting issues.
  • With indicators, analysis and continuous improvement (Operational Excellence), you will ensure stable processes and measurable progress.
  • You will continue to develop end-to-end digital processes in accounting and will leverage standardization, automation and artificial intelligence to increase efficiency.

The one you bring with you.

  • You are characterized by strong analytical and conceptual skills, combined with a high capacity for implementation and spirit of initiative.
  • Thanks to your safety and communication adapted to the different interlocutors, you can convince stakeholders with different needs and profiles.
  • You have a first experience in the management of results-oriented and development personnel, in the financial field and on accounting issues.
  • Securely use ERP systems and know financial processes. They benefit from the experience with SAP S/4HANA and familiarity with digital tools, such as those based on artificial intelligence.
  • You have an expert/accounting degree or an equivalent qualification and several years of accounting experience, ideally in a digitalized context or in a service organization.
  • You have a knowledge of oral German and written at least C1 level. Knowledge of other national languages is an advantage.

Salary and benefits.

We are actively committed to equal pay. This assumption is governed by the bond code and is based on a comparison with the team, the market and the industry.
