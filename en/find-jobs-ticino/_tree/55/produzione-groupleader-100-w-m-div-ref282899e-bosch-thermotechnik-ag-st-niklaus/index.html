Full description

Your tasks As Groupleader Production (w/m/div.), you will take on the technical and disciplinary leadership of around 65 employees. You are responsible for order processing, adhering to qualitative, quantitative, and time-related requirements. Your responsibilities include mainly:

  • As a coach, you support your employees with technical and disciplinary leadership, promoting, motivating, enabling, and evaluating them
  • Defining measurable goals for team leaders and their teams, so that a continuous improvement process can be established as a routine
  • Workshop management is part of your responsibilities
  • You are responsible for optimizing new and existing production processes, with a focus on organizational flows
  • You create and maintain documentation
  • Assistance in technical processes and plant optimizations, as well as the leadership of system CIP projects, complete your area of responsibility ## Your profile
  • Education: Basic technical training (e.g. Polimechaniker, Automatiker (w/m/div.)) ideally with further training at university level (e.g. Technician)
  • Personality and work style: Flexible, expressive, and communicative personality with a high sense of quality and a systematic and solution-oriented work style
  • Experience and know-how: Leadership and planning experience, with secure application of MS-Office, SAP skills are a plus
  • Languages: Good communication and expression in German, English is a plus ## Contact & additional information Benefits
  • A dynamic team with a collegial approach in a family-like environment awaits you
  • Flexible working hours allow you to balance work and private life
  • Your area of responsibility allows you to have space for the realization of your ideas
  • You have the opportunity to benefit from international collaboration With us, you have the opportunity to optimally integrate your ideas and skills. Advanced employment conditions and benefits, high technological standard, innovative machine park from own special machine building and numerous career opportunities.
