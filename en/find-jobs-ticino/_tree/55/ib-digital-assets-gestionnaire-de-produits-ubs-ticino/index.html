Full description

Are you passionate about shaping the future of capital markets through Digital Assets and Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT)? Do you want to help define and scale regulated, institutional digital asset products and services across a global Investment Bank? We’re looking for a Product Manager to join the IB Digital Assets (Products & Services) team. In this role, you will shape and deliver the Investment Bank’s digital assets agenda, translating the potential of digital assets and DLT-based business models into scalable products, services, and business models embedded within and alongside core IB businesses.

We’re looking for a Digital Assets-focused Product Manager to:

  • act as end to end Product Manager for defined Digital Assets products, services, and DLT based business models across the Investment Bank
  • own the pipeline and roadmap for assigned Digital Assets products, services, and business models, from ideation and prioritisation through to pilot/MVP execution and handover to Change SMEs for scaling and BAU adoption
  • originate, structure, and progress business cases for IB Digital Assets pilots and MVPs, including value proposition, economics, risks, dependencies, and success metrics
  • partner closely with Front Office, Financing, Risk, Operations, Legal, Compliance, and Technology to enable IB Digital Assets pilots/MVPs in existing IB workflows and operating models
  • evaluate internal and external digital/DLT capabilities to determine optimal build, buy, partner, or reuse strategies based on feasibility, maturity, cost, and time to market
  • appraise future state IB business and operating models required to scale Digital Assets products and services within IB and Group control frameworks
  • act as a senior point of contact and thought partner, engaging internal stakeholders and selected external participants to shape IB positioning, partnerships, and market developments
