Full description

Thanks to its 800 employees, the Intercantonal Hospital of the Broye (HIB) provides a mission of acute care and rehabilitation. His ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative and excellence-oriented health institution for the benefit of the Broyard population. To meet the many challenges ahead, HIB is looking for a:

Chief Medical Officer in Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation from 80% to 100%

Your Mission

The context and your future service

The rehabilitation service (CTR) has 47 beds, about half of which are dedicated to musculoskeletal rehabilitation at the d'Estavayer site. The service is currently in a dynamic of strong development, led by a project to implement neurological rehabilitation and the deployment of our outpatient offer. You will integrate a multidisciplinary medical team (internists, geriatrists, rheumatologists, neurologists) working in close synergy with all our rehabilitation specialists (physiotherapists, occupational therapists, neuropsychologists, logopedists, dieticians and psychologists).

Your missions and objectives

  • Clinical Excellence: Ensure and guarantee the quality of clinical services offered in the area of rehabilitation.
  • Development: Actively participate in the creation and development of outpatient activity in musculoskeletal and neurological rehabilitation.
  • Leadership and Management: Contribute to the management, organization and representation of the service.
  • Training: Develop and participate in the teaching of doctors in training, as well as in the conduct of various symposia.
  • Interdisciplinarity: Work hand-in-hand with health and therapeutic teams to ensure comprehensive, coordinated and focused care to achieve patient goals.
  • Network: Work closely with acute care teams, other HIB specialists and our external partners.
  • Continuity of care: Participate in 2nd line pickets with the other executive physicians of the service.

Your assets

  • You have a federal degree in medicine (or a foreign diploma recognized by MEBEKO) and an FMH degree in physical medicine and rehabilitation or a foreign diploma recognized by MEBEKO.
  • You have a particular and strong interest in rehabilitation and rehabilitation.
  • You are recognized for your ability and taste to work in an interdisciplinary team.
  • You show initiative, an excellent sense of organization and strong collegiality.
