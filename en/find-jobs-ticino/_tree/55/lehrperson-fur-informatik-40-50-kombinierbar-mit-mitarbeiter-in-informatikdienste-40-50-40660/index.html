Full description

Portrait The Gymnasium Neufeld is a state school of the Canton of Bern with around 1,850 students, pupils, 220 teachers and 50 employees. The school provides clear and high

  • Requirements for teachers and learners and attaches importance to comprehensive advice and support. The three gymnasial departments (Department of Humanities and Human Sciences, Department of Mathematics and Natural Sciences, Department of Economics and Law) are organized along the main subjects and lead to a gymnasial matriculum. Training in the fourth department, the Bernese Maturity School for Adults, leads to a high school maturity degree or passerellen examination. The fifth department, the Cantonal School of Applied Sciences, offers a three-year training course with a cantonal secondary school or specialist degree.

Tasks

Support the Gymnasium Neufeld in computer science! We fill a position for teaching at the Gymnasium and the FMS, which can be combined with the participation in our computer services (ID). While the lessons begin on 1 February 2027, the ID position is to be filled officially from 1 August 2027. However, a gradual start in the IT services is already possible and desired from February.

Requirements

For this task, we are looking for a dedicated teacher with completed specialist training and the teaching diploma Sek II (or shortly before graduation) and ideally teaching experience at the high school or FMS. For the participation in the IT services, you should like to think conceptually and also have in-depth knowledge in system administration and network technology. be interested in working in these areas.

We offer

Look forward to a task that is demanding and exciting: With us you can expect a lot of space for your own ideas and a varied activity in a well-organized school. In addition to modern employment conditions and targeted training opportunities, we offer you an environment characterized by a motivated management team and committed colleagues. Together we cultivate a lively culture that promotes teaching and learning. So that you can start well, we accompany you with a mentor.
