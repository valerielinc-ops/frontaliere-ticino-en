“tailor-made solutions for HR.” Stellenwerk has stood for this since 2005. In the annual survey “Best Personnel Service Providers” of the Handelszeitung, Stellenwerk achieved maximum points in three categories in 2024: “Professional Search”, “Executive Search” and “Temporary Work”. Domiciled in Zurich, Chur and Schaan – and works for you throughout German-speaking Switzerland and Liechtenstein.

For our customer an international company in the field of medical technology in the region of Graubünden, we are looking for a committed, willing and motivated personality for 6 months as:

Logistics 80-100% (m/f/d)

Are you a flexible and motivated person and are you also characterized by your team-capable personality and a quality-conscious way of working? If so, please continue reading...

Her interesting field of activity includes

  • Verification of goods delivered by lorries, buses, post office or courier services
  • Unpacking and checking of the consignment on the basis of accompanying documents
  • Complaint and transmission of damages
  • Preparation of items for storage (e.g. labelling, repackaging)
  • Collection of delivery data in the D365 system
  • Organisation of transport between the two locations
  • Various activities with the truck

For this position, bring the following profile

  • Completed vocational training, preferably as a logistician EFZ, and/or several years of professional experience in a comparable job
  • Safe handling of the MS Office package
  • Knowledge of the Microsoft Dynamics 365 ERP system is an advantage
  • Good physical fitness (regular lifting and moving of goods up to 15 kg)
  • A forklift driver’s license is an advantage
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Independent and precise working methods as well as high responsibility and quality orientation
  • Motivated, team-capable personality with pleasure in actively shaping work
  • Efficient, careful and structured workflow

Our customer offers you the following

  • A modern workplace, good social benefits and a motivated team!

Are you also an open and uncomplicated personality? If so, we would be looking forward to your application! Applications by post cannot be considered. We guarantee absolute discretion.
