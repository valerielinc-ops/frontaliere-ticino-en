Head of Party

Permanent contract / Crans\-Montana, Switzerland

Les Roches, part of Sommet Education group, is a globally renowned institution shaping the future of hospitality and the experience economy through transformation and experiential education in 3 campuses: Crans\-Montana (Switzerland), Marbella (Spain) and Dhabi (UAE).

2nd in the 2026 QS World University Ranking in Hospitality and Leisure Management and 2nd in employing reputation category.

Accredited by the New England Commission of Higher Education (NECHE) and Swiss Accreditation Council (SAC) as a University of Applied Sciences Institute.

Roches Marbella, certified ISO\-14001 and ISO\-9001\.

Our values, Development, Distinctivity, Joint Commitment, Openness and Sense of Service, are the foundation of our actions. We offer an innovative, collaborative and thriving environment.

Mission The Chef de Parti is responsible for the preparation, production, and presentation of food within an assigned kitchen section. Working under the supervision of the Executive Sous\-Chef and Kitchen Sous\-Chef, the role concludes the consistent application of basic standards, food safety requirements, and operational procedures. As part of Les Roches' experiential learning environment, the Head of Part Supports students during practical sessions by demonstrating professional critical techniques and fostering a culture of excellence, professionalism, and hospitality.

Main Responsibilities 1\. Execute Food Production Activities \- Prepare, cook, and present fishes according to established recipes, production plans, and quality standards. \-Ensure consistency in food quality, presentation, and portion control. \- Contribute to the smooth operation of daily kitchen activities within the assigned section. 2\. Support Student Practical Training \- Assist students during practical kitchen sessions and operational service period. \- Demonstrate culinary techniques, kitchen organization, and professional work methods. \- Promote discipline, teamwork, and compliance with operational standards. 3\. Ensure Hygiene and Food Safety Compliance \- Maintain full compliance with HACCP standards, food safety regulations, and internal procedures. \- Ensure cleanliness and organization of worksstations, equipment, and storage areas. \- Report any food safety or operational concerns to supervisors. 4\. Maintain Kitchen Organization and Resources \- Assist with the property storage, rotation, and handling of food products. \- Support inventory counts and the monitoring of stock levels. \- Contribute to reducing food waste and ensuring responsible use of resources. 5\. Contribute to Operational Excellence \- Collaborate effectively with kitchen colleagues, instructors, and service teams. \- Support special events, functions, and academic activities as required. \- Actively contributing to maintaining a professional, positive, and guest\-focused kitchen environment.

Key Competencies Strong ultimate funding and technical cooking skills. understanding of food safety and HACCP requirements. Ability to work efficiently in a fast\-paced environment. Strong attention to detail and quality standards. Team\-oriented mindset and collaborative approach. Ability to support and guide students through example. Flexibility and adaptability in a multicultural environment.

Key Indicators Compliance with food safety and hygiene standards. Quality and consistency of food production. Preparedness and organization of assigned kitchen section. Student and instructor satisfaction during practical activities. Contribution to waste reduction and own stock management. Reliability and team within kitchen operations.
