Full description

ELCO offers premium heating solutions with first-class service designed to meet the needs of our customers through the entire life cycle of products and heating systems. The focus is on excellent consulting as well as convincing products and heating systems. ELCO has been a leader in burner, condensing and solar technology for decades and is represented throughout Europe with 1.7 million installed heating systems. Since 1928, ELCO has been a leading European brand offering intelligent solutions and technologies tailored to the needs of private and commercial projects, ensuring maximum energy savings.

SERVICETECHNIKER/IN OL \- OBERWALLIS

Your missions:

  • Meeting our customers’ expectations, both technically and by maintaining a quality customer relationship by effectively contributing to their satisfaction
  • After a training period, you will be responsible for the commissioning, maintenance and troubleshooting of plants in the geographical area
  • Your professionalism and rigor will allow you to carry out your activities with the utmost respect for contractual obligations and the regulations on the security of goods and persons.
  • As an ambassador for the image of our company, you advise your customers, also in matters of restructuring.
  • In addition, you support your colleagues in the field as part of the sale of equipment
  • They also offer periodic on-call service.

Your profile:

  • Combined with the quality of service and equipped with an excellent relationship with customers, you have a professional training (with a CFC) in the field of electricity or heating preferably or another technical profession. Knowledge of electrics and hydraulics is also required.
  • Your team spirit, commitment and sense of challenge are all pluses that enable you to succeed in your role and develop in our company.
  • Readiness for piquetting services

We offer you: We offer you the opportunity to work for a leader in their field where the customer experience is an opportunity every day to make a difference for our customers. You will become part of a team of professionals and a pleasant working environment where you will benefit from quality training on our heating solutions, a fully equipped service vehicle and modern work equipment.

We look forward to your full application (cover letter, CV and certificates).
