Full description

Amstein + Walthert is an engineering company with approximately 1100 employees. As engineers, planners and consultants, we deal with all aspects of building technology and infrastructure throughout their life cycle. Our solutions are innovative, elegant and durable. Your missions: Do you want to put your knowledge of AEAI Fire Protection at the service of varied and demanding projects? Join a multidisciplinary team and pilot fire protection missions from design to the reception of works, with real autonomy and a key role with our clients and partners. In particular, you will be responsible for:

  • Develop fee offers and assist clients in defining their needs.
  • Develop fire protection concepts (AQ1 to AQ3 according to profile) that meet the AEAI requirements.
  • Implement and coordinate fire protection plans (PDF / DWG).
  • Study and propose technical variants and optimized solutions.
  • Coordinate with architects, contractors, construction managers and specialist agents.
  • Define and manage quality assurance procedures for fire protection.
  • Perform on-site performance checks and accompany the removal of non-compliances.
  • Establish enslavement matrices and participate in defining technical interfaces.
  • Organize and coordinate comprehensive testing of security facilities.
  • Advise contractors and project teams on all fire protection issues.
  • Represent projects to competent authorities.
  • Prepare closing files, declarations of compliance, and all quality assurance documentation.
  • Conduct fire protection audits and expertise. Your skills and qualifications:
  • Holder of the AEAI fire protection specialist certificate, at least
  • Engineer, architect or equivalent training in construction.
  • Strong technical knowledge of building trades. Excellent analytical capacity, synthesis and problem solving.
  • Practical spirit, rigour and sense of responsibility. Very good writing skills in French.
  • Very comfortable reading technical plans.
  • Ability to work independently while fostering collaboration. Affluence in dealing with clients, agents and authorities.
  • Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously in a dynamic and multidisciplinary environment. Benefits:
  • A stable position in a dynamic and solidary team composed of three other fire protection specialists and experts.
  • Access to telework and flexible schedules.
  • Exchange of internal skills in the development of multidisciplinary concepts and projects
  • Access to certification training in fire protection AEAI (depending on profile) and continuous
  • Benefits and benefits of a large company
