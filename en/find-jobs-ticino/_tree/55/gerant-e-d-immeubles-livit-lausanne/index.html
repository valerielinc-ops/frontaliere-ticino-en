Your missions

  • Active and comprehensive management of a portfolio of buildings
  • Status of places of entry and exit when developing and changing tenants
  • Conduct and continuing training of subordinate assistant(s) (including setting annual targets and monitoring their achievement)
  • Personal and telephone supervision of tenants
  • Maintenance of the building in accordance with the management contract
  • Periodic inspection of land with corresponding status reports
  • Make lease changes and adjust rents and indexed rents
  • Supervision of the current rent policy and advice to the owner for the establishment of market rents
  • Control and visa of invoices payable, accounts of stewardship and incidental costs

  • Mastery of the French language in oral and writing with good editorial qualities
  • Digital affinity and ability to participate actively in change
  • Strong customer orientation and partnership relationship management
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and action with entregent
