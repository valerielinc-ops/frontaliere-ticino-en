Full description

Chronic pain is sustained by complex mechanisms and can have disastrous consequences for social, family and professional life. The Pain Clinic proposes a comprehensive and comprehensive approach, from initial assessment to management.

To complete our service, we recruit one (e)

Nurse for CDMax Pain Clinic 12 months at 70% (ref: 2043)

In collaboration with the various specialists present in our institution, the pain clinic has the mission to welcome and propose innovative treatment plans for patients with chronic pain.

Your mission

In a state-of-the-art facility, you hold a varied position on a multidisciplinary team of nurses, hospital helpers, medical secretaries, a psychologist, and anaesthetist physicians.

As such you will be led to

  • providing specific technical care
  • assist doctors in their actions
  • Performing delegated medical procedures
  • participate in research and protocols of ongoing studies
  • participating in continuing training

We are looking for a profile that meets the following criteria:

  • Holder of nursing degree for at least 5 years with Red Cross recognition compulsory for foreign degrees
  • Empathy, listening, patience are essential qualities for the management of chronic patients
  • At ease in teamwork
  • Ability to adapt to service constraints
