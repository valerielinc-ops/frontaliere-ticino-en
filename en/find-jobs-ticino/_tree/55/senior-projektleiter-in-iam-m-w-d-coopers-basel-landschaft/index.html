Full description

For our customer, a cantonal administration in northwestern Switzerland, we are looking for an experienced:n external:n project manager who will take over the ongoing project Identity & Access Management (IAM) after HERMES 2022 and lead it into implementation on his own responsibility.

Your tasks

  • Structured takeover of the ongoing IAM project from the previous internal project management in the final phase of initialization
  • Modernization of the topics IGA, PAM, SSO/MFA and identity lifecycle processes as well as the replacement of the existing SAP IdM solution
  • Coordination of pending work, preparation of the implementation mandate for approval and ensuring an orderly transition to the implementation phase
  • Review and update of project planning, project organisation, resource planning and risks and dependencies
  • Self-responsible management of the overall project according to HERMES 2022 incl. Management of services, quality, deadlines, costs, resources and risks
  • Active stakeholder and committee management as well as preparation and implementation of project and steering meetings
  • Early detection of deviations and conflicts of objectives, establishment of binding decisions and transparent, user-friendly reporting
  • Planning and coordination of procurement and implementation activities as well as management of external suppliers and implementation partners

Your profile

  • Proven practical experience in the management of projects according to HERMES 2022 according to competence level, as well as secure application of the methodology and its formal requirements
  • Experience in managing IAM projects or comparable ICT infrastructure or security projects with a sound technical understanding
  • Proven experience in the management of interdisciplinary project teams and in dealing with demanding stakeholder constellations and complex decision-making processes
  • Experience working with external service providers as well as coordinating procurement and implementation activities
  • Strong communication, negotiation and conflict resolution competence
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Experience with the implementation of IAM/IGA solutions, SAP IdM replacements and migration projects in heterogeneous ICT landscapes
  • Knowledge of IGA, PAM, SSO, MFA and Identity Lifecycle Management is beneficial
  • Experience in public administration and public procurement
  • Project management certification (e.g. HERMES Advanced, IPMA or PMP) as well as experience with organizational change processes
