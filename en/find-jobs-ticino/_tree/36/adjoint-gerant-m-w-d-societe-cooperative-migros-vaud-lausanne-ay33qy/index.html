Full description

Your commitment and personality make a difference! Passionate about retail and management, you're ready to coordinate a store and unite a team around ambitious goals. A true ambassador of the brand, you contribute to the performance of your store, to the satisfaction of our customers and to the development of your team.

Luogo di Lavoro: Société coopérative Migros Vaud, M Bellevaux, Route Aloys-Fauquez 97, 1018 Lausanne

Important remarks

All applications are welcome, but only online applications can be considered. Applications sent by mail will be eliminated for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Guarantee a warm customer experience and exemplary point-of-sale outfit
  • Coordinate day-to-day operational activity: Ray Management, Goods and Flows
  • Supervising, motivating and developing store teams
  • Develop the store's commercial performance and contribute to achieving the objectives
  • Budget monitoring and cost analysis

Required

  • 3-5 years: Experience confirmed in team management and sales
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): as a Retail Specialist
  • Trade and customer orientation
  • Leadership, inspiration and ability to unite by engaging collaborators around common goals
  • Active listening, constructive feedback and a culture of collaboration.
  • Adaptability in a changing environment (digital)
  • Analysis and sound financial management
  • French (Very good knowledge)
