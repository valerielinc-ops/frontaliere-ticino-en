Join our team!

For Banca Raiffeisen Lugano, a successful bank oriented to local customers based in Lugano and Pregassona, we look for a person for the role of collaborator/authority Credit Administration.

What's up?

Tasks

  • Processing of credit applications: registration and control of data entry and verification of completeness of documents, preparation of contractual documents, opening of necessary accounts, punctual payment and recovery, storage of fully processed credit dossiers, closure and recovery of credit positions.
  • Management of credit positions: preparation of periodic re-authorizations, data recording for budget analysis, construction credit processing, mortgage-based management, basic data management of guarantees, generation of interest charges and for depreciation, monitoring and execution of the procedure of removal of open positions.
  • Support for Credit Counselling in the preparation of credit questions and in data registration for further processing.
  • Detailed clarifications with Credit Consultant or with third parties (e.g. public authorities, pension funds, insurance) regarding credit processing.

What do you offer?

Requirements

  • Full banking training
  • Good knowledge and experience in credit administration
  • Good computer knowledge and openness to digital developments
  • Mother language: Italian; excellent knowledge, spoken and written by German
  • Domicile in Ticino
