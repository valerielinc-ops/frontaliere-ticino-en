Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. Graubünden is the largest canton in Switzerland with 7106 km2 and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days per year. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the Cantonal Administration Graubünden together with us.

The Office for Migration and Civil Law currently has 230 employees at more than 20 locations and is responsible for the entire area of responsibility in the area of migration, i.e. in particular granting and withdrawing permits, accommodation and support, executing expulsions and integration. In addition, ID cards are issued to Swiss nationals, naturalisations are made and supervision of the civil status authorities is exercised. The headquarters of the Office of Migration and Civil Law is in Chur.

We are looking for the Office for Migration and Civil Law, Specialist Office for Integration, as of 01\. January 2027 or by agreement a specialist responsible /\-n Early promotion.

Your responsibilities

  • Initiation and implementation of integration projects in the area of early funding in the Canton of Graubünden
  • Processing and checking of project input as well as related planning, coordination and control activities for quality assurance
  • Operational project consultancy and support of the sponsors in the implementation of integration projects
  • Cooperation with cantonal authorities, municipalities and third parties
  • Person responsible for language acquisition of pre-kindergarten age asylum children
  • Provision of suitable language support offers and support of families and sponsors during the duration of the measure.

That is what we want from you.

  • Completed tertiary education, preferably university / university of higher education in pedagogy or educational sciences
  • Sound practical and theoretical knowledge in the field of early promotion and language acquisition with corresponding professional experience
  • Intercultural competence and sensitivity for a diverse society
  • Knowledge and experience in project and case management
  • Own initiative and decision-making

Our advantages

Flexible working time models and where possible incl. Home office and mobile work\-life\-balance with mind. 5 weeks holidays and service leave Special social allowance in addition to child allowance and support for third-party childcare Attractive wage and social benefits Various training and further education opportunities to shape the future of the canton
