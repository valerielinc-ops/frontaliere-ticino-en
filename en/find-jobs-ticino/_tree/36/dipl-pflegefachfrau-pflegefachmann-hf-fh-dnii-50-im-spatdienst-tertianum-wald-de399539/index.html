Full description

Your heart beats for the elderly? Is nursing your calling? Do you see opportunities to meaningfully apply your skills and experiences with us to have a positive impact on the lives of our clients? Then you are perfect for us!

For the Grünegg House in Wald in the Zurich region, we are looking for a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH/DNII in the evening shift 50% starting September 1, 2026, or by agreement.

Your tasks

  • Holistic care and nursing of guests according to our nursing philosophy and the nursing process
  • Taking on tasks as a nursing contact person
  • Responsibility and management of the nursing process of the assigned guests, including RAI nursing needs assessment
  • Electronic nursing documentation using CareCoach
  • Planning and accompanying doctor visits
  • Taking on evening shifts, optionally from 16:00 or 17:00
  • Leading case discussions and nursing reports
  • Taking on tasks as a theme carrier in a specialist topic and thus participating in the group-wide network for nursing quality

Your profile

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH/DNII or an equivalent training recognized by the Swiss Red Cross
  • Work experience in long-term care is an advantage
  • RAI knowledge is desirable or willingness to attend these courses (paid)
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Enjoyment of diverse challenges
  • Good German language skills, both spoken and written
