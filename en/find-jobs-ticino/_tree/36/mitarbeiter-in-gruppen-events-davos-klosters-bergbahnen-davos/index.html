Full description

The Davos Klosters Mountains are five ski and mountain resorts in the Swiss Alps. In addition to the cable cars, Davos Klosters Bergbahnen AG operates under the brand Mountain Hotels and Resorts over 23 accommodations ranging from simple group houses to upscale four-star superior hotels. To support the team at the Mountain Plaza Hotel, we are looking for a person for the role of Group & Events Coordinator 80% - 100%. The Mountain Plaza Hotel is located in the heart of Davos Platz and is just a few minutes' walk from the Jakobshorn or the Bolgenareal. Since November 24, 2023, the resort has been operated by Davos Klosters Bergbahnen AG. The hotel has 210 different rooms, 8 conference rooms, a restaurant and a pub, a wine bar and a large wellness area.
