Location: Hirslanden Clinic St. Anna | Lucerne

Occupation by 1 August 2027

Type of employment: Temporary

Reference number: 72833

Clinic St. Anna is the leading private acute care hospital in Central Switzerland. With our three locations in the greater Lucerne area, we are one of the largest employers and training companies in the region. Every year we accompany numerous young people as they enter their professional future – maybe soon you too?

YOUR TASKS

  • Co-responsible for the cleanliness and cleaning of patient rooms and all public areas
  • Assignments in the in-house laundry with an internship in a large laundry
  • Hotel service: customer support on the bed stations incl. Serving meals
  • Restaurant: Taking care of customers in the respective restaurants
  • Internships in different areas, e.g. at the reception, logistics, etc.
  • Handling of various cleaning machines and equipment

Your profile

  • Minimum age 15
  • Completed school education level B or C with good grades
  • Motivation and commitment to three-year training
  • Enjoyment of domestic activities
  • Sense of cleanliness and order as well as hygiene awareness
  • Service orientation with good manners
  • Collaborative and capable of teamwork
  • Readiness for weekend and holiday assignments

DURATION OF TRAINING

  • 3 years, start of training in August

CLOSURE AND TITLE

  • The training is completed with the Federal Certificate of Competence Professional Hotel Industry-House Economy EFZ

FOLLOWING DOCUMENTS HAVE TO BE INCLUDED

  • Letter of motivation
  • CV with photo
  • All upper-level certificates and, where applicable, last training
  • Reports or confirmations of all trial internships, internships and employment certificates
  • Signalling test result
  • Copy of identity

Have you become curious? Then we look forward to your online application.

Please note: Unfortunately, we cannot consider applications by e-mail or post.
