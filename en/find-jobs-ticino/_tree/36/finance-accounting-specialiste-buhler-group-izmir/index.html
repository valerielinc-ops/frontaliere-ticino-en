Full description

Buhler Group Türkiye, based in Izmir, is looking for a skilled and proactive professional to join our Finance Team as a "Finance & Accounting Specialist". The role will be responsible for managing accounting, tax, and finance operations, ensuring accurate execution of account reconciliations, bank transactions, sales invoicing, and month-end closing activities. The position will also support the Finance Manager in financial reporting and finance-related processes, contributing to the effective and compliant operation of Bühler Türkiye's finance function.

Aufgaben

  • Manage daily treasury operations, including cash positioning and liquidity planning as well as relations with banks to optimize banking services,
  • Execute vendor payments and employee expense reimbursements
  • Monitor accounts receivable aging report and ensure timely collections from customers
  • Record daily bank transactions in the ERP system
  • Submit monthly tax declarations accurately and on time
  • Prepare quarterly and annual tax declarations, filings and government payments in coordination with Finance Manager
  • Manage fixed asset records and perform monthly depreciation calculations
  • Issue customer invoices in coordination with service, sales, and order management teams
  • Prepare and maintain accurate, well-supported balance sheet reconciliations
  • Support timely and accurate completion of month-end close activities
  • Support preparation of financial reports in line with IFRS/Swiss Gap Fer, corporate guidelines, and local regulations
  • Ensure proper documentation and approval for manual journal entries
  • Perform analytical reviews of GL accounts and key transactions
  • Coordinate internal and external audit activities in cooperation with the Finance Manager

Anforderungen

  • Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Finance, Accounting, Economics or a relevant field
  • Minimum 5 years of relevant business experience in finance and accounting
  • Prior experience in an audit firm is preferred
  • Fluency in English
  • Active user skills of “Logo-Netsis 3 Standard” and SAP
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications
  • Strong team player with the ability to work under pressure
  • Agile, proactive, self-motivated, and results-oriented
  • A hands-on attitude and willingness to take ownership of tasks of varying complexity
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment with a strong problem-solving mindset and a data-driven approach
  • Proven ability to build strong relationships with stakeholders at all levels of the organization
  • Residing in İzmir or willing to relocate to İzmir
