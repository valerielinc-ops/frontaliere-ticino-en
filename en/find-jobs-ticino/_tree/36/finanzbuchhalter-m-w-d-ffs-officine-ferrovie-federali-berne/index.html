Financial Accounting Officer (m/f/d) 60-100%

Move Switzerland with us. Financial Accounting Officer (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, 01.03.2027 or suitable, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

You will assume a leadership function with responsibilities that will have a direct impact on the transparency, quality and reliability of financial figures. With a committed team, you will develop modern accounting processes, advance the digitization of financial accounting and ensure the efficiency of operations, from day-to-day operations until the accounts are closed. You will have room for improvement, develop your team's professional and methodological skills and collaborate with the control, tax, audit and IT services. To find out about social benefits, such as free AG and foreign travel at reduced prices (for family as well), visit our website under "Social benefits". What you can move.

  • You lead the Financial Accounting team in technical and hierarchical terms and develop the team according to clear objectives and skills.
  • You manage monthly and annual closures on time and with a high level of quality, and ensure consistency of processes and reliability of numbers.
  • As head of the Shared Services Internal Control System (IOS), you advance optimizations and ensure compliance with applicable accounting standards, directives and requirements.
  • With internal and external audit, specialized units and service units, you coordinate collaboration and are the focal point for accounting matters.
  • Through indicators, analysis and continuous improvement (Operational Excellence), you ensure the stability of measurable processes and progress.
  • You develop digital processes from end to end in accounting and rely on standardization, automation and artificial intelligence to increase efficiency.

What you bring.

  • Strong analytical and conceptual capabilities distinguish you, and you implement your decisions effectively and proactively.
  • A clear and public-friendly communication allows you to convince various stakeholders.
  • You have first experience in staff management, focused on results and development, in the financial and accounting fields.
  • You control ERP systems and financial processes. You can ideally showcase an experience of SAP S/4HANA as well as ease with digital tools, including artificial intelligence tools.
  • An accountant's degree or equivalent qualification is acquired and you have several years of experience in accounting, ideally in a digital environment or a service organization.
  • You master German, at least at the C1 level. Knowledge of other national languages is an asset.

Your salary and benefits.

We are actively committed to equal pay. This commitment is in accordance with the Code of Obligations, taking into account a comparison with the team, market and industry.
