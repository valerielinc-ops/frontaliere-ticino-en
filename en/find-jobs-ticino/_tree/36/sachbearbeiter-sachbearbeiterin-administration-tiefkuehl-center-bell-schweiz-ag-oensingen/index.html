Full description

To strengthen our administration team, we are looking for you – a committed and reliable personality who enjoys the interface between operational processes, administration and customer contact. If you have a commercial education, feel comfortable in the field of warehouse logistics and forwarding and enjoy working in an international environment, then you are exactly right with us.

  • Pensum: 100%
  • Working time model: day shift
  • Job: from now on
  • Language: German
  • Location: Bell Schweiz AG, Südringstrasse 14 4702 Oensingen

Tasks

  • Coordination of outsourcing
  • Customer support incl. Customer complaints
  • Transport organisation
  • Maintenance of master data
  • Processing of customs matters (import/export)
  • Internal coordination with goods inlet/outlet and control room
  • Archiving
  • Miscellaneous administrative activities

Requirements

  • Commercial education or training completed
  • Knowledge and experience in warehouse logistics and/or forwarding
  • SAP knowledge in the field of ERP and/or EWM is an advantage
  • Multilingualism (English, French) beneficial
  • Structured, reliable and team-oriented way of working

  • Mobility: Benefit from convenient connections to public transport, attractive subsidies for tickets and sufficient parking in the car park.
  • Professional development opportunities: Expand your knowledge through extensive further education offers, high-quality training in the Coop Education Center and a wide range of personal development opportunities.
  • Health: Your well-being is important to us: Benefit from our health management, attendance bonus, discounted Reka checks, exclusive employee discounts and subsidized fitness subscriptions – for health and leisure!
  • Company events: Experience unforgettable moments at our departmental event and strengthen the team spirit during the company run – celebrate, move and make contacts together!
  • Market-friendly wages and benefits: Secure your future with our above-average retirement savings, receive anniversary bonuses, Christmas bonuses and attractive employee referral bonuses.
