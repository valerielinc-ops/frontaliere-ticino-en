What awaits you.

The SBB assumes operational responsibility for the SBB Transport Police. It is a security police force specialized in public transport. Through their competence and professionalism, Transport Police officers ensure order and safety at stations and trains. This, by exercising one of the more than 150 professions represented in the SBB. What you can move.

  • Join a specialized security police, interoperable (with cantonal and regional police, customs and complex railway environment) and multi- cantonal (we cross cantonal borders).
  • Intervening in uniform or in plain clothes, community and security police.
  • Fighting at-risk situations in railway stations, checks in trains, fighting shooting, mobile patrols, law enforcement (GMO).
  • Ensure safety in trains and train stations during special events (ball game, ice hockey, political tours, etc.) and security features in the context of major events and major events.
  • You have great freedom of action and the responsibilities that flow from it.
  • We offer continuous training in several fields
  • Policeman, driver, computer, railway.

What you bring.

  • Federal police-police patent (Swiss patent).
  • Several years of experience as a police officer.
  • Strong knowledge of police technique and tactics.
  • Strong computer and administrative knowledge.
  • Excellent physical condition and psychological resistance.
  • An ease in and with the public.
