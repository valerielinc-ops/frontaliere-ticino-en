Company

SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. Our mission in passenger traffic is simple: we want to get on board. By offering door-to-door mobility, with the train at the heart of the offer and easy-to-understand services, we attract more people to public transport, increase revenues and promote sustainable mobility. In this position, you are part of the Marketing and Market Development Directorate. You lead a team of about twenty people divided into three teams and turn the findings into concrete results. The data and the AI, a judicious combination of the four Ps of marketing and a marketing mix that affects customers at the right time are your main levers. You combine strategic responsibility with practical implementation, and contribute to shaping the Swiss public transport of tomorrow. What you can move.

  • As a member of management, you set priorities for strategy and digital marketing, defend your area's interests in marketing planning and develop the three teams — Growth Strategy, Media and Performance Marketing, as well as Marketing Automation and CRM — to make it a powerful unit.
  • You are changing the marketing strategy in the areas of B2B, international passenger traffic and leisure traffic. You define value proposals and offers to increase consumption, and then deduct effective measures using all marketing instruments. You systematically observe and analyze trends in the areas of mobility, marketing and technology.
  • You improve the effectiveness of media mix and campaigning, manage media agencies strategically as well as financially, and ensure that every franc invested in marketing produces results with clear reports, forecasts and indicators. You define the main themes of marketing and prioritize them within the Maison de la commercialisation.
  • You take responsibility for the omnichannel automation marketing project. Through customer path data analysis, you identify the most effective channels beyond e-mail and use the AI to further customize offers and make internal processes more efficient.
  • Effective leadership is part of your daily life. You accompany and develop about twenty collaborators, promote cross-sectional team work with an action-oriented mindset and anchor a culture of testing, measurement and learning. You also advance the transformation of the entire sector and develop new processes, roles and working methods for digital marketing (based on AI) and data driven.
  • You develop and evaluate innovations based on their impact on customer behaviour, manage budgets and portfolio by setting priorities and ensure close collaboration with creative teams, sales, Data and IT teams.

What you bring.

  • You are distinguished by your analytical, conceptual and solution-oriented mindset, as well as your presentation skills at all levels of management.
  • You have several years of experience in leading large teams at several hierarchical levels. You lead cross-sectional teams with a focus on results and development, encourage a culture of change and have already successfully carried out (digital) transformations in complex environments.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of the digital transformation of marketing and practical experience in marketing performance, media planning, digital contact points, social media and CRM. You take an innovative approach in your projects and use AI as an essential lever: you identify, drive and responsibly introduce use cases to improve efficiency and customization. You take into account data protection, quality and brand management, and ensure that the results bring measurable added value.
  • You have experience in strategy development and have worked operationally in a growth environment. You know how to achieve optimal results despite the pressure on costs, thanks to your experience in financial accountability (P&L).
  • You have a university degree (university or high school) in marketing, business management, strategy or a similar field. Additional AI and change management training complements your profile.
  • You are fluent in German, both oral and written (level C2), and have knowledge in another national language. These skills are essential for this position.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
