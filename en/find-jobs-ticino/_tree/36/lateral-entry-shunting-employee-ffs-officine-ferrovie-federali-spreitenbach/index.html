Cross-entry shunting staff:in 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Cross-entry shunting staff:in Spreitenbach, from now on or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

At SBB Cargo, you are on the pulse of the Swiss economy. SBB Cargo transports 185,000 tons a day, relieving road traffic of 15,000 trucks a day and the environment of 460,000 tons of CO2 a year. You help ensure that our customers are served reliably and on time. An exciting working environment awaits you, in which you move Switzerland together with your teammates. Attractive working conditions are important to us, which is why you get paid full wages during your training. Night and weekend work is also paid with us - e.g. with 6 francs per hour for night work. For night assignments, you also get time surcharges between 10 and 30%. This means that you have more free time than employees without night and weekend assignments. You can develop yourself internally, for example by completing the training as a shunting specialist:in or shunting engine driver:in. You can move that.

  • You perform shunting movements by moving, assembling and disassembling freight trains and wagons.
  • Your daily tasks include coupling and uncoupling locomotives and freight wagons as well as carrying out brake tests.
  • You prepare freight trains for the journey and ensure that parked vehicles are secured in the track area.
  • In everyday work, you use various tools such as radios and tablets to perform your tasks efficiently.

Here you will learn more about the job profile. You bring that with you.

  • Due to your security awareness, it is natural for you to work according to regulations.
  • For this demanding work in 24-hour shift operation, you are physically fit and like to work outside in any weather.
  • Ideally, you have completed a basic education or already have professional experience in the artisanal, logistical or comparable field.
  • You have good oral knowledge of German (at least level B1).
  • You have an impeccable sense of color (no red / green weakness).In the application process, we will ask you for a current criminal record and enforcement record (not older than 3 months). The document can be ordered at the invitation for personal acquaintance.
