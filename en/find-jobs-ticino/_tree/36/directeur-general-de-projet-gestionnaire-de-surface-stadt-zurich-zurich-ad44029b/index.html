Full description

We design Zurich from the ground up: liveable and climate neutral. Bring your expertise and work with us on a future-oriented city. For the realization department, we are looking for reinforcement as a result of retirement from 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • In this function, you lead challenging, coordinated infrastructure projects, primarily in the phases of submission, execution project, construction management and commissioning. You are responsible for operational planning, management (including appointment and cost control), management and professional support of exciting projects.
  • They conduct negotiations with internal and external stakeholders and committees, coordinate the factories/services involved and develop challenging problem solutions.
  • You are responsible for submissions, evaluations and assignments of engineering and construction services.
  • They carry out legal procedures, perform public tasks and hold public information events in the context of project work.
  • They create the basis for decision-making for senior positions and deal with parliamentary affairs.

Profile

  • Graduate degree as civil engineer (ETH/FH)
  • Several years of professional experience in civil engineering and in the project management of demanding infrastructure projects in the urban environment (experiences in special civil engineering or trenchless processes are advantageous), both in execution planning and in construction management or construction management.
  • Enforcement and conflict ability, negotiating skills and entrepreneurial action as well as interest and understanding of politics and administration
  • Stylistic German in word and writing, strong communication skills and pleasure to negotiate and cooperate with different stakeholders
  • Interest in project implementation in the sense of digital planning, construction and operation, experience in implementation with BIM methodology of advantage

We offer

  • The opportunity to shape the future of the largest Swiss city.
  • A culture that is respectful, reliable, ambitious and inclusive, and an employer who lives equality, diversity and inclusion.
  • A workplace located within walking distance to HB Zurich.
  • Attractive employment conditions, such as contribution to catering costs, a solid pension fund with an above-average employer share and the opportunity to reconcile work and private life with flexible working hours and home office.
  • Targeted education and training opportunities; personally, professionally, but especially with regard to digitalization in the construction industry.

About us

For a high quality of life: The approximately 350 employees of the civil engineering office shape the mobility and public space in the largest city in Switzerland every day.
