Doctor Perito SAM with specialization in general internal medicine - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite hospital of Ticino is present with its institutes on the entire cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of more than 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of more than 41,000 patients per year and providing more than 600,000 outpatients. To complete our team at the Bellinzona Medical Insurance Service (SAM) in Bellinzona, which is responsible for performing multidisciplinary and bidisciplinary medical examinations we look for a (a) Medical Perito SAM with specialization in general internal medicine (work permit to be defined) Profile: The person we are looking for has professional experience and autonomy, motivated to acquire skills in the perital / insurance (if not already in possession).

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
