Full description

Want to take the next step and take on more responsibility? In this role, you support team management, coordinate bathing operations and contribute together with your team to safety, quality and unforgettable guest experiences.

Luogo di lavoro: Bernaqua, Bernaqua Wellness & Spa, Riedbachstrasse 98, 3027 Bern-Brünnen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You coordinate the bathroom operation and the personnel deployment during your shift and are the first contact person for the bathing master team
  • You ensure compliance with operating, safety and quality standards and ensure the proper functioning of the bathing facilities
  • You support team management in the training of employees, take on management tasks in absence and plan the personnel assignments
  • You supervise the bath, wellness and outdoor areas, provide first aid and actively contribute to the prevention of accidents
  • You will receive and advise our guests competently, sell offers and ensure a friendly, service-oriented guest experience
  • You work actively in the company, take on a role model and carry out general hygiene and cleaning work

Requirements

  • Professional experience: in bathing supervision or equivalent function
  • You bring your first leadership experience and take on responsibility with self-image
  • You communicate safely in German and French and convince with your host and service orientation
  • You have a valid BLS-AED-SRC Generic Provider and the Brevet Basis Pool SLRG
  • You work in an organized, administratively adept way and enjoy training employees and developing processes
  • You are physically fit, resilient and ready to regularly perform evening, weekend and holiday assignments
  • Initial or continuing education completed
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • French (good knowledge)
  • English (good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks maternity leave and 4 weeks paternity leave
  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits guarantee the financial security of employees in the third stage of life
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment terms with very good social benefits
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation on self-selected courses
  • Sports and fitness offers: cost-sharing in subscriptions of more than 50 Migros-Fitness, Wellnes-, Aquaparks
