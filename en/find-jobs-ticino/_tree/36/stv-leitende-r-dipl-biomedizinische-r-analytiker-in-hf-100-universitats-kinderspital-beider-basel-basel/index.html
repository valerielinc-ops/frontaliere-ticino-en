Full description

The University Children's Hospital of Basel (UKBB) is an independent university centre of excellence for pediatric and adolescent medicine as well as for teaching and research in northwestern Switzerland. Due to a retirement in the team, we are looking for our accredited pediatric laboratory at 1. January 2027 or by agreement a Stv. Leitender Dipl. Biomedical analyst HF 100%.

Entry date: as of January 1, 2027 or by arrangement

Their tasks

  • Deputy technical and organisational director of the paediatric laboratory
  • Participation in the management, operational planning and development of the laboratory team
  • Technical support and training of new employees
  • Ensuring efficient and smooth laboratory operations
  • Co-responsibility for quality management according to DIN EN ISO 15189 as well as for its further and new development
  • Cooperation in the introduction and optimisation of new analytical methods and laboratory processes
  • Ensuring compliance with quality, hygiene, safety and occupational safety standards
  • Performance and validation of medical-technical laboratory analyses (haematology, clotting and special analysis)
  • Implementation and monitoring of internal and external quality controls

Your profile

  • Completed training as a dipl. BMA HF or training degree with SRC recognition
  • Several years of professional experience in a medical laboratory, ideally with first management or deputy experience
  • Good knowledge of current laboratory information systems
  • Very good user experience in Microsoft Office
  • High social competence as well as enjoyment of cooperation and development of employees
  • Good communication skills and cooperation with the management team, interdisciplinary departments and external institutions
  • Organizational skills, initiative and a sense of responsibility
  • Interest in the further development of processes and quality standards

Our offer

  • A varied and meaningful activity in a professionally experienced, motivated and committed team
  • Comprehensive and structured, appreciative training
  • Attractive and contemporary employment conditions with participatory service planning
  • Participation in internal and external training
  • Discover numerous other benefits under "our benefits" that we offer our employees
