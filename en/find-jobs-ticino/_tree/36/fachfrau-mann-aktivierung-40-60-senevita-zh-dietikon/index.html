Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland.

For the Senevita Limmatfeld in Dietikon we are looking for a professional activation 40-60 immediately or by agreement % Their tasks

  • You create a varied and activating everyday life for our residents and carry out individual and group activities independently
  • You plan and organize creative, cultural and socio-cultural offerings based on the interests and needs of our residents
  • You accompany our residents on excursions and support them in participating in social life
  • You bring your own ideas and contribute to the design of festivals, seasonal activities and internal events
  • You work closely with the care, hospitality and other areas

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed training as a specialist / specialist supervision EFZ (FaBe), preferably with specialization people in old age, or a comparable recognised training
  • Ideally, several years of professional experience in the care and activation of older people, including in long-term care.
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing (at least level B2)
  • Secure handling of the common MS Office applications (Word, Excel and Outlook).
  • An independent, structured and reliable working method and organizational skill
  • Enjoy developing varied activities, contributing new ideas and flexibly adapting offers to different needs
  • Empathy, team spirit, flexibility and a healthy dose of humor

We offer them

  • Versatile task with design possibilities in cooperation with our established, dynamic, motivated team
  • Modern infrastructure and bright, well-equipped workplace (near Dietikon station)
  • Extensive further education offer of the Senevita Academy
  • Unique culture and various generous employee events
  • Discounted food in the in-house gastronomy
  • Various perks (SBB half-ax, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacy, etc.)
  • Mind. 5 weeks holiday (from 50 years 6 or from 60 years 7 weeks)
