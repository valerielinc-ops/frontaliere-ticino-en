Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We create transparency in the banking and insurance market and advise our customers holistically and comprehensively. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements for our damage team at our headquarters at Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • You accompany our customers in loss situations and ensure that they feel well looked after.
  • You receive damage reports by phone and in writing, clarify the most important points and coordinate the next steps.
  • You are increasingly self-employed in the areas of property, motor vehicle and liability insurance.
  • In more complex cases, you will be supported by experienced colleagues and develop your expertise continuously.
  • Completed insurance studies, ideally supplemented by initial practical experience in claims processing
  • Enjoying direct contact with customers as well as telephone and written exchange
  • A careful, reliable and solution-oriented way of working
  • Motivation to develop professionally and take on more responsibility step by step
  • Very good knowledge of German, French a plus
  • You will receive a careful introduction and will be accompanied in everyday work by an experienced damage team.
  • With us, you can expand your knowledge in a targeted manner and develop yourself professionally and personally.
  • Because we grow strongly, many doors are open to you. Make your way with us.
  • Important principles of our success are independence, transparency, fairness and a refreshingly straightforward culture.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
