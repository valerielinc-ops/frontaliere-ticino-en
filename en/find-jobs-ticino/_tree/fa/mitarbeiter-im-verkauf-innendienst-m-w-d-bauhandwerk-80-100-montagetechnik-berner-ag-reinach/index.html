Full description

BERNER pushing the limits - for those who dare more

Deciding for your profession and your employer is a decision for your future – a future in which you will invest your passion and a large part of your time, energy and capacity.

Do you love being in touch with people, enjoy building relationships and have a sense of good advice? Then you are exactly right with us! We are looking for communicative personalities who sell with passion and inspire our customers on the phone.

Your tasks

  • Active sale of our products on the phone
  • Consulting & support: You are the first point of contact for our customers – competent, friendly and solution-oriented
  • Offer and order management: From the request to the complaint you keep the overview
  • Telephone maintenance of existing customer relationships
  • First point of contact in sales for customers and field service
  • Administrative processing and maintenance of customer and order data

What sets you apart

  • Completed commercial or craft training and ideally experience in sales or telephone sales
  • Product knowledge in the field of construction or automotive engineering
  • Communication skills and negotiating strength
  • High commitment and success orientation
  • Good knowledge of MS-Office (especially Excel) and SAP
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German and Italian (min. B2), optional French

What we offer

The values and sustainability of a family business. At the same time, we are setting ourselves up for the future today: customer satisfaction and digitalization are a strong priority for us. Become part of this unique transformation process and pave the way for your and our further development with your successful work. You get a guaranteed salary and do not work on a commission basis.

Who we are 165 employees throughout Switzerland. Over 36,000 articles for the construction and automotive trade. Professional advice and individual service with over 50 years of experience. In short: a leading multichannel distribution company. So much for the pure facts. But behind the BERNER brand there is something even more valuable: a family business with just as much heart as mind. We look forward to your detailed application (curriculum vitae, cover letter and employment certificates) with a salary presentation.

As an employer, the BERNER Group dares to differentiate itself and give every employee the opportunity to dare to be more. Together, we push our own boundaries and help create a better tomorrow.
