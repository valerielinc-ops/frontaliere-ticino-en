Full description

Digitization is your passion! Are you a doer who actively participates and promotes modern software architecture? Then you've come to the right place! Support us actively in defining and implementing planned technical projects. We are a motivated and dynamic team of product and project managers and are looking for YOU as a technical solution link to our development teams. Your Role

  • Co-responsibility for the solution architecture of our complete software solution TAXA for Swiss tax authorities and central role in pre-sales by defining and representing the technical solution design at the customer's site
  • Presentation of the solution and integration into existing IT landscapes as well as representation of the tax department in internal architecture committees
  • Leading solution design and coordinating technology and development partners in innovation projects (proof-of-concepts)
  • Responsible for the architecture part of tenders of the cantonal and municipal tax offices

Your profile

  • At least five years of experience as a solution architect or technical lead in agile software development of complex software solutions with in-depth customer contact (presales, project presentations), ideally also in the public sector
  • Confident appearance in customer meetings, presentation skills at level C and the ability to translate technical arguments into sales arguments
  • Team player with an open, communicative personality, who likes to work out solutions in small teams and represent them both internally and externally
  • Analytical, quick to understand, structured and solution-oriented with a high level of assertiveness and perseverance
  • Very good knowledge of German and English as well as a degree in computer science or equivalent professional experience
  • In-depth knowledge in the areas of microservice and microfrontend-oriented architecture, SaaS, Kubernetes, containers and related activation systems, as well as experience in dealing with the technologies Java, Quarkus, Kafka, Maven, Hibernate, Liquibase and PostgreSQL; IAM knowledge (OAuth 2.0 and OpenID) is an advantage

Your benefits

  • Holiday purchase (2 weeks per year)
  • 41-hour week with flexible working hours with a full-time workload
  • At least 25 days of vacation per year
  • 100% funding for further training in terms of funding or time
  • Free SBB Half-Fare travelcard for all employees
