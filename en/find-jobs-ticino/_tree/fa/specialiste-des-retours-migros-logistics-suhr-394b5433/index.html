Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow – networked and efficient.

Mansioni

  • Sorting out the various fractions (cardboard, PET, plastic) of the migrolino returns
  • Provision of replenishment work
  • Processing and printing delivery notes
  • Maintenance and operation of drawbar equipment
  • Cardboard processing and pressing
  • Sorted-out grains
  • Rolling container task stations
  • Resolve simple technical failures on site
  • Disposal: separation of recyclables such as cardboard, glass, film and metal
  • Accompany employees and provide practical support in everyday work

Requirements

  • 2-5 years: logistics / returns
  • No training required: Experience in a returns department is advantageous
  • Flexible handling of unexpected situations in different areas (returns, disposal, manual picking food and KML)
  • The truck test is advantageous
  • Working hours: Monday to Friday 12.30 - 21.30 and Saturday 10.30 - 19.30
  • German (very good knowledge)
