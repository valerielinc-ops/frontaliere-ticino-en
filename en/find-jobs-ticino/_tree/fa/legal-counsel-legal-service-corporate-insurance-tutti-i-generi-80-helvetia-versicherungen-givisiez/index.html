What you bring

  • You deal autonomously with complex legal issues in the specialized areas of the Romandie Claims Management Centre (motor vehicles, property damage, civil liability and personal injury), with particular emphasis on civil liability law and insurance law
  • You monitor and conduct judicial proceedings as soon as the legal action is initiated
  • You support departments specializing in appeal files
  • You design and deliver in-house training
  • You collaborate closely with the Sinister teams and specialized departments in French-speaking Switzerland and German-speaking Switzerland
  • The planned workplace is Givisiez, but it can also be located in Basel

Your profile

  • You have a Swiss university degree in law and a Swiss lawyer's certificate
  • You have several years of practical professional experience, ideally in the insurance field and/or in a study of lawyers
  • You have strong knowledge of civil liability law and insurance law
  • You stand out for your negotiating skills, your sense of assertiveness and a solution-oriented approach
  • You have a great learning capacity and a strong commitment
  • You have excellent knowledge of French, both oral and written (imperative), in order to follow up the sites in French-speaking Switzerland, and you also communicate with ease in German (communication language of the team concerned)
  • You are ready to make some trips, especially for hearings throughout French-speaking Switzerland and to participate in team meetings in Basel
