Full description

As a customer service provider: in Support E-Trading you are the first point of contact for our private customers: in the securities business. Thanks to your language skills, your service orientation and your flair in dealing with people, our customers are in the best hands. With your commitment, you contribute to a positive customer experience every day and support our customers competently in all matters relating to e-trading.

You can do that.

  • You answer inquiries from our customers: inside the electronic securities platform E-Trading competently and solution-oriented by phone, e-mail and chat
  • You support our customers in using the platform and show them the added value of e-trading
  • You assume responsibility for the recording and settlement of exchange orders
  • You actively use your language skills in daily customer contact and ensure a positive customer experience
  • You work flexibly from Monday to Friday between 8 a.m. and 6 p.m. In addition, you make bets 3 to 4 times a month until 22:00.

You bring that with you

  • Commercial or banking basic education completed
  • Proven professional experience in telephone customer support, ideally in the financial services industry
  • High affinity and strong interest in the stock market and securities business
  • Enjoying direct customer contact as well as a pronounced service and service orientation
  • Independent, reliable and responsible working methods coupled with a high level of teamwork
  • Negotiation-proof German and French skills in words and writing as well as a secure handling of relevant banking terms
