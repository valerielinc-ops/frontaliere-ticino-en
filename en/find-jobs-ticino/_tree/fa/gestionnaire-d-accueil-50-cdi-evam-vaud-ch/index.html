What you bring to our mission

Within the administrative branch located in Montagny-near Yverdon, you direct, respond, filter, the requests of the beneficiaries as well as those of internal/external partners by phone, physically at reception and by e-mail.

The word of your future leader, Géraldine

"Provides solid experience in public administration, both in managing complex files and in coaching teams, I joined EVAM 6 years ago as head of the Home and Service Department. Since 2024, I have been a proud leader of Montagny's Antenna.

  • In everyday life, I work with a team that is strong and caring, where self-help is not a hollow word but a reality. The mission at the reception is to welcome, guide, collect, inform beneficiaries and partners. If you share our values of solidarity and respect, and want to invest in a meaningful business, we will be delighted to welcome you among us!" *
