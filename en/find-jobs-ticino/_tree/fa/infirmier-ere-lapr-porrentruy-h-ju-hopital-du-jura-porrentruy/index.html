Full description

Our Residence Waiting Bed Unit (LAPR), located at the Porrentruy site, accommodates elderly patients requiring specific medical, nursing and psychosocial care. We accompany our patients in an interdisciplinary approach aimed at preserving their autonomy, improving their quality of life and supporting their loved ones in a caring and secure environment.

In order to complete our team within the LAPR unit, we are looking for an entry into office from 1 February 2027 :

Responsibilities

  • Provide and provide nursing care tailored to the specific needs of the elderly.
  • Assist patients and their families in the various stages of care, by promoting communication and understanding of clinical situations.
  • Provide psychological support to patients and their families in their life change acceptance process.
  • Work closely with the medical, therapeutic and social team to ensure a comprehensive and coordinated approach.
  • Contribute to the prevention of age-related complications (nutrition, risk of falling, cognitive disorders, disengagement).
  • Participate in unit life and ongoing projects to improve quality of care.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor of Science HES in Nursing or equivalent.
  • Red Cross recognition for foreign degrees.
  • Experience in a recommended geriatric or psychogeriatric service.
  • Internal training on the specificities of the elderly in hospital settings.
  • Empathy, patience and adaptability to the complexity of age and death situations.
  • Geriatric clinical evaluation skills.
  • Ability to work independently and as a multidisciplinary team and to collaborate with relatives.

Benefits

  • Rapid decision-making pathways with a positive work culture that promotes open and respectful communication.
  • A stimulating working environment within a dynamic and committed team.
  • A cafeteria restaurant with preferential rates.
