Key responsibilities Are you looking for a new challenge in the dynamic environment of Remote Sales & Advice (RSA) Personal Banking where you can support and develop your customer portfolio in the affluent sector? Do you appreciate the personal contact and cooperation in the team in which different specialists act as a unit? At Remote Sales & Advice – our digital customer service unit – you are exactly right! The demand for digital and at the same time personal customer communication is indispensable today. Our customers benefit from being able to get into personal contact with the bank regardless of location.

  • They are responsible for their own customer portfolio and focus on supporting their customers throughout the entire life cycle.
  • You recognize opportunities for a sustainable growth of the customer relationship
  • Advising customers and sales activities are our core business
  • They ensure compliance with regulatory and compliance regulations and handle administrative and organizational tasks related to customer concerns and operational needs.
  • They rely on teamwork and are supported by specialists such as Outbound (appointment agreement) or Investment Advise (investment topics).
  • They use innovative tools, apps and video technology in customer support to create the best possible customer experience
  • You keep your focus even in challenging situations and work with a high willingness to perform and service and thus lay the foundation for your professional development

The team You are part of the RSA team for Affluent customers in a modern office with the latest infrastructure in Brugg or Basel. «New Ways of Working» enables you to combine work in the office and at home. As a team, you have the opportunity to actively shape the development of RSA and become part of our success story. You are continuously expanding your know-how in the digital and banking sector, this is the ideal basis for your personal and professional development. Diversity helps us to grow together. That is why we are committed to promoting and nurturing diversity, equal opportunities and inclusion. This strengthens our business and adds value to our customers.

Your skills and experience

  • Experience in Customer Consulting as a Client Advisor Affluent
  • Solid expertise in financing, investing and pensions
  • Independent, efficient and structured working method and high service orientation
  • Hands-on mentality, team player and aspiration to continuously develop RSA
  • Great affinity for digitalization and enthusiasm for the use of new tools, media and channels as well as their further development
  • Flowing German as well as negotiating security of another national language such as French or Italian is desirable. Language competence in English is also an advantage
