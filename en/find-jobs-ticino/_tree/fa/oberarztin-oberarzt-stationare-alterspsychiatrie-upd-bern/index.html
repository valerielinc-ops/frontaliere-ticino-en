Full description

University Hospital for Age Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

Aging psychiatry means combining medical excellence with foresight, humanity and scientific curiosity. At the Universitary Psychiatric Center (UPZ) Bern, as a senior physician or senior physician, you actively participate in age psychiatry – technically, structurally and in terms of content. You assume responsibility in clinical care, shape the further development of the offers and contribute your expertise in research, teaching and interdisciplinary cooperation. In a university environment with a high degree of flexibility, you make a sustainable contribution to the care of older people with psychiatric diseases and cognitive changes.

Their tasks

  • You lead a medical age psychiatric ward (all clinical pictures) and actively participate in offers
  • They treat patients integrated psychiatric-psychotherapeutic
  • You will lead advanced training courses and fallup visions in the interprofessional team
  • They lead and promote assistant doctors as well as the interprofessional team and participate in the clinical teaching of medical students
  • They network and coordinate care with the adjacent psychiatric and somatic clinics and across sectors
  • You support research projects and, if you are interested and have a corresponding profile, develop your own scientific projects as part of a doctorate or habilitation

Your profile

  • You have a specialist qualification in psychiatry, ideally with a focus on old-age psychiatry, alternatively with a specialist qualification in neurology, general internal medicine or geriatrics with corresponding clinical experience in psychiatry during the training period
  • You have completed a medical degree and are recognised in Switzerland, EU or EFTA (MEBEKO recognition)
  • You are interested in neurocognitive disorders and age medicine
  • They like to work interprofessionally, are flexible and enjoy teamwork
  • You act on your own initiative, proactively and structured
  • You master the German language in word and writing at level C2

The UPZ

The University Psychiatric Center Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
