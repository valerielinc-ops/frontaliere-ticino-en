Full description

The technical services of SFS Group Schweiz AG develop customized and efficient automation solutions that are used at production sites worldwide. In this function, you can expect a lively environment in internal special machine construction with varied tasks. This includes work from electrical installation to commissioning. Are you interested in a creative job where you can develop? Your Tasks:

  • Responsibility for the professional electrical installation as well as the commissioning of the PLC and HMI software (Siemens TIA Portal) for state-of-the-art special machines and comprehensive revisions of cold forming machines
  • Assisting in the ongoing development of our facilities, eliminating vulnerabilities and optimizing processes
  • Support as a competent contact person for 2nd- and 3rd-level support for challenging technical issues
  • Secure grid connection of systems at our international locations and support of rollouts on site (travel readiness about 10%)
  • Maintenance of the technical documents and schemes Your profile:
  • Successfully completed basic training as an automatic technician (or a comparable technical qualification)
  • Sound know-how in switchgear construction, electrical installation and proven experience in industrial plant construction
  • Knowledge of PLC programming, ideally in the Siemens TIA Portal
  • Analytical and solution-oriented thinking, you burn for automation technology
  • Good English skills for international cooperation We offer:
  • Employment in a future-oriented company with attractive employment conditions
  • Six weeks of holidays & additional holiday purchase possible Annual participation in success
  • Staff restaurant with attractive prices
  • Flexible mobility concept for your commute
  • Other benefits: https://join.sfs.com/ch/de/benefits/
