The Position Your Team

As a pharmacist during the internship, you will have the opportunity to gain an exciting insight into the pharmaceutical industry as part of the 6-month internship for prospective pharmacists. Our Quality Systems and Compliance team is part of the quality assurance at Pharma Technical Operations Penzberg. We produce protein-based drug agents for the Roche network in Penzberg with biotechnological processes. Our team coordinates the conduct of inspections and audits by authorities and customers at our site.

We strengthen the compliance of our site through self-inspections.

As an intern, you will get to know the area of inspection management and audits in detail. This includes preparing together with the modified areas and performing on-site. You will also get insights into other areas at the site such as production and quality control. In addition, you will actively support our team in further challenges in the field of quality systems and compliance.

Tasks

  • Support of the Pharma Quality Systems and Compliance department in the preparation and implementation of official inspections and audits
  • familiarization of various areas at the site in the context of planning and carrying out self-inspections, for example in production or in quality control
  • You will learn about typical tasks of a pharmacist (according to the A.M. and AM.H.V.)

Requirements

  • You have successfully completed the second phase of the pharmaceutical examination (second state examination) and you have good academic performance
  • According to the approval regulations for pharmacists, you want to use half of your practical year to get to know the pharmaceutical industry more closely
  • Ideally, you have already gained first practical experience in industry
  • By your structured, solution-oriented and independent working method you are enthusiastic as well as by your analytical thinking
  • You have a quick understanding, look forward to working in the team and bring commitment and initiative with

We offer

  • Flexible working hours (37.5 hours week)
  • 2268€ monthly remuneration for a full-time internship
  • Discounted food prices (-5%) in our employee canteen
  • Training opportunities and exciting guest lectures
  • Networking with other interns
  • Gym on Roche Campus
  • Possibility of accommodation in the Roche Boarding House
  • Well-organized transport connections to Munich with our Roche shuttle

Period: 6 months, start on 01 November 2026 in Penzberg
