Put to the Point

Do you like working with young adults and sharing your knowledge? Our training centre for electronic learners in Payerne is looking for your expertise, your accuracy and your enthusiasm.

  • Date of entry: 01.04.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20891
  • Location: Caserne DCA, 1530 Payerne

You can make this contribution

  • Ensure the training of learning electronics technicians
  • Ensure the achievement of performance and behavioral goals in accordance with industry specifications
  • Lead intercompany courses (EC) in the following subject areas: basics of mechanics, manufacturing engineering, basics of measurement, digital measurement, microcontrol engineering and programming
  • Act as an audit expert at cantonal industry audits (VPA/IPA)
  • Maintain good relations with partners (cantonal training bodies, partner companies; in German and French)
  • Organise information events and events for learners and parents as well as selection/orientation trials for young professionals
  • Prepare and monitor the regular training reports

This makes them unique.

  • Electronics higher vocational training (technician RF or equivalent) and at least five years of relevant practical experience
  • Further training in the field of vocational pedagogy (Federal certificate trainer, qualification as vocational trainer/in the main profession) or express willingness to acquire it with noticeable enthusiasm for the training and supervision of learners / young adults
  • Mastering the PCB and CAD development chain (Altium Designer, KiCAD, FreeCAD) as well as manufacturing and prototyping techniques (including 3D printing with FDM and SLA processes)
  • Excellent knowledge of the C programming language for application to microcontrollers (especially STM32 architecture) as well as in-depth knowledge of Python (OOP), practical experience in version management (Git) and technical documentation (XWiki or LaTeX)
  • Good knowledge of web infrastructure and databases (PHP, SQL) as well as proven experience in dealing with AI assistants and agents as a tool for understanding, maintaining and developing existing solutions and tools
  • high level of social competence for good cooperation with partners in the training sector (training companies, public authorities); Good knowledge of technical English
