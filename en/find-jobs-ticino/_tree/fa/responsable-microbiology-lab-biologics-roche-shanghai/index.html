The Position

Engineering Project Support and Operation Readiness

  • CCS Support: Support the establishment of the Contamination Control Strategy (CCS) for the aseptic production line.
  • Environmental Monitoring (EM): Support EMPQ and establish risk based environmental monitoring strategy and ensure its effective implementation.
  • Microbiology Method Validation: Responsible for the validation of microbiology methods, including supporting the confirmation of cleaning and disinfection processes, and the efficacy of disinfectants.
  • Lab Setup: Lead the setup of a microbiology laboratory that complies with regulatory requirements and build a BSL-II (Biosafety Level 2) lab management system.
  • Equipment Support: Support the Performance Qualification (PQ) of laboratory equipment and ensure routine operation.

Routine Operation

  • Compliance Leadership: Lead the execution and oversight of environmental monitoring and critical facility testing in accordance with Chinese GMP, EU GMP, U.S. cGMP regulations, and Roche/Genentech standards.
  • Methodology Management: Lead the continuous effort to ensure all microbiological test methods are validated and that their validation status is maintained as effective.
  • Audit Readiness: Be accountable for audit readiness by ensuring compliance with effective standards and regulatory/statutory guidelines. Actively lead the team's response to regulatory inspections and global quality audits.
  • CCS and Training: Drive the continuous support of the effectiveness of the Contamination Control Strategy (CCS) and lead the organization of microbiology-related training.
  • Product Release Leadership: Be accountable for product release testing, ensuring timely delivery according to lead time requirements and guaranteeing compliance with Data Integrity (DI) standards.
  • Team Development: Lead the development of a competent team through coaching, mentoring, and training by having a skills competencies development and appraisal program in place to strengthen the technical expertise and capabilities.
