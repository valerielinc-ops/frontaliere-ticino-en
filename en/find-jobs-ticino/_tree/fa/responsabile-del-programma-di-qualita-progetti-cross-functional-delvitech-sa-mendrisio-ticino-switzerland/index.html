Full description

Lead cross-functional quality projects at Delvitech, aligning teams, improving processes and turning data into measurable results. Job Summary We are looking for a Quality Program Manager to lead and coordinate cross-functional improvement initiatives involving Quality, R&D, Production, Application Engineering, HPT, IT and other functions. The role turns quality and improvement objectives into structured, measurable and executable programs with clear ownership, priorities, resources, milestones and completion criteria, combining project management, process improvement and technical coordination to improve both company processes and product performance. Key Responsibilities

  • Coordinate cross-functional quality improvement programs covering business processes and the technical performance of products and systems.
  • Translate high-level objectives into projects, deliverables, milestones, responsibilities and measurable success criteria.
  • Work with functional managers to prioritize activities and secure the necessary resources.
  • Monitor dependencies, risks, delays and open decisions, escalating when required.
  • Facilitate alignment and decision making between Quality, R&D, Production, Application Engineering, HPT, IT and Management.
  • Analyze processes, identify inefficiencies and bottlenecks, and coordinate improvement actions.
  • Define KPIs to measure the effectiveness of initiatives and ensure improvements are embedded in daily processes.
  • Prepare concise management reports, risk registers and progress updates, using AI and digital tools for analysis and action tracking. Requirements
  • Experience managing or coordinating technical, industrial, product or cross-functional projects.
  • Strong organizational, planning and project management skills.
  • Proven ability to coordinate stakeholders across functions without direct authority.
  • Ability to understand technical topics and complex systems to support decision making.
  • Experience in process analysis and improvement, with a structured, data-driven approach.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Familiarity with Jira, Confluence or similar tools and with AI tools.
  • Professional working proficiency in English. Nice to Have
  • Experience in product-based, industrial or technology companies with technically complex products.
  • Experience coordinating projects involving both software and hardware.
  • Familiarity with Lean, Six Sigma or Agile.
  • Experience with KPIs, dashboards or Business Intelligence tools. What We Offer A permanent position in a fast-growing technology company that develops its own AI-based optical inspection systems, with strong opportunities for professional growth as the company expands.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
