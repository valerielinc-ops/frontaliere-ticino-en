Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The post is attached to the Directorate of Immobilie r (DI) which has the task of defining a strategy of the housing stock, to develop the coherence of the follow-up of the major projects according to the hospital master plan 2022-2040, in strong partnership with the main State departments attached to the Department of the Territory, the Department of Infrastructure and the Department of Health, to optimize the monitoring of the surfaces and its future planning, while guaranteeing the proper functioning of the services. More than 230 professionals and professionals carry out their missions on a daily basis with a constant concern for requirements, with a sense of service and environmental responsibility. The Infrastructure Business Continuity Service (SCOI) is a service provider whose main mission is, thanks to the presence on each site of collaborators from different trades, to ensure the maintenance of the hospital heritage consisting of medical and logistical equipment, buildings and their facilities.

Description of post

You manage, analyze and process 24 hours a day information received from various computer networks and telephone calls arriving at the Control Centre. You intervene immediately in the event of a disturbance endangering the patient, staff, visitors or the HUG heritage. You provide 24-hour surveillance, alerting and a call-assistance service for hospital services, taking care of all HUG requests at the technical level. In addition, you process and manage all 24-hour fire alarms on the expanded Cluse-Roseraie site. You manage and control the thermal power plant installations to allow uninterrupted operation of the thermal power generation and distribution. You manage and monitor the various vital parameters of the institution (e.g. water, electricity, medical gas, air conditioning, heating, ventilation, cold, etc.) in collaboration with the technical workshops. You intervene as an expert in a discipline or as a specialist in several disciplines (pluridisciplinarity). You ensure an adequate level of security and comfort of infrastructure and equipment. In case of emergencies or major events, you manage and process the information received with appropriate and immediate decision. You decide independently on the actions to be undertaken in accordance with the priorities defined and direct the mobilization of stakeholders. You like to find solutions to complex technical problems and you also thrive in performing simpler and more routine tasks.

Qualifications

You have an Electrical Installer or Installer CFC, or a Mechanic CFC, or an Automatician CFC, or an Electromechanic CFC or an equivalent diploma, In addition, your training is complemented by at least 5 years of professional experience in the maintenance and troubleshooting of building facilities. You also have good knowledge in other areas of the building (sanitary, heating, cold, etc.). You took the course for boiler room personnel and obtained the certificate ( SWISS TS course for heating staff). The person who does not have the certification of the boiler room personnel course undertakes to take the course and obtain the certification within 24 months of taking up his or her duties according to the planning established by his or her hierarchy. In addition, it will also have to undergo internal fire training. Compulsory further training is required (on-the-job): in-house technical training CVSE + Froid, home automation, specialization in areas such as elevators, fire, pneumatics and computer science. You have a good knowledge of the usual office software (Microsoft Office) as well as the use of GMAO software. Your keen sense of customer service, team spirit and openness to change allow you to operate smoothly and integrate quickly. You master perfectly the spoken and written French by expressing yourself clearly and concisely and with basic knowledge in English and/or German. You are able to work independently and have a very good analytical capacity and stress management. You are adaptable and a person with a good team spirit and initiative, dynamism and professionalism. Thanks to your calm, laid and discreet personality, you can easily establish a relationship of trust with your interlocutors, you can cooperate and collaborate to help colleagues while being sensitive to the patient's presence and hospital constraints.

Further information

You want to exercise a vital activity for the safety of patients, rich and diverse, even complex, and to work in a team with irregular schedules, including 24/7 planning and a picket service.
