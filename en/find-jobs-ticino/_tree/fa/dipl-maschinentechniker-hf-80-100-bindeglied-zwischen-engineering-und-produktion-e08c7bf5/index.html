# Dipl. Machine technician HF 80-100% link between engineering and production #

Employment: 80-100 %

Location: Klosters

Industry:Industry diverse

Release date: 29.10.2026

Our client is a company based in the canton of Graubünden. Thanks to its central location in the Alps, it has developed over decades into a special supplier of valves for hydropower plants. Today, customers worldwide draw on the company’s expertise to deploy reliable safety organs in hydropower plants. For more than 50 years, it has specialized in the development and production of valves for hydropower plants.

Your tasks

  • Development and construction of valves for hydropower and industry
  • Performance of analytical calculations and production of production drawings, parts lists and order documentation
  • Close cooperation in an interdisciplinary team with project management, AVOR and production
  • Communication and coordination with customers and suppliers

What to expect

  • Varied activity in the field of renewable energy
  • International environment and technologically advanced products
  • Self-employed and self-responsible work
  • Flexible working hours and attractive employment conditions
  • Long-term employment with training options
  • Strong and highly motivated team

Your profile

  • Experience in the design and dimensioning of mechanical components
  • Safe handling of a 3D CAD system
  • Good user knowledge in MS Office
  • Networked thinking, high flexibility and independent and responsible working methods
  • Very good knowledge of German, English skills are advantageous
