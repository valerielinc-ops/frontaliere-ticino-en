Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Independent processing of daily business in the expense report for several companies of the group
  • Checking, recording and recording of incoming creditors' invoices and credits in the accounts payable
  • Control of the creditor release process and maintenance of creditor master data
  • Implementation of weekly payment orders, dunning orders and adjustment of open positions
  • Technical contact point for employees, suppliers and internal departments
  • Preparation of evaluations and maintenance of instructions and process documentation
  • Participation in system and process optimization and support for audits and internal controls

You bring that with you

  • Basic commercial training, ideally with further training in finance or accounting (accounting officer)
  • Good professional experience in accounts payable and expenses
  • In-depth user knowledge in SAP (ideally S/4HANA)
  • Secure handling of the common Office programs (especially Excel)
  • Interest in digitalization and automation topics as well as an accurate, reliable and independent working method
  • Very good knowledge of German, further language skills beneficial

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.

Your advantages

Your well-being is important to us. That is why you have many advantages with us. These include, for example:

  • Wages and ancillary benefits: We offer you a performance-oriented and market-oriented salary, 13 monthly wages and generous premiums and allowances. Together with other ancillary services, this results in an attractive overall package.
  • Flexible working: We attach great importance to our employees finding a healthy balance between work and private life. That is why we offer flexible working hours and home office options, among other things.
  • Perks: At RUAG you benefit from perks in the areas of mobility and food. In addition, you benefit from attractive offers on our benefit portal and have fleet discounts at various renowned car brands.
  • Career and further education opportunities: Promoting and developing the potential of our employees is important to us. Through training, courses and further education, we support them in continuously developing their skills in order to achieve their professional goals.
  • Leisure activities: Our employees are close to our hearts. That is why we give them a day off for their birthday, offer a diverse sporting offer and the opportunity to receive additional days off against pay deduction in addition to the annual holiday credit.
