Full description

Do you already have an idea of what you will do after completing your school-leaving qualification? Embark on your future with us and take advantage of the development opportunities at Lidl Suisse. Our stores are at the heart of our business. As an apprentice in retail (with a focus on creating shopping experiences), working towards an AFP or CFC, you contribute to the success of your Lidl store. Start/End AFP: 1 August 2027 – 31 July 2029 Start/End CFC: 1 August 2027 – 31 July 2030

Your responsibilities

  • Managing the cash registers and preparing the cash reconciliations
  • Ensuring freshness, quality and cleanliness
  • Stocking and displaying goods
  • Checking incoming goods in the stockroom and assisting your team during inventories
  • Assisting with the planning and implementation of promotions and seasonal displays
  • Determining merchandise requirements and ordering goods in the 3rd year of apprenticeship (CFC)
  • First experience in team leadership in the 3rd year of apprenticeship (CFC)

Your profile

  • Average to very good marks in the lower-secondary level I diploma
  • AFP: A1 level in a foreign language and CFC: at least A2
  • Enjoyment of working with people
  • Independent working style and quick thinking
  • Team spirit, reliability and a sense of responsibility
  • Willingness to learn and availability
  • Physical stamina
  • Good knowledge of French

Our offer An apprenticeship that opens many doors for you. After successfully completing your apprenticeship, and if you have good personal qualities, we will help you progress to a management position in the store. In addition, you will benefit from the Lidl collective labour agreement and many attractive benefits
