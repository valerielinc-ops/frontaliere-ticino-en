Full description

The Prison Office shall be responsible for the administrative and practical execution of penalties and measures. Solothurn Prison (JVA) operates inpatient penalties and measures on adult men. For the Solothurn Prison (JVA) we are looking for an employee at the Deitingen location as a specialist in employment, 100%.

Your responsibility

  • You take over and plan orders within the electrical workshop or the assembly workshop, which you accomplish purposefully and reliably with the involvement of the instructed persons.
  • As part of our Agogik team, you work with the instructed persons according to work-agogic criteria – offering the instructed persons development potential for self- and professional skills.
  • They are committed to the guidance, promotion and accompaniment of male adult instructed persons in everyday work.
  • In your role, you manage the reporting of the instructed persons in your work area and ensure representation at networking meetings with internal and external specialists.
  • With exemplary appearance and commitment, you are leading the way in the work execution.

Your profile

  • They have completed craft training EFZ and have already gained extensive practical experience.
  • Ideally, you will bring a further education in labour agogy or as a specialist in the prison system - or you are ready to complete a corresponding further education.
  • You are a team player and the interdisciplinary cooperation with other professional groups brings you pleasure.
  • They like to take on responsibility in an equally challenging and meaningful activity.
  • For this position, you bring good IT knowledge and an impeccable reputation.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity to work mobile
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
