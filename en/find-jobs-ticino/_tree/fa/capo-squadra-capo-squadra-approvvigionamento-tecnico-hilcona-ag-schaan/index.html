Full description

Do you want to take responsibility, actively shape investments and lead your team to success? With us, you combine technical know-how with strategic purchasing – and ensure that our production remains sustainable. Tasks:

  • Professional and personnel management of a team of currently 3 employees
  • Responsibility for the procurement of Indirect Spend (machinery, capital goods, services, spare parts, equipment)
  • Control and implementation of investment projects in close cooperation with the technical and production departments
  • Ensuring efficient and sustainable procurement processes
  • Negotiation and conclusion of supplier contracts at national and international level
  • Analysis of markets, technologies and cost structures to optimize purchasing strategy
  • Further development of processes and active participation in purchasing organisation Requirements:
  • Basic technical or food technology training, supplemented by business administration training in purchasing or studying (e.g. mechanical engineering / food technology)
  • Several years of profound experience in technical purchasing, ideally in the food or plant industry
  • Experience in managing or participating in investment projects
  • Initial leadership experience or proven leadership potential
  • Very good knowledge of German and negotiating English
  • Analytical approach, negotiation skills and strong communication skills are just as close to you as your team orientation
