We awaken spaces with passion and professionalism. For more than 75 years, teo jakob has been the leading company for demanding interior design in the business and private customer sector with locations in German and Western Switzerland. Continue writing the teo jakob story with us. To strengthen our team delivery in Bern-Liebefeld we are looking for you! Do you love to mount furniture professionally and delight customers with perfectly furnished rooms? Then become part of our team. Our offer

  • Varied activity in the exciting environment of furnishing consulting
  • Upscale collections in the demanding customer segment
  • Open and good working atmosphere
  • Purchasing benefits

Your tasks

  • Loading and unloading of vehicles
  • Carrying out the transports
  • Customer delivery according to defined tours
  • Assembly of various furniture, lights and furnishings
  • Customer service

Your profile

  • Completed craftsmanship, preferably as (furniture) carpenter
  • Routine in assembling furniture
  • Ideally experience in building USM furniture
  • Routine in dealing with digital work equipment
  • Cat B driving licence mandatory
  • Precise and careful working methods
  • Resilient and robust nature
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German
