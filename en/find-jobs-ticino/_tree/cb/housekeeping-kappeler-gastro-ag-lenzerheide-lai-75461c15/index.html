Full description

The Roland Arena is located in Lantsch/Lenz, about five kilometres from the village centre Lenzerheide. Thanks to the multifunctional architectural architecture, the arena has developed into a well-known sports hotspot and is a meeting point for elite and young athletes, recreational athletes, clubs and sports federations. Almost 60 beds spread over double and shared rooms, the Bistro Bualino and the warm team are there to make the sporting stay unforgettable.
