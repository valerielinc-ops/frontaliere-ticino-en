Full description

Introduction

For our Procurement, Support and Infrastructure Division, we are looking for a metrology technician (M/F) whose main mission will be to perform metrological checks and to ensure the corrective maintenance of measuring means for a defined perimeter taking into account all tasks, status and changing needs.

Responsibilities

  • Perform accurate and systematic calibrations of measuring instruments and means according to existing standards, with a view to continuous improvement of processes
  • Support corrective maintenance of measuring devices by diagnosing and resolving malfunctions to minimize production stops
  • Participate in the acquisition and integration of new means of measurement, ensuring their adequacy with technical and operational requirements
  • Ensure technical documentation, formalization of work procedures and capitalization of knowledge, thus contributing to the sustainability of know-how
  • Develop synergies with services (industrialization, buildings & infrastructure, IT and safety, security and environment) in an efficient manner
  • scrupulously ensure compliance with internal directives in the performance of the tasks entrusted, reflecting the image of excellence of the brand

Profile

  • Diploma in physical measures, quality or equivalent, ES or HES Technician
  • Experience in measurement management
  • Master's degree in fleet management
  • Ability to write uncertainty reports and certificates, read plans and apply ISO standards
  • Knowledge in the field of industrial electricity and mechanics
  • Team spirit, sociability and excellent communication
  • Synthesis, rigor and technical curiosity
  • Autonomy and capacity to take initiatives
  • Flexibility and alignment with company values

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
