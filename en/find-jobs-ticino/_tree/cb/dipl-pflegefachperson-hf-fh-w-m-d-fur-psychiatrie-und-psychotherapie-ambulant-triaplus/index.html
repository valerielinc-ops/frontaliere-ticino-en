Full description

Their tasks shall include

  • Applying different therapeutic models such as mindfulness, topic-centered group talks, creative working with different materials
  • Preliminary discussions with day care patients
  • Independent conception and organization of group therapies
  • Participation and co-responsibility in preventive, diagnostic and therapeutic measures
  • Cooperation with social institutions and authorities
  • Responsibility and participation in interprofessional competent cooperation
  • Responsible for the ongoing documentation of the individual group history
  • Participation in reports, case meetings, intervisions, team meetings, supervision and training
  • Independent organisation and implementation of leisure activities and leisure offers
