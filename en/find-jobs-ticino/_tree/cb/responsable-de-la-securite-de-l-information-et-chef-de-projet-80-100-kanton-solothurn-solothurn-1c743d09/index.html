Full description

The Departmental Secretariat supports the departmental management and is responsible for the digital transformation in the Department of Construction and Justice. The project management pool of the Office for Geoinformation supports the Departmental Secretariat and the Famier in the development and implementation of digital projects. For the Services department, we are looking for an IS manager and project manager, 80-100%, as of 04.01.2027 or by agreement at the Solothurn location.

Your responsibility

  • They initialize, plan and lead IT and digitization projects for the Departmental Secretariat and the Fariefers. Together with the departments, you will analyze problems, processes and requirements and develop solution concepts and decision-making principles.
  • They support the maintenance of the departmental project portfolio as well as the coordination and prioritization of projects, also in the AI sector.
  • As an information security manager, you assess the need for protection, risks and security requirements and advise the offices.

Your profile

  • You have a bachelor's or master's degree in (business) computer science, engineering or a comparable qualification.
  • Your in-depth experience helps you in the collection of technical requirements and in the creation of solution concepts.
  • Your experience in managing projects and your methodological competence in project management round off your profile.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity for mobile work
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
