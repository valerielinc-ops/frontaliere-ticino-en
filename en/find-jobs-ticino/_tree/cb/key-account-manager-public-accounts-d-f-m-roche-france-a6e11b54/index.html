The Position

Ideal location: Paris, North or North / West of France - Remote item

As a Key Account Manager Public Accounts (d/f/m) and attached to the SubChapter Lead Key Account Manager, you are a dynamic team and a Strategic Account Expert.

You are the voice of customers and represent Roche with them. As such, you have a perfect understanding of the mapping of the interlocutors and the decision-making matrix of accounts, the environment, the issues and strategic visions of clients. You also have a precise view of Roche's competitive position vis-à-vis these issues, allowing you to define and bear responsibility for the commercial and financial strategy of the accounts - existing or future(s). Finally, you define and implement the negotiating strategy.

Your main responsibilities

  • In line with Roche Diagnostics France's strategy, you determine the plans to deploy for major public accounts under your responsibility, identifying the levers that support the development of strong partnerships and that can generate value for all parties.
  • At all stages of the business relationship, you identify and seize the different opportunities within the accounts to contribute to the achievement of Roche's strategic objectives
  • In collaboration with internal experts, including commercial teams, you co-design and propose Roche solutions tailored to the needs of customers and differentiating. You are responsible for the achievement of business objectives, the evolution of turnover and the profitability of accounts.
  • In close collaboration with the Call for Offers Project Leader, you will coordinate the project team involved in building the response and ensure that it will achieve the expected commercial and financial objectives
  • You also make sure that you have the right contract in line with the rules of the group and the subsidiary.
  • Responsible for the proper execution of the framework contract and customer satisfaction, you set up and follow the relevant performance indicators for the accounts under your responsibility and engage the teams on continuous improvement of the actions
  • You are the guarantor of the relevance and effectiveness of the communication scheme within the accounts.
  • Squad lead: in collaboration with chapter/subchapter lead, you identify the required skills and form a team committed to customer needs, business strategy, achievement of objectives and action plan.
  • You make sure that the members of the squad understand the current account and our competitive position so that the team can prioritize its actions. You keep the KPI team informed of the accounts and engage with it all the actions necessary to achieve our objectives and customer satisfaction.
  • Ambassador Compliance: you guarantee compliance in customer relations
  • You guarantee the accuracy and completeness of the customer information you have in the CRM

Who are you?

  • You master the Techniques of Commercial Negotiation; of Project Management; A scientific culture is a great asset.
  • You have excellent relational ease; excellent analytical and synthesis capability; data management
