Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Linde | Biel / Bienne

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Temporary

Reference number: 46670

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments.

Clinic Linde is the leading private hospital in the Biel-Seeland-Jura area. With its high medical quality, its selected offerings and its supra-regional reputation, it is the first choice for patients and very well-qualified medical doctors.

YOUR TASKS

  • Ensuring patient-oriented care and medical tasks according to competence profile
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation in the team with our attending physicians, nurses and other clinical areas
  • Correct and complete documentation in the clinic information system
  • Ensuring the housekeeping, administrative and logistical tasks on the bed department taking into account the hospital's internal requirements and framework conditions

Your profile

  • You are currently an FH student nursing and you are only missing the additional module B at the conclusion
  • Very good knowledge of German (verbal and written), knowledge of French advantageous
  • Joy of working in different teams High social competence and customer orientation

We offer you an interesting professional spectrum on one of our two interdisciplinary bed departments (surgery, mixed specialist department). After an intensive introductory phase by vocational training managers and trainers, you will continue to be accompanied professionally. In addition to transfer coaching, self-study days, case discussions and learning days, we regularly offer sequences for practicing the clinical assessment. In addition, you are cordially invited to actively participate in the specialist development. We promote your competences as a bachelor graduate and use them accordingly. We also offer:

  • Free coffee, also cappuccino and latte macchiato (yes, every day!)
  • Healthy and cheap menus in the staff restaurant
  • Cool employee discount platform (often with e.g. 11% Zalando or 40% Adidas)
  • Paid changing time for a 100% workload four additional days off (with a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly)

As part of the Hirslanden Group, Klinik Linde is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector. As an employee, you can get involved, develop yourself and actively shape your working environment. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer you the opportunity to benefit from up to 7 weeks of holidays with our «Leisure Plus Model». These are just a few reasons why the Hirslanden Group is one of the most attractive employers in the Swiss healthcare sector.

For additional information, please contact Anne Paradies, Training Manager, at anne.paradies@hirslanden.ch, Anne Paradies, Training Manager, at anne.paradies@hirslanden.ch or Jennifer Perez at jennifer.perez@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.

Please note that we cannot consider paper dossiers (not returned) or applications by email. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via this tool.
