Full description

The International Sales Specialist supports the development of B2B business in international markets, managing customer relationships and coordinating sales activities in collaboration with the sales team and other departments.

Responsibilities

  • Identify and qualify prospective customers and business opportunities in assigned international markets through research and outreach.
  • Develop and maintain relationships with international customers, distributors and agents, responding to enquiries and identifying opportunities to grow existing accounts.
  • Prepare commercial proposals and quotations, support negotiations and follow up on opportunities through to order confirmation, in line with company policies.
  • Coordinate with marketing, operations and other internal teams to support customer requests, order processing and timely delivery.
  • Analyse sales performance, margins, pricing and market data to identify trends and opportunities, and turn findings into actionable recommendations for the sales team.
  • Maintain accurate customer and sales information in the CRM and prepare updates on pipeline, sales activity and performance.
  • Support sales forecasts and commercial planning by providing information on opportunities and customer demand.
  • Participate in international trade shows, customer meetings and industry events as required.

Qualifications

  • Degree in business, international trade or a related field.
  • Experience in B2B sales or a commercial support role, preferably involving international markets.
  • Knowledge of international sales processes and an interest in developing business across different markets.
  • Strong communication, interpersonal and negotiation skills, with the ability to work with people from different cultural backgrounds.
  • Outstanding numerical and business analysis skills, with the ability to interpret complex data, identify trends and translate insights into clear commercial recommendations.
  • Ability to work independently and collaborate effectively with internal teams and external partners.
  • Fluency in English; knowledge of additional languages is a plus.
  • Advanced Excel expertise, including pivot tables, complex formulas, data modelling and dashboards; familiarity with CRM systems and standard office software.
  • Availability to travel internationally as required.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
