Cook/chef at 80\-100 %

The La Résidence Foundation operates three socio-medical facilities: EMS St. in Sitten, APH St. in Visp and APH Emserberg in Unterems. These three homes provide living space for elderly people in need of care.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a dedicated cook (80\-100%) who will pamper our residents with passion and creativity.

Their tasks

  • Preparation of balanced and varied meals taking into account the dietary requirements of our residents
  • Planning and organisation of meal plans in cooperation with the kitchen team
  • Ensuring compliance with hygiene and quality standards (HACCP)
  • Responsibility for ordering and storing food
  • Support for the training of learners in the kitchen

Your profile

  • Completed training as chef/chef EFZ or equivalent qualification
  • Experience in communal catering is beneficial
  • Knowledge of dietary cuisine desirable
  • Teamwork, flexibility and resilience
  • Joy of dealing with older people
  • Good knowledge of German

We offer

  • A meaningful activity in a motivated and collegial team
  • Modern infrastructure and well-equipped kitchen
  • Attractive employment conditions and training opportunities
  • A pleasant working environment in an appreciative atmosphere
  • The opportunity to actively contribute to the quality of life of our residents

So they apply.

We look forward to your application! Further information about the office and the La Résidence Foundation can be found on our website.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
