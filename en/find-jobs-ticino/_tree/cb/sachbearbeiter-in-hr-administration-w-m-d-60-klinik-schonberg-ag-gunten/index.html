Full description

You like to ensure that everything runs smoothly in the background, keep an overview in a dynamic environment and bring structure into the action? Then we could be a perfect match!

As a result of retirement, we are looking for reinforcements for our HR administration immediately or by agreement. You can expect a versatile task in a unique team where your thinking is appreciated and you can make a difference with your work every day.

Your tasks

  • Independent processing of administrative and organizational tasks along the entire employee lifecycle
  • Maintenance and management of personnel and wage data in the HR systems Opale and PEP
  • Management of social security matters, daily allowances and family allowances
  • Cooperation in monthly processing of wages
  • Support for leaders and the responsible person in administrative HR and payroll issues
  • Active participation in the further development and standardization of HR processes and processes as well as in HR projects
  • If you are interested, gradually deepen your payroll knowledge and take on additional tasks in the processing of wages

Your profile

  • Commercial basic education with further training in the field of HR (e.g. HR assistant:in with certificate)
  • Experience in HR administration, ideally with knowledge of the time recording system PEP
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (level C1)
  • Flair for numbers and digital tools as well as a careful, structured and independent working method
  • High reliability, discretion and a strong understanding of services

Our offer

  • A versatile and responsible task with varied activities in HR administration
  • Design room for manoeuvre in the development and further development of basic HR work
  • A responsible task in the middle of the action and close to people and company (regular home office is not planned)
  • Flexible working hours that allow you to use our special location on Lake Thun for a walk, a sporting round or a break in between
  • Opportunities for personal and professional development
  • Relaxing rest (fitness classes, free wellness offers at our partner companies and discounts on fitness subscriptions)
  • Contemporary employment conditions with progressive non-wage benefits

Interested? Then we look forward to your online application. Klinik Schönberg AG, Estelle Fuchs, Human Resources, Schönbergstrasse 40, 3654 Gunten
