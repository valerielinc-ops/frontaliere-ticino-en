We’re looking for a Freelance Senior Software Engineer (iOS) who will have a significant impact on the software development process, moving your entire team forward. Essential skills for this mission

  • You have at least 5+ years of experience building native apps
  • You have a desire to craft beautiful, usable interfaces across all different devices running iOS

  • Excellent written and verbal English communication skills
