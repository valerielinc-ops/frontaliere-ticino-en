The Position In your role as Global Technical Transfer Leader, you will be accountable for scoping, planning and executing technical transfers of small and large molecules between clinical & commercial internal and external manufacturing sites. You will become a core member of the Technical Development Team (TDT) and the Product Technical Team (PTT) responsible for managing timelines, resources, risks, and escalation of issues. You will also be accountable for ensuring adherence to established standards and tools to ensure consistent project planning and execution, informed prioritization across the portfolio, and knowledge sharing with peers and stakeholders. Your role has a significant impact on the achievement of company goals and objectives and requires partnering with stakeholders from across the PT organization such as from Product Strategy Teams (PSTs), Technical Development Teams (TDTs), relevant site functions, etc.

  • Your key responsibilities will include:
  • Lead planning and/or execution as well as provide oversight of assigned transfer projects incl. product launches, line extensions, commercial tech transfers, multi-node transfers, and multi-product programs
  • Deliver E2E supply chain implementation by establishing new manufacturing nodes for Drug Substance, Drug Product, Combination Products, and Secondary Packaging
  • Implement and apply project management knowledge to the Product Technical Teams (PTTs) and Technical Development Teams (TDTs)
  • Escalate and drive resolution of complex risks and issues. Exercise judgment in selecting methods, approaches, and evaluation criteria to provide solutions to the organization
  • For external transfers, own the Joint project team (JPT) with team members from the CDMO
  • Lead cross functional teams with a Lean and continuous improvement mindset and with a strong focus on outcomes and impact for patients. Drive lessons learned to proactively identify and address network level issues.
  • Lead and/or participate in cross-site/product assessments
  • Identify all interdependencies to other products, projects to ensure transparency of project risks and alignment with the current network and product strategies
  • Support site selections triggering new technical transfers by appropriate project scoping and planning, evaluation of relevant project risks and mitigations, and securing the required project resources/budget with key stakeholders
  • Act as mentor for new hires to help onboard them to their new job successfully
  • Provide transparency of the project status to key customers and stakeholders in a highly visible global setting
