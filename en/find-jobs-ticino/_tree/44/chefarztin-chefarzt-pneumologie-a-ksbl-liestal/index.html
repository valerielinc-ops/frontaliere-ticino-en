Full description

Admission as of 1 April 2027 · Bruderholz, Liestal, Pneumology · Job reference 13352 · Permanent employment

Tasks

  • Overall responsible professional, personnel and organizational management of the Pneumology Clinic within the Universitäre Zentrum Innere Medizin (UZIM) at the Liestal and Bruderholz locations
  • Great scope for design with the opportunity to further develop and shape the department at KSBL. Development of ambulatory pulmonology. Leadership and further development of the further education institution Pneumology (category B, ideally development to category A).
  • Implementation and further development of diagnostic and interventional bronchoscopy and use of the entire modern pneumological diagnostic spectrum, in particular tumor diagnostics, sleep medicine, pulmonary function diagnostics and sonography; stand-alone hours working as a specialist doctor
  • Further development of efficient and patient-oriented clinical processes and new care offerings
  • Active participation in shaping an appreciative, performance-oriented leadership culture
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation within UZIM as well as with other disciplines and external referees
  • ensuring and actively participating in the specialist pneumatic pikett and background service
  • Participation in the internist cadre background service
  • Active participation in teaching (training, education and training)
  • Participation in clinical and scientific projects
  • Possibility to actively shape the future medical management structure of pulmonology: there is the option to bring an already familiar senior medical personality into the team

Requirements

  • Specialist title Pneumology and General Internal Medicine or an equivalent MEBEKO-approved qualification
  • Broad clinical experience in pneumology with proven expertise in diagnostic and interventional bronchoscopy
  • Leadership experience in a clinical cadre function and pleasure in the development of employees and teams
  • Prerequisites and willingness to take technical responsibility for and further develop the pneumology training centre in accordance with SIWF requirements
  • A habilitation/strong academic track record beneficial, no requirement
  • Strong ability for interdisciplinary and interprofessional collaboration
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and creative will
  • Clear, binding communication and an integrative leader
  • High quality, patient and service orientation
