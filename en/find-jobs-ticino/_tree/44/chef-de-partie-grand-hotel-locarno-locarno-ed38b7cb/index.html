Full description

The employer is looking for one

Chef De Partie (marked)

Mansions

  • independently manage the assigned match (e.g. Entremetier, Garde Manger, Saucier or Pastry) during the preparation and service stages
  • prepare, cook and package dishes strictly with standardized recipes, grammatures and five-star aesthetic standards
  • flank the Executive Chef and the Sous Chef during the pre-opening phase in the preparation of the stations, in the test of the recipes and in the definition of the operating flows
  • ensure precise and smooth execution during à la carte services, banquets and special events
  • oversee, train and support the kitchen and apprentices assigned to their match
  • ensure full compliance with food safety regulations, HACCP guidelines, allergen protocols and cleaning standards at all times
  • organize daily mise en place, check the freshness of raw materials, manage the rotation of stocks (FIFO) and minimize waste
  • collaborate on the daily orders of the match, the inventory control and the proper preservation of fresh and semi-finished products
  • communicate proactively with the other matches and the team of room to ensure a harmonious and smooth service
  • contribute with creative ideas and proposals for continuous improvement to the evolution of the hotel’s gastronomic offer

Requirements

  • with AFC diploma
  • proven experience of at least 3 years
  • excellent knowledge of Italian and good English
  • availability to work on shifts, weekends and public holidays

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
