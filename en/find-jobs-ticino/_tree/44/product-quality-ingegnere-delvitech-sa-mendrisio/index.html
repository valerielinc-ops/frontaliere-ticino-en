Full description

Safeguard the quality of Delvitech's AI inspection products from requirements to release, through validation, traceability and change control. Job Summary We are looking for a Product Quality Engineer to ensure that requirements, validation activities and product changes are managed through structured, traceable and evidence-based processes. The role works closely with R&D, Testing, DevOps, Production, Application Engineering and Quality across software, embedded software and firmware, electronics, hardware, mechanics and system-level changes. It does not replace R&D ownership of technical design and validation: its objective is to ensure clear Quality criteria, traceability, verifiable evidence, complete documentation and adequate readiness before a change is introduced into the product. Key Responsibilities

  • Define and maintain, together with R&D, the Quality framework for requirements, validation, product changes and releases.
  • Define minimum criteria for requirement quality, so that requirements are clear, verifiable, traceable and consistent.
  • Verify traceability between requirements, design, implementation, testing, validation evidence and releases, and identify gaps.
  • Define release and change readiness criteria and Quality gates across product development.
  • Verify that, before a release or change, the required evidence and documentation are complete and that Production, Application Engineering and customer-facing functions are informed and prepared.
  • Participate in design, release and change reviews, providing an independent assessment of the available evidence.
  • Ensure that hardware, mechanical, electronic and embedded changes are correctly managed in terms of revisions, configurations and compatibility.
  • Monitor quality trends such as escaped defects, regressions, requirement and validation gaps and field feedback, and define related KPIs.
  • Collaborate with Field Quality, Testing, Production & Supplier Quality, the Technical Writer and the Knowledge Systems & AI Engineer.
  • Use AI and digital tools to support requirement reviews, traceability analysis and evidence consolidation, keeping human validation of critical decisions. Requirements
  • Experience in Product Quality, Software Quality, Validation, Requirements Engineering, System Engineering, Testing or equivalent roles.
  • Good understanding of the development life cycle of a technical product, from requirements to validation and release.
  • Ability to work across software, embedded, electronics, hardware and mechanics projects without necessarily being a specialist in each discipline.
  • Good understanding of traceability, verification and validation principles.
  • Ability to assess the readiness of a release or product change, including documentation, communication and impact on other functions.
  • Structured, evidence-based approach and strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Familiarity with requirements, issue, test, change or documentation management tools and with AI tools.
  • Professional working proficiency in English. Nice to Have
  • Experience in product-based, industrial, automation or machine-building companies.
  • Experience with release governance, engineering change management or product quality assurance.
  • Familiarity with Jira, Confluence, GitHub, CI/CD, PLM or test management systems.
  • Familiarity with risk-based testing, regression testing or readiness assessment. What We Offer A permanent position in a fast-growing technology company that develops its own AI-based optical inspection systems, with strong opportunities for professional growth as the company expands.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
