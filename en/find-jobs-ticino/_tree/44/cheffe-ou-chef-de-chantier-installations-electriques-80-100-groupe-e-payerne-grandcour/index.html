Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Group E Tech , thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of electrical installations, tables and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment and maintenance of installations.

Do you want to meet new technical challenges, pilot large-scale construction projects and develop into a welded team? Our Payerne branch is looking for its future Chief of Construction! You will take charge of various projects, mainly projects of CHF 500'000 to 1 million, and will supervise a team of 3 to 5 employees. At Groupe E Tech, we focus on trust, mutual support and a good atmosphere on a daily basis. You will enjoy a stimulating environment where your expertise will be valued and where you can continue to develop your skills and develop your career.

Chief or construction manager electrical installation 80-100%

Mission

  • Pilot major electrical plant sites, from preparation to completion, in close collaboration with the project manager.
  • To supervise and unite a team of 3 to 5 employees, promoting mutual assistance, the quality of work and the transfer of knowledge to apprentices.
  • Organize interventions, anticipate equipment requirements and coordinate orders to ensure the smooth running of the work.
  • Provide technical and administrative follow-up to construction sites, including metres, governance reports and participation in construction sites.
  • Propose innovative and cost-effective technical solutions, while ensuring the quality of installations and compliance with safety rules.

Profile

  • TECHNOLOGY TECHNOLOGY
  • At least 5 years of experience in electrical installations, industrial and/or residential sites.
  • Natural leadership and team spirit: you like to accompany your collaborators, share your knowledge and advance together.
  • Autonomous and organized, you know how to anticipate, manage priorities and find concrete solutions to the challenges on the ground.
  • With a common sense of communication, you appreciate the exchanges with the teams, partners and various stakeholders in the construction projects.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance, with four-and-a-half days of working time and free Friday afternoon
  • A function vehicle also suitable for private use
  • Social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate "Together, we are moving towards success"
