Full description

WHO ARE WE?

Champittet College, a reference school in the German region since 1903, offers excellent training to generations of Swiss and foreign students. Its unique spirit revolves around academic performance, multilingualism and values such as respect and trust. The College is part of the North Anglia Education family, the world leader in international schools with 88 schools in 32 countries. In this dynamic environment, both local and international, we invite you to join our team.

MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Develops a climate conducive to learning and learning the skills expected
  • Formulates and implements the section's objectives in accordance with the general objectives of the College
  • Collaborates with colleagues to harmonize educational progress
  • Defines and adapts with colleagues, leaders and dean, the objectives and contents of the Primary Section program
  • Conducts periodic assessment of the level achieved
  • Regular meeting of parents for a situation point
  • Manages the budget allocated to its class for activities and outputs

PROFILE AND SKILLS

  • Higher education diploma in education science and/or pedagogy
  • 5-7 of experience in primary classes
  • Excellent mastery of French and English
  • Excerpts from blank criminal records
  • Swiss nationality or valid work permit
