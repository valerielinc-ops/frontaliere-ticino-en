Put to the Point

Are you looking for an exciting challenge? Do you want to contribute your personality, your sense of responsibility and your pleasure in leading a team in an important and interesting role while helping to ensure the efficient and sustainable operation of defence and logistics infrastructures? Then you are exactly right with us.

  • Entry Date: Immediately
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20651
  • Location: Les Rochettes, 1680 Romont

You can make this contribution

  • Manage and organise the operation of defence and logistics infrastructures and manage subordinate staff
  • Planning and ensuring the provision, operation and maintenance of the infrastructure and ensuring coordination with the force, users and partners
  • Manage operational and maintenance services, use of resources and expenditure, ensuring their effectiveness and efficiency
  • Ensure compliance with safety regulations and with regard to the protection of persons, safety at work and the environment
  • Actively participate in construction, conversion and liquidation projects, represent the interests of the operator and ensure coordination with the various stakeholders

This makes them unique.

  • technical or craft vocational training, ideally supplemented by higher education (university or equivalent); Federal certificate as maintenance specialist or equivalent training
  • Demonstrated experience in the operation and maintenance of infrastructures and in managing staff
  • Strong leadership skills, organizational and responsibility awareness, goal and result orientation as well as good communication, cooperation and conflict management skills
  • Good knowledge of process and project management, experienced handling of common computer science applications and SAP as well as good knowledge of a second official language
  • Military training as an officer or higher non-commissioned officer is advantageous

Your commitment to security and freedom The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the materials, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.

Additional information
