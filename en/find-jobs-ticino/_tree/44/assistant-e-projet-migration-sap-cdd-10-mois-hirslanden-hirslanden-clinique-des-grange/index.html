Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Clinique des Grangettes According to agreement Type of employment: Fixed term

Reference Number: 75197

As Switzerland's largest medical network, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centres and emergency services. First-class medical quality and individual well-being are at the centre of our concerns.

MAIN ACTIVITY OF THE POST

Strengthen the project team during the migration of the Opal ERP to SAP, especially during the testing, documentation and training phases.

Contribute to the timely implementation of SAP and the proper appropriation of the new tool by users.

Operational support function, without project management or supervision responsibility.

MAIN TASKS

  • Demonstrate professionalism, rigour and availability.
  • Maintain team spirit and foster collaboration with project leaders, user services and providers.
  • Respect the planning and priorities defined by the project leaders.
  • Report any anomaly, delay or blockage to project leaders without delay.

Tests and recipe

  • Prepare and execute functional and end-to-end test scenarios
  • Test interfaces between SAP and connected software (Mediboard, billing, operating unit, admissions, etc.)
  • Document test results, capture and track anomalies until resolved
  • Participate in the control of migrated data from Opale

Procedures and documentation

  • Draft and update procedures and procedures for new processes
  • Develop training materials (user guides, reflex sheets, presentations)
  • Classify and maintain project documentation

Training and support

  • Organize logistics of training sessions (planning, rooms, registrations)
  • Conduct training with users, alone or in pairs
  • Provide first-level support to users when starting

The incumbent may be required to perform tasks on an ad hoc or sustainable basis and/or to replace a colleague to ensure the sustainability of the service at the request of the supervisor.

The holder is responsible for continuing training in collaboration with the Stream Stream and Performance Manager and the Flow and Performance Manager.

TRAINING REQUIRED

  • SWC business employee, Bachelor HES (business economy, management IT) or equivalent
  • SAP user training or certification
  • Knowledge of administrative processes
  • Knowledge of the hospital or health environment
  • Mastery of common office automation tools (MS Office)
  • Writing experience (procedures, teaching materials)
  • Knowledge of English
  • Autonomy and ability to be quickly operational
  • Rigor and sense of organization
  • Educational and relational ease with users of various profiles
  • Stress resistance and flexibility during start-up
  • Team spirit

PREVIOUS EXPERIENCE
