For the house Tertianum Pretty Autumn in the Lausanne region, we are looking for the 01.11.2026

Graduated nurse HES / 100 % ASSC

Your tasks

  • Overall host care and care in the spirit of our care model and the care process
  • Care as a reference person
  • Responsibility and control of the assigned host care process, including classification of RAI/BESA/PLAISER care needs
  • Collaborate with external stakeholders (physio, doctor...)
  • Planning and accompanying medical visits
  • Participation in care reports and transmission of important observations
  • Support for body care, movements and other daily activities Organization and proposal of daily activities for our guests with creative occupations, walks, etc.
  • Writing care notes in our CareCoach care documentation system (possible for mobility with the CareCoach smartphone)
  • Daily responsibility with coordination of the daily team
  • Case Discussion and Nursing Reporting Management
  • Task-carrying as a theme holder in a specialized theme and therefore participation in the group-wide quality care network

Your profile

  • Training of a graduate nurse completed or equivalent training recognized by the CRS / Training of an assistant in Community Health and Care CFC completed
  • Professional experience in the care of several years is an asset
  • RAI/BESA/PLAISIR knowledge desired or willingness to take these courses (paid)
  • Organised, calm, rigorous, committed and motivated
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Pleasure meeting various challenges
  • French mother tongue or common practice
