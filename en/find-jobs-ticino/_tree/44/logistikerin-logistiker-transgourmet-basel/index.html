Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss delivery and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals and retailers). Position note: The training takes place during the first three months in the Prodega Markt Neuendorf. From April 2027 the place of work is in Basel.

Tasks

  • You check incoming goods on time and error-free – including MHD check.
  • You book the goods correctly in the warehouse management system and store them under system control.
  • You ensure the container and deposit item exchange with suppliers.
  • You pick customer orders according to regulations and ensure timely replenishment.
  • You hand over loaded transport aids (THM) to the right tour site.
  • You process returns correctly and dispose of waste properly.

Requirements

  • You have completed an apprenticeship as a logistician / logistician EFZ/EBA or at least 3 years of professional experience.
  • You have a valid forklift certificate (SUVA) with solid driving practice.
  • You work goal-oriented, reliable and with great initiative.
  • You have experience with warehouse management systems.
  • You are physically resilient and ready for use in the refrigerator (3–5 °C).
  • You speak and write good German.
