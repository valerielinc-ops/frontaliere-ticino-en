Previdential Consultant – career change (La Ponte Tresa area)

Are you looking for a new professional challenge and want to pursue a challenging career in the security industry? Convit offers you the opportunity to acquire specialist skills and develop your potential. We prepare you for a successful future as a qualified social security consultant.

Main information Employer: Convit International GmbH Workplace: Via al Mulino 22a, 6814 Cadempino, Switzerland Recruitment area: Ponte Tresa and its surroundings – the workplace is our headquarters in Cadempino. Contract: Indefinite recruitment; degree of employment according to the approved position Retribution (OTE): CHF 1'500–7'500 per month (fix + variable part, depending on the degree of employment) Language: Italian B2 or higher

Activities

  • Acquire in-depth knowledge of Swiss social security products and regulations.
  • Prepare for the VBV qualification exam with the support of our team.
  • Support the organizational and administrative activities of the consulting team.
  • Develop your communication and analysis skills through internal support and training.

Your profile

Pre-existing VBV qualification is not required to start the route. The availability of training and the commitment to acquire the necessary qualifications are essential. Counselling and intermediation activities begin only after fulfilling the applicable requirements and internal authorization.

  • You are motivated to learn and play in a new sector.
  • You have a strong attitude to service and you like to interact with people.
  • You have good organizational skills and are oriented to your goals.
  • You are proactive and eager to build a long-term career.

What we offer

  • Structured insertion and comparison with experienced colleagues.
  • Digital tools and clear procedures for preparation and documentation.
  • A clear learning path with practical support.
  • Hybrid working methods agreed, with presence in the reference location.

Training and qualification

The VBV admission exam preparation course is free for you: Convit supports course costs. The qualification requires overcoming the relevant examination and compliance with the applicable requirements.

Right to work

Applications of persons resident in Switzerland are welcome. For this campaign, those who reside in Italy must already have a valid G permit. The individual situation is verified before hiring.

Application

Send the resume and any certificates via the application form or to Recruiting@convit.ch.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes, if you are resident in Italy within the 20 km border zone. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks, then automatic yearly renewal for as long as the employment continues. Weekly return to your Italian home is required to keep the status. Note: this listing allows remote work — teleworking above 25 % of your time can shift your social-security base to Italy, so confirm the agreed remote share with HR before signing.
