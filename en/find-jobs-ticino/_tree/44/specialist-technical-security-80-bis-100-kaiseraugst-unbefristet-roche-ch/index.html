The Position

The organization “Site Network Basel/Kaiseraugst”, part of the global group function “Corporate Strategy, Sustainability & Sites”, provides the infrastructure and services for the entire site and continuously develops them in an innovative way. We work with all stakeholders along the entire value chain, from early research to patient care. With us you can look forward to a modern organizational model in which you can contribute your talents in meaningful work. We guarantee freedom of design and decision-making in our organization. You are looking for classic supervisors in vain. As a Specialist Technical Security (m/f/d), you are an important contact for specific security topics at the Kaiseraugst/Basel location. Your main task area includes locking technology and access control. In addition, you plan and implement complex projects in the area of locking systems. With your work you contribute significantly to the sustainable security of our company

The possibility

  • Operation, maintenance, extension and data maintenance of technical security systems (access control, locking system, key management, OSS)
  • Crafting activities in the context of troubleshooting
  • Cooperation and support of projects in the field of security with a focus on technical access controls and locking systems.
  • Responsibility of the entire locking systems and their extensions
  • Ensuring efficient and economical execution of projects
  • General administrative tasks
  • Readiness for Picetting

Who you are

As a successful employee of Site Security, you have courteous and polite manners, a very good technical understanding and a controlling eye. You have completed vocational training (EFZ) in the electrotechnical or mechanical field and have at least 5 years of professional experience.

It would be desirable to have several years of experience in the areas of locking technology and/or access control systems. In addition, you have a strong technical understanding in the planning and operation of complex systems with a focus on door engineering.

Furthermore, you have the following professional and personal competences:

  • You convince through your craftsmanship, are extremely capable of teamwork and are also resilient under increased pressure. You also work independently and can implement tasks independently.
  • You have strong systems thinking. Thanks to your affinity to always give you an overview, you recognize the need for future action in your field and implement it proactively.
  • You bring very good PC knowledge (especially Office and special user programs). You are able to quickly familiarize yourself with new software and systems.
  • You can communicate well with different contacts and have very good oral and written knowledge of German. Good English skills round off your profile.
  • You are honest and discreet, treat confidential information with the utmost care and have an impeccable reputation.
  • You value open and honest communication and always work loyally and respectfully in the team. You know how important it is to find solutions together and support each other.
  • You are flexible and can quickly adapt to new challenges.
