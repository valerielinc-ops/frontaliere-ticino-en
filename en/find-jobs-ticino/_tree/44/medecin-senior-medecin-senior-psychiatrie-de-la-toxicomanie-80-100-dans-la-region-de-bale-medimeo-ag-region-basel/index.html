Are you still missing the focus title Dependence Diseases? In this clinic, you acquire it where the treatment begins after withdrawal: As a senior physician, you shape a dual diagnosis program with your own handwriting. Six weeks takes the path from physical withdrawal to the first real change. Withdrawal takes two weeks, followed by four weeks of motivational therapy. During this time, the patients deal intensively with their dependence in individual and group discussions. Most bring more than the addiction: trauma follow-up disorders, affective disorders, ADHD or personality disorders are part of everyday life on this openly guided station. The addiction area is a separate clinical focus with chief physician and senior physician. On the station, you’ll work with nursing, social work, and your own psychology team. In addition to the station, the focus is on three addiction tampering agencies, a substitution site and daily clinical services. Patient: inside can thus be treated after exit in the same network. Work is done in the Basel bid, maybe soon in Basel. The city is exactly where Switzerland, Germany and France meet. In Alsace or South Baden, take the tram. With around 40 museums, Art Basel in June and a car night that begins with the Morgestraich at four o’clock in the morning, Basel offers more culture than one would expect from a city of this size. On lukewarm evenings you travel with one of the old Rhine ferries, which are driven only by the current.

  • You will develop the inpatient treatment program for dual diagnoses conceptually further
  • You work closely with the multi-professional team as well as with internal and external partners

  • Clinical experience with addictions desirable, but not a requirement
  • Desire for conceptual work and to take on responsibility in the team
  • Knowledge of German at least. at level B2

  • Concept with your handwriting: You are actively developing a modern dual diagnosis program instead of just managing existing processes

  • Addiction medicine in all settings: ward, day clinic, three ambulatories and substitution under one professional roof
  • Comprehensible wage development: clear wage bands, salary in 13 monthly wages
