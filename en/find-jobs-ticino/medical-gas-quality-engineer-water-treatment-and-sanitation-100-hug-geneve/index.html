Original description

You are part of a team where your main mission is to monitor the quality of medical gas, water and sanitation facilities and to contribute to the development and maintenance of the Quality Management System (QMS). You monitor the quality of medical gas, water treatment and sanitary facilities and ensure compliance with applicable regulatory and normative requirements. As such, you perform quality audits in the field and monitor the compliance of facilities, equipment and interventions. You are involved in the qualification, validation, reception and commissioning of medical gas networks. You also monitor the quality of gases, including medical air, and monitor particulate contamination controls when necessary. You identify and track non-compliance. You conduct the investigations necessary to identify root causes and guide the definition, implementation and monitoring of corrective and preventive actions (CAPA). You contribute to the analysis and control of risks in accordance with applicable requirements, in particular ISO 14971, and according to ODIM participate in regulatory and normative monitoring to ensure that developments are taken into account in practice and organisation. You participate in internal audits, supplier audits and inspections carried out by the competent authorities, and contribute to the follow-up of actions resulting from them. You support operational teams in the application of quality and regulatory requirements, including training, awareness raising and support for technical teams. Finally, you contribute to the documentation monitoring and continuous improvement of the Quality Management System, ensuring traceability and compliance of activities related to medical gases, water treatment and sanitation.
