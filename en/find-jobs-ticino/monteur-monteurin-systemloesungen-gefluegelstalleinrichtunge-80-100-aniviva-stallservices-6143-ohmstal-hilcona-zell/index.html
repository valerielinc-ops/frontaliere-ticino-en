Role overview

Aniviva in Ohmstal/LU is part of the poultry organisational unit and develops innovative solutions for Swiss poultry production.

With the latest air conditioning, control and production technology, we ensure reliable and efficient operation of poultry houses.

As an installer / installer system solutions poultry house equipment, you are the central contact person for the professional installation of our poultry house equipment: You install stable and system components, carry out structural adjustments and support the installation of air conditioning, ventilation and production technology directly on site.

  • Aniviva in Ohmstal/LU is part of the poultry organisational unit and develops innovative solutions for Swiss poultry production.
  • With the latest air conditioning, control and production technology, we ensure reliable and efficient operation of poultry houses.
  • Fitting of poultry houses such as winches, nipple drinkers, fodder trays and other sheds

Main responsibilities

  • Fitting of poultry houses such as winches, nipple drinkers, fodder trays and other sheds
  • Installation of ventilation, air conditioning and heat recovery systems in poultry houses and installation of organic huts
  • Cutting, adapting and assembling wall panels, insulation and structural elements in houses
  • Execution of mechanical and structural works in close cooperation with electricians and service technicians
  • Support for the installation and commissioning of air conditioning, control and production technology
  • Guidance for auxiliary fitters and farmers as well as transfer of practical knowledge about the construction, use and maintenance of the facilities
  • Material organisation and warehouse management including material scaffolding, picking and preparation of assembly operations
  • Ensuring quality, safety and animal welfare as well as order and cleanliness on the construction site
