Role overview

To complement our computer science team in Kirchlindach, we are looking for an IT Application Manager at the immediate or by appointment (clinic south slope, Kirchlindach employment level 80% job opening by immediately or by appointment) (pdf) What makes your work particularly attractive)

  • Leading role in the operation and further development of the administrative application landscape (Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, ID Diacos, Polypoint PEP, Microsoft Power BI)
  • LifeCycle Management of the supported applications (update and release management)
  • Perceiving 1st and 2nd level application support
  • Coordination, specifications and evaluation of new system requirements
  • Parameterizations of applications and their interfaces
  • Participation and assumption of IT project tasks and sub-project management
  • Preparation of training concepts and documentation
  • Close cooperation with the specialist areas (finance, controlling, patient administration, medical controlling, HR), external service providers and manufacturers
  • Deputy in the clinical IT application area and representation of the IT service desks What distinguishes you
  • Training in computer science/medical informatics and professional experience in a comparable role
  • Enjoyment of customer-oriented IT service provision
  • Quick comprehension and a strong technical understanding
  • Friendly and team-oriented personality with a strong service mentality
  • Healthcare professional experience as well as experience with Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, Polypoint PEP and Microsoft Power BI are beneficial
  • Knowledge of the integration of applications (e.g.

via Mirth Connect) is an advantage

  • Committed, structured and independent working method
  • Project experience, analytical and networked thinking skills What you can look forward to You can expect a versatile field of work, advanced employment conditions with internal and external training opportunities and a workplace with a modern infrastructure.

Short decision-making paths and the opportunity for co-creation distinguish us.

Become part of our team and actively shape the future of our clinic.
