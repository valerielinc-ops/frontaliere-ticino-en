Original description

IMAD collaborates with several partner schools and higher education institutions, including the CFPS, the HEdS Geneva, the HETS Geneva and the HESAV. Internship places are planned within the framework of training agreements established with these institutions. Requests are therefore generally coordinated directly between the partner schools and IMAD, in order to guarantee a reception in accordance with the objectives of the courses. In order to guarantee an adapted and secure learning environment, IMAD reserves its internships for adults (18 years of age or older). If there are currently no internship offers that match your profile, you can send us a complete unsolicited application. However, we prefer to process applications in response to published internship offers.
