Role overview

Supporting the Human Resources Officer in the Medical Department, you provide information and advice to employees and managers on HR issues and you manage administratively for the target audience for which you are responsible, in synergy with two HR management colleagues.

Thus, you intervene administratively throughout the life cycle of our teams, from recruitment to start, and in connection with many partners of HRD and the institution (wage and social insurance service, recruitment service, medical-care and technical managers, ...).
