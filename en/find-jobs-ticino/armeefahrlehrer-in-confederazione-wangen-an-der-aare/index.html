Original description

Mansioni

  • Accompany motorists (trucks/trailers) during training, assess them and prepare them for their missions in the military transport service
  • Give category C theory lessons based on civil road traffic regulations as well as accompany the driver training on the simulator and analyze driving situations (FSM24)
  • Take civilly recognized driving tests as a military traffic expert
  • Further training modules in accordance with the Chauffeur Licensing Ordinance (CZV) as well as courses on the interchangeable loading system (WLS) and dangerous goods law ADR/SDR
  • Support training and practice sequences at various training locations in Switzerland
  • Requirements
  • Completed training as a truck driving instructor with driving instructor license 202 in accordance with the driving instructor ordinance
  • Further training in accordance with asa guideline No. 9 must be current at the time of employment
  • Enjoy adult education as well as a confident and convincing demeanor
  • Ability to remain calm and find solutions even in demanding situations - Exemplary, responsible, team-oriented personality with the motivation to pass on knowledge - Oral and written knowledge of a second official language
