Original description

We are the bank of Helvetia – an innovative financial services provider that intelligently combines banking and insurance. Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than simply working through tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high demands and have the courage to go new ways. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, initiative and contribution count. So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development. As part of the newly formed Helvetia Baloise, the future of Swiss banking has been at the forefront. You shape the synergies between insurance and the bank directly from the customer service of the bank. Payment transactions are a core service: they must function reliably and be customer-oriented. Use your creative leeway to achieve a measurable impact in constant change and to continuously increase our performance. Tasks:

  • You lead the payment transaction team professionally and in terms of personnel and establish a positive performance culture.
  • You guarantee an efficient, safe and high-quality operational operation.
  • You ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and the implementation of internal instructions and controls.
  • You continuously optimize processes, services and workflows and drive efficiency gains.
  • You represent the area significantly in cross-sectoral initiatives.
  • You are actively developing the strategic orientation of our payment transactions.
  • You work in partnership with risk, compliance, audit and outsourcing partners. Requirements
  • Several years of professional experience in the payment transactions of a bank.
  • In-depth knowledge of payment processes and relevant regulatory requirements.
  • Leadership experience or strong potential and clear motivation for the next career step.
  • High quality awareness, analytical thinking skills and a solution-oriented, structured way of working.
  • Strong implementation strength with pleasure in actively promoting change.
  • A strong communication personality with a strong team and service orientation.
  • stylish knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of French, Italian and English is an advantage. We believe in cooperation at eye level, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions – for our customers as well as for us as a team.
