To support our plate production in Basel we are looking for a motivated person as Operator Plates (a) Your responsibilities Cooperation on CNC-controlled machinery Performing visual inspections of implants Execution of bulk sampling Manual and mechanical deburring of implants Reshaping the implants with the aid of a press Cleaning the implants by means of an ultrasonic washing system Support for the optimization of existing manufacturing processes Your qualifications Technical vocational training with a specialist card is an advantage Professional experience in mechanical workshops is an advantage Good knowledge of German (in word and writing, at least B2) Handling the PC as well as knowledge of MS-Office applications required Readiness for shift and piquet work About Medartis Medartis is one of the leading manufacturers of medical products for osteosynthesis in the area of the facial skull and extremities.
