Role overview

The Office for Forest and Natural Hazards (AWN) is the cantonal specialist for forestry and protection against natural hazards.

The employees of the AWN are committed to the needs of society in the forest and ensure that the forest is handled sustainably.

  • The Office for Forest and Natural Hazards (AWN) is the cantonal specialist for forestry and protection against natural hazards.
  • The employees of the AWN are committed to the needs of society in the forest and ensure that the forest is handled sustainably.
  • As the GIS manager for the area "Forest status and development", you are responsible for the tracking, harmonization and further development of our geodata.
  • In cooperation with experts, you calculate forest indicators and organize their storage and accessibility.

Main responsibilities

  • As the GIS manager for the area "Forest status and development", you are responsible for the tracking, harmonization and further development of our geodata.
  • In cooperation with experts, you calculate forest indicators and organize their storage and accessibility.
  • You automate periodic calculations and modeling.
  • Depending on your qualifications, you will support the area in the analysis, interpretation and use of digital data and key figures.
  • You participate in interdisciplinary GIS projects. Requirements
  • University or technical college degree in geoinformatics, computer science, data science, geography, forestry science or a related discipline.
  • Applicants with appropriate professional experience and further education in the GIS/IT area as well as graduates are welcome.
  • High willingness to learn and pleasure in methodically analysing complex problems, developing pragmatic solutions and quickly putting new things into practice
  • Knowledge of ArcGIS Pro, R and Python are advantages
  • Initiative, reliability and a solution-oriented working method

Additional details

  • You participate in interdisciplinary GIS projects.
