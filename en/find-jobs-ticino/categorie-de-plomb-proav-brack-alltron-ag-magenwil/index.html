Original description

Tasks

  • Personnel and professional leadership as well as further development of the category team
  • Overall responsibility for sales, contribution margin, budget and earnings targets
  • Development and implementation of the category, range, pricing and procurement strategy
  • Derivation of strategic priorities from the corporate strategy as well as ensuring the achievement of goals
  • Analysis of market, competition and technology trends for the continuous development of the portfolio
  • Establishment and further development of long-term manufacturer and supplier partnerships
  • Responsibility for the product group structure in the webshop as well as its continuous optimization
  • Control of storage strategy, pricing strategy and manufacturer conditions
  • Initiation and implementation of projects for digitalization and process optimization
  • Close cooperation with sales, marketing, purchasing, logistics and other internal stakeholders
  • Organization of customer events in cooperation with manufacturers as well as participation in international trade fairs

Requirements

  • Completed basic business or technical training with higher education (HF/FH/HFP), management training beneficial
  • Several years of professional and managerial experience in category management, purchasing or product management ideally in the ICT, ProAV, UCC, or e-commerce environment
  • Demonstrable successes in the development of assortments, manufacturers and strategic business fields
  • In-depth know-how in the field of ProAV and a good understanding of market trends, customer needs and pricing strategies
  • Experience in project management as well as in the optimization and digitalization of processes
  • Very good language skills in German and English, French or Italian are advantageous
  • Convincing communication and negotiation skills as well as a secure appearance towards manufacturers and internal stakeholders
  • Strategic thinking skills, entrepreneurial actions and a pronounced customer and solution orientation

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.
