Original description

Your key responsibilities

You will complement our Housekeeping team and be responsible for the following areas:

  • In close collaboration with the Executive Housekeeper, you will lead a team of approximately 70 Room Attendants and Floor Porters.

  • You will actively contribute to staff scheduling and help ensure the smooth and efficient running of daily operations.

  • • Your responsibilities will include inspecting arrival, stayover and departure rooms, as well as checking VIP setups, public areas and staff accommodation.

  • You will organise and order the materials required for daily operations and ensure that all deliveries meet our quality standards and are complete.

  • Through targeted training and continuous guidance, you will support the professional development of our employees and ensure compliance with our quality and hygiene standards.

  • You will act as a competent point of contact for our guests, responding to questions and individual requests while ensuring attentive and personalised service.

• What you bring to the job

For this varied and rewarding position, you ideally bring the following qualifications and experience:

  • You have completed professional training in the hotel industry and have gained relevant experience in a five-star hotel environment.

  • You have previous leadership experience and are confident in supporting the Executive Housekeeper with the management and coordination of a large team.

  • You have a very good command of written and spoken German, as well as good spoken English and Italian.

  • You are proficient in the use of standard Microsoft Office applications. Knowledge of Protel and Mirus would be an advantage.

  • You demonstrate a high level of initiative and commitment, combined with a strong focus on quality and guest satisfaction.

  • Your confident and well-groomed appearance, together with your excellent manners and interpersonal skills, complete your profile.

Our offer

  • Fixed-term seasonal position from mid-November 2026 until the beginning of April 2027

  • 5-day working week averaging 43.5 hours, with 5 weeks' holiday and 6 public holidays per year.

  • 13th month's salary from the start of employment (pro rata temporis).

  • Accommodation in one of our new staff houses.

  • Meals available in our new and modern staff restaurant.

  • Opportunity to work in an attractive environment with development and career growth opportunities.

If you appreciate an interesting, challenging and varied role in a dynamic environment and would like to join a well-established team, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.

Recruitment Fraud Warning

Badrutt’s Palace Hotel will never request payments or financial information as part of the recruitment process. All official communication is conducted exclusively through email addresses ending in @badruttspalace.com.

Should you receive a payment request or any suspicious communication, please disregard it and contact us directly through our official website.
