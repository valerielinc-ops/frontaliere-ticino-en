Social Services Team Leader 80% (h/f/x)

Our French-speaking Swiss Migration & Integration Department – an office of Caritas Switzerland in Freiburg employing about 100 employees – is responsible for the support and integration of statutory refugees in the canton of Freiburg. On a cantonal mandate, we welcome new arrivals to our Matran home, help them find their own housing, provide personal and material assistance according to the standards of social assistance and support them in their efforts towards professional and social integration. In addition, we run an active community interpretation service in several French cantons as well as a listening and counselling service against racism for the inhabitants of the canton of Fribourg. In order to strengthen the supervision of our Social Assistance Service, which consists of about 20 assistants and social assistants and administrative assistants divided into three teams, we are looking for a team leader. This person will join the two current team leaders and contribute to the conduct and development of the Service's activities.

  • Establish monthly social assistance budgets/accounts and target direct payments

  • Training in social work or training deemed equivalent
  • CAS in Team Conduct and/or Continuous Project Management Training
  • 2-5 years experience in team management (approx. 7-8 people) in a similar context
  • Mastery of legal bases for social assistance and CSIAS standards

  • Skills in migration and refugee integration

  • Excellent organizational, analysis and prioritization capabilities in managing complex situations
