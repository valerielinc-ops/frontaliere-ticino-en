Role overview

The Meiringen Private Clinic is located in the Haslital in the Bernese Oberland.

Here people with all forms of psychological suffering find reception, treatment and care.

The clinic with 187 beds is open to patients from all over Switzerland and abroad and is responsible for the inpatient psychiatric primary care of the eastern Bernese Oberland.
