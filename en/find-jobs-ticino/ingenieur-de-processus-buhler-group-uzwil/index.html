Original description

Wishes Do you solve technical challenges directly on site, optimize production processes and at the same time participate in the further development of innovative solutions in the vehicle manufacturing market? In this exciting position as a process engineer: in die casting, you represent application technology and act as a central interface between customer, development, engineering and customer service – both internationally at the customer and in the test laboratory.

Tasks

  • They support the start of production of new plants directly at the customer’s site – from commissioning and starting up the plant to troubleshooting, troubleshooting and optimizing the casting process.
  • They optimize the production output and ensure the function and availability of the plants at our international customers.
  • They define process parameters, develop them further and actively contribute to the stability, efficiency and quality of production processes in die casting.
  • They monitor and analyse quality data, identify causes in committees and initiate appropriate corrective measures.
  • They work closely with our development department, support application engineering development projects, operate test installations in the test laboratory and are deployed internationally with a travel activity of up to 60%.

Requirements

  • Completed training as plant and apparatus manufacturer:in, automatic engineer:in, polymechanic:in, electrician:in, agricultural machinery mechanic:in, automotive mechatronic engineer:in or comparable training
  • Experience in commissioning, process optimization and application technology in die casting
  • Basic knowledge of metallurgy, fluid engineering and thermodynamics is advantageous
  • Knowledge of electrical/electronics and hydraulic components is desirable
  • Good knowledge of German and English and willingness to travel internationally up to 60%
