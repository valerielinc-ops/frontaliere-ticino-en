Original description

The approach of the Canton of St.Gallen and the municipalities of St.Gallen is unique in Switzerland, because with "eGovernment St.Gallen digital.", the two levels of state have decided for a higher-level management. This allows centrally binding standards for municipalities and cantons to be defined and the strategic e-government services to be defined. The first services are already connected and enjoy great popularity. Together digital for cantons and municipalities. eGovernment St.Gallen digital. designs and coordinates the digital cooperation between the canton of St.Gallen and the municipalities of St.Gallen. For our team, we are looking for a committed personality who likes to organize, communicate and keep an overview.
