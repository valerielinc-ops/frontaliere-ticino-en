About the role

Job Summary The (Mechanical) Engineering Supervisor is responsible for supervising a team of mechanical engineers and support staff engaged in the design, development, and testing of mechanical systems and components. This role ensures that engineering projects are executed efficiently, meet technical and quality standards, and align with organizational goals. The supervisor also plays a key role in mentoring junior engineers, managing project timelines, and fostering innovation in mechanical design and manufacturing processes. Purpose of the Job

As a Principal Piping Engineer, you will participate in Sulzer projects at all execution stages (Conceptual design, Skid and Modular design, DD, Procurement / Construction / Commissioning support etc.). Principal Piping Engineer reports to the Chief Piping Engineer and/or Engineering Manager. 主要任务与职责:

Main tasks and responsibilities

  • Take lead in overall piping design of skid
  • Act as overall mechanical engineering IC in skid projects
  • Train and guide piping engineers
  • Design, 3D modeling and review of main metal structures of skids and modules
  • Piping stress analysis
  • Development of the drawings and other design documents within discipline
  • Material selection within discipline
  • Value engineering within discipline
  • Interface management
  • Participate in the development of technical specifications for design, technical requirements for supply, etc.
  • Supervision and support of subcontractors' activities
  • Checking and approval documentation from subcontractors.
  • Participation in the development of design philosophy and internal standards within the discipline
  • Participation in the project meetings, 3D models review etc.
  • Contribute to development of the overall project schedule.
  • Tracking and managing changes within discipline
  • Participation in the project progress report development.
