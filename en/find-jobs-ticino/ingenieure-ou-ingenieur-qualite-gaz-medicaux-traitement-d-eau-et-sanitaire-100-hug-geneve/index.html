Role overview

You will join a team in which your main mission is to ensure quality monitoring of medical gas, water treatment and sanitation installations and to contribute to the development and maintenance of the Quality Management System (QMS).

You monitor the quality of medical gas, water treatment and sanitation installations and ensure their compliance with applicable regulatory and standard requirements.

In this capacity, you carry out quality audits in the field and control the conformity of installations, equipment and interventions.

  • You will join a team in which your main mission is to ensure quality monitoring of medical gas, water treatment and sanitation installations and to contribute to the development and maintenance of the Quality Management System (QMS).
  • You monitor the quality of medical gas, water treatment and sanitation installations and ensure their compliance with applicable regulatory and standard requirements.

Application process

  • You support operational teams in the application of quality and regulatory requirements, in particular through training, awareness and support actions for technical teams.
  • Finally, you contribute to the documentary monitoring and continuous improvement of the Quality Management System, ensuring the traceability and compliance of activities related to medical gases, water treatment and sanitation.

Company and context

  • You contribute to the analyzes and control of risks in accordance with applicable requirements, in particular the ISO 14971 standard, and according to the ODIM, participate in regulatory and standards monitoring to ensure that developments in practices and the organization are taken into account.
  • You participate in internal audits, supplier audits as well as inspections carried out by the competent authorities, and contribute to monitoring the resulting actions.
