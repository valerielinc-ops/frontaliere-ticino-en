Original description

Within the Unit of Medicine and Traffic Psychology (UMPT), you take part, under supervision, in the driving skills assessments carried out at the various sites of the unit. To do this, you work closely with UMPT psychologists to assess the situations entrusted to you and contribute to quality interdisciplinary care. As part of your activities, you also conduct biological samples for toxicological analysis and prepare expert reports that are validated and co-signed by the unit physician(s). In order to develop your skills in this specialized field, you take part in scientific seminars as well as in pre-graduate training courses in trafficking medicine in French and/or German.
