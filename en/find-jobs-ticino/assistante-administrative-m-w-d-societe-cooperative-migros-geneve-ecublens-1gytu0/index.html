Original description

Do you like juggling figures, projects and human relations? Join us to take advantage of your organizational sense and versatility! As a key support for our product managers, you will be the key interface between our teams, subsidiaries and suppliers, ensuring the smooth running of our operations. Integrate a dynamic team where every day is a new adventure!

Mansioni

  • Administrative support: you support our products management in the entry of our regular orders.
  • Invoicing support: you support the reconciliation of invoices by monitoring the accuracy of the data and capturing them.
  • Communication: You support communication with our subsidiaries.
  • Polyvalence: you are adapting and proactivity to meet the various tasks of the support service.

Required

  • 2 years of experience: in an administrative assistant role, technical or in the management of relations with external partners.
  • Completed studies (Uni/VET, HES, ES): in the field of management, administration or a related technical field.
  • Mastering IT tools: You commonly use office automation and SAP tools to manage administrative data and processes.
  • Communication skills: You communicate effectively in French and German, facilitating exchanges with stakeholders.
  • Analytical capability: You analyze the performance data of the products to produce accurate and useful reports. - English (Good control) - German (Preferably)

Cosa offriamo

  • Occupational pension: Primary contributions, contribution from 20 years doubled by Migros and possibility of early retirement from 58 years.
  • Restoration: Reduction of meal of Fr.4 (from purchases of 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Cum Points (2008): Reduction of 2% and 5% according to assortments.
  • Migros Bank: Interest at preferential rate and without charge for a savings account.
