Specialist Post-Trade Education & Client Testing (80 - 100%) Olten | up to 40% Home Office possible | Reference 8082 Would you like to work in a dynamic team in the field of Post-Trade Education & Client Testing? Are you a communicative personality who has a strong customer understanding and keeps a cool head even in hectic times? Do you have a good knowledge of the stock market and securities world? Then apply and become part of our Post-Trade Education & Client Testing Team. As a specialist in Post-Trade Education & Client Testing, you will support our team in the creation of e-learning courses as well as in the planning and implementation of face-to-face courses. The online and face-to-face courses are aimed at our customers and employees and impart knowledge about several areas of the securities world and various SIX applications. In client testing, you are responsible for central coordination between internal stakeholders and our customers and ensure coordinated implementation of the test scenarios. Working closely with your teammates, you ensure that the concerns of new and existing customers regarding testing activities are recorded and successfully implemented. The training and client testing activities include various specialist areas of SIX such as Settlement, Corporate Action, Tax, Funds, Clearing and others. These are your tasks

  • Creation and implementation of training content for employees and customers (e-learning and face-to-face courses) in various post-trade subject areas such as settlement, corporate actions, tax, etc. in close cooperation with internal stakeholders.
  • Conducting high-quality and customer-oriented demo sessions or presentations of applications and services, online or at SIX locations, possibly also at customers on site
  • Internal interface for client testing requests and central contact point for coordinating and executing smooth client testing activities with new and existing customers
  • Creating and updating test scripts for various business areas such as corporate action, settlement, clearing etc.
  • Regular customer contact, telephone as written in German and English You bring that with you
  • Compulsory, several years of professional experience in securities processing and post-trading, ideally Custody & Clearing (Settlement, Corporate Action, Clearing etc.), SWIFT knowledge also beneficial
  • Open-minded and eloquent personality who likes to impart their knowledge and can perform safely in front of an audience
  • Experience in dealing with demanding customers as well as a distinct customer flair
  • Resilient and team-capable personality who is not afraid to contribute his own ideas
  • Very good German and English skills in word and writing (minimum level B2), French and Italian as well as any other language is an advantage If you have any questions, visit our FAQ page or contact Beata Czyzewska on +48 22 104 6571 / +41 58 399 9800 / +34 91 330 51 49.
