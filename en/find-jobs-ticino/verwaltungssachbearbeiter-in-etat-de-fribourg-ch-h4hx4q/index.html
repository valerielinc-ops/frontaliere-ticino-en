The Office of the Commercial Police (GePoA) is responsible for various economic sectors such as public restaurants, the alcohol trade, the taxi industry, the money games or the travel industry. In these areas, it issues authorisations, performs control tasks and ensures the application of relevant legislation. Through its work, it contributes to ensuring a reliable legal framework for the canton’s professionals and population.

Their tasks

  • Written, telephone and personal information, information and advice to citizens, managers and municipalities on the procedures in the field of activity of the Office for Commercial Police
  • Administrative processing of the dossiers of public restaurants, mainly for the districts of Sense and See
  • Checking the completeness of applications, collecting dossiers and processing until the necessary authorisations are issued
  • Applying the legal and regulatory bases on public restaurants and other areas of activity of the Office
  • Cooperation with local authorities, cantonal offices and various partners involved in the procedures
  • Execution of various administrative tasks in the fields of activity of the Office and contribution to ordinary operations

Desired profile

  • Employee EFZ or equivalent training
  • Interest in the management and application of legal bases
  • Professional experience of several years
  • Sociability and service orientation
  • Excellent organizational skill, conscientiousness and sense of responsibility
  • Self-employment and teamwork
  • Mastering common computer science applications
  • Mastering the German language with very good oral and written knowledge of French
