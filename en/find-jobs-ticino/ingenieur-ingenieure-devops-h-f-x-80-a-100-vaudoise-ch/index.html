Original description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. True to its cooperative nature and values, it encourages the creation of a healthy, inclusive and inspiring work environment, ideal for developing your talent. Strongly committed to its digital transformation, Vaudoise Assurances wants to strengthen its Corporate Functions Solutions team and seeks a DevOps Engineer, between 80 and 100%. This position is located within the TD/SI Department. Through this role, you will integrate an agile team and gradually become a privileged interlocutor to support the various development teams (Dev, Ops, Product Owners and Business Analysts). The missions that await you:

  • Create and improve services within our internal development platform
  • Support development teams in the implementation of IC/CD pipelines and project infrastructure
  • Automate processes using a standardized DevOps toolchain
  • Support IT teams in adopting good practice
  • Provide the necessary support for continuous improvement and incident resolution
  • Putting in place the tools to track, track and report Your profile:
  • Higher education type HES, University, EPF, engineering or equivalent in computer science
  • 3 years of minimum experience in a role of DevOps Engineer, and excellent mastery of DevOps concepts and DevOps tools (Azure DevOps, Jenkins, Sonarqube, Artifactory, Bitbucket, Ansible)
  • Confirmed experience in continuous integration on IC/CD projects
  • Mastering Infra as Code and Terraform, Ansible and Groovy scripting concepts
  • Mastering Grafana-type monitoring tools
  • Languages: French (mother tongue), English (minimum B2) With strong technical leadership, you show proactivity and a strong team spirit. You are able to popularize technical concepts with non-technical interlocutors and demonstrate an excellent sense of organization, with a strong quality orientation. We offer you :
  • A healthy working environment in a mutual company labeled "Best Employer"
  • Multiple social benefits, including flexibility in work organisation
  • Working in a dynamic and passionate team
  • Human values focused on the well-being of employees We look forward to receiving your complete application package digitally (with curriculum vitae, cover letter, copies of work certificates and diplomas).
