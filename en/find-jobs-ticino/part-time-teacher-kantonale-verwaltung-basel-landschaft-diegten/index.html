Original description

Tasks

  • They teach in our two 6. Classes mainly music and French. Part of the teams work together cooperatively.
  • They maintain contact with parents and school-related institutions.
  • They support the students in the development of their skills and work closely with a dedicated teaching team.

Requirements

  • You have a BA degree for the primary level and have the teaching qualification for French, if possible also for music. They teach with pleasure and are a committed and team-oriented personality.
  • Inclusive work is a matter of course for you and you know how to support and promote the students to be accompanied in a targeted manner in cooperation with the teachers and specialists.

The district school TED is composed of the three school communities Tenniken, Eptingen and Diegten and is run at two locations: The lower level and a kindergarten are located in Tenniken, the middle level and a double kindergarten in Diegten. The school comprises 15 classes from kindergarten to 6th classes, which are taught by around 40 teachers and specialists. Around 245 students are currently attending classes. The district school TED is an integrative school and attaches importance to differentiation and a good cooperation, true to its motto "Zämmeachse - zämme wäch".
