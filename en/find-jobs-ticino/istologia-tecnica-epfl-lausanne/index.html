Role overview

Mission

Within the framework of the Faculty of Life Sciences at EPFL, various technology platforms offer high-quality technical support and training for researchers at EPFL and the Lémanique region.

The histology platform (MHCF, MultiOmics Histology Core Facility http:\\\hcf.epfl.ch ) is a centre of skills and training for basic techniques such as tissue cutting and staining, but also for immunofluorescence, in situ hybridization and new technologies in spatial biology.
