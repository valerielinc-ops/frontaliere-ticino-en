The HR Office implements strategic and operational tasks as a competence centre for the entire cantonal administration of Graubünden.

  • Develop strategies, standards and best practices for effective and inter-administrative cooperation

Main responsibilities

  • Develop strategies, standards and best practices for effective and inter-administrative cooperation
  • Advising and supporting administrative units on team and cooperation development issues
  • Support for the introduction and sustainable use of digital collaboration solutions in close cooperation with the Office of Informatics
  • Design, implementation and moderation of training, workshops, consultations and exchange formats on collaborative forms of work
  • Monitoring technological and cultural developments and deriving appropriate fields of action for administration

Company and context

  • In the area of organizational development, we accompany the administration within the framework of the digital administration of Graubünden in the introduction of modern forms of collaboration as well as in the further development of leadership, cooperation and organizational culture.
  • Participation in organizational development, change and transformation projects within the framework of the digital administration of Graubünden Requirements
  • Completed higher education or equivalent higher education in business administration, psychology, human resources, organizational development or a related field
  • Experience in monitoring cross-cutting projects and dealing with different stakeholders
  • Experience in applying and introducing digital collaboration solutions and understanding the connections between working methods, organization and technology
  • Experience in accompanying change processes as well as knowledge of team and group dynamics
  • Analytical and structured working method, strong communication and persuasion as well as high empathy for different perspectives
