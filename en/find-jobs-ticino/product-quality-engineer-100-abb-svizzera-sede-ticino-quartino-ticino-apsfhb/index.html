Main responsibilities

  • Your role and responsibilities:
  • We are looking for a Product Quality Engineer with a strong background in power electronics and proven experience in the design and validation of Uninterruptible Power Supplies (UPS).
  • The Product Quality Engineer plays a pivotal role in collaborating with the R&D department for product design and implementation. The work model for the role is: hybrid
  • This role is contributing to the Critical Power, Smart Power division in Quartino, Switzerland. You will be mainly accountable for :
  • Acting as a quality interface between Agile Teams (R&D & Product Management), Operations, and Suppliers to ensure product robustness before the release (G5).
  • Furthermore you will be analyzing test data, quality KPIs, and field return issues and providing critical feedback during the definition of product requirements, influencing design for quality and reliability.
  • Managing quality product validation, according to ABB PCP (Product Creation Process) or equivalent methodologies (e.g., APQP), including test planning, design, and execution.
  • Actively supporting the Design FMEA, Process FMEA, and PPAP activities in cross-functional teams.
  • Additionally, you will contribute to improving Design for Manufacturing (DFM) and Design for Testing (DFT), with a focus on PCBA production processes.
  • You will join a dynamic, talented and high-performing team, where you will be able to thrive.

Key requirements

  • for the role:
  • Master’s or Bachelor's Degree in Electronic Engineering.
  • Previous experience in product development as well as 5+ years of experience in the Power Electronics industry, preferably in the UPS sector.
  • Strong expertise in UPS architecture (single-phase and three-phase systems from 10 kW to 2 MW), including both hardware and software is preferred.
  • Hands-on experience with product validation plans, including definition, execution, and analysis of lab test results.
  • Solid knowledge of FMEA, PPAP, PCP/APQP quality tools and methodologies is desired.
  • Deep understanding of PCBA manufacturing processes, including the ability to influence design for testing and manufacturability.
  • Fluent or professional-level English (both spoken and written) with strong team spirit and ability to work under pressure in a fast-paced environment More about us
