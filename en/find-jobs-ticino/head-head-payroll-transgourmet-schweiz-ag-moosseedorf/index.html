Head / Head Payroll

Transgourmet Schweiz AG — Moosseedorf, Bern, Svizzera

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional consulting and innovative services.Transgourmet Schweiz AG belongs to the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss delivery and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Tasks

  • You bear overall responsibility for payroll, time management and social insurance for around 2,800 employees
  • You lead the payroll team with three employees professionally and personnel
  • You ensure timely and correct wage processing including monthly and annual accounts as well as account reconciliations
  • You are responsible for social security, withholding tax and daily allowances and accompanying audits
  • You advise executives and employees competently on wage- and social insurance-related issues
  • You will continuously develop the payroll processes and drive their digitization forward. In addition, you work in HR projects or take over their management
  • You are responsible for HR systems (SAP HCM) including master data management and ensure high data quality
  • You work closely with the entire HR as well as the areas of finance and controlling

Requirements

  • You have several years of experience in a comparable payroll function, ideally in a larger company
  • You have a commercial or business education with a further education in the field of payroll, social insurance or finance and accounting
  • You bring in-depth knowledge of SAP HCM (R/3 HR), very good Excel knowledge as well as a high IT affinity
  • You have experience in the optimization and digitalization of payroll processes
  • You communicate stylishly in German and have good knowledge of French
  • Italian is a plus
  • You work in a structured, precise and independent manner and are characterized by a high level of quality and responsibility.
  • You are a motivating leader with implementation strength and a pronounced customer and service orientation

Your contact person

Lisa Röthlisberger HR Marketing
