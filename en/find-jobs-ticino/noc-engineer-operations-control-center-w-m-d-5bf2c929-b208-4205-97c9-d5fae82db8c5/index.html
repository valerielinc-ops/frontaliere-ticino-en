Original description

Pen sum 80-100%

As an independent subsidiary of the Axpo Group, Axpo Systems AG is the Group's competence center for telecommunications, cyber security and data center solutions.

Our customers have a system-relevant order and are in the critical infrastructure segment. They only know the highest possible standard, because they operate the system-relevant services of Switzerland. This defines the requirement of Profession, reliability, quality and safety standards and the technical solution competence for us uncompromisingly high – because our performance only enables the crisis-proof execution of these indispensable services.

In our operations control center (OCC), we bundle NOC, SOC and Service Desk under one roof – the central hub for 24/7 operation of critical infrastructures – completely "Swiss-meat". As NOC engineer, you bring in expertise and your commitment to the successful implementation and implementation of Axpo Systems. We are looking for personalities with a flair for network and IT technologies that work motivated, whiskery and solution-oriented.

You bring curiosity, initiative and joy of learning, are communicative, interested and do not passively wait for specifications, but actively shape them.

Tasks and Responsibilities

  • Monitoring and operation of telecommunications, IT and OT systems
  • Incident Management: detection, classification, prioritization and tracking of malfunctions and events by creating tickets
  • Security Monitoring & Event Handling: monitoring and handling of security incidents in the Security Operations Center (SOC)
  • troubleshooting management and troubleshooting: identification, analysis, limitation and resolution of complex IT, network and security system malfunctions
  • coordination of field services and 2nd level and external service providers in case of troubleshooting

Profile and skills

  • Completed studies in computer science, network technology or cyber-security
  • Technical foundation in network and IT
  • Experience in handling monitoring and ticketing tools
  • Interest in security, monitoring, automation and AI-supported tools
  • Motivated, knowledge-hungry and independent
  • You are actively looking for solutions
  • Communicative and team-oriented – Enjoying exchange and cooperation
  • German and English
  • A plus
  • Ready for 24/7 shiftwork

What we offer

  • Monitoring the safety and functionality of critical infrastructure in Switzerland
  • Varied tasks with fulfilment
  • Dynamic team with charm
  • Part of a rapidly growing company with various development opportunities
  • Great Place to Work certified
