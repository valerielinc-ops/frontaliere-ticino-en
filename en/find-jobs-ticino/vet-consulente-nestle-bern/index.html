A place where you can do what you love. And love what you do.

As a Veterinary Advisor/Vet Advisor at Purina, your mission is to build a strong veterinary advocacy and recommendation for the Pro Plan Veterinary and Pro Plan Maintenance ranges. You make the difference by working closely with veterinary practices and local commercial and brand teams to translate scientific evidence into practice-relevant protocols, trainings and decisions. This is your chance to enrich the lives of pets, the people who love them and our common planet.

So you will make a difference as a veterinary advisor / in / Vet Advisor

  • Building a strong, trust-based cooperation with veterinarians. Each interaction is translated into clear next steps that speed up the recommendation of the Pro Plan ranges.
  • Translating veterinary science and clinical evidence into practical practice protocols and effective trainings that influence nutritional choices.
  • Profiling and prioritizing your field of practice to devote time and expertise where you can make the greatest impact on animal health and business success.
  • Develop practical success plans together with champions and key opinion leaders as well as coordination of relevant partners to accelerate introduction and loyalty.
  • Capture field service insights and customer needs in CRM and translate them into next-best actions as well as measurable results using dashboards.
