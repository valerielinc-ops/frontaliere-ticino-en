Original description

Tasks

  • You bring internal topics to the point and develop contributions for intranet, newsletters and other communication channels.
  • Townhalls, employee events and internal formats accompany you from preparation to post-processing
  • Presentations, briefings and documents for the management you prepare clearly, precisely and appropriately
  • Media releases, statements and editorial contributions are developed together with the PR team
  • In the newsroom you research relevant topics, observe the media landscape and keep media distributors up-to-date
  • For media inquiries, PR activities and projects, you work closely with various internal and external interfaces
  • You maintain our communication channels, put structure into coordination tasks and put your own ideas into practice

Requirements

  • You are currently completing a bachelor’s degree or have recently completed it, ideally in communication, media, languages, business or a related field of study
  • For the internship you are available for at least six months
  • Language is your strength: You write very well, stylishly and appropriately in German
  • You also bring good English skills
  • Knowledge of French is a plus
  • First experiences in communication, journalism, PR or editorial are advantageous, for example through an internship, a part-time job, a university medium or association work
  • With Word, PowerPoint and teams, you work safely
  • Knowledge of CMS, Adobe Creative Cloud, intranet platforms or AI tools is welcome
  • You work in a structured, reliable and independent way, deal discreetly with confidential information and bring curiosity and team spirit

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.
