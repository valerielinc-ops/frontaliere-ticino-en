Arxada is a global leader in innovative solutions to protect our world. Our groundbreaking technologies, extensive regulatory expertise and manufacturing and process development expertise help our customers efficiently protect nutrition, health and infrastructure through chemistry and biotechnology that promote sustainability. We offer a broad portfolio of ingredients and services for various end markets, including health & nutrition, household and personal care, professional hygiene, paints & coatings and wood protection. With customers in over 100 countries, the company achieved a turnover of CHF 2 billion in 2024. The headquarters of Arxada is located in Basel (Switzerland). Worldwide, the company employs 3,200 people at 24 production sites and 14 R&D centers, all committed to the success of our customers. To strengthen our Analytical Development Team at Arxada in Visp, Switzerland, we are looking for a laboratory assistant for our analytical development laboratory. Main tasks

  • Analytical support for chemical process development and process optimisation

  • Discussion of analysis orders and results with internal customers
  • Maintenance, maintenance and qualification of analytical equipment and support of software systems
  • Preparation of test instructions, validation reports and device SOPs Qualifications and experience
  • Completed training as a chemical laboratory technician with professional experience
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Practical experience and expertise in instrumental analysis (especially chromatography, titration, UV/Vis and IR spectroscopy)
  • Interest in analytical questions
  • Ability and willingness to guide employees and learners and share knowledge
  • Independent, responsible personality with good communication and team skills
  • Structured and safety-conscious working methods
  • Motivated, committed and a high degree of initiative Arxada offers world-class solutions in two clearly defined areas: Microbial Control Solutions (MCS) focuses on threats from microorganisms to humans and the environment.
