Original description

Aufgaben

  • Supports and advises Project Engineers on projects, on all technical belonging related to his specialization, and ensure that design will meet he customers' expectations
  • Develop and implement demanding automation projects in the field of plant control systems
  • Define the software concept based on the documentation and specifications
  • Project planning and controlling
  • Responsibility for budget, schedule, and quality
  • Coordination of the various project stakeholders (customers, sales, mechanical engineering, technology)
  • Participate in Project Reviews as support of the Project Manager/ Director if required
  • Actively participate in project meetings with cross - functional team members, suppliers, and/or customers
  • Program and test PLC software in accordance with standard guidelines
  • Carry out commissioning of plant control systems both domestically and abroad

Anforderungen

  • Graduate of Engineering or any related Degree
  • Minimum 6 years relevant experience or proof of sufficient skills
  • Experience in industrial process automation and in implementing PLC-based control systems is an advantage
  • Proven experience in designing and implementing automation solutions
  • Proficient in PLC and HMI systems (Siemens S7 / TIA Portal)
  • Skilled in technical documentation and specification writing
  • Professional experience in project management
  • Strong planning and organizational skills
  • Good verbal and written communication skills in English
  • Willingness to travel
