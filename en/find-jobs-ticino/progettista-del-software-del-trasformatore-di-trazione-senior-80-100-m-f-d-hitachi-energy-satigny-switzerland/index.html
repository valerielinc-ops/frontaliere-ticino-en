Role overview

The opportunity We are looking for a senior engineering professional with extensive experience in traction transformer design and engineering automation.

  • The opportunity We are looking for a senior engineering professional with extensive experience in traction transformer design and engineering automation.

Company and context

  • As part of the Traction Transformer Platform development team, you will play a key role in developing and maintaining the mechanical design tools that support our global engineering organization.
  • Collaborating with experts across multiple locations, you will drive the evolution of design automation solutions, support users worldwide, and contribute to engineering excellence through standardization, continuous improvement, and knowledge sharing.
  • This is a unique opportunity to make a lasting impact on a critical platform that directly influences the efficiency and quality of our transformer design processes.
  • How you'll make an impact Define, standardize, document, and continuously improve design rules for traction transformer design automation tools in collaboration with R&D, Engineering, Manufacturing, Quality, and Procurement teams.
  • Ensure design rules are robust, practical, and compliant with customer, internal, and international standards.
  • Coordinate the development and maintenance of automated design tools with global team members across multiple locations.
  • Develop and enhance CAD models and parametric design solutions using advanced design automation technologies.
  • Ensure seamless integration of design tools with Windchill PLM, Bill of Materials (BOM), and SAP-related data processes.
  • Train and support users and developers on design automation tools and best practices.
  • Monitor applicable internal and external standards and ensure ongoing compliance.
