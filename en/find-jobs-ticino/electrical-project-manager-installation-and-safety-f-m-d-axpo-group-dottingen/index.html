Original description

Pensum: 80% or more | Beznau

As Electrical Project Manager Installation and Safety, you support the Department of Electrical Engineering Maintenance and ensure a smooth, professional and safe execution of electrical installations and electrical engineering work. You take on a central role in the coordination of installation and maintenance work and support our business in all matters of electrical safety.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Coordination and support of maintenance work and new installations in the power plant sector
  • Professional and personnel management of installation teams as well as monitoring of work execution for quality, deadlines and costs
  • Control of electrical installations according to NIV including the creation of safety proofs
  • Advice and support on safety issues in low-voltage installations
  • Contact person for compliance with applicable standards, regulations and safety guidelines in the field of electrical installations
  • Adaptation of schemes, documents and documents

Profile and Requirements

  • Electrical installer/EFZ training completed
  • Further training as an electrical project manager/in installation and security with a federal certificate or equivalent training (e.g. electrical safety consultant after previous designation)
  • Professional experience in the control of electrical installations, ideally in the industrial environment
  • In-depth knowledge of industry standards and regulations (NIV, NIN, etc.) as well as experience in their practical application
  • Independent, reliable and solution-oriented working method
  • High sense of responsibility and a strong understanding of security
