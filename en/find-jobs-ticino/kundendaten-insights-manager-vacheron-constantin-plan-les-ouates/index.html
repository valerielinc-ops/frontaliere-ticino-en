CLIENT DATA & INSIGHTS MANAGER

CDI

Plan-les-Ouates

FOR THOSE WHO SEEK EXCELLENCE

Join one of the most respected Maisons in fine watchmaking where excellence is both a given and a never-ending quest. Play your part in driving our collective success.

YOUR ROLE

We are seeking a Client Data & Insights Manager who is responsible for driving the global client engagement strategy through rigorous data governance, disciplined performance monitoring and cross-functional collaboration.

This role ensures that client data is transformed into actionable insights to support business growth, client development, and CRM excellence across markets.

PLAYING YOUR PART

Client Data Management

  • You will track and analyze key KPIs to monitor performance across markets and support client engagement growth
  • You will translate business insights into data-driven action plans
  • You will support markets in implementing client development strategies based on insights
  • You will develop and maintain dashboards to ensure clear data performance visibility tailored for retail teams and top management
  • You will supervise client segmentation and scoring methodologies
  • You will ensure the highest standards of data governance and compliance
  • You will support retail teams in embedding a strong client data culture

Database Quality & Targeting

  • You will Ensure a healthy, compliant, and actionable client database at all times
  • You will monitor data quality, completeness, and enrichment across markets
  • You will drive continuous improvement in targeting accuracy
  • You will ensure relevant targeting strategies for campaigns, in close collration with the client team
  • You will support retail teams in embedding a strong client data culture

Client Performance Governance

  • You will manage and track requests through a centralized ticketing system
  • You will ensure proper prioritization and resolution of data, CRM, and reporting requests
  • You will standardize KPI frameworks and reporting methodologies
  • You will strengthen executive visibility on client performance globally

Group Interface & Cross-functional Collaboration

  • You will act as key contact for client data topics with the Group
  • You will ensure alignment with Group frameworks, tools, and reporting standards
  • You will collaborate closely with Regions, Marketing, Retail, Commercial, CS and key maison stakeholders to fuel business growth
  • You will promote best practice sharing across markets

WHAT WILL MAKE YOU SUCCESSFUL?

Success in this role will require

  • A solid experience in client analytics, CRM, or data-driven environments
  • An expertise in CRM ecosystems (Salesforce, a plus) and understanding of database management and data quality principles
  • Ability to operate in a complex, international matrix organization, with a high level of precision and business acumen
  • Ability to work cross-functionally and communicate complex insights effectively
  • Solution approach and client-centric mindset with attention to detail
  • Adaptability and agility, Collaborative and Team spirit
  • Fluency in English and French
