Role overview

We are seeking an experienced Database Administrator (DBA) with strong expertise in Oracle technologies and solid knowledge of other database platforms.

Key requirements

  • Qualifiche
  • 4+ years of experience as a Database Administrator, with strong focus on Oracle Database
  • Solid hands-on experience with Oracle Database administration in enterprise environments
  • Good knowledge of at least one additional database technology, such as PostgreSQL, SQL Server, MySQL, or MongoDB
  • Proven experience in database migration activities, including upgrades, platform changes, and cloud migration projects
  • Strong knowledge of backup and recovery strategies, business continuity, and disaster recovery concepts
  • Experience with performance tuning, SQL optimization, and troubleshooting of complex database issues
  • Good understanding of Linux/Unix environments and scripting for automation
  • Familiarity with database security, access control, and auditing principles
  • Ability to work effectively in project-based environments and coordinate with multiple stakeholders Strong communication skills in English

Application process

  • The role focuses on the administration, evolution, and migration of enterprise database environments, supporting critical applications and transformation initiatives across the organization. Your key tasks
  • Administer Oracle Database environments as the primary technology, including installation, configuration, patching, upgrades, and lifecycle management
  • Manage and support additional database platforms such as PostgreSQL, SQL Server, MySQL, MongoDB, or other NoSQL solutions
  • Execute and coordinate database migration projects, including cross-version upgrades, platform migrations, cloud migrations, and database consolidation initiatives
  • Plan and perform migrations from on-premise to cloud-based database services, including Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, AWS, or Azure
  • Support heterogeneous migration scenarios between different database technologies, ensuring data consistency, performance, and minimal service disruption
  • Implement and maintain high-availability and disaster recovery solutions, including Oracle Data Guard, RAC, and equivalent technologies for other platforms
  • Define and execute backup, restore, and recovery procedures across multiple database systems
