Original description

Organization and technology - both are yours? Then you are exactly right with us! To strengthen our team at the St. Moritz site, we are looking for a service coordinator with technical understanding. Tasks: • Acceptance, coordination and scheduling of service orders in building technology (HLKS) as well as ensuring timely and efficient order processing • Professional advice and customer support • Preparation and follow-up of offers and ensuring timely procurement of materials • Responsible for the correct and timely billing of services • Planning and organization of work preparation including provision of all necessary materials, tools and documentation Requirements • Completed basic training in building technology • Experience as a technical clerk or in an administrative activity of advantage • Strong customer orientation, IT skills and communication skills • High degree of independence, flexibility and entrepreneurial thinking • Valid ticket, impeccable reputation and very good knowledge of German and Italian As a leading full-service provider for holistic building and energy solutions in Switzerland, we are driving the transformation and digitalization of our customers and working together on the energy transition. In addition to building technology, building automation and facility management, our offer also includes special disciplines such as energy supply, transport technology, telecommunications and energy optimization. With around 6,000 employees, we are present at more than 100 locations throughout Switzerland. This allows us to realize regional projects as effectively as nationwide solutions. Every day we implement our promise: "real something move".
