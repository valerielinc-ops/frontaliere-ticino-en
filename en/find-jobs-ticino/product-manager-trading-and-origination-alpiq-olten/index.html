Original description

Mission

At Alpiq, we are looking for a Product Manager – Trading and Origination to join the New Product Implementation team. In this role, you will manage product life cycle activities with a strong focus on new product design, accurate deal representation, and implementation. You will ensure a structured and comprehensive product offering across Alpiq’s value chains through streamlined and effective end to end processes.

Your main responsibilities

  • Manage the full product life cycle, including product design, approval, and phase out processes for Trading and Origination products
  • Collaborate with key stakeholders such as Front Office and Risk on new product ideas, assessing product potential, costs, and risks
  • Secure efficient product approval, implementation, testing, and handover to the respective business lines
  • Foster strong collaboration within New Product Committee (NPC) stakeholders and ensure end to end implementation across systems and processes, identifying and addressing gaps where needed
  • Manage and continuously enhance the new product approval process
  • Initiate and manage product management related projects and initiatives
  • Review product management documentation and ensure the Alpiq Product Catalogue is accurate and up to date
  • Lead weekly product implementation calls with all stakeholders and coordinate communication with internal and external counterparts for product and service implementation

Your profile

  • Master’s degree in a relevant field
  • At least 3 years of experience in the energy industry within product management, Sales & Origination, Trading, or Risk, with direct exposure to complex energy transactions
  • Strong knowledge of structured energy products and origination activities, including emerging energy market solutions such as BESS, together with a strong understanding of standard energy products
  • Excellent stakeholder management capabilities and a self starting personality
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills
  • Strong analytical skills with a structured, organized approach and high attention to detail
  • Fluent in English
  • German, French, or Italian are an advantage
