Company and context

  • We are seeking a highly motivated and digitally fluent Quality Metrics & Digital Insights Specialist to join the PT Quality (PTQ) organization.
  • In this impactful role, you will partner across global sites and functions to design and deliver data-driven solutions, dashboards, and advanced analytics that power decision-making and promote a culture of continuous improvement across the Pharma Technical (PT) network.
  • Working at the intersection of Quality, Data Science, and Digital Innovation, you will be a key enabler of Roche’s digital quality transformation and a contributor to governance forums such as MRoQ.
  • You are responsible for developing and managing scalable, AI-enabled dashboards and performance scorecards using tools like Tableau and generative AI to deliver automated insights and proactive risk signals.
  • You are building on modern data platforms like Snowflake to create trusted, reusable data products that enhance traceability and drive consistency across the quality network.
  • You are harmonizing Key Quality Indicators (KQIs) in collaboration with PT sites and global functions to ensure consistency, data integrity, and comparability.
  • You are driving digital quality solutions and predictive analytics use cases in close partnership with QDE, IIx, and other technical teams.
  • You are applying Design Thinking and Human-Centered Design principles to advance user-centric, intuitive, and scalable digital analytics tools.
  • You are fostering cross-functional collaboration, leading data literacy initiatives, and coordinating the MRoQ network to align quality reporting and regulatory readiness. Who you are
  • You hold a Bachelor’s in Life Sciences, Engineering, Data Science, or a related field.
