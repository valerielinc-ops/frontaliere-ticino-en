Role overview

Build logistics from the beginning!

For our new location in Oberbuchsiten, we are looking for logistics specialists who independently understand, safely execute and take responsibility for processes in a food business.

In this way, you actively shape structures, standards and processes right from the start.

  • Build logistics from the beginning!
  • For our new location in Oberbuchsiten, we are looking for logistics specialists who independently understand, safely execute and take responsibility for processes in a food business.
  • You pick and palletize finished products according to the order and ensure a timely delivery.
  • You prepare freight forwarding orders, handle them cleanly and ensure correct documentation.

Main responsibilities

  • You pick and palletize finished products according to the order and ensure a timely delivery.
  • You prepare freight forwarding orders, handle them cleanly and ensure correct documentation.
  • You organize packing and packaging materials and ensure timely availability in daily business.
  • You process returns, register them in the lock warehouse and coordinate the further steps with the quality assurance.
  • You take over the collection and transfer of goods and materials with chauffeurs and ensure a smooth process.
  • You consistently comply with hygiene, quality and biosafety requirements and actively participate in the ongoing improvement of logistics processes at the new location.
  • You operate forklifts such as cross-seat and drawbar trucks safely in daily use. Requirements
  • You have experience in a warehousing, picking or production logistics environment, ideally in the food industry.
  • You understand logistical processes and their connections, from incoming goods to picking and packaging to transport and returns.
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): You have completed vocational training in the logistics or warehouse sector or have several years of practical experience in this environment.
