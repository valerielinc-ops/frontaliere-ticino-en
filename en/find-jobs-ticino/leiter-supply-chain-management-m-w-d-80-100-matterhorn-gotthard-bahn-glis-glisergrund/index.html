Original description

Your drive The heart of BVZ Holding is the railway company Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn and the international top brands Gornergrat Bahn and Glacier Express. Passionate and qualified employees form the basis of our success. Every day, around 700 employees are committed to offering our guests a first-class experience. We live diversity and have one thing in common: emotions are our drive. What is your drive? Your field of activity

  • You take over the strategic leadership and further development of the Supply Chain Management department with 17 employees and learners
  • You are responsible for the holistic management of the supply chain - from demand planning and procurement to scheduling and logistics to material provision, return and disposal.
  • You develop and are responsible for the implementation of the procurement and supplier strategies of the BVZ/MGB Group and control the supply chain based on relevant cost, inventory, availability, quality and performance indicators
  • You optimize material flows from supplier to customer and ensure high material availability with optimal stocks and costs
  • You work actively in the management team of the Rolling Stock & Traction division as well as in working groups and specialist committees within the railway industry

Your profile

  • You have several years of leadership experience in supply chain management functions as well as experience in supplier management and contracting
  • A higher business or technical training is ideally supplemented by further training in purchasing (e.g. purchasing manager), supply chain management or logistics.
  • You have a strong understanding of material, goods and information flows as well as a high affinity for digitalization, automation and data-based solutions
  • You are a winning person and bring a lot of initiative, implementation strength, a networked and entrepreneurial thinking
  • You think ahead, question existing things and implement changes

Your advantages

  • A key role in an experienced team
  • Flat hierarchies, short decision-making paths and constructive cooperation
  • Flexible working from home and on the road
  • GA-FVP and other attractive benefits for you and your family
