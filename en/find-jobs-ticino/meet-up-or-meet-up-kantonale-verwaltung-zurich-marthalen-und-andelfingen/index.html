Original description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Vocational Advice Center Breitenstein Meeting Director 10% Our youth meetings are looking for new meeting leaders! As one of a total of 15 child and youth welfare centres in the Canton of Zurich, the Breitenstein Centre ensures a uniform, regionally and municipally sound provision of services in the areas of child and adult protection, personal assistance, advice and prevention for the population of the Andelfingen district. The youth work of Zentrum Breitenstein leads three youth meetings in the district of Andelfingen with six meeting leaders and additional jumpers. The management of Jugendarbeit takes over the professional support. For our youth meetings in Marthalen and Andelfingen we are looking for a meeting leader, about 10%. What is your job with us? • You lead the youth meeting together with another meeting leader every Friday evening (except during school holidays and public holidays) • You will participate in exchange and networking meetings as well as specialist inputs • You are the contact person for the young people in the youth meeting and involve the young people in the meeting. • You are interested in the life world and topics of the young people • You already have initial experience in dealing with young people (youth work, pathi, sports club, etc.) • You are motivated to support young people in their leisure activities • You are at least 21 years old • You are reliable, independent and responsible Have we made you curious? Then we look forward to your call to Egzona Borter, Head of Youth Work, on 043 258 46 43 (Tuesday or Thursday) or directly to your online application. Pensum: 10%
