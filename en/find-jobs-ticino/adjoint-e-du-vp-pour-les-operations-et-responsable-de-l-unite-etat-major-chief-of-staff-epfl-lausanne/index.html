Original description

Context and Mission

The Vice-President for Operations manages all the activities that make campuses live on a daily basis — restoration, information systems, security, operation, library and scientific information — and plans to develop its infrastructure for the future.

To support his leadership, the Vice-President for Operations is looking for his/her future Deputy and Head of the State-Major Unit (Chief of Staff). A true strategic right arm and think tank partner, you play a key role as a facilitator and driver at the heart of a large area covering an annual budget of CHF 200 million and about 400 collaborators.

At the head of a transdisciplinary team of 5 people and at the interface of 4 main areas (i) Information systems, ii) Development and construction, iii) Infrastructure, security and operation and iv) Library and institutional archives), you lead the coordination of the cross-cutting activities of the Vice-President (strategic files, finance, project governance and data management, communication).

Main tasks and responsibilities

Your main responsibilities are

  • Leadership and leadership of a transdisciplinary team: Lead the State-Major unit in human, technical, financial and organisational terms.
  • Strategic Impulse: Actively contribute to the strategic development of the Vice-President and initiate, lead and lead strategic or cross-cutting organizational projects.
  • Reflection partnership: Acting as a senior advisor to the Vice-President and to policy makers and managers, providing a fine analysis, a capacity to constructively challenge policy and a systemic vision that informs decision-making.
  • Institutional alignment: Prepare, structure and monitor strategic issues in cross-cutting connection with the Presidency, other Vice-Presidents, Faculties and Adjacent Areas, while ensuring the consistency of narrative and internal communication.
  • Performance and impact measurement: Develop and coordinate the implementation of performance indicators (KPIs) to measure the effectiveness of service delivery and the satisfaction of campus users.

Profile

  • Training: Graduate university education (Master or equivalent) in Business Administration, Management or equivalent.
  • Experience: Strong proven experience (5 years minimum) in managing complex projects and piloting staff or management structures (Hermès, PMP or Prince certification or equivalent title is an asset). A first successful experience in team coaching is essential.
  • Behavioural skills:
  • Political intelligence and cross-cutting leadership: Proven ability to navigate with ease between multiple stakeholders, to federate and negotiate.
  • Agility and stress resistance: High priority, flexibility and excellent organizational capacity to lead challenging files under pressure.
  • Analytical spirit and orientation solutions: Rigor, proactivity, autonomy and strength of proposal to solve multidisciplinary problems.
  • Writing and linguistic skills: Outstanding writing skills. Perfect command of French and good command of English
  • Knowledge of German is a valuable asset.
  • Culture: A strong interest in the ecosystems of training, research, innovation and high technology.
