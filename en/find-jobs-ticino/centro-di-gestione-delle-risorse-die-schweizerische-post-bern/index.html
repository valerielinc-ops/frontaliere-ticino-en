Role overview

As part of Post Immobilien’s portfolio management, Asset Management is responsible for the strategic support and development of the approximately 600 pompous properties.

We ensure that existing value-added and development potentials are identified and developed and that Post’s interests as a landowner with ownership and market understanding are perceived.

For the Mitte region, we are looking for an experienced asset manager with a clear view for the forward-looking and sustainable development of real estate.
