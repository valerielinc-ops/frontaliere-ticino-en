Original description

Pensum 80-100 %

As an independent subsidiary of the Axpo Group, Axpo Systems AG is the competence center for telecommunications, cyber security and data center solutions in the Group.

Our customers have a system-relevant order and are in the segment of critical infrastructures. They only know the highest possible standards, because they operate the system-relevant services of Switzerland. This defines the demands on profession, reliability, quality and safety standards and the technical solution competence for us uncompromisingly high – because our performance only enables the crisis-proof execution of these indispensable services.

As a Contract Manager, you are responsible for legally correct contracts. In close cooperation with our lawyers and account managers, you negotiate contracts with our customers and suppliers in our favor and ensure the contract administration and billing.

Tasks and responsibilities

  • You make sure that all contract law aspects from tendering to contract signing are handled properly
  • You are the interface between our lawyers and the account managers and ensure high-quality contracts
  • You maintain and update our contract database of service contracts in order to keep an overview at all times and to trigger the account managers early on regarding contract renewals
  • You are responsible for billing recurring services
  • You support in the preparation, review and negotiation of contracts and service level agreements (SLA)
  • You identify risks in contracts at an early stage and develop appropriate risk reduction measures

Profile and skills

  • Bachelor's degree in business administration or comparable education
  • Good expertise in Swiss contract and bond law
  • Experience in handling contracts, ideally in the technical field (e.g. telecommunications or computer science)
  • Communication and negotiation skills
  • Knowledge of SLA management and service business is an advantage
  • Very good knowledge of German and English (verbal and written)
