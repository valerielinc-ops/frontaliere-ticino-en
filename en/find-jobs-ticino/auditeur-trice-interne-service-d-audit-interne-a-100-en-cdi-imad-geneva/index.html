Role overview

The internal audit department reports hierarchically to the Chairman of the IMAD Board of Directors and functionally to the Chairman of the audit committee.

The internal audit service operates in all areas of activity, covering operational departments, support functions as well as management and governance processes.

Under the supervision of the Director of Internal Audit, the internal auditor independently carries out audit, compliance, alert processing, review or other mandates entrusted by other control bodies in accordance with legal, regulatory requirements, professional standards and other institutional directives.
