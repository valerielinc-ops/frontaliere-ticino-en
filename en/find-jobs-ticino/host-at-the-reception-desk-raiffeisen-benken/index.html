Original description

Raiffeisenbank Benken is a medium-sized, regionally anchored cooperative bank with around 40 employees and locations in Benken, Gommiswald and Uznach. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed and service-oriented personality as a host. Tasks: • Reception of our customers, processing of service requests directly at the counter and ensuring a professional process in the customer zone • Support our customers in e-banking, TWINT, card issues and digital processes • Identification of customer needs as well as appointment agreement and mediation to suitable consultants • Processing of customer mutations such as address and name changes, powers of attorney, orders and general administrative tasks • Responsibility for cash management and coordination of the supply of cash devices as well as ATMs Requirements • Successfully completed banking training, an apprenticeship in the catering/hotel industry or retail trade with a willingness to learn new interesting topics of the banking world and to train accordingly • Delight in customer contact and enthusiasm through an open, service-oriented and winning way • Interest in financial services and digital solutions as well as motivation to familiarize yourself with new tasks • Self-initiative, reliability and sense of responsibility – combined with pleasure in working together in a team • A connection to the region and proximity to the local people round off the profile What do we offer you? • A dedicated, established team with a positive spirit • Attractive social benefits, including generous employer contributions to the pension fund and various employee benefits (e.g. free on birthday) • More than five weeks of holidays (100%) and the possibility to buy up to two additional weeks • Modern jobs • Early evening on Friday – from 2 pm possible • A varied, versatile field of activity • Diverse further development and co-creation possibilities We offer: Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness.
