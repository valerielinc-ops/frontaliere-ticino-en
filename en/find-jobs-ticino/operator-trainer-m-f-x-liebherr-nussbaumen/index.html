Original description

At Liebherr, innovation, quality and reliability are our top priorities – particularly in the earthmoving and mining sectors. Our machines set the standard, and thanks to a global service network, we offer fast, professional on-site support. Become part of the global Liebherr world and shape the future with us. Your Responsibilities: Field-Based Activities (approx. 70%)

  • Planning, organizing, and delivering customer and operator training sessions for Liebherr machines on-site.
  • Promoting safety standards and best-practice working methods in accordance with company and customer requirements.
  • Advising machine operators, production managers, and operations managers on the optimal use of machines, tools, and work processes.
  • Conducting competency assessments and recommending appropriate training and development measures.
  • Supporting customers in improving operational processes, maintenance practices, and machine efficiency. Remote / Office-Based Activities (approx. 30%)
  • Providing technical advice and support to dealers and end customers regarding machine operation and application.
  • Preparing and maintaining training materials, reports, evaluations, and related documentation.
  • Coordinating and exchanging information with internal departments and manufacturing plants.
  • Supporting dealer assessments, quality evaluations, and Customer Service Management projects.
  • Performing administrative tasks and working remotely from a home office in accordance with company policies. Your Profile:
  • Completed technical education in mechanics, hydraulics, electromechanics, or a comparable field.
  • Several years of hands-on experience operating earthmoving, mining, or other heavy-duty equipment.
  • Solid technical knowledge of hydraulic, mechanical, and electrical systems.
  • Excellent communication and training skills, combined with a professional approach when interacting with customers and dealers.
  • Good command of English, both written and spoken; additional language skills are an advantage.
  • High willingness to travel, flexibility, and the ability to work independently with a strong sense of responsibility.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications. Willingness to continuously develop skills and adapt to new technologies and processes. Our Offer: At LIEBHERR, you can expect an independent role in a full-time position, offering a varied and exciting challenge within an internationally successful group of companies, along with attractive employee benefits and social benefits. Have we awoken your interest? Then we look forward to receiving your online application. If you have any questions, please contact Sonja De Ceglia. Please note that we only accept applications for this position through our online application system. Applications submitted via email will not be considered. One Passion. Many Opportunities.
