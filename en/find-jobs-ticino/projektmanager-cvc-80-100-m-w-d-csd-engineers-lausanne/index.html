Original description

More than 1,000 employees at 30 locations in Switzerland and Europe: the CSD Group is a dynamic and growing company. Thanks to the ingenuity, competence and multidisciplinarity that characterize our teams, CSD offers innovative and sustainable solutions for our customers with a "plus" for quality of life and the environment. We are looking for our Construction Techniques Branch in Grand-Lancy (GE) or Lausanne (VD) for a HVAC 80-100% (m/f/d) project manager. Your tasks

  • Ensure the study, design, dimensioning, implementation and monitoring of projects in the field of HVAC installations Heating Ventilation Air conditioning
  • Ensure calculations and hydraulic design of installations
  • Prepare estimates, preliminary draft document and comparison of variants
  • Develop plans and outline principles at all stages of a project
  • Participate and monitor spatial coordination of HVAC facilities
  • Ensure pre-metering and bidding
  • Supervising construction sites and managing their costs
  • Ensuring sound financial management of mandates

Your profile

  • HES/ES Engineer in Building Techniques (CVC), or equivalent
  • 10 years of professional experience in the field, including at least experience in the Swiss study office and experience in multidisciplinary project management
  • Significant experience in the industrial field (STEP, Factory, clean rooms, watchmaking)
  • Increased knowledge of Swiss technical standards, requirements and guidelines
  • Knowledge of CVC techniques in the industrial environment required, concepts in other building techniques CVSE, RCM, building physics and fire protection AEAI would be an asset
  • Good control of DAO software and common tools (Suite Office)
  • Ability to prepare bids using Plancal Snap software an asset
  • Ability to work in teams and networks

We offer you

  • A job in exciting projects
  • A network of professionals from different fields of engineering
  • Multiple opportunities for professional and personal development
  • A dynamic working environment with mutual respect
  • Attractive conditions allowing great flexibility in the organization of working time

Offers of employment agencies are not taken into account in this recruitment. Consequently, CSD will not enter into any negotiation for the payment of any claim.
