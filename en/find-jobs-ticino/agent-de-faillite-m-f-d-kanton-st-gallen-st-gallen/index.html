Role overview

With its four offices and five services and its approximately 200 employees, the Department of Health provides for the protection, promotion and preservation of human and animal health.

This includes ensuring high-quality basic medical care thanks to optimal hospital planning in the field of acute somatics and psychiatry, health prevention and consumer protection and veterinary services.

The Cantonal Medical Office advises the departmental management, the Department of Health and other departments on health and disease issues.
