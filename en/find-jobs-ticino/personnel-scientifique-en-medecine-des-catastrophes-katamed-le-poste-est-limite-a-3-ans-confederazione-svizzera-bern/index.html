Original description

Build the medical staff within the National Alarm Centre NAZ. Helping to establish a strategic patient distribution that allows political, operational and technical assignment, transport and delivery of a very large number of patients over a longer period of time.

  • Elaborate the processes of leadership in the National Association of Catastrophic Medicine KATAMED between the Confederation, cantons, third parties and international bodies.
  • Plan and conduct exercises with a focus on leadership, emergency and disaster management.
  • Work on strategic-political issues in the realignment of KATAMED and in the protection of the population in war.
  • Ensure controlling and reporting for the Faculty of Disaster Medicine KATAMED.
  • Completed studies at master level or equivalent education with several years of functionally relevant experience.
  • Training in the staff work of larger organizations / formations and / or degree in emergency and crisis management or equivalent training with several years of functionally relevant experience.
  • Proven experience with leadership responsibility in the staff work of larger organizations. Very good knowledge of health and security policy structures and instruments in Switzerland and many years of experience in cooperation with civil protection partners (police, fire brigade, military, civil protection and health care). Polyvalent, pragmatic, resilient, socially competent and willing to perform. Active knowledge of two official languages, passive knowledge of a third official language and English.
