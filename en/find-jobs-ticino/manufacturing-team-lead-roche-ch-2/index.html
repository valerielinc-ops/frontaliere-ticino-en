The Position This role is a valuable part of our Manufacturing and Incoming Quality Control (IQC) team, which is committed to ensuring high quality, safety, and delivery standards for our products . As a member of this team, you will have the opportunity to collaborate closely with key stakeholders in Manufacturing, Technical Supplier Management, Supplier Quality, Procurement, Warehouse, Engineering, and Metrology . You will lead production and IQC teams, drive continuous improvement, and ensure strict compliance with Good Manufacturing Practices and Quality System Regulations .

The Opportunity As the Supervisor of Manufacturing & Incoming Quality Control, your primary function is to lead 1st-shift manufacturing and IQC operations to ensure the timely inspection, release, and compliance of incoming materials and products while driving operational excellence .

  • Team Leadership & Operations: Lead, plan, and schedule 1st-shift manufacturing and Incoming Quality Control (IQC) activities, effectively allocating personnel and equipment to meet departmental delivery, safety, and productivity goals .
  • Quality & Material Release: Oversee the timely inspection and release of incoming materials, managing Material Review Board (MRB) Hold and IQC Hold areas to prevent nonconforming materials from impacting production schedules .
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with Technical Supplier Management, Supplier Quality, Procurement, Warehouse, Engineering, and Metrology to resolve quality, component, and technical issues .
  • Regulatory Compliance & Audits: Ensure full compliance with Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) and Quality System Regulations (QSR), maintain team training compliance, and represent Manufacturing and IQC during internal and external audits .
  • Continuous Improvement & Lean Execution: Drive Lean manufacturing initiatives (e.g., standard work, 5S, Kaizen, root-cause analysis) to reduce process variation, optimize inspection cycle times, and eliminate waste .
  • Metrics & Performance Monitoring: Maintain and analyze key operational metrics—such as Right First Time performance, material release times, CAPA/nonconformance resolutions, and scrap rates—to drive actionable improvements .
  • People Development: Recruit, train, coach, and mentor direct reports, applying servant-leadership principles to build a high-performing, adaptable team .
