More about this search for cross-border applicants

Roche is one of the largest private employers in Basel-Stadt, with headquarters in Basel and additional research operations in nearby Kaiseraugst and Schlieren. The company hires across pharmaceutical research, diagnostics, clinical development, biostatistics, regulatory affairs, manufacturing, and corporate functions such as finance, legal, and IT. Vacancies are typically posted through the Roche careers portal and aggregated on external boards. Candidates from neighbouring regions, including cross-border commuters, should note that employment contracts are governed by Swiss labour law and cantonal permits apply. Basel is reachable from the Italian border in roughly two hours by train, making daily commuting feasible for some roles, particularly those located at the main campus or the Roche Tower in Basel.
