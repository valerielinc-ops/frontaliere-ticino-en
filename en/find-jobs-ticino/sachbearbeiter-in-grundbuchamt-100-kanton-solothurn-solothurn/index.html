Role overview

The Administrative Court, as the organizational department of the Higher Court, is the last cantonal authority to assess a wide variety of disputes in administrative matters. For the administrative court we are looking for a court clerk, 60-100%, at the Solothurn location as of 01.01.2027 or by agreement.

  • Their main task is to process written proceedings and draft judgments for the judges.
  • At oral hearings, you keep the minutes, participate in the judgment consultation with an advisory voice and process it into a reasoned judgment.
  • You will get an in-depth and exciting insight into the judicial activities of a second instance.
  • In a young and motivated team, you shape the case law. We offer you a flexible work design and time allocation.
  • You have completed legal training at a Swiss university and experience in administrative law, preferably in construction and planning law and/or in the court sector.
