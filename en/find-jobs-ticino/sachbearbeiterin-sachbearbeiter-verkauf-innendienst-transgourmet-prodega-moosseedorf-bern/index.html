Personnel / Personnel Sales Internal Service

Transgourmet Schweiz AG — Moosseedorf, Bern, Svizzera

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional consulting and innovative services.Transgourmet Schweiz AG belongs to the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss supply and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Tasks

  • You support our customers as well as the sales and key account management team in administrative and procedural tasks
  • You process customer inquiries and take over the hub function between customer, sales and internal specialist offices
  • You create relevant statistics, sales evaluations, key figures and analyses
  • You coordinate internal customer activities
  • You process price requests and create price overviews
  • You contribute to the implementation of internal projects
  • You keep statistics and are responsible for general correspondence

Requirements

  • You have a degree in the commercial sector or comparable
  • You are well-versed in MS-Office applications, especially Excel
  • You have a flair for numbers
  • In addition to your fluent knowledge of German, you will speak French and/or Italian
  • You have a quick apprehension, recognize connections and have a networked mindset
  • You are an independent and reliable personality
  • Ideally, you already have industry experience in the gastronomy, hotel industry or retail trade
