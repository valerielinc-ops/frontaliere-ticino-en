Role overview

CordenPharma is a leading provider for the development and manufacture of pharmaceutical active ingredients (APIs), adjuvants, finished medicines and packaging activities with more than 2,600 employees worldwide.

With our services, we support pharmaceutical and biotech companies in the production of medicines with the primary goal of improving people’s lives.

Our network in Europe and the USA offers flexible and specialized solutions on five technology platforms: Peptides, Lipids & Carbohydrates, Injectable, Highly Potent & Oncology; and Small Molecules.
