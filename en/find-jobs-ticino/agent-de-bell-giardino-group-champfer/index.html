Original description

Giardino Group is looking for a Bell Attendant for the Giardino Mountain in Champfèr. Giardino Hotels is a characterful Swiss luxury hotel group with locations in St. Moritz, Ascona and Locarno.

Tasks

You are jointly responsible for a smooth arrival and departure of our guests and a positive first and last impression. Your day-to-day work includes the luggage service, the hotel transfers with the shuttle, the parking of guest cars and various messengers in the hotel as well as outside. Furthermore, you are jointly responsible for the cleanliness of the right of way, the garage and the lobby and perform your tasks even in stressful situations at the highest level.

Requirements

  • Many years of experience in a comparable position in the luxury hotel industry
  • Mandatory driving licence category B, advantageous: driving licence category D1
  • Good language skills in German and English, Italian and any other foreign language is an advantage
  • A warm and competent guest care
  • A well-groomed appearance and safe appearance
  • A sense of duty, resilience, flexibility and enthusiasm
  • Dealing respectfully with colleagues and superiors
