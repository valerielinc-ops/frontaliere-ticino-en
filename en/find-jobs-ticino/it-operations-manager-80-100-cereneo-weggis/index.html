Tasks

In this versatile function, you combine operational IT operations with project coordination and compliance issues in a dynamic health environment. You report directly to the management and gradually assume more leadership and management responsibility. IT operation & service provision

  • Leadership and coordination of the clinic support team as well as ensuring daily IT operations
  • Support for workplace IT, user onboarding and lifecycle management
  • Cooperation with external IT service providers and infrastructure partners as well as continuous improvement of processes and service quality Projects & further development
  • Management and coordination of operational IT projects as well as implementation and optimization of clinical and administrative systems
  • Coordination of implementations with departments and internal stakeholders
  • Participation in building sustainable standards, governance and support structures Data Protection & Compliance
  • Operational coordination of data protection and compliance activities
  • Cooperation with external data protection and compliance specialists The function does not assume the formal role of the Data Protection Officer.

Requirements

  • Experience in IT operations, service management or infrastructure coordination
  • Good technical understanding of workplace IT and operational support
  • Experience in project coordination and process optimisation
  • Structured, independent and responsible functioning
  • Strong communication and coordination capabilities
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Studies in computer science, business informatics, medical informatics or a related field
  • Experience in healthcare or in a regulated environment
  • Familiarity with Microsoft 365, endpoint management, identity management or the operation of IT infrastructures
  • Experience with GDPR or operational compliance processes
  • Interest in healthcare technology, digital transformation and AI

  • MOTIVATION: Joint goal achievement and support of patients: women to improve their quality of life

  • INNOVATION: Research and testing of new therapeutic approaches and devices If you are an open, committed person who would like to work in an interdisciplinary team and continuously develop, we look forward to your application.
