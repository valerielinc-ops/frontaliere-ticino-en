Head External Service Decoration

Coop Genossenschaft — Vufflens-la-Ville, Vaud, Svizzera

Mission

  • Plan and organize weekly tours related to the installation of decorations and POS at the outlets
  • Coordinate and monitor the various mandates and decoration work
  • Monitoring of VDP openings, transformations or maintenance
  • Work closely with the various managers to ensure effective project implementation
  • Ensure the consistent application of Corporate Branding throughout all outlets
  • Support, motivate and support a team of 6 employees
  • Processing and tracking invoices related to these tasks
  • Replace the decorator and colleagues during their absences
  • To be a force for proposal in the development of new concepts and in the continuous improvement of processes
  • Timely support the shipping sector and preparation of workshop decoration materials

Qualifications

  • Polydesigner 3D CFC or equivalent formation
  • Experience confirmed several years in decoration, visual merchandising or similar function
  • Good experience in planning tours and organizing field interventions
  • Significant experience in team management
  • Excellent manual skills for mounting, fixing and installation on site
  • Sense of detail, aesthetics and careful work
  • Organized, autonomous, reliable and team-minded
  • Ability to manage multiple projects in parallel while meeting deadlines
  • Good mastery of MS Office tools, including Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook
  • Essential driving licence
  • German as an asset
  • Discounts and discounts: up to 20% discount in all Coop Group formats
  • Conditions of engagement: attractive conditions of engagement and guaranteed equal pay
  • Working time: 5 weeks of vacation
  • Continuing training: support for external training and refresher courses
  • Sustainable development: A commitment to man and animals and nature with more than 400 acts
  • Communication: internal app dedicated to staff
  • Health: sports activities: volleyball tournaments and hikes
