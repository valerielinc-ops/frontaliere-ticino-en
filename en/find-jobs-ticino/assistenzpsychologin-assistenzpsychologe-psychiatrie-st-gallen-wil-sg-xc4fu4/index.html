Main responsibilities

  • In our interdisciplinary treatment team, you look after patients in the daily clinical setting.
  • After in-depth training, you take over the independent treatment of patients in individual and group therapies and thus carry the treatment concept with you.
  • As an assistant psychologist or assistant psychologist, you will conduct individual and multi-person interviews, plan the therapy process and carry out psychological evaluations.
  • In your varied day-to-day work, you will encounter the entire spectrum of psychiatric diseases and work closely with colleagues from other specialist areas as well as with assignment offices to ensure the best possible care for patients.
  • With the support of our service-oriented secretariat, you will complete the case-related documentation and record services in the hospital information system.
  • At our location in Rapperswil, you will actively participate in reports, case discussions and training courses and represent the psychiatry St.Gallen professionally together with the team. Requirements
  • You have completed a psychology degree and have experience in adult psychiatry.
  • Ideally, your further education in a federally recognized psychotherapeutic procedure with a cognitive-behavioral-therapeutic orientation is already advanced.
  • Advantageously, you have a psychotherapy assistant permit from the Canton of St.Gallen (permission to work under supervision) or meet the necessary requirements to obtain it before starting your job.
  • Your interest is in day-to-day clinical, interdisciplinary treatment, which is oriented towards the needs of patients and their environment.

Key requirements

  • You have completed a psychology degree and have experience in adult psychiatry.
  • Ideally, your further education in a federally recognized psychotherapeutic procedure with a cognitive-behavioral-therapeutic orientation is already advanced.
  • Advantageously, you have a psychotherapy assistant permit from the Canton of St.Gallen (permission to work under supervision) or meet the necessary requirements to obtain it before starting your job.
  • Your interest is in day-to-day clinical, interdisciplinary treatment, which is oriented towards the needs of patients and their environment.
  • When dealing with mentally ill people, you convince through commitment, commitment and communication skills.
  • You enjoy working independently, are open-minded and appreciate working together in a committed team.
  • With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatments and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations.
