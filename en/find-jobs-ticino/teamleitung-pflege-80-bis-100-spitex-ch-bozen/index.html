Original description

Spitex Fricktal AG is an open-minded, modern and innovative non-profit Spitex. With around 100 employees and 20 apprentices, we ensure every day that people in the Fricktal can live at home in a self-determined and safe way. For more than 600 customers, we provide around 140,000 assignments every year – from somatic care and psychiatric services to specialized palliative care. We are in an exciting development phase and invest in people, processes and digitalization. For this we are looking for personalities who want to take responsibility, think and shape the future together with us. In order to strengthen our team in Bözen, we are looking for a committed personality who will take responsibility and not only ensure the core business of the nursing staff, but also develop it further. Team management Care 80 to 100% You bring that with you

  • At least professional health EFZ or equivalent training

  • You live a positive feedback culture in leadership, have the courage to address conflicts; A good team culture is important to you
  • Several years of professional experience in health care, preferably outpatient (Spitex)
  • Experience in accompanying teams and employees in change processes
  • Knowledge of moderation, coaching methods and employee development
  • Good computer science user knowledge, Perigon knowledge is an advantage
