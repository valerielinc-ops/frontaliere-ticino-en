Role overview

In order to strengthen its team of the Mobile Community Psychiatry Device (DMPC), the Nant Foundation is putting to the competition a position of 100% CDI nurse THE MISSION The mission of the DMPC nurses is to intervene, if indicated, with persons with psychological difficulties who encounter obstacles to access to the usual care and support structures.

Their intervention consists of conducting a specialized clinical evaluation and providing appropriate support in the person's living environment.

The population covered is between 18 and 65 years of age.
