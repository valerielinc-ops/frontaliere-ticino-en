Description

Become part of an experienced team that together designs and operates a modern, scalable database and infrastructure platform. With us you take responsibility, design architectural decisions and bring your own ideas.

  • You are responsible for the stable operation, engineering and further development of our Oracle and PostgreSQL database platform in both Daily Business and Projects and in the new ones.
  • You are working on the further development of our database and data warehouse platforms, on the migration of Oracle to PostgreSQL and on the expansion of our database platforms in the Azure Cloud.
  • You are supporting the team in automation, performance analysis, scripting, monitoring and logging.
  • Infrastructure as Code is an integral part of our work: You are actively developing our automation and infrastructure standards. Together we are driving forward topics such as declarative infrastructure, versioned Changes and reproducible deployments – with tools such as Ansible, Terraform and CI/CD Pipelines.
  • You are supporting the further development of our Data Warehouse with GoldenGate and DataHub and helping to automate manual processes.
