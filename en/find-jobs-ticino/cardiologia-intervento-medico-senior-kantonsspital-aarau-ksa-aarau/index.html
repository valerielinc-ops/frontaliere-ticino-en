Role overview

Tasks

  • They support our team in a modern cardiology that covers the entire spectrum of invasive and non-invasive cardiology at the highest level (coronarographies, Mitraclips, TAVI, PFO/ASD/LAA closures).
  • They independently perform interventional interventions on the coronaries using the latest techniques.
  • Together with the cadre doctors, you are responsible for the supervision and further education of our assistant doctors.
  • They perform leadership tasks and actively develop cardiology, especially the field of interventional cardiology.
  • They are part of the cardiology background team and actively participate in colloquia, rapports and visits.
  • The brisk maintenance of contact with the assigning doctors both internally and externally is part of your daily tasks.

Requirements

  • As a committed, communicative personality, you attach importance to a collegial, professional and constructive cooperation.
  • You bring a Swiss medical diploma or an equivalent education with recognition MEBEKO and already have a specialist title FMH Cardiology.
  • Furthermore, you have in-depth clinical experience with a focus on interventional cardiology (coronary interventions and, if necessary, structural interventions).
  • In addition to professional competence, we expect a high level of social competence, which is a prerequisite for the successful exercise of this interesting and versatile position.
