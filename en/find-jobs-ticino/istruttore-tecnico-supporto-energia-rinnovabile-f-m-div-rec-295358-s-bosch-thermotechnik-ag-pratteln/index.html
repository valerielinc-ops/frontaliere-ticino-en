Your tasks

You will provide technical support for Buderus and Bosch wholesalers as well as group leaders and end customers.

You ensure the deputy of technical instructors in the fields of fossil and renewable energies.

  • Training: Basic technical training in the field of heating, building or energy technology or a comparable training - personality and working method: Self-employed, self-initiated, responsible, structured, solution-oriented, analytical, strong in communication and sovereign in appearance.
  • Joy of knowledge transfer and presentation as well as readiness for travel activities and continuous further education - experience: several years of professional experience in heat pump and heating technology, in-depth knowledge in heat pumps, hydraulics, control and plant technology as well as experience in carrying out technical training.
