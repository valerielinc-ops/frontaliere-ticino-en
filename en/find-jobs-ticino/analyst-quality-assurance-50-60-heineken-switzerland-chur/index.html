Original description

Respect, enjoyment and quality based on passion are among our core values. As a beer producer with brewery locations in Chur and Lucerne as well as our own beverage trade with 19 locations throughout Switzerland, we are a company of national importance. We focus on a mix of tradition, innovation and customer proximity. With our brand beers, we contribute to the diverse Swiss beer culture. For our Quality Assurance department at the production site in Chur we are looking for approx. 6 months an independent and experienced person as an analyst quality assurance 50% - 60% You are part of a small, experienced team and report directly to the head of quality assurance. To your • Essential tasks include: Analyses: Perform chemical-technical, microbiological and sensory analyses according to the specifications of the test plan and in consultation with the production to check compliance with the established specifications of Heineken. Documentation of the generated data in the data acquisition system Continuous improvement management: cooperation in the daily control and improvement system (DCS), implementation of cause analyses, cooperation and management of improvement teams, cooperation in the development and creation of corresponding documentation Maintenance: maintenance and calibration of the measuring devices in the laboratory and in production as well as documentation of the data in the corresponding systems General administrative • Tasks within quality assurance • Requirements: Completed training as a laboratory technologist or similar and corresponding experience in the field of laboratory/quality assurance Experience in chemical-technical analysis and ideally in microbiology Experience in a food business or in the field of chemistry/biology Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English Flexibility in the design of work assignments Interested? Then Claudia Tschuor Magnin looks forward to your complete application documents.
