Role overview

Senior Physician (80–100%) | Specialist in Psychiatry and Psychotherapy Attentive and competent.

We are a private clinic for psychosomatics, psychiatry and psychotherapy with 108 beds in a central location in Rheinfelden and two outpatient clinics in Rheinfelden and Aarau.

The clinic is recognised by the FMH as a further training centre for the specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy (2 years inpatient, 2 years outpatient), as well as by the SAPPM (2 years).
