More about this search for cross-border applicants

Lugano, the largest city in the Swiss canton of Ticino, is a thriving hub for healthcare professionals. With its picturesque lakeside setting and proximity to Italy, Lugano attracts cross-border workers (frontalieri) seeking opportunities in clinics and hospitals. The city's healthcare sector is diverse, offering roles in public and private clinics, with a strong demand for specialists in various medical fields. Whether you're a nurse, doctor, or allied health professional, Lugano presents a dynamic job market with competitive salaries and a high quality of life.
