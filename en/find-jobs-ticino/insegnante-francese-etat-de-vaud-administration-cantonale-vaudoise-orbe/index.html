Role overview

In your capacity as a French teacher at the Etablissements de la plaine de l'Orbe (EPO), you provide individual, collective and adapted teaching that allows inmates to acquire a good level of French.

You assess the level of start-up and progress of people in training by valuing their participation, their involvement and the quality of their work.

You organise your teaching by ensuring the differentiation of teaching practices in order to make the training accessible to all learners, regardless of their abilities or background.
