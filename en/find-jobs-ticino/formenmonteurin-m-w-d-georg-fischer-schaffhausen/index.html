Role overview

As a leading supplier of plastic and metal piping systems for industry, supply and home technology, we offer high-quality and innovative products as well as application-oriented solutions. We have been setting standards for more than 200 years and are now represented at more than 30 locations worldwide.

As we continue to grow steadily and the demands in the Micro electronics (ME) and Datacenter segments are increasing exorbitantly, we are looking for highly motivated and well-qualified specialists to expand our team accordingly. In this technologically demanding position, you are responsible for the conversion and impeccable condition of the molds needed for the production of the products. In this way, they ensure that production can always produce the products in the quality desired by the customer using complex tools.

  • Ensuring processes and their clean documentation
