Main responsibilities

  • You choose a profession with a future in an extremely varied industry.
  • Meat specialists/meat specialists receive basic training from us.
  • It deals with the basic competencies such as boning, cutting and dressing cuts of meat, production of food ready for kitchen and pan food and compliance with hygiene, safety and sustainability requirements.
  • In this profession, you choose a focus that suits you best:
  • In the "meat harvesting" you will learn everything about animal husbandry and the professional slaughter, cutting and boning.
  • When focusing on harvesting with "specialization poultry" you will learn the meat harvesting and processing of poultry.
  • You process, package and declare the products for the end user.
  • Ensuring sustainability and quality and hygiene requirements are also important learning objectives.
  • The focus of "commercial meat processing" is the production of meat and other raw materials for high-quality food.
  • In the "meat refinement" your creativity is required.
