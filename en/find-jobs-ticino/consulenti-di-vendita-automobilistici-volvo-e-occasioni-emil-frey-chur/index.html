Original description

What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. Emil Frey AG Chur is the official sales and service partner of 7 global brands at three different locations. To strengthen our team in Chur, we are looking for a customer-oriented and initiative person for the Volvo brand and Occasions of all brands, either immediately or by agreement. Their tasks

  • Success-oriented and active sale of new and occupation vehicles
  • Advising on the full range of Volvo brand models
  • Acquisition of new customers and maintenance of existing customers
  • Preparation of vehicle offers, purchase contracts and vehicle dossiers
  • Preparation of leasing and insurance quotes
  • Presentations of New Cars and Occasions, Labeling of Vehicles Your Profile

  • They love to advise and have sales flair

  • You work independently, are motivated and open to people what we offer you You will find a new challenge with us with exciting tasks and much more:
  • Numerous discounts (new and used cars, service work and spare parts, vehicle and health insurance, fuel cards, hotels and much more)

  • Individual training and development opportunities
