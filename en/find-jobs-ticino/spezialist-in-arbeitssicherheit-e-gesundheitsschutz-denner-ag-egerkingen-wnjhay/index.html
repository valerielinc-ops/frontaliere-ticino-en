Original description

Behind the scenes ensure that colleagues can safely carry out their jobs in the shops and distribution centres? Then come with your talent, because that is in demand in our winning team. We have quick decisions and appreciation. Denner. This is our shop!

Mansioni

  • Design, control and anchoring of occupational safety & health protection at Denner
  • Regular visits and security tours in the branches and distribution centres
  • Maintenance of AS/GS documentation
  • Support for coordination and implementation of training and instruction
  • Planning and controlling of prevention measures
  • Advise and support the company management and line in questions about AS/GS

Requirements

  • 2-3 years of professional experience: in a comparable function
  • Higher technical school: e.g. specialist for occupational safety and health protection (ASGS) with federal law. Specialist card
  • An independent, organized and responsible personality
  • A pragmatic way of thinking that takes our occupational safety and health protection to the next level
  • An attitude that promotes willingness to change and cohesion in the team
  • Good user experience MS Office
  • German (flowing)
  • French (very good knowledge)

  • Part of the Migros Group: Strong brand anchored in the retail trade under the Migros Group umbrella
