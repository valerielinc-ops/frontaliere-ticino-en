Original description

Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Stephanshorn | St. Gallen

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 73435

Become part of our dedicated team!

Do you love to make people feel good? You enjoy contact with patients, think service-oriented and enjoy working in a team? Then you fit us perfectly. You work in a modern and appreciative environment that combines humanity and quality. Together we pull together – with enthusiasm and commitment.

YOUR TASKS

  • You take care of our patients actively, emphatically and warmly – and are the first point of contact for non-medical wishes.
  • You ensure a smooth food order up to the service incl. Drink service – with a smile and a lot of feeling.
  • You coordinate food requests between medicine, nutritional advice and kitchen.
  • Order in the patient’s room is important to you – you create an atmosphere to feel good.

Your profile

  • You have experience in the hotel industry, gastronomy or healthcare.
  • Training as a hotel specialist or restaurant specialist is a plus.
  • You communicate clearly, friendly and fluently in German.
  • You have a good feeling for people, are team-oriented and flexible (Mo–So).
  • You can handle computer programs and work structured.

Do you want more than just a job? With us you get the opportunity to develop personally and professionally. Your ideas are in demand, your contribution counts – day after day.

You can rejoice.

  • Attractive holiday models with up to 7 weeks of holidays per year
  • Paid changing time with 4 extra free days
  • An own staff restaurant with low prices, free coffee & tea, offered monthly breakfast buffet
  • Generous public transport participation, cheap Reka checks and much more!

Find out more about our benefits here: Clinic Stephanshorn as an employer

We work shifts from Monday to Sunday, between 6:30 and 18:30 – without shared services, without room hours. There is also time for your private life.

For additional information, please contact Shqipe Thaçi, Head of Guest Services, at shqipe.thaci@hirslanden.ch or Beatrix Zoppas-Sturzenegger at beatrice.zoppas@hirslanden.ch, HR Business Partner. We look forward to your contact.
