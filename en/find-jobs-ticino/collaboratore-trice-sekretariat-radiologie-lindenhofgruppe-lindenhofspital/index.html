Role overview

Tasks

As the first contact person, both at the reception and by phone, you are the business card of Radiologie Lindenhof.

  • You receive our patients, complete admission formalities and process customer-oriented inquiries.
  • The processing of general administrative tasks rounds off your varied everyday work.
  • You work in fixed services (without weekends and holidays).

Requirements

  • You have training as a medical assistant or an equivalent basic training with further training as a medical secretary.
  • Existing professional experience in the healthcare sector is of great advantage.
  • You attach importance to reliability, quality-conscious and efficient work and you are happy to bring your IT knowledge into the work.
  • With your stylish German and good oral French and English skills, it is easy for you to receive our patients in a customer-oriented manner.
