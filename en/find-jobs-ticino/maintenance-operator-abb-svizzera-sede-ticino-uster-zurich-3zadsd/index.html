Maintenance Manager

important • Your tasks and responsibilities


In this role, you have the opportunity to operate process plants or machines to convert raw materials or semi-finished parts into components or finished products – on schedule and in the required quality.
You carry out the assigned tasks every day. • Tasks according to agreed workflows and in compliance with ABB standard processes and safety guidelines and report any deviations. In addition, you contribute your expertise by operating machines according to operating instructions, work plan and/or production orders.


Work model:on site

This position is part of ABB PMA in Uster.


Your main tasks include:

  • Implementation of routine machine settings, adjustments and repairs and replacement of simple spare parts
  • Sicing of regular standard maintenance of equipment, setup of tools, programming as well as troubleshooting and reporting of possible problems
  • Keeping detailed records of machine settings, repairs and maintenance
  • Monitoring and documentation of released, faulty units and end products
