Role overview

The resource will be responsible for defining, monitoring and optimizing the production schedule for semi-finished and finished products, ensuring an efficient balance between demand, production capacity and material availability.

  • The resource will be responsible for defining, monitoring and optimizing the production schedule for semi-finished and finished products, ensuring an efficient balance between demand, production capacity and material availability.
  • Define the production schedule for semi-finished and finished products with the objective of meeting demand and optimizing costs and inventory levels.
  • Verify with the purchasing department the availability of raw materials and components necessary to ensure the feasibility of the plan.

Main responsibilities

  • Define the production schedule for semi-finished and finished products with the objective of meeting demand and optimizing costs and inventory levels.
  • Verify with the purchasing department the availability of raw materials and components necessary to ensure the feasibility of the plan.
  • Monitor the production schedule in the short, medium and long term, adapting it to variations in forecasted demand, production capacity and company production strategy.
  • Translate recipes into production bills of materials, set or define production cycles and the management and launch logic of the MRP.
  • Monitor work orders and analyze any deviations from the expected standards, proposing and implementing improvement actions.
  • Manage safety stock levels and monitor the rotation of materials.
  • Assess risks related to possible inefficiencies in production flows and develop efficiency improvement strategies. Requirements
  • Bachelor's degree in Management Engineering or equivalent qualification.
  • Previous experience of at least 3 years in production planning and demand planning.
  • Excellent knowledge of IT systems, with particular reference to SAP (planning and forecasting modules)
