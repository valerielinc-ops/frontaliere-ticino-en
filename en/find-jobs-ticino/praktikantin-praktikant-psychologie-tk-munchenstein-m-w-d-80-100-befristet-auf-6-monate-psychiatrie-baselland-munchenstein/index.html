Key requirements

  • They are in the master's degree in psychology, preferably with a clinical-psychotherapeutic focus or already have a bachelor’s degree in this field
  • If necessary, you have already gained clinical psychological experience
  • They bring willingness to work in a multidisciplinary team, openness and interest in new experiences in clinical practice, especially towards psychodynamic approaches.
  • It distinguishes a reliable and careful, also independent, always integrated in the team working method
  • Job opening: Internships are available for 6 months starting in January 2027, June 2027 or November 2027.

Company and context

  • Hospitation in the psycho- and sociotherapeutic treatment program, assuming pro rata tasks such as coordination, organization, documentation
  • Assumption of patient-related tasks in the case of admission, therapy planning and completion
  • Collaboration in diagnostics and test psychology (implementation, evaluation, reporting, feedback)
  • Collaboration in the milieutherapeutic setting, in particular.
  • Co-creation of the morning round of the patients: inside, accompaniment at lunch
  • Participation in reports, case and team meetings, supervisions and intervisions
  • Hospitation in indication discussions, assuming pro rata tasks such as collection of findings, medical history, test diagnostic evaluations and documentation or Reporting
  • Opportunity for internship in art, music, theatre and movement therapies
  • Cooperation in administrative tasks and possible projects of the day clinic
  • Unoccupied periods can/should be used for visits to activities in the clinic, study of files and literature, internship reports and/or library visits (especially when working in any projects).
