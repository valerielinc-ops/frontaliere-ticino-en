Original description

Public transport is excited and you like to lead and coach a team? Do you want to join the yellow class and participate actively in CarPostal's success? So get started and apply for our place. With you, we will always find the best solution.

Make a difference

  • You lead, motivate and develop the driving staff of your team, you ensure a pleasant working environment and you strengthen the performance and availability of your team through individual interviews and regular team meetings.
  • You ensure that staff resources for the transport service are made available on time and at a high level of quality. You accompany your team members throughout the HR lifecycle, recruitment and integration, through individual development to the start.
  • You also support them in difficult situations such as diseases or absences. On the other hand, you always keep an eye on the relevant key figures such as absences, fluctuation and time balances, and deduct from them optimisation measures.
  • You are responsible for the operation of the on-site operation and ensure its proper operation. If necessary, you also intervene with other internal stakeholders to ensure effective and coordinated management of the operation.
  • You plan and coordinate measures in the management of stops and yards, take into account deviations and shutdown changes, collaborate with internal and external partners and manage the stop management system.
  • In addition to your management tasks, you yourself carry out transport services, whether on a fixed schedule or to compensate for a lack of one-time resources.

What you bring

  • For this position, you have completed vocational training (CFC). In addition, you have the federal certificate of team driving specialist, an equivalent qualification or you are considering obtaining it in the near future.
  • You have experience in the tasks related to infrastructure (construction, operation, process) and you have a good understanding of security aspects in public space.
  • Thanks to your communication skills, you bring people together, you conduct exchanges on an equal footing and you find viable solutions even in difficult situations.
  • You are able to manage conflicts and you are professional and confident in negotiations and interviews with staff, driving managers, representatives of the CoPe and/or the social partners.
  • In addition, you have a Class D driver's licence.
  • You have very good knowledge in French, both oral and written. Good knowledge in a second national language is an asset.
