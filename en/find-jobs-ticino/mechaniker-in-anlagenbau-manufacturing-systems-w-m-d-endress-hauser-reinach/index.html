Original description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general. We are looking for a mechanic in Plant Engineering Manufacturing Systems (m/f/d) to complement our team and contribute to the successful development of our production facilities with technical skills, initiative and leadership responsibility. What is this point about? Do you not only want to assemble technical systems, but also actively shape their construction? Does it appeal to you to accompany international projects on site and take responsibility for the mechanical construction of complex systems? Do you enjoy guiding employees and external partners and passing on your knowledge? Then you can expect a varied task with high personal responsibility, international assignments and direct influence on the successful implementation of plant construction projects. You work in an experienced team within the Manufacturing Systems division and are a central contact for the mechanical setup of our production facilities at home and abroad. What tasks will you take on? They shall: • Install production plants at home and abroad and ensure their mechanical construction. • Planning, organizing and efficiently coordinating process steps for plant construction projects. • Ensure the mechanical construction of plant construction projects at home and abroad. • Collaborate with external suppliers, train assembly processes and ensure the quality of the work performed. • Actively participate in project planning and contribute your practical experience to the technical implementation. • Perform international assembly missions and accompany projects on site (about 10% travel activity, including individual longer missions overseas). What do we expect from you? They: • have completed training as a polymechanic, machine mechanic or industrial mechanic • bring several years of professional experience in the industrial environment as well as profound experience in plant construction. • have extensive knowledge of assembly as well as experience with external assembly inserts. • master the mechanical production of components and have several years of experience in conventional turning, milling and drilling. • work independently, solution-oriented and find pragmatic and sustainable solutions even for demanding assembly tasks. • have knowledge of hydraulics and pneumatics and would like to develop continuously. What can you expect from us? • We are a family-run, committed company with a sustainable vision. • We promote lifelong learning with versatile training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning. • We support work-life balance with flexible working hours. • We offer support in sustainable mobility solutions such as public transport. • We support family-friendly policies, including childcare places and childcare services during the holidays. How do you apply?
