Original description

Introduction

For our Commercial Division, we are looking for a Transit - Declaration in Customs whose main mission will be to ensure the customs conformity of our import/export flows. The selected person will be able to provide expertise in tariff classification (HS codes), preferential origin and customs regulations, while accompanying internal teams and our external partners.

Responsibilities

Customs classification and HS codes

  • Search, analyze and assign HS codes adapted to our articles
  • Study the technical characteristics of the products in order to ensure correct classification
  • Check the consistency of HS codes on import and export documents
  • Maintain the internal database of tariff classifications
  • Identify and report risks of classification, tax impact or regulatory non-compliance

Preferential origin

  • Check the eligibility of goods for preferential origin schemes
  • Check proof of origin and supporting documents
  • Prepare and follow certificates or declarations of origin where necessary
  • Ensure the application of preferential agreements between countries of origin and destination

Compliance and continuous improvement

  • Ensuring the application of customs regulations in international flows
  • Ensure regulatory oversight of customs, HS codes and rules of origin
  • Advise internal teams and alert against risk of non-compliance
  • Participate in continuous improvement of internal customs processes and procedures

Coordination and support

  • Interlocutor of freight forwarders, carriers, customs agents and competent authorities
  • Provide support to logistics teams, supply chain, sales administration, procurement, quality and finance
  • Ensuring the quality and consistency of customs information provided to internal and external partners

Profile

Training

  • Federal patent holder of customs declaration
  • Further training in international trade, logistics, supply chain or customs regulations is an asset

Experience and skills

  • Experience confirmed in customs, import/export and customs regulations, ideally at a shipper
  • Good control of international flows, documentary requirements and shipping operations
  • Strong knowledge of tariff classification, rules of origin and preferential origin
  • Experience in dealing with freight forwarders, carriers and customs authorities
  • Ability to control commercial, shipping and customs documents

Personal suitability

  • Rigor, precision and organizational sense
  • Spirit of analysis and great attention to detail
  • Autonomy, reliability and ability to manage priorities
  • Relationship and team spirit
  • Adaptability, discretion and confidentiality

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
