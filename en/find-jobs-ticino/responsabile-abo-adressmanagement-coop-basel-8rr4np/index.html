Main responsibilities

  • You are responsible for the subscription and address management of the Coopzeitung throughout Switzerland as well as other media and communication formats
  • You plan, control and monitor editions, selections, mutations, returns, address preparations and other quality-relevant processes
  • You coordinate the cooperation with internal departments as well as with mail, printers, call centers and other upstream and downstream offices and ensure smooth processes
  • You support data-based communication measures, for example in newsletter management, address procurement and target group-specific selections, evaluations and reporting
  • You will accompany projects, tests and system adjustments and actively develop subscription/address management in the SAP environment and beyond. Requirements
  • You have technical training in newspaper printing, shipping, processing or similar training and have experience in address, subscription or master data management
  • You have a good understanding of systems, processes and data contexts and ideally have experience with SAP as well as very good office user knowledge – especially in Excel
  • You work in a structured, exact and independent manner, keep an overview even in complex situations and set priorities in a targeted manner.
