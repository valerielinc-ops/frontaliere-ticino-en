Role overview

Builder’s overall responsibility for demanding construction projects.

From the selection process to project planning and realization until handover to our customers

  • Builder’s overall responsibility for demanding construction projects.
  • From the selection process to project planning and realization until handover to our customers

Application process

  • Planning, application and control of human and financial resources as well as legally compliant management of project loans in accordance with the Financial Budget Act
  • Ensuring effective cooperation with users, operators, planners, entrepreneurs and specialist consultants in the interest of the Bauherr Bund
  • Implementation of procurement procedures according to BoeB/VoeB as well as ensuring effective project-related quality management and controlling
  • Recognised degree as architect, civil engineer or equivalent training in construction
  • Several years of experience in the overall management of complex construction projects as well as in-depth practice in the client representation
  • Proven competence in integral project management (quality, costs, deadlines) and knowledge in public procurement (BoeB/VoeB)
  • Strong leadership traits, strategic and business thinking
  • High level of networking competence and skilful expression

Company and context

  • Define project goals and risks, define strategic foundations (schedule planning, processing models, process steps) and lead interdisciplinary project organizations
