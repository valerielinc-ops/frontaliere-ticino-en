Original description

You need it whenever there is no more electricity on our railway network somewhere. Weighing, deciding and acting forward and safely is your world. Exactly in such situations you are in the central control system in your element. But even if everything goes smoothly, you will not get bored. This is waiting for you:

  • Ensuring the railway power supply for the entire BLS network
  • Monitoring and control of operationally relevant plants and systems
  • Coordination of troubleshooting at railway installations with assessment of urgency and prioritization
  • Inclusion of specialist services as well as alerting for extraordinary events
  • Maintenance of basic plans for electrical station and route schemes This is what you bring with you:
  • Completed apprenticeship with technical background or railway-specific training
  • Professional experience in a supervising function is beneficial
  • Readiness for irregular working hours and assignments on weekends (24 hours)
  • Motivation to complete a multi-month, subject-specific training and interest in railway technology
  • Independent, responsible and safety-conscious working methods Your advantages – you can look forward to:
  • We promote the most flexible planning of your tours and shifts possible
  • Get a GA 2nd class, a GA 1st. Class or a half-tax subscription.
  • You have free time when others work. For pennies at night and on weekends there are surcharges.
  • It is possible to buy an additional five to ten holiday days per year.
  • Financial support for external further education and an exciting internal learning and course offer are at your disposal.
  • With seasonal events and courses we support your health With almost 4,000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. Moreover, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What connects us? Proud of what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And lots of exciting jobs.
