Description

Restaurant Manager 100% (f/m/i) - Bürgenstock Resort --> An icon of the Swiss hotel industry and a legend since its sensational opening in 1873. Located on the Bürgenberg, a 500-meter-high rocky outcrop in central Switzerland, the Bürgenstock Resort Lake Lucerne stands out for its bold design, groundbreaking sustainability, breathtaking landscapes and state-of-the-art leisure activities. Around 700 employees from 54 nations provide excellent hospitality and provide the perfect setting for moments beyond imagination. Three exclusive hotels, the Bürgenstock Hotel & Alpine Spa (5Superior) with the two accommodation options The Contemporary and The Heritage, the Waldhotel by Bürgenstock (5 Superior) and the Tavern 1879 (3*) are available to guests. Each of these houses knows how to create a unique atmosphere. Twelve restaurants, lounges and bars are the showcase for culinary excellence

  • At the award-winning Bürgenstock Alpine Spa and Waldhotel Spa, guests will find the ultimate relaxation. The resort is not only a dream destination for travelers from all over the world, but also a workplace with an excellent corporate culture. For the third consecutive year, Bürgenstock Resort has been officially certified as a “Great Place to Work” in Switzerland – an award that fills the entire team with pride. To strengthen our team, from November 2026 or by appointment we are looking for a restaurant manager 100% (f/m/i) In this hospitable position, you are responsible for: Leadership and further development of the outlet

Coordination and ensuring daily operations and activities in accordance with the Company’s Food & Beverage quality standards and the Resort’s policies, procedures and objectives

Setting and achieving short- and long-term goals of the department as well as ensuring F&B, quality, hygiene and LQA standards and employee safety
