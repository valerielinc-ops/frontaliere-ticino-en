Original description

Tasks

  • You are part of a newly created role and take on a central function in our waste management logistics in Willisau
  • Together, you make sure that more than 1,000 tons of cartonboard, 150 tons of residual waste and 42 tons of packaging films are processed efficiently every year.
  • You help with the collection rounds in the internal waste and recyclables logistics
  • You transport separate materials with industrial trucks or hand cars to the disposal station
  • You load trailers with residual material bales (especially cardboard and residual waste) by means of truck and prepare them for further processing
  • You actively participate, think and take responsibility for improvements in processes and processes
  • Together as a team, you will take care of the weekly cleaning of the outdoor premises Requirements
  • You have completed an apprenticeship (EFZ of advantage, e.g. as a recycling list:in or comparable – also EBA possible)
  • You have an SUVA-approved truck ID or are ready to purchase it internally (mandatory)
  • You have good knowledge of German for a secure understanding in everyday work
  • You work independently, reliably and like to take responsibility
  • You like to work in a team, think along and actively address problems and ideas
  • You are interested in recycled materials and are ready to acquire knowledge on the job
  • You are ready to work in a flexible shift model between 07:00 and 22:00 (start times around 07:00, 10:00 or 12:00) – including possible Saturday assignments We offer:
  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.
