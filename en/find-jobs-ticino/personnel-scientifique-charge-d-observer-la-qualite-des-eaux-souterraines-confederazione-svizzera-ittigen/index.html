Original description

  • Technically and organisationally manage the national groundwater quality monitoring network (NAQUA)
  • Design and coordinate the programme of chemical analyses for monitoring groundwater quality together with the cantonal specialist bodies
  • Contribute to the conceptual and operational development of national groundwater monitoring
  • Support projects of research institutions, private laboratories or engineering offices
  • Follow scientific developments regarding groundwater quality; participate in working groups and represent the FOEN in committees
  • Writing specialist publications and reports as well as answering questions from the media and politicians on the subject of groundwater quality monitoring
  • University degree in engineering or natural sciences, ideally supplemented by a doctoral thesis
  • Experience in environmental chemistry and groundwater quality
  • Strong analytical skills, negotiation skills and the ability to communicate effectively with different interlocutors (federal and cantonal authorities, experts, etc.)
  • Experience in project management and in supporting working groups and expert groups
  • Innovative and independent personality who likes to work with many different contacts and accepts challenges
  • Good active knowledge of at least one second official language and, if possible, knowledge of a third official language
