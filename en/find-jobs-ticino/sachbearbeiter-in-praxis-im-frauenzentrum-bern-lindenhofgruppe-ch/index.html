Role overview

Tasks

As a clerk in our practice at the Women’s Center, you actively support the administration team in ensuring a professional practice organization.

  • You coordinate appointments, process requests and take on a wide range of administrative tasks such as providing documents for DKG certification and helping to organize internal meetings.
  • You also coordinate marketing activities, manage the websites and organize events such as team events or medical training.
  • Controlling performance billing is an important part of your field of activity.
  • In addition, you create and send the annual appointment orders of the patients and take care of the office material inventory of the practice.

Requirements

  • You bring a completed commercial basic education and have an advantage of first professional experience.
  • You know how to use your organizational skills and quickly gain an overview in the daily work.
  • You are familiar with the common MS Office applications and bring a flair for new IT systems.
  • Thanks to your innovative, flexible and thoughtful way of working, changes in the working environment are no problem and you like to proactively suggest improvements.
  • A team- and service-oriented basic attitude is very important to you.
