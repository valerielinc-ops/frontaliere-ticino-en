Original description

Mission

  • You learn how to transform milk into a wide variety of products – you manage the stages of production, filling, refining, packaging and even tasting.
  • You put in service, monitor and clean ultra-modern production facilities, always in compliance with hygiene standards.
  • You analyze the products in the laboratory – chemically and microbiologically – to ensure an irreproachable quality.
  • You work in different departments and in several production sites, which allows you to understand the whole profession.
  • You discover how a simple milk becomes a finished product ready to be enjoyed – and you actively contribute to every step. - Thanks to your commitment, you ensure that our products arrive fresh and in perfect condition to consumers.

Profile sought

  • You have completed the compulsory school with a good level in French, mathematics and natural sciences.
  • You like dairy products and you have a good sense of smell and taste.
  • You are comfortable with the technique and you like to understand how machines work.
  • You pay attention to hygiene, you work with care and you always keep a good overview.
  • You live in the area where the cheese factory is located and you have a reasonable way to get to work.
