The team mission

  • We are looking for a Product Engineer who combines strong software engineering skills with a product mindset to build AI-powered solutions for our customers and employees.

Application process

  • The team mission is to develop & maintain the new Customer Care Center Application for Swissquote agents, together with the different banking services (cards, payments & transfer) that Swissquote clients use on a daily basis.
  • You will design, develop, and deploy AI-native applications, owning the full lifecycle from backend services and model integration to user experience and performance monitoring.
  • Working closely with Customer Care and Sales Business teams, you will help transform our legacy solutions through practical and reliable inhouse AI solutions.
  • Build and maintain AI generated-code applications using coding agents such as ClaudeCode
  • Integrate with other components of Swissquote ecosystem such as Genesys and micro service architecture into production environments.
  • Collaborate with Data Scientists and business stakeholders to deliver impactful and straight to the point solutions.
  • Ensure solutions meet security, compliance, and governance requirements expected in a regulated financial environment.
  • Experience developing production-grade software applications.
