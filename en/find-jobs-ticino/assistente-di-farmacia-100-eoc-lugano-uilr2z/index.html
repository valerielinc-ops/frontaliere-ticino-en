Company and context

  • The organization allows us to effectively combine a local approach and an overall vision, guaranteeing the population a global and local hospital offer, regardless of the place where the services are required.

  • To complete the team of the Hospital Pharmacy Service of the Pharmacological Sciences Institute of Italian Switzerland (ISFSI) at the Lugano Regional Hospital, we are looking for a Pharmacy Assistant (100%) Profile: The person we are looking for knows how to actively listen to their interlocutors, ensuring adequate advice, correct handling of medicines according to current directives and provisions and effective interaction with patients.
