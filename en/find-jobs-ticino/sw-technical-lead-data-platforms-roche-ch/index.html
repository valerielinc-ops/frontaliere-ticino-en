The Position

Software Development Technology Expert

In this role, you will provide global support for technical initiatives, share knowledge internationally, and shape the organization's technical direction. We are looking for deep expertise across multiple technology areas, a commitment to high code quality and security standards, and a proven ability to remove team obstacles while optimizing developer productivity across the software development lifecycle.

The Opportunity

  • Scope: Provide strategic technical guidance and set the direction for digital product development in alignment with business goals and priorities. Take ownership of developing new components and ideas while collaborating with a multidisciplinary team to deliver end-to-end experiences to end users.
  • Architectural Evolution: Provide strategic technology and architecture advisory for AI-ready digital products while enforcing domain boundaries and maintaining high performance.
  • AI-based software development: Drive the team toward adopting best practices and tools in AI-based software development.
  • Stakeholder Management: Act as an advisor on technology and software development topics, providing direction for mid- to long-term goals and objectives.
  • Impact/Strategy: Provide global support for innovative and technological initiatives. Share knowledge locally and on global platforms, while actively engaging in RDT Data Domain and site-related activities.
  • Business/Technical ability: Actively contribute to the advancement of technical knowledge. Inspire innovation, drive solutions in line with technology trends, and focus on designing new approaches to existing problems.
  • Data & AI Knowledge: Build frontier digital products that create a 'Data for AI' and 'AI for Data' portfolio, enabling Roche business units to discover the true value of Data and AI. Serve as the technology lead in a team developing digital products to manage AI and Data assets—an essential capability for implementing the Group Data Strategy and building an AI-ready organization.

Who you are

  • 7+ years of proven experience driving cross-functional collaboration to solve complex technical problems and align disparate engineering efforts toward common goals.
  • Deep understanding of data processing principles and data requirements for AI readiness.
  • Deep expertise across multiple technology domains with a demonstrated ability to select and apply optimal tools to solve complex challenges.
  • Proficiency in Java, Spring Boot, Angular, AWS, ECS, Docker, PostgreSQL, GitOps, Event-Driven Architecture, or similar technologies.
  • Strong grasp of software development lifecycle (SDLC) best practices.
  • Extensive knowledge of modern software development tooling, with the ability to evaluate and integrate tools that enhance developer productivity and experience Roche-wide.
  • Solid understanding of software validation, regulatory processes, and software quality management practices ( a plus ).
  • Proven track record of clearing obstacles for technical teams, empowering them to deliver exceptional products.
  • Proactive mindset with a proven ability to anticipate future technical challenges and opportunities, developing strategies to keep the organization ahead.
  • Fluency in English (written and spoken).

What you get

  • Salary range 19,000 - 35,400 PLN gross based on the employment contract.
  • Annual bonus payment based on your performance (target 20% ).
  • Hybrid Work Model (2 days per week in our Warsaw/Poznań office).
