Role overview

The modern residential and care center Tertianum Feldegg and the family-run Tertianum Steinegg in Degersheim, each with 30 care beds, offer people in the fourth phase of life a comfortable home.

The Feldegg specializes in the needs of people with dementia and is beautifully and quietly located on the edge of the village, while the Steinegg is in a quiet residential area.

The public restaurants in both houses contribute a lot to the quality of life.
