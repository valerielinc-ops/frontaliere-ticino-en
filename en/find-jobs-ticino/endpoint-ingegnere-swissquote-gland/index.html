Role overview

The Endpoint Engineer is a key member of the Endpoint Administration & Protection team, responsible for the management, compliance, protection and lifecycle of corporate endpoints across the organisation.

The role brings specialist expertise in macOS and mobile platforms including iOS, iPadOS and Android, complementing the team's existing Windows capabilities.

Working primarily with Microsoft Intune and Microsoft Defender for Endpoint, the Endpoint Engineer ensures that endpoints remain secure, compliant, operational and aligned with business and security requirements.
