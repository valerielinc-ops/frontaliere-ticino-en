Role overview

Your mission

We are offering a challenging position as a full-time (100%) PhD Student at ENAC Faculty at EPFL, Sion.

As a PhD student, you will develop technology for humanitarian data mining and analysis in the framework for the project CARE (Cross-modal AI for Relief Efforts), a collaborative project between EPFL, ETH and the ICRC.

Main responsibilities

  • Main duties and responsibilities include:
  • Design multimodal deep learning-based methodologies linking satellite image and textual data about conflicts.
  • Develop approaches for events localization, with emphasis on robustness across events (domain adaptation).
  • Develop tools for visualization and data analysis together with colleagues at the ICRC.
  • Write publications.
  • Attend international conferences, both in machine learning and remote sensing.
  • Participate in the educational activities of the ENAC faculty.

Key requirements

  • You have a master’s degree in computer science.
  • You have experience with the humanitarian sector and/or in remote sensing.
  • You are proficient in Python coding and deep learning frameworks.
