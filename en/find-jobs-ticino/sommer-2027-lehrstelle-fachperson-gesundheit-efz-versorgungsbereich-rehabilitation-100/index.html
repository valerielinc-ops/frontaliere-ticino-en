Role overview

The Hasliberg Rehabilitation Clinic is a renowned Swiss clinic in the field of internistic-oncological, psychosomatic and musculoskeletal rehabilitation.

The clinic has 83 beds and is located on the hospital lists of the cantons of Bern, Lucerne, Solothurn, Nidwalden and Uri.

The ISO-certified company works process-oriented and interdisciplinary, based on the guiding principle of "REHA competence", with the characteristics: respect, efficiency, cordiality, attention and competence.
