Application Developer (80-100%)

Olten | Onsite | Reference 7835

Are you passionate about modern technology and building high-performance applications? Do you thrive in an agile environment where collaboration, trust, and ownership matter? Join our Application Development Team and help deliver innovative, high-quality solutions for our customers. As a Lead Application Designer & Developer, you will work closely with business and IT stakeholders, design enterprise-grade solutions, and take an active role in guiding and performing key development tasks.

What You Will Do

  • Build scalable web and core systems for SIX’s Post-Trading business, focusing on robust architecture and efficient implementation.
  • Modernize and maintain existing enterprise applications and web services.
  • Collaborate with Product Owners and Architects to define solutions and maintain technical documentation.
  • Perform reviews, enforce best practices, ensure security and regulatory compliance, and provide technical guidance to development teams.
  • Promote automation in development processes, including CI/CD pipelines, and foster open communication within the team.

What You Bring

  • At least 5 years of experience in designing, developing, and maintaining enterprise applications.
  • Strong skills in Java (OpenJDK), Spring Boot, and Angular; experienced with OpenShift, GitLab, Ansible, Tomcat, and WildFly; and hands-on experience working with relational databases.
  • Ability to lead and support development teams with technical guidance while actively contributing as a lead developer.
  • Knowledge of secure coding practices and web security principles.
  • Proficiency in English (written and spoken); German and/or Spanish are a plus.
