Original description

Are you looking for a sensory challenge with heart and effect? Then come to us from the school year 2026 / 27 to the Heilpädagogische Schule Bachtelen in Grenchen. Together with your teammates, you teach in small classes with 6 – 8 students with a special educational need. Together, you create a teaching that is characterized by individual, development-oriented education. We are looking from August 2026 a curative pedagogue or an apprentice 80 – 100 % for the basic stage and substage A small school (circa 42 pupils) awaits you, in which we cultivate a valuable and respectful working environment. You shape learning spaces that are individual, structured and loving. You work with parents and various subjects, and above all with a lot of heart and humor. What else awaits you established colleagues; we are a manageable team that makes mutual support fair working conditions according to cantonal guidelines a dedicated school management that is interested in you and supports you a relationship-oriented school day that creates orientation with structures a lot of space for educational co-design You bring that and we look forward to you when... you enjoy individualized teaching you are patient, creative, humorous and flexible you enjoy the self-sponsored work in a great team The accompaniment of children with special educational needs you have or are willing to complete an approved EDK teaching diploma with or without additional education Curious? Then we look forward to your application! If you have any questions, I'd like to take time for you.
