Role overview

Portrait The BVS Unteres Emmental is a student of around 120 cognitively impaired, socio-emotionally striking, as well as neurodiverse children and adolescents in 21 classes at four locations in Burgdorf and Utzenstorf.

It is an important pillar of the SAZ, the Burgdorf Training and Work Centre, which has been offering itself for over 50 years as a cognitively, physically or mentally impaired place for learning, working and living.

The SAZ is a regionally strongly anchored institution with a wide range of services, recognized by the federal and cantonal authorities.
