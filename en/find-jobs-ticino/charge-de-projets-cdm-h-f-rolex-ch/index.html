Role overview

Introduction

For our Logistics Methods department, we are looking for a Project Manager

CDM (H/F) whose main mission will be to analyse business needs and field constraints in order to propose concrete functional solutions in connection with our L.E.S (Logistic Execution System) solution.

  • For our Logistics Methods department, we are looking for a Project Manager
  • Participate in the design of L.E.S.-related logistics solutions: Analyze needs expressed by internal clients, understand business flows and constraints and propose the most appropriate functional response

Main responsibilities

  • Participate in the design of L.E.S.-related logistics solutions: Analyze needs expressed by internal clients, understand business flows and constraints and propose the most appropriate functional response
  • Translating business needs into a functional solution: Resuming the need expressed by the trade and formalizing the necessary elements to enable the implementation teams to evaluate the solutions, secure the encryption and prepare the implementation
  • Ensure business coordination and understanding of the issues: Encourage stakeholder exchanges (Industrialization & Logistics Method, IT, Management) to align objectives, priorities and arbitrations.
  • Provide visibility on advancement, impacts and decisions, with appropriate level of detail
  • Contribute to the improvement and harmonization of logistics processes: Identify levers for optimization and simplification of processes related to the use of the L.E.S.
  • Participate in the harmonization of practices between sites, the reduction of recurring problems and the continuous improvement of the solution
  • Ensure the functional consistency of the solution: Ensure the overall consistency of the changes made taking into account existing uses and impacts on the different sites.
