Original description

Our institute is equipped with the most modern imaging technologies for digital radiological diagnostics. This includes general radiology including mammography, fluoroscopic examinations, sonography and interventional radiology. We have the Canon 4D CT hybrid system, two Philips spectral CTs, a 1.5 Tesla MRI from Siemens and a 3 Tesla MRI from Philips. Tasks:

  • Participation in a diverse area of application in conventional radiology including fluoroscopy as well as in interventional radiology (depending on experience), computer tomography (CT) and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)
  • Active support in the area of X-ray registration and ultrasound examinations if necessary
  • Accompaniment and support of students during their training
  • Participation in weekend and night shifts on a rotating basis, on-call duty only as a team member in interventional radiology Requirements:
  • Completed training as dipl. Radiology specialist HF
  • Sound experience in computer tomography (CT) as well as conventional X-rays
  • Experience in magnetic resonance imaging, good knowledge of using RIS and PACS systems desirable
  • Independent, responsible and committed personality
  • Willingness to work in shifts
  • Friendly, empathetic and patient-oriented approach
  • Enjoy working in a varied rotation system We offer:
  • Depending on age, 28-33 paid vacation days per year
  • Extensive opportunities for further training and further education, e.g. own seminar program with a wide range of educational opportunities
  • Company health management with preventive measures such as ergonomic advice in the workplace
  • Part-time work and the possibility of job sharing, including in management positions
  • Bright, friendly rooms with modern infrastructure
  • Distinctive values and actively practiced corporate culture
  • Employees shape and help shape the Claraspital The Claraspital, Basel's largest private hospital, is a University teaching and research hospital with a comprehensive service mandate for highly specialized abdominal surgery and national exposure of its "Abdomen" and "Tumor" centers as well as its special areas. At Claraspital, employees find jobs where they can develop both professionally and personally. At Claraspital, we attach great importance to consciously maintaining and developing our corporate culture and actively living the cultural criteria we have developed together: “together – competently – responsibly”.
