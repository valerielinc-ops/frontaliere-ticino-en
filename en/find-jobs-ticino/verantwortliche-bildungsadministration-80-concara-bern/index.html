Original description

Tasks

  • You develop and implement measures to secure and further develop our quality of training in basic vocational training (pre-apprenticeship, EBA and EFZ).
  • You combine operational requirements and training quality and thus promote a holistic view of learning and practice.
  • You will observe trends in professional and adult education and bring innovative impulses to our educational work.
  • You participate in educational strategy concepts and lead educational and innovation projects (e.g. implementation of new BiVo FaGe, training concept Concara, talent management learners, etc.) – from cooperation to project responsibility.
  • You develop structures that support learners with learning disabilities and promote talents in a targeted manner.
  • You help shape the vocational training offer and advise vocational trainees and other specialists competently, practically and appreciatively.

Requirements

  • Graduation at tertiary level in health or social sciences (HF/FH or equivalent)
  • Further training in pedagogy/adult education (minimum 600 h)
  • Several years of experience in basic vocational training
  • Very good knowledge of the Swiss education system
  • Healthcare experience is beneficial
  • Expertise in Quality Management and Organizational Development
  • Strategically thinking, conceptually strong
  • Independent, proactive, structured and quality-oriented
  • Sovereign appearance and clear communication
  • Mindful in dealing with your own resources
  • Enjoy embracing and driving trends in learning and development

We offer

  • Flexible annual working hours, innovative working time models and possibility for home office
  • Competent, committed and collaborative team
  • Appreciative corporate culture and value-oriented leadership culture
  • Acting responsibly in a motivated and supportive working environment
  • Many design possibilities in a meaningful working environment

Spitex Bern and Domicil have joined forces under the umbrella of the non-profit Concara Group. With more than 2000 employees, we are committed to integrated all-round care, both inpatient and at home. At Concara Services AG, we provide various services for all operating units of the Concara Group, implement support processes and provide professional support.
