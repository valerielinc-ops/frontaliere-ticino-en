Main responsibilities

  • Your responsibilities
  • As a core member of the Design System team, you build and maintain the frontend foundations of Swiss Post's digital products, used by millions of customers.
  • You work closely with DevOps teams across the organisation and help them adopt the Design System and solve frontend challenges.
  • Through your open-source work, your impact isvisible to developers both inside and outside Swiss Post.
  • You contribute to the evolution of Swiss Post's design and accessibility standards and turn them into reus-able components.
  • You code on a daily basis and, as a senior member of the team, mentor teammates, conduct reviews, write technical specifications, and drive technical initiatives.

Key requirements

  • You have strong expertise in HTML, CSS, and TypeScript, while knowledge of Sass, Stencil JS, or Storybook is an advantage.
  • You are curious about emerging web platform capabilities and evolving W3C standards and enjoy mentor-ing or teaching others.
  • You ideally have experience with Angular and knowledge of frameworks such as React, Vue, or Svelte, while experience contributing to a Design System or working with Web Components will set you apart.
  • Experience with WCAG standards is a plus, but most importantly you are passionate about accessibility and inclusive experiences.
  • This hybrid role combines team-agreed office days with the flexibility to work from home.

Contacts

  • We’re looking for a senior frontend developer to join the Design System team in Neuchâtel.
  • You have a very good command of English, enabling you to collaborate effectively within our multicul-tural team, and a good command of French for your day-to-day exchanges with colleagues in the Neuchâtel office.
  • The Design System team is distributed across Bern Wankdorf and Neuchâtel, and we're looking for a new col-league to strengthen our team in Neuchâtel.
