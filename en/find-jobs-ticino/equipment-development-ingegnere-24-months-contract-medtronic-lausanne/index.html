  • may include the following and other duties may be assigned:

  • Track vendor punch-list resolution and verify facility and safety integration.
  • Installation Qualification (IQ): Draft and execute Installation Qualification (IQ) protocols in compliance with ISO 13485 standards, ensuring all physical, electrical, utility, software, and calibration requirements are verified and documented.
