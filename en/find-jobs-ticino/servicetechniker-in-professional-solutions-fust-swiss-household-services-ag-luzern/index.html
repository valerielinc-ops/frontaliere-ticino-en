Service technician:in Professional Solutions

Join our team and help us to continue creating customer experiences! A job at Fust, and it's running!

  • operating training for customers

  • Category B driving licence
  • Willingness to use outside normal office hours

  • Training: internal training, support for external training and further training,

Development opportunities

  • holidays: minimum 25 days holidays or according to valid GAV, if possible support for unpaid holidays
