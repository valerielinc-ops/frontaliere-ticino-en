Main responsibilities

  • As a team leader: in the assistance team for the central area, you take on a central role in the design of the new team structure and create the conditions for professional and service-oriented administrative support of the departments.

Company and context

  • They shape the transition to the new team structure and bring employees from different previous organizational contexts together in a joint assistant team.
  • They take over the personnel and professional leadership of the employees and accompany and promote their professional and personal development.
  • They are responsible for the operational operation of the assistance team and ensure professional, efficient and service-oriented administrative support for the organizational units of the central area.
  • They control and coordinate tasks and resources within the team and ensure balanced and needs-based service delivery.
  • They work closely with the organizational units of the central department as well as the specialist departments of human resources and finance, network the relevant stakeholders and ensure partnership-based cooperation as well as clear and functioning interfaces.
  • They continuously develop processes, standards and workflows and thus make an important contribution to the further development of the administrative organization within the framework of Fit for Future. Requirements
  • Basic commercial education or similar training, ideally supplemented by tertiary education (e.g.
  • HF, professional card) in business administration, management, organisation or a related field
  • Several years of professional experience in a comparable function, ideally in a complex administrative environment
  • Several years of leadership experience as well as experience in the development and support of teams
