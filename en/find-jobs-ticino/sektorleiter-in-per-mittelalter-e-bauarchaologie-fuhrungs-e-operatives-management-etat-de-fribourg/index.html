Role overview

Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The Office of Archeology of the Canton of Friborg (AAFR) identifies, inventories and documents archaeological cultural assets.

It accompanies the administrative processes that concern the archaeological monuments located in archaeological zones and provides resources to carry out excavations and, if necessary, to preserve archaeological monuments.
