Original description

Tasks

  • At KIGA Baselland, you will undergo a commercial apprenticeship in accordance with the public administration training and examination industry and visit the commercial vocational school in Liestal.
  • Thanks to a clearly structured training in modern workplaces, you will get to know the professional practice in various departments.
  • Our motivated and committed trainers will teach you the skills for the learning and performance goals so that you can successfully complete the apprenticeship after three years.

Requirements

  • Very good school performance in the subjects German, Mathematics, French and English
  • Minimum secondary education level E
  • Very good oral and written language
  • Knowledge of MS-Office applications and nimble writing in the ten-finger system
  • Organizational talent, willingness to perform and learn, exact working methods
  • Responsible, reliable and team capable personality with good manners

The cantonal administration of Basel-Landschaft is the largest employer in the canton. It offers interesting jobs and a variety of training opportunities. In a wide range of professions and fields, more than 6,000 employees provide services for the population and the economy on a daily basis. In doing so, they contribute to a high standard of living and promote the attractiveness of the canton. Actively shape the future with us!
