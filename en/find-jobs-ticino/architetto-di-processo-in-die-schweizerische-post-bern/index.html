Role overview

Would you like to help shape PostNetz’s processes across areas and channels?

In our Omnichannel Excellence team, you will further develop the process architecture and create clear foundations for cooperation.

You combine the strategic orientation with the requirements of everyday life and make visible how processes, systems and customer interfaces interact.

  • In our Omnichannel Excellence team, you will further develop the process architecture and create clear foundations for cooperation.

Main responsibilities

  • Together with relevant stakeholders, you define roles, responsibilities and decision-making paths in process management and ensure that these are successfully implemented in practice.

  • In addition, you develop KPIs and target systems for central processes and ensure that improvements are transparently controlled and measurable.
