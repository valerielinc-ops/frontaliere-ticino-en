Original description

Portrait The Biel-Madretsch high school center is one of the three German-speaking high school centers in the city of Biel. Our school is well organized, multicultural and diverse. We attach great importance to the successful coexistence of all students. A good 300 students attend the 7th to 9th grades in six secondary, eight secondary classes and two mixed-age secondary classes. They are taught by around fifty class and subject teachers. We use model 3a (Manuel), in which the subjects German, French and mathematics are taught in level classes, the other subjects in the core classes.

Tasks

Our mixed-age secondary school class is looking for a co-class teacher and teacher. The class consists of 10 to 12 students mixed from 7th, 8th and 9th grades. The students have special needs, which we support with this smaller class and team teaching lessons.

Subjects: Mathematics, French, Nature & Technology, Career Orientation Worked as IF support in German, RZG, ERG, music, sport

Teaching days

Monday: 7:30 a.m. - 4:20 p.m Wednesday: 7:30 a.m. - 11:00 a.m Thursday: 7:30 a.m. - 11:50 a.m Friday: 7:30 a.m. - 3:20 p.m

Are you committed wholeheartedly? Do you enjoy working in a team, having discussions with parents and specialist departments and have a high level of organizational talent? You are familiar with IF, eU and bVSA. You accompany classes as an integrative support teacher, adapt materials to the individual learning levels of the students and write reports on those students.

Requirements

For fulfilling work as a teacher, a PH degree and a few years of experience are highly desirable. Natural talents who are still studying PH are also welcome. Willingness to work together within the team and take part in various school events and working groups is required.

We offer

  • Exciting field of work with a high level of personal responsibility.
  • Diverse design options for lessons according to LP21.
  • Pleasant working atmosphere in a well-coordinated and very helpful team that assumes responsibility together.
  • Support from the school management, the school secretariat, an IF teacher in secondary classes and a functioning home service.
  • New teachers receive a Gotte/Götti.
  • Modern infrastructure with a projector and visualizer in all classrooms, a PC room and a laptop cart for borrowing in the classroom.
  • Central school location, reachable on foot from Biel train station in 15 minutes or by bus with a two-stop journey. Limited parking spaces are available.
