Role overview

Customer Service Transport Specialist Back Cippà Transportation is a solid and dynamic company that for over 50 years supports companies and partners with transport solutions, logistics and customs services in Italy and the world.

For our headquarters in Chiasso we are looking for a passionate person, with a spirit of initiative and a desire to build lasting relationships with customers and partners who have experience in the field of international shipments.

You will join a dynamic team and you will have the opportunity to grow and interpret your role in a broad way: you will develop and manage relationships with customers and also follow the operation of shipments, all ensuring a high quality service.
