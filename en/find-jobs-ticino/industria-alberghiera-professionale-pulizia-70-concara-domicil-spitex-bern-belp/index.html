Role overview

Tasks

With your commitment, you ensure that our residents and guests feel comfortable in a clean and well-kept environment. You support our housekeeping team in the maintenance, intermediate and basic cleaning and ensure a careful and hygienic execution of the work. Another important part of your activity is the service at the meals in the dining room or in the group, as well as the service of guests in the public restaurant. You work independently and reliably, keep an overview even in lively situations and support your teammates where your help is needed. With your friendly and attentive nature you contribute to a pleasant atmosphere and meet our residents respectfully and appreciatively.

  • Experience in long-term care is beneficial
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Flexibility, readiness for regular weekend assignments
