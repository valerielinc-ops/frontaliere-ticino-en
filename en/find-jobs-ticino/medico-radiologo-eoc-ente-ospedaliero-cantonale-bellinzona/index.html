Company and context

  • The organization allows us to effectively combine a local approach and an overall vision, guaranteeing the population a global and local hospital offer, regardless of the place where the services are required.
  • Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 6,500 collaborators and their attention to human relationships, the EOC ensures quality healthcare by taking care of over 41,000 inpatients per year and providing over 600,000 outpatient consultations.
  • The Integrated Diagnostics Institute of Italian Switzerland (IDISI) brings together the clinics of radiology, nuclear medicine, pathology and laboratory medicine and, with its approximately 600 collaborators and almost 4 million tests and analyzes performed every year, offers a complete and high-quality diagnostic service, responding to the needs of patients residing throughout the cantonal territory.
  • Overcoming traditional specialist barriers, IDISI adopts a multidisciplinary approach to promote more efficient and personalized diagnostic-therapeutic pathways, capable of responding in a targeted manner to the specific needs of each patient.
  • Furthermore, the Institute is oriented towards university teaching and the promotion of biomedical research.
  • The Radiology Clinic performs over 260,000 tests per year, thanks to the effective integration of the skills of its 45 doctors and 150 radiology technicians and a cutting-edge technological infrastructure.
