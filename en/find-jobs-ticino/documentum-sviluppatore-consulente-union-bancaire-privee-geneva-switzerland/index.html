Mission

We’re looking for a hands-on Senior Consultant to support an electronic content management (ECM) migration between two banking information systems.

Our mission is to deliver a secure, timely, and compliant migration by assessing counterparty data, aligning data semantics, and operating robust integration processes.

Main responsibilities

  • Main responsibilities
  • Assist Business and IT ECM teams to plan and execute the migration of content and metadata from one bank’s information system to the target bank’s system.
  • Organize and lead data mapping workshops with business units; capture requirements and align data semantics across both banks.
  • Conduct system assessments using input from domain business lines to evaluate counterparty data, identify integration challenges, and propose remediation strategies.
  • Design, implement, and operate ETL processes and migration pipelines to move content and metadata into target ECM systems in a timely manner; leverage FME Migration Tool where applicable.
  • Execute migration tasks hands-on, coordinating cutover and post-migration stabilization activities.
  • Emphasize maintainability (modular, well-structured migration components), observability (structured logging, metrics, traceability), and operability (standardized error handling and troubleshooting runbooks) across the migration lifecycle.
  • Ensure data quality and compliance through reconciliation checks, consistency validations, and regulatory controls; resolve discrepancies with stakeholders.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to align integration goals and manage expectations across business and IT.
  • Document data mappings, functional and technical specifications, runbooks, and operational procedures to enable IT–business alignment and knowledge transfer.

Key requirements

  • Proven experience delivering ECM/content/data migrations in complex, regulated environments (preferably banking/financial services).

  • Captiva / InputAccel / Intelligent Capture platform knowledge

  • Knowledge of the FME Migration Tool is a strong plus.

  • Excellent facilitation and communication skills; ability to translate business needs into precise data mappings and technical specifications.
