Original description

What you can do with us

As a Night Auditor, you are an important contact person for our international guests during the night and ensure that the front office processes and high service and quality standards are run smoothly.

They complement our Front Office Team and are responsible for the following

  • tasks:
  • You receive our international guests professionally and warmly, perform check-in and check-outs and take care of your requests and wishes individually.
  • They guarantee a first-class guest service during the night according to the high standards of an international 5-star luxury hotel.
  • You will take over the tasks at the front office, the cash register and the back office and will be responsible for the completion of the day's accounts as well as the control of the relevant bookings, transactions and cash registers.
  • You will work closely with the office, Housekeeping, Security and other operational departments and support the Front Office, Guest Relations and Guest Experience Team if necessary.
  • Your profile

For this very varied position, you ideally bring the following:

  • Completed training in the hotel industry or completion of a hotel school.
  • At least 2-3 years of professional experience at the front office, ideally as a night auditor or in a comparable position in the 5-star luxury hotel industry.
  • Very good knowledge of operational and administrative front office procedures.
  • Experience with day-to-day accounts, cash management and control of front office transactions
  • Flexibility and good knowledge of the working processes in the front office area.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, Italian or Russian.
  • Good computer skills of MS Office programs.
  • Impeccable manners and well-kept appearance.
  • High level of commitment, flexibility and joy in working at night.
  • A strong team orientation and at the same time the ability to make decisions independently.

Our offer

  • Temporary season position from the end of November 2026 until the end of March 2027.
  • 5-day week on average at 43.5 hours.
  • 5 weeks of holidays and 6 holidays per year.
  • 13. Pay from entrance (per rate of temporis).
  • On request at the beginning accommodation in one of our new staff houses.
  • Food in the new, modern staff restaurant.
  • Work in an attractive environment with development opportunities.

If you appreciate an interesting, demanding and versatile activity in a dynamic environment and want to join a well-trained team, then we look forward to your complete online application.
