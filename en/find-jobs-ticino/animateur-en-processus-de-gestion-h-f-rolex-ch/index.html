Role overview

Introduction For our Production Division, we are looking for a Support Production Technician (H/F) to deploy and ensure the application of a specific mode of animation to our activities in different production workshops.

Its role will be to support field teams so that they can appropriate this approach and implement it in accordance with the objectives defined.

He/she will evolve within a team of technicians competent in the field, who will accompany him/her in taking up his/her function.

Responsibilities The main tasks of the Support Production Technician (H/F) are to:

  • Train internal users and assist them in their development of skills in this specific mode of activity
  • Animate the relevant themes daily with field teams
  • Ensure a good understanding of the issues, as well as the appropriation of the knowledge and tools associated with them
  • Actively contribute to the administration of the device and the resolution of incidents
  • Participate in the dynamics of the system
  • Training of ES, Bachoror or equivalent technician
  • Professional experience of at least 3 to 5 years in an industrial environment, preferably in a field close to production
  • Good mastery of computer tools, including SAP and Excel
  • Initiative, with good analytical and synthesis skills
  • Ability to work as a team and ability to learn quickly
  • Excellent relational qualities, ease of learning Communication and customer service
  • Profile responsible, rigorous, organized, curious and open minded Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.
