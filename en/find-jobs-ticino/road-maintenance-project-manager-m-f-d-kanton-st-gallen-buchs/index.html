Original description

The street district inspectorate (SKI) Buchs operates 153 kilometres of cantonal roads – from Balgach along the Rhine Valley to Sargans and from Murg am Walensee to Bad Ragaz.

What to expect

  • Project and construction management of maintenance and construction projects
  • Representation of the building authority as well as control and coordination of external planning offices, specialists and companies
  • Preparation of tenders, monitoring of procurement procedures and ensuring complete project and final documentation
  • Coordination and active cooperation with municipalities, specialist bodies, authorities and enterprises
  • Responsibility for costs, deadlines, quality and risks of the allocated projects
  • Operational maintenance support

What sets it apart

  • Completed studies as a civil engineer (FH/ETH), technician (TS/HF) in the field of civil engineering or a comparable qualification with in-depth experience
  • Experience in project planning and construction management
  • Independent, structured working method and communication strength
  • Resilience and enforcement
  • Good IT user knowledge (MS Office) and driving licence category B
  • Readiness to take over the piquetting services
