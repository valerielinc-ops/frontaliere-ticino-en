Original description

When the last trains roll through the Bernese Oberland at night, your work often starts properly. With an overview, technical flair and a strong team, you will realize complex construction projects and renew the road where thousands of people travel every day. So the BLS remains reliably on track – today and tomorrow.

This is waiting for you

  • Carries out complex construction projects with a focus on the roadway and contributes to the planning of the execution
  • Together with specialist areas, coordinate railway technology
  • Assuming responsibility for costs, quality and deadlines in railway construction
  • Planning personnel, machines and intervals predictively and efficiently
  • Actively shape safety on construction sites and consistently implement standards

This is what you bring with you

  • Further training as a construction manager:in, technician:in HF or equivalent qualification
  • Use basic knowledge in roadways, automation or energy specifically
  • Safely coordinate different stakeholders with a clear presence
  • Confidently communicate in German – verbally and in writing
  • Be flexible along BLS lines with category B driving licence

Your advantages – you can look forward to

  • Get a GA 2nd class, a GA 1st. Class or a half-tax subscription.
  • Work where it suits you best. Whether in the home office or in the office.
  • Share your working time freely. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to buy an additional five to ten holiday days per year
  • Financial support for external further education as well as an exciting internal learning and course offer are at your disposal.
  • With seasonal events and courses we support your health.

About the BLS

With almost 4,000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. Moreover, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What connects us? The pride of what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And lots of exciting jobs.
