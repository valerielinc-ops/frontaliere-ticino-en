Original description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Join the first universal bank in the canton of Vaud, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Services division, we are looking for one or more:

Position Manager Announcement - Head of Service Your main missions:

  • ensure control of the bank's cash (day) and/or securities (monthly) positions with correspondents/depositories by means of the available tools while respecting the procedures specific to each correspondent/depositary
  • set up/analyse/optimize/use service performance indicators (statistics) and take all necessary corrective/informative measures and inform the hierarchy and line
  • master and develop the IT systems available to the business and the technical and legal directives/rules specific to the tasks entrusted. Ensure application/training/monitoring
  • participate actively by putting its expertise in its field of activity at the service, both of the bank's strategic projects and those relating to organisational and/or IT procedures and those directly affecting it
  • prevent problems and errors by actively monitoring specific situations and legal/business developments to propose/implement organisational and IT improvement measures
  • apply the procedures put in place for customer payment traffic operations that fall under the OBA-CBD rules, respecting the legal and technical (internal/external) constraints specific to its field of activity. Your profile:
  • training related to the field
  • Experience confirmed in a similar position
  • ability to define a vision and translate it into operational actions
  • excellent communication skills, relationship ease and stakeholder management
  • analytical capacity, high autonomy and rigour,
  • leadership, sense of responsibility, decision-making and Federated
  • excellent mastery of French, English C1 or C2 and German would be a plus

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write together three stories, that of your career, that of your bank, that of our region.
