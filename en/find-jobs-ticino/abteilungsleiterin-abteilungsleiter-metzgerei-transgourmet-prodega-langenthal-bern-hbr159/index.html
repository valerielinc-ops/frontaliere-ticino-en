Head of department / Head of department butchery

Transgourmet Schweiz AG — Langenthal, Bern, Svizzera

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional consulting and innovative services.Transgourmet Schweiz AG belongs to the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss delivery and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Tasks

  • You lead the department operationally according to business principles and implement the defined company and market performance processes
  • You support and develop employees, including the implementation of employee and holiday planning as well as horizontal support (TOJ)
  • You ensure compliance with internal and legal QS and ASIB guidelines as well as implementation of internal regulations and instructions
  • You maintain the flow of information and communication within the department, market and MCM
  • You build a relationship network with our customers
  • You are responsible for the daily sales readiness and presentation of goods, the implementation of sales activities according to annual sales planning and ensuring the availability of goods

Requirements

  • You already have leadership experience and bring several years of experience in a comparable function
  • You enjoy active customer contact
  • You are well-versed with the MS Office range
  • Exciting yourself with new challenges
  • You speak perfect German
  • You already have the advantage of forklift and/or drawbar training (SUVA standard)
