Role overview

University Clinic for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

The University Hospital for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy of the UPZ, as one of the leading institutions in Switzerland, provides outpatient, semi-inpatient and inpatient psychiatric care as well as emergency care for children and adolescents in the Canton of Bern as well as in parts of the Cantons of Solothurn and Freiburg.

As a university hospital, it also has an extended mandate in the supraregional specialized care as well as in teaching and research.
