The Position

Technical Product Owner - Legal - RDT Group Functions

We are looking for an experienced Technical Business Analyst and Product Owner to partner with Business Product Owners and stakeholders in Legal to translate requirements into strategic digital solutions. You will work closely with the IT Product Manager to shape the strategic vision, roadmap, and delivery of Legal-related products within Roche. This includes supporting platforms that span global teams across both Pharma and Diagnostics.

The Technical Business Analyst and Product Owner will play a key role in analyzing complex business processes, identifying optimization opportunities, and enabling the automation and digitization of workflows - ranging from review and approval to oversight, monitoring, and governance frameworks - while ensuring alignment with the "One Roche" philosophy.

About Our Team: RDT Legal Product

Sitting within the broader Legal, Compliance & Group Communication division, our team partners with the Legal department to enable efficiency and effectiveness through legal-specific solutions. Working hand-in-hand, we blend IT expertise with business know-how to support Legal at every step of their digital journey.

The Legal Product portfolio covers solutions for legal spend and matter management, document management, and contract management, as well as workflows and reporting. We handle the entire lifecycle of these solutions - whether we build (make) or buy, manage, or operate them.
