Original description

BLS is also a real estate company with a broad portfolio. Develop attractive real estate projects in central locations together with your team and create sustainable added value. We want to provide rail passengers with the best possible customer benefit and at the same time increase the value and income of our properties with professional real estate services. This is what is waiting for you:

  • Responsibility for the development and implementation of project and area developments
  • Personal and technical leadership of a team of specialists
  • Active stakeholder management including negotiations and maintaining contacts
  • Financial management and responsibility for real estate developments
  • Operational management and support of demanding projects This is what you bring with you:
  • Degree as an architect, spatial planner or real estate developer (FH, university)
  • Further business management training as well as knowledge of planning and construction law
  • Several years of experience in the Real estate development with in-depth knowledge of the SIA phases of planning and implementation
  • Experience in stakeholder management with communication skills as well as proven leadership skills
  • Negotiation skills in German and French Your advantages – you can look forward to this:
  • Receive a GA 2nd class, a GA 1st class or a half-fare subscription
  • Work where it suits you best. Whether in the home office or in the office
  • Arrange your working hours freely. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to purchase five to ten additional vacation days per year and to take a sabbatical of 40 working days every seven years.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses.
  • Financial support for external training, a leadership development program and an exciting internal learning and course offering are available to you. With almost 4,000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. What's more: We are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What connects us? The pride in what we do every day. For us, mobility has many facets. And lots of exciting jobs.
