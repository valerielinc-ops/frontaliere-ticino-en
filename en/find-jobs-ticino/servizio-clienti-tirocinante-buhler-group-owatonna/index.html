Original description

Sputtering Components Inc, a member of Buhler Group, is a growing business in Owatonna, Minnesota that has served customers all around the world for over 20 years. We are currently hiring for a highly motivated Customer Service Intern for the summer of 2027.
Every day, millions of people around the world come into contact with products made using SCI equipment. Our thin film technologies are used in vacuum coating systems to make windows, smartphones, display screens, solar panels, automobile components, decorative hardware and more.
Gain hands-on experience supporting a dynamic Field Service team while building skills in customer support operations, technical documentation, training development, and process improvement. You'll collaborate with cross-functional teams to create tools and resources that enhance service delivery, improve efficiency, and contribute to real business outcomes! Aufgaben: • Plan, script, record, edit, and maintain instructional or training videos in collaboration with technical subject-matter experts. • Review and improve manuals for clarity, structure, terminology, formatting, and overall usability. • Gather requirements, organize parts data, develop calculation logic, test outputs, document functionality, and maintain calculators. • Observe and document processes, consult process owners, create and revise SOPs, and ensure safety, quality, and document-control requirements are met. • Map workflows, identify inefficiencies and bottlenecks, analyze performance data, support improvement activities, help implement solutions, and monitor results. • Conduct workplace audits, coordinate 5S activities, develop visual controls, support employee training, assign improvement actions, and monitor results. Anforderungen: • Actively pursuing a Bachelor's degree in business, engineering, or a related field preferred. • Microsoft Office experience is required, specifically Excel. • Basic experience with video-editing software is helpful but not required. • Good communication, creativity, and organizational skills. • Strong writing, proofreading, and attention-to-detail skills. • Comfortable working with numbers, data, and basic Excel formulas. • Good writing, observation, organization, and teamwork skills. Must be willing to learn and document workplace processes. • Good problem-solving, analytical, and teamwork skills. • Organized, observant, and comfortable working with others. Two billion people eat food every day that was produced with Bühler equipment. One billion people drive vehicles whose parts were manufactured with our machines. Bühler aims to balance humanity, nature, and the economy in every decision as it develops solutions that unlock sustainable business opportunities in the global food, feed, and mobility industries. We strive to create innovations for a better world, with a special focus on healthy, safe, and sustainable solutions. Therefore, we team up with customers, start-ups, multinationals, and academia to accelerate impact together.
