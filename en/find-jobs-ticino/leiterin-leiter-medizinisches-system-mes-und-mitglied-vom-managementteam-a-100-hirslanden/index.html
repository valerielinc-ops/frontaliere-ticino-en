Original description

Location: Hirslanden Klinik Linde | Biel / Bienne

Occupation by 1 January 2027 or by arrangement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 73430

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments.

Clinic Linde is the leading private hospital in the Biel-Seeland-Jura area. With its high medical quality, its selected offers and the supra-regional reputation, it is the first choice for patients as well as for highly qualified medical doctors.

YOUR TASKS

  • Management and further development of the medical system (excluding nursing service) as well as personnel and professional management of subordinates
  • Operational and business responsibility including budget and cost responsibility for the medical system
  • Ensuring high medical quality and patient safety as well as further development of treatment standards and relevant quality measures
  • Optimization of processes, interfaces and structures as well as control by means of KPIs for a sustainable increase in productivity
  • Management of projects and medical panels, including chair of the MES panel, as well as further development of the organisation of structures and procedures
  • Active participation in the management team, performing the interface function between the medical profession, the clinic and the corporate office, completing the management picket service and participating in relevant clinic and group committees

Your profile

  • Completed higher education or studies in health, management, business administration or in a comparable field
  • Several years of professional and managerial experience in the health care sector, ideally in a hospital or clinic setting (medical training is desired, but not required)
  • Strong understanding of medical processes, interfaces and business relationships
  • Experience in the management and further development of teams as well as in process optimization, quality management and control via key figures
  • Strategic, structured and solution-oriented working method with high implementation strength as well as very good communication and leadership skills
  • Good knowledge of German and French
  • Further language skills are advantageous

Why does it work for us?

  • We work appreciatively and respectfully at eye level with each other
  • Possibility of working with flex and leisure plus models
  • Up to 4 additional free days per year as compensation for changing time (pension-dependent)
  • Above-average night and weekend allowance of CHF 10.- per hour
  • Many discounts and perks in the fitness center, over 100 service providers, leisure events and mobile subscriptions

Benefit as a Hirslanden employee: in many more benefits!

For additional information, Stefanie Ruckstuhl, Director, is available at stefanie.ruckstuhl@hirslanden.ch or Jennifer Perez at jennifer.perez@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.
