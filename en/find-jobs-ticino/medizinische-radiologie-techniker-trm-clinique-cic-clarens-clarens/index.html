CIC Switzerland Clinic is currently operating in two clinics in Clarens and Saxon. In order to strengthen its team, Clinique CIC Montreux is looking for an e: Medical Radiology Technician (TRM) 80 - 100% Within this function, the main tasks are:
  • Perform diagnostic imaging images on all service devices: MRI, CT, RX, MG, DEXA.
  • Produce diagnostic quality images.
  • Respect medical protocols and meet dosimetry and radiation safety standards.
  • Overall care for patients in collaboration with physicians.
  • Preparation and installation of patients for examinations (possing of venous route, injection of contrast product under the responsibility of the radiologist doctor, preparation of various sterile sets...).
  • Monitoring of various medical imaging protocols and evaluation of the images obtained.
  • Archiving and billing of examinations and equipment used (RIS and PACS).
  • Medical assistance in interventional radiology (ponction, biopsy, infiltration under scopy and scanner and ultrasons).
  • Monitor patient reactions. Profile desired:
  • To hold a TRM diploma or equivalent recognised by the Swiss Red Cross.
  • To justify several years of experience in a similar function.
  • MRI experience, with a preference for cardiac MRI expertise.
  • Scanner experience (CT), with preference for Siemens equipment.
  • Have strong IT skills.
  • Have an excellent command of French, both oral and written.
  • Demonstrate a strong team spirit and support. Actively contribute to maintaining a collaborative, respectful and caring work environment.
  • To be a proactive person, able to take initiatives and contribute to the continuous improvement of practices.
  • Demonstrate a strong sense of responsibility and organization.
  • Be motivated, dynamic and committed to the quality of patient care.
  • Have excellent relationship skills and benevolent communication with patients, colleagues and physicians.
  • Demonstrate flexibility, adaptability and a positive attitude to the challenges of everyday life. We offer you:
  • An attractive function in a dynamic sector.
  • The ability to evolve in a developing and human-sized group.
  • An attractive contingency fund.
  • A varied activity.
  • The opportunity to integrate a team invested, passionate and focused on continuous improvement.
