Original description

Project manager: in technology

Coop cooperative — Schafisheim, Aargau, Svizzera

Tasks

  • You lead challenging technical investment projects up to 10 million. CHF, from the first idea to the successful handover.
  • You keep an overview of planning, ordering, implementation and cost and appointment management at all times.
  • As a hub, you coordinate internal departments and external partners through all project phases.
  • You conduct contract negotiations with suppliers and prepare the corresponding documents.
  • With your concepts and preliminary projects, you create the basis for decision-making for site management.
  • You build and maintain sustainable supplier relationships.
  • In addition, you support the operational technical teams with your expertise.

Requirements

  • Completed training with further training as an engineer:in FH/uni or technician:in HF.
  • Several years of experience in managing technical projects, ideally in the food industry.
  • You convince with implementation strength, assertiveness and a quick comprehension.
  • Structured, goal-oriented and networked thinking sets you apart.
  • You act independently, but you also like to participate in the team.
  • With your communication and enthusiasm you create trust among all stakeholders.
  • Practical written and oral expression round off your profile.

  • Conditions of Employment: Pension Fund: 2/3 is paid by Coop
  • Working hours: 5 weeks holidays
  • Training: Over 600 internal training courses
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people and animals and nature with more than 400 actions
  • Communication: internal app for employees
  • Health: Update Fitness: Fr. 200.– Annual subscription discount
