Role overview

Introduction

For our Purchasing, Support & Infrastructure Division, we are looking for a (M/F) Occupational Safety Officer whose main mission will be to track "ergonomics" files in connection with our external ergonomism and the Health department.

  • Introduction
  • For our Purchasing, Support & Infrastructure Division, we are looking for a (M/F) Occupational Safety Officer whose main mission will be to track "ergonomics" files in connection with our external ergonomism and the Health department.
  • Ergonomic observations and analyses of workstations at different sites: production, assembly, quality control, logistics, maintenance and administrative activities

Main responsibilities

  • Ergonomic observations and analyses of workstations at different sites: production, assembly, quality control, logistics, maintenance and administrative activities
  • Identify risk factors related to MSDs, binding postures, handling, visual stresses and precision work
  • Propose, coordinate and monitor ergonomic improvement measures, taking into account the quality, productivity, safety and confidentiality requirements of the industrial environment
  • Participate in the design or transformation of workstations, in collaboration with the Production, Methods, Industrialization, Maintenance, Quality and Infrastructure teams
  • Ensure the operational link with external experts in ergonomics: preparation of interventions, transmission of necessary elements, coordination of field visits, follow-up of recommendations and steering of their implementation
  • Promote awareness-raising and training activities: ergonomics at the post, prevention of MSDs, etc Profile
  • Bachelor HES in occupational therapy, or equivalent
  • Confirmed experience of more than 3 years in ergonomics, prevention of MSDs, vocational rehabilitation in an industrial environment, ideally in the fields of microtechnical, watchmaking, electronics, medical or precision industry
  • Mastering the process of activity analysis and ergonomic evaluation of workplaces
  • Knowledge of biomechanics, handling, postural stresses and occupational risk prevention
