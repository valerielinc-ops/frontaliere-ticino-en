Role overview

Tasks

As a hair stylist, you ensure the professional implementation of the services taking into account the individual guest wishes. This also includes:

  • Support of regular and regular customers (hotel guests) along the entire service process; from consulting to implementation in the areas of cut, color and styling
  • Advising and selling exclusive hair care and styling products
  • Careful cash management and settlement in daily business
  • Support in the reception area and organization and maintenance of scheduling
  • Sovereign and solution-oriented handling of demanding guest feedback

Requirements

You have completed training as a Coiffeuse / Coiffeur EFZ and bring several years of professional experience in a demanding, ideally luxurious salon environment. In addition, you bring with you

  • Very good knowledge of German as well as good knowledge of English, other languages are advantageous
  • Safe handling of common PC applications and reservation systems
  • Strong passion for high-quality products, excellent styling and a keen sense of aesthetics and trends
  • Independent, structured and organized working methods with high quality standards
  • Respectful and discreet treatment of guests as well as a solution-oriented attitude
