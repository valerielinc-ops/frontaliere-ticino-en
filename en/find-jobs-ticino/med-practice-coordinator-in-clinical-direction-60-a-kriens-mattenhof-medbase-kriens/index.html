Original description

Welcome to Medbase Kriens Matthof

Our versatile, modern medical center is located directly at the S-Bahn stop Kriens Matthof. The Mikropole Matthof attracts with a wide range of sports, shopping and catering options. Around 30 employees from the disciplines of General Internal Medicine, Endocrinology and Diabetology as well as OHAMology with Orthooptics and ORL work together interprofessionally. In addition, we carry out an audience pharmacy integrated into the Medical Center. We maintain a close cooperation with our partners LUKS, Spital Nidwalden, Oviva as well as Medbase Luzern Allmend.

At Medbase Kriens Matthof we pay attention to a balanced work-life balance. Working in the part-time shop is possible in all functions and the working hours can be flexibly designed.

You can also use your knowledge and skills as a medical practice coordinator/in clinical direction in the care of our diabetics. Medbase offers you a dynamic team that attaches importance to quality and collegiality. We are looking for support for the working days Wednesday, Thursday and Friday.

  • Terminology and documentation, performance recording by means of Axenita
