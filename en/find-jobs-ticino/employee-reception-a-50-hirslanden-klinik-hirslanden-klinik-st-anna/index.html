Location: Hirslanden Clinic St. Anna | Lucerne

Occupation by: immediately or by agreement

You may welcome people – with a smile that makes the difference?

Then you are exactly right with us! At Klinik St. Anna you are part of a warm team that welcomes patients and visitors with empathy and professionalism. You not only welcome guests – you accompany people in important moments of their lives and actively shape their first impressions.

YOUR TASKS

  • You are our first business card: warm welcome on the phone, by mail and on site
  • Check-in and administrative processing for inpatient and outpatient patients
  • Professional management of payments, mail, cash and internal processes
  • Collaboration in internal processes such as pikett services lists, employee offers or process improvements
  • Collaboration with colleagues from different areas – always with the aim that our patients feel well looked after

Your profile

  • Basic commercial education and several years of experience at the reception, ideally in the hospital or in the upscale hotel industry
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English are a must-see criterion, Italian knowledge a big plus. All other foreign languages are beneficial.
  • Joy of direct contact with guests, resilience and a professional appearance even in hectic situations
  • Organizational talent, flexibility and IT affinity
  • Openness to contribute your own ideas and actively shape processes

Our offer

  • A warm, family team that supports and welcomes you
  • A workplace where appreciation and team spirit are not phrases
  • An attractive mobility concept and additional financial benefits for parents
  • Up to 4 extra free days per year thanks to paid changing time
  • For only CHF 5.00 a day you are provided with a meal in our varied staff restaurant as well as coffee, tea, mineral and seasonal fruits.
  • Further employment conditions and offers can be found here
