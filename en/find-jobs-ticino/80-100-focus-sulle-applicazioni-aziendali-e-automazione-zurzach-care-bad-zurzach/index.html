Role overview

Your chance to actively shape our cloud applications

You know applications of HR, FM, finance or marketing? Do you want to accelerate the development, integration and automation of digital solutions through modern platforms, DevOps and AI?

  • You are responsible for our business applications: Together with your colleagues, you develop and operate our central corporate applications in the areas of HR, FM, finance and marketing
  • You develop business apps: With Power Apps, Copilot Studio and other low-code and no-code technologies, you create and manage practical solutions for the specialist areas
  • You are close to everyday business: You work closely with our company’s specialists and translate professional requirements into functional solutions
  • You automate business processes: With modern workflow and automation platforms, you identify and realize efficiency potentials along company processes
  • You ensure end-to-end integration: You design, implement and manage API-based interfaces between cloud applications, third-party systems and internal platforms
  • Develop the platform further: Using infrastructure as code and platform automation, you contribute to a standardized, secure and scalable cloud landscape
  • You provide stability: You provide 2nd-level support, analyze disruptions and ensure reliable operation
  • You establish modern DevOps practices: You automate build, test and deployment processes of our business applications and support the establishment of efficient development and deployment processes
