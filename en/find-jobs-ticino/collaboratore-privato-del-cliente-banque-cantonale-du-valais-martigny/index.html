Do you have commercial training and have initial experience in the banking and/or administrative world? If you are looking for an opportunity to leverage your skills and potentially move into customer advisory activities, this position is your chance.

Concretely?

  • You will assist private customer advisors with advice and sales support activities

Are you tempted by the challenge? This position is for you if…

  • You have commercial training with excellent interpersonal skills
  • Dynamic and pragmatic, you benefit from a strong sense of initiative and enjoy teamwork.
  • Mastery of IT tools, you have strong analytical skills
  • You are able to manage your priorities and work independently
