Role overview

The LANDI cooperatives support Swiss farmers in the economic development of their farms.

Together with the brands Volg, TopShop and AGROLA, the LANDI cooperatives also supply the population in rural Switzerland with food, everyday goods and energy.The LANDI Bucheggberg-Landshut cooperative is active in the fields of retail trade, agriculture, gastronomy and energy business.

At the Bätterkinden, Lohn-Ammannsegg, Utzenstorf and Fraubrunnen locations, LANDI Bucheggberg-Landshut is present on the market with LANDI shops, shop & bistro, agricultural center, drying plant, grain collection points, petrol stations and a TakeAway restaurant as a regional company with around 95 employees.For our Blitz-Chuchi in 4573 Lohn-Ammannsegg we are looking for an open, reliable and flexible personality who likes to work with people, thinks and takes responsibility.
