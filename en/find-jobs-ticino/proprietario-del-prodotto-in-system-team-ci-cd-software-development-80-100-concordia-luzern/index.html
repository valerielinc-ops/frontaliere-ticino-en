Role overview

You do not want to simply develop an existing product, but build a new technical capability for our entire IT?

With your passion for software development, automation and CI/CD, as the technically-savvy product owner of the system team, you shape our technical foundations, platforms and standards for software development and modern IT operations.

Application process

  • In a first phase, your focus is on three central topics:
  • You are responsible for setting up and further developing standardized CI/CD pipelines for our central technology stacks.
  • Together with the participants, you define binding minimum standards for code, security and automation.
  • You support the teams in dealing with pipelines and standards and ensure that they are anchored in an understandable, usable and effective way.
  • In a next step, you will develop the System Team further into a central enabler for further parts of the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC):
  • You drive automation through the entire development and delivery process.
  • You promote professional software development with clear standards, effective automation and a high level of maturity.
