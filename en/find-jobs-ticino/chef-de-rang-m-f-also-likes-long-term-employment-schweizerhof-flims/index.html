Original description

Chef de Rang 100% (m/f/d) Chef de Rang (m/f/d) as of December or by appointment Hosting is in your blood and quality of service is more than a standard for you? Then you are exactly right with us. Our traditional family business stands for warmth, passion and personal responsibility. With us, not only professional competence counts, but above all personality – an open appearance, joy in dealing with people and the proverbial heart in the right place. Our guests are as diverse as our everyday life: from marriage proposals and weddings to incentive travelers to family holidays. We don't get bored. In the summer and winter season, you work together with around 50 dedicated and charming hosts. Several times in succession we were voted the 75 best winter hotels and the 100 best holiday hotels in Switzerland. We see these awards as an incentive to continuously question and redefine our service. Our goal is to always surprise our guests with a real WOW moment.

  • In this position, you take responsibility for your own service station and together with the team ensure a first-class guest experience.
  • Your tasks: Independent management of a service station in the half board and à-la-carte service Professional, heartfelt support and advice of guests Expert advice of food, drinks and wines Guidance and support of commis de rank and learners Ensuring a smooth service flow and compliance with service standards Cooperation in banquets, weddings and events Preparation and follow-up (Mise en Place) of the service area Compliance with hygiene and quality guidelines (HACCP) This is what distinguishes you: warm host personality with charisma and real pleasure in the profession Education and / or experience in gastronomy Very good knowledge of German, other languages are beneficial Positive, open and friendly personality Teamplayer with creativity and tranquility – even in lively situations Therefore you should come to the Schweizerhof: We are a dynamic, international hotel with more than one hundred years of family tradition – with us experience, heart and modern hospitality combine. Enjoy an attractive workplace in the dreamlike destination Flims Laax Falera, with numerous leisure opportunities right on your doorstep. Benefit from exciting benefits, in-house discounts and special conditions for overnight stays at our partners of Romantik Hotels International and Swiss Historic Hotels. Experience a family working atmosphere characterized by respect, appreciation and a strong team spirit. We promote your personal and professional development through targeted further education and training. All hosts are welcome to participate in our active weekly programs (Yoga, Pilates, Shinrin Yoku...) and use the indoor pool for relaxation in the evening. On Sundays, the restaurant remains closed in the evening (high season excluded), which allows attractive and guaranteed Fridays. Of course, you have two free days per week. We are happy to tell you more about this responsible position and the philosophy of our company. We look forward to meeting you! Send us your complete application documents in German (motivation letter, CV and certificates). Unfortunately, we cannot consider incomplete applications!
