Company and context

  • The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required.
  • Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, the EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing over 600,000 outpatients.
  • The Regional Hospital of Lugano is the heart of acute medicine for Italian Switzerland, a training and university research hub where clinical excellence, research and training intertwine.
  • To complete our team of doctors of the Pneumology Service we look for a specialist doctor (100%) Profile: EOC Pneumology is a dynamic and integrated service, present on 4 cantonal locations with 15 specialists, covering the entire spectrum of respiratory diseases and sleep medicine.
  • The Regional Hospital of Lugano is the centre of reference, where the most complex and innovative activities are concentrated.
  • We offer a varied and stimulating clinical work, articulated among clinics at the Italian Hospital, endoscopic activity, decency and consultations at the Civic Hospital, flanked by the inclusion in a committed and multidisciplinary team, characterized by strong collaborative spirit and constant openness to innovation.
  • The candidate/ideal will take care of patients with respiratory disorders, ventilating sleeping disorders and perform non-invasive and invasive bronchoscopy and instrumental examinations.
  • It will also be available to make pneumology spikes for out of time consultations from all EOC locations.
  • The role plays a key role within a transversal organization, offering concrete opportunities to actively contribute to the strategic development of the Service, with margins of growth and real responsibility.
