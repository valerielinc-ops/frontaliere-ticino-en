Company and context

  • As a key member of the Clinical Dermatology Team, the successful candidate will work closely with and report to the Head of Clinical Dermatology and contribute to the daily organization and development of the service.
  • The position includes independent specialist-level clinical work, with responsibility for assigned cases from consultation to follow-up, including appropriate documentation and communication with owners and referring veterinarians.
  • The candidate will contribute to the supervision of the dermatology resident and to the clinical and didactic teaching of veterinary students, residents, interns and technical staff.
  • The main clinical focus is companion-animal and equine dermatology, with occasional involvement in large-animal, exotic and zoo dermatology.
  • Participation in selected clinical research projects and academic collaborations is expected.
  • We are looking for a collaborative colleague with good communication skills who is willing to contribute to the continuous improvement of the Dermatology Service.
  • Salaries will be commensurate with professional and academic credentials and experience.
