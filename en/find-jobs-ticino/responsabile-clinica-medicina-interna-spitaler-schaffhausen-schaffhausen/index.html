Role overview

As a category A continuing education clinic for general internal medicine, we combine broad clinical care with a high level of specialist specialisation and a dynamic interdisciplinary environment.

Every year we look after around 3,500 inpatients.

In our interdisciplinary emergency center, we also provide over 30,000 patients per year - versatile and with high medical quality.

  • Every year we look after around 3,500 inpatients.

Company and context

  • You contribute to budget, forecast, controlling and reporting and create a sound basis for decision-making with meaningful analyses
  • You take responsibility for interdisciplinary projects as well as transformation and organizational development processes and accompany them from the idea to implementation
