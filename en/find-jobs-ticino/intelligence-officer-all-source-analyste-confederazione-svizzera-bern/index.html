Original description

  • Using versatile analytical methods, gather, and evaluate information from complex cases.
  • Identification and assessment of threats to Switzerland’s security interests by networks of actors and their activities. Recognize trends in the development of these threats within Switzerland.
  • Preparation of various types of reports and conducting briefings for clients and partners of the NDB.
  • Support the strategic governance of intelligence investigation and analysis, in particular through participation in working groups and coordination projects within the NDB and between the NDB and its national partners.
  • Ensure cooperation and relationship maintenance with partners of the NDB in Switzerland and abroad.
  • University degree at least at master level, or proven experience as a criminal investigator, or as a professional military officer with intelligence experience. Broad knowledge on national and international security policy topics. Ideally, professional experience in the field of intelligence services and/or other security areas of the Federal Administration, including operational aspects. Profiles from other sectors can also be considered.
  • Investigative and methodological skills, ability to structure and make comprehensible complex and heterogeneous data, excellent analytical and editorial skills, ability to deliver presentations to a demanding audience.
  • Teamwork, ability to work with different interlocutors, openness to other opinions and cultures.
  • Balanced, discreet and professional personality. Adaptability, ability to cope with high workloads even within short deadlines.
  • Swiss citizen with impeccable reputation
  • Good active knowledge in two official languages orally and in writing; ideally good knowledge of the third official language. Very good oral and written knowledge in English. Russian, Chinese or Farsi are an advantage.
