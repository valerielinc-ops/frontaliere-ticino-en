Role overview

The North Face EMEA is looking for an exceptional Senior Manager, Retail Marketing to join our Marketing team in Stabio, Switzerland. The Role

As Senior Manager, Retail Marketing, you will lead the translation of brand strategy into impactful retail experiences across EMEA.

This role sits at the intersection of brand, consumer, and commercial priorities, ensuring that retail remains a powerful platform to connect with consumers and bring the brand to life consistently across all touchpoints.
