Original description

Tasks

  • Supporting our key account managers in sales discussions and assuming a central role in strategic and professional customer consulting around the Brack Business Cloud Service Platform
  • Ensuring competent, solution-oriented and sustainable consulting in the area of Modern Work & Cloud, both remotely and on-site at the customer
  • Analysis of customer requirements, evaluation of possible solutions and derivation of sound recommendations for implementation
  • Development of suitable offers, accompaniment of the sales process until successful completion and co-responsibility for sales, conversion rate and the technical implementation of the projects
  • Maintaining close relationships with our partners in particular on Pax8, identifying market trends early and deriving new business opportunities
  • Ensuring data quality in our CRM and ERP systems and independent maintenance of your projects
  • Actively contribute your knowledge to customer marketing campaigns, partner training, customer events and internal training
  • Through regular training and relevant certifications, always stay up to date with the latest technology
  • Close cooperation with sales, product management and marketing, as well as other internal teams and contribute with your know-how to the further development of the area

Requirements

  • Commercial or technical basic training with further training in sales, marketing or ICT (ideally higher technical school or comparable qualification)
  • Several years of experience in account management in the field of ICT cloud solutions, in sales and / or in consulting / consulting as specialised sales
  • You convince through strong consulting skills, negotiation skills and final certainty as well as profound experience in the development of complex offers and contracts
  • Secure handling of IT infrastructures, CRM and ERP systems
  • Confident knowledge of German, good knowledge of English and French
  • Professional appearance with high communication strength and negotiating skills
  • Structured, solution-oriented working method, personal responsibility and teamwork
  • Willingness to travel up to 60% within Switzerland

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.
