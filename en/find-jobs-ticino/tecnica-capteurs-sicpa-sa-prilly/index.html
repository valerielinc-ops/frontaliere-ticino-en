Role overview

The Sensor Technician is a technical contributor within the Machine-Readable Features Laboratory, supporting the assembly, integration, testing, and maintenance of advanced sensor systems used for machine-readable authentication and traceability solutions.

The role covers a broad range of hands-on technical activities including PCB assembly, soldering, wiring, electro-mechanical integration, sensor assembly, troubleshooting, and laboratory support.

Working closely with electronics engineers, software engineers, optics specialists, chemists, and scientists, the Sensor Technician contributes to the construction and validation of prototypes, demonstrators, and industrial sensor systems.
