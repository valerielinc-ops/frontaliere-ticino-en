About the role

Job Summary The Sales Engineering Specialist supports the sales process by providing technical expertise and helping customers understand how products meet their needs. Typically coaches and reviews the work of lower level professionals. Summary of the post

We are looking for a passionate Technico-Commercial Engineer to join our team at Sulzer Ensival Moret France SASU. The incumbent of the position will be responsible for the promotion and sale of our technical solutions, as well as the development and maintenance of customer relations in a defined territory.

Post responsibilities

  • Develop and implement sales strategies to achieve sales and margin targets.
  • Identify and explore potential new customers in the sectors targeted by Sulzer Ensival Moret France SASU.
  • Understand the technical needs of customers and offer tailored solutions among our range of products and services (pumps, agitators, etc.).
  • Prepare and submit detailed technical and commercial proposals.
  • Negotiate contracts and terms and conditions of sale with customers.
  • Ensure regular monitoring of existing customers to ensure their satisfaction and identify new opportunities.
  • Work closely with the technical and production teams to ensure that the proposed solutions are achievable and meet customer expectations.
  • Attend trade shows and industry events.
  • Conduct business activity reports and sales forecasts.
  • Ensure compliance with company policies and procedures.

Qualifications required

  • Degree in engineering (BAC+5) in mechanics, electromechanical, process engineering or equivalent technical field.
  • Significant experience (minimum 3-5 years) in a technical-commercial engineering role, ideally in industrial equipment, pumps or rotating machines.
  • Strong technical skills and understanding of the operating principles of industrial equipment.
  • Excellent communication, negotiation and presentation skills.
  • Ability to establish and maintain strong professional relationships.
  • Autonomy, proactivity and orientation results.
  • Mastery of French and English (written and oral).
  • Valid driver's license and availability for frequent travel.
  • Mastery of computer tools (CRM, Pack Office).
