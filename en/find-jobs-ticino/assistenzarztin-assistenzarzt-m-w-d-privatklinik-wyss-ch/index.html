Role overview

Privatklinik Wyss AG is a leading specialist clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy.

Special areas of expertise include the treatment of depression including burnout as well as anxiety and obsessive-compulsive disorders.

The clinic offers its outpatient, daily clinical and inpatient services in the region of Bern, Münchenbuchsee and Biel and employs around 340 employees at all three locations.

  • This is what your professional day looks like with us in this position Together with the chief physician, you become involved in the supervision and teaching of case-leading psychologists and assistant doctors, take over case management yourself and coordinate psychiatric diagnosis and treatments.
  • You are responsible for the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic patient treatment in the individual setting e.g. on the ward for supplementary insured persons and work closely with an interprofessional team.
  • In the absence of the chief physician, you represent the professional management of the private department (25 beds).

  • Background services as well as networking and public relations work round off your versatile field of activity.
  • You have a completed state examination in medicine (CH) or an equivalent qualification from the EU/EFTA as well as a specialist qualification in psychiatry and psychotherapy.
