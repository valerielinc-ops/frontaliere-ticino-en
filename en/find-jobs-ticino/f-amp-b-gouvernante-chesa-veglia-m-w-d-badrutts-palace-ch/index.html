Original description

Your key responsibilities

  • You are responsible for the dishwashing and cleaning operations across all areas of Chesa Veglia, ensuring the highest standards of hygiene are delivered by the team.

  • You plan, coordinate, and actively supervise daily dishwashing and cleaning tasks, as well as seasonal setup and closing activities.

  • You manage and oversee the work schedules of the dishwashing team, ensuring efficient staffing and smooth operations.

  • You lead, train, and support a team of 8, maintaining a hands-on presence in daily operations.

  • You are responsible for inventory and stock control of detergents, dishwashing supplies, and operational materials, ensuring availability and cost control.

  • You ensure strict compliance with hygiene and safety standards (HACCP) and work closely with the kitchen team to maintain efficient back-of-house operations.

What you bring to the job

For this responsible position, we are seeking candidates who bring the following:

  • Experience in a leadership role within cleaning or dishwashing operations, preferably in a high-standard or luxury environment.

  • A high level of organizational skills, flexibility, and attention to detail.

  • Very good communication skills in Italian and English; additional languages are an advantage.

  • Swiss or EU citizenship only, due to work permit regulations.

Our offer

  • Seasonal position from mid-June to mid-September 2026, with the option for future seasons.

  • 5-day work week, averaging 43.5 hours.

  • 5 weeks of vacation and 6 public holidays per year.

  • 13th salary from the start date (pro rata temporis).

  • Possibility of accommodation in one of our attractive staff residences.

  • Meals available in our modern staff restaurant.

  • Work in a dynamic and international environment with opportunities for development.

If you have a passion for perfection and a keen eye for detail, and would like to be part of a motivated team, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
