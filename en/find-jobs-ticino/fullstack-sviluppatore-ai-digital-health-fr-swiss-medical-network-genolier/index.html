Application process

  • You act as a technical bridge between medical requirements and advanced technologies, ensuring that our digital health applications are secure, scalable and user-friendly. Your area of activity
  • Development & Solutions Architecture: Design and implementation of scalable Fullstack (Frontend & Backend) web applications for clinical and administrative use.
  • AI integration: Development and integration of machine learning models (Machine Learning), LLM (Large Language Models) and deterministic AI services (explainable AI) into existing and new applications to support diagnostic or administrative processes.
