Role overview

Mission

The National Centre of Competence in Research (NCCR) Separations, hosted by EPFL and funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation, unites leading Swiss institutions to transform separation science for environmental and energy sustainability.

A cornerstone of the NCCR's long-term vision is the creation of the Materials Centre for Advanced Separations (MCAS), a shared national platform that will provide academic, government, and industrial researchers with access to the infrastructure, tools, and databases developed within the NCCR, and will ensure the longevity of its activities beyond the funding period.

Main responsibilities

  • Main duties and responsibilities
  • Develop the strategic plan for the launch of MCAS, intially focused on the EPFL Valais Wallis site, including its mission, governance structure, access model, service portfolio, and operating model.
  • Design a financial sustainability model, including cost structures, pricing of services, and diversified funding streams from public and private sources.
  • Conduct stakeholder mapping and needs assessments across academia, industry, and government, and translate findings into the MCAS value proposition.
  • Coordinate with EPFL services (technology transfer, legal, finance, infrastructure) and with NCCR partner institutions to align the centre's setup with institutional frameworks.
  • Map the landscape of related Swiss research infrastructures and identify opportunities for future strategic alignment and partnerships beyond EPFL.
  • Establish a phased launch roadmap aligned with the equipment commissioning schedule, including clear milestones, resource requirements, and risk management.
  • Support industry engagement and partnership development in coordination with the NCCR's knowledge and technology transfer activities.
  • Prepare strategic documents, business plans, and presentations for the NCCR Management, EPFL leadership, and funding bodies. Profile
