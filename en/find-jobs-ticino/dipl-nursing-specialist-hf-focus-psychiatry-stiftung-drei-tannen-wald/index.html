Original description

together with heart – dynamically on the road Spitex Rothenburg offers services for the approximately 8,000 inhabitants of the municipality of Rothenburg in the areas of somatic care, outpatient psychiatric care and care as well as housekeeping. The nursing assignments in Haus & Hof Hermolingen with about 36 residents and the provision of psychiatric services in an extended environment round off our Spitex operation and make it something special. As of January 1, 2027, we are joining forces in the Sempachersee region and starting into the future together as a new, modern and future-oriented organization Spitex Sempachersee Plus AG. In order to strengthen our psychiatry team at the Rothenburg base, we are looking for a committed Dipl. Nurse HF Psychiatry 40 - 90 % Their tasks

  • Professional and holistic care and care for people with mental illness – in their usual home
  • Assuming case responsibility and managing the entire care process, including needs assessments according to InterRAI-CMH
  • Active cooperation with everyone involved in the care process
  • If necessary, advise the employees of all professional groups from the somatic team
  • In the psychiatry team independent planning and coordination of the various tasks and client assignments
  • Co-shaping the further development of the dynamic department of psychiatry

They bring with them

  • Diploma as a nurse HF / FH psychiatry
  • At least 2 years of professional experience in the department of psychiatry to meet the legal requirements in order to be able to make clarifications of needs independently
  • Experience / interest in child and adolescent psychiatry desirable
  • High resilience and a great sense of responsibility
  • Independent, structured and forward-looking working method
  • Solution- and resource-oriented basic attitude
  • Teamwork
  • Private car or scooter
  • Through your open, cheerful nature and clear communication, you will find easy access to the Spitex team and all other participants

We offer them

  • Working days Monday – Friday, fixed days possible
  • Careful familiarisation with the task area
  • Motivated and helpful team
  • Regular expert advice / supervision by external person and participation in the regional network psychiatry
  • Professional and personal development and training opportunities
  • Appreciative farm culture
  • 5 weeks holiday, from the 50th. Birthday 6 weeks
