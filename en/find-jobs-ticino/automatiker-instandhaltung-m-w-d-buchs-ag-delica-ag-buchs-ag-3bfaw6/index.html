Main responsibilities

  • You take responsibility for the electrical and automation support of projects and actively contribute to their success.
  • You carry out smaller projects independently, successfully implement them and actively drive the optimization and automation of our processes.
  • You train learners to become automaticians and pass on your expertise.
  • You identify preventive measures and evaluate existing strategies to maximize the availability of our production lines.
  • You are jointly responsible for carrying out maintenance and repair work on our production facilities and carry out maintenance and calibration work on various production lines.
  • You carefully document all work carried out in the maintenance system (SAP-PM). Requirements
  • Several years of professional experience: in the field of maintenance in an industrial production environment
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): in the electrotechnical sector (e.g. electrician, automatic engineer, electric installer) or equivalent training (4 years)
  • Technical know-how, understanding in electrical and control engineering as well as programming knowledge of PLC controls
  • High level of problem-solving competence and forward-looking thinking as well as respectful handling and pronounced customer orientation Experience in the training of learners
