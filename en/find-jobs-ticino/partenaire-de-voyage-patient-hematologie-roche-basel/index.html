Company and context

  • The Patient Journey Partner (PJP) is at the center of the new Roche organization, bringing in actionable customer insights to the organization to ensure value creation for patients, the ecosystem, and Roche.
  • What is the impact of a Patient Journey Partner?
  • ● The PJP is an empowered leader operating within a cross-functional business team and the local face of Roche.
  • S/He partners closely with all stakeholders directly touching the patient journey in the ecosystem, gaining a deep understanding of their needs to connect Roche’s capabilities to co-create meaningful solutions that support the triple-win ambition.
  • ● The PJP role focuses on in-depth scientific discussions, product promotion, and partnerships/projects.
  • ● The PJP stewards Roche’s products through their lifecycle and supports their appropriate use, which includes sharing their merits with HCPs in a truthful and balanced manner.
  • Product discussions form the basis and help us to understand the journey of patients going through treatment.
  • ● Profoundly understand the ecosystem (both public and private) needs and co-create solutions with external stakeholders.
  • ● Establish trust & credibility with ecosystems based on partnerships and a common purpose for better access to novel care.
  • ● Educate & provide targeted information to stakeholders, enabling them to make informed decisions.

Additional details
