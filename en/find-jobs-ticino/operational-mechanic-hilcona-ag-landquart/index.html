Role overview

You keep our production going – and find solutions when others get stuck?

Our production places high demands on the technical availability and reliability of our plants.

We are looking for an independent, solution-oriented and resilient personality that quickly analyzes technical disruptions, sustainably fixes causes and ensures that our production and supply facilities function reliably.

Main responsibilities

  • Ensuring the technical availability and operational readiness of our production and supply facilities
  • Systematic analysis, localisation and sustainable repair of mechanical failures and technical failures
  • Carrying out maintenance, maintenance and repair work – independently and together with internal and external partners
  • Rapid and targeted intervention in the event of plant failures to minimise downtime
  • Identification of weaknesses and implementation of technical optimizations and sustainable improvement measures
  • Active participation in conversions, technical projects and plant improvements
  • Support for the continuous improvement of plant availability, process reliability and maintenance processes Requirements:
  • Completed training as business mechanic, polymechanic or equivalent technical training EFZ
  • Several years of experience in industrial maintenance, ideally in a production plant
  • In-depth knowledge of mechanical fault analysis and troubleshooting.
