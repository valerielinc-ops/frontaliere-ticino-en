What you bring. - CFC holder in an electrical field with several years of experience in the construction of contact lines (catenary). Experience and knowledge of maintenance processes and operating conditions in the contact line area are essential. - Strong sense of responsibility and high sensitivity to security issues, good resistance to stress.

Experience in field team management essential.

  • Easy use of computer tools, good health and optimal hearing and visual faculties.
  • Home near the workplace (possibility of getting there in 30-45 minutes).
  • B driving license required.
