Role overview

The Nestlé Product Technology Centre Coffee (NPTC Coffee) is the leading R&D organization that discovers, develops and deploys coffee and beverage products and technologies for in-home and out-of-home consumption. It consists of units in Konolfingen (CH), Marysville (USA), Orbe (CH) and Singapore and provides new products and processes to all Nestlé markets worldwide.

Our mission – to delight the consumer wherever, whenever, however by delivering moments of genuine pleasure and indulgence - drives the need for us to attract, select and develop the right people for the right jobs throughout the value chain of our business.

Company: Nestlé Product Technology Centre Coffee Act. Rate: Full-Time Act. Rate 100% Type of contract: Permanent contract

As a Coffee Automation Digital Engineer , you will lead and drive digital technology exploration and technology transfer to industrialization within the coffee category, ensuring the integration of innovative solutions that enhance operational efficiency and product development. This is an amazing opportunity to drive innovation and have a direct impact on advancing the digital and automation agenda for the manufacturing technologies of Nestlé at global scale.

A Day in the Life of a Coffee Automation Digital Engineer

  • Serve as the technical expert on state of the art digital and automation tools and technologies used in coffee and build organisational capability through the train the trainer approach.
  • Lead the exploration and development of digital capabilities in collaboration with IT, Zones, and technology partners to continuously evolve the Digital & Automation roadmap for coffee.
  • Oversee the deployment and adoption of the Digital & Automation roadmap across Zones and operations, ensuring effective technology transfer and sustainable implementation.
  • Drive and technically evaluate proofs of concept, assessing improvements and supporting the successful scale up of validated technologies.
  • Establish and maintain digital and automation technology standards, ensuring alignment across Zones and promoting best practice sharing throughout the coffee manufacturing network.
