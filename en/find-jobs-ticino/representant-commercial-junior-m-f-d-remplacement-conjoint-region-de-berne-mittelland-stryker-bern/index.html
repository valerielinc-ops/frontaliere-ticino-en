Original description

Work Flexibility: Field-based

Do you want to combine the entry into medical technology sales with innovative products that make a real difference for patients?

In this role, you will have the opportunity to establish an exciting sales area in the Bern-Mittelland region, further develop customer relationships in the Swiss healthcare sector and, together with experienced teams, advance modern solutions in the field of joint replacement and maco-robotics.

This is what awaits you

  • Take over the sales activities for innovative hip and knee implants as well as our Mako-Robotics system from the Joint Replacement product division in the Bern-Mittelland region in Switzerland.
  • Responsibility for the operational and strategic management of the sales area as well as the sustainable expansion of existing customer relationships.
  • Acquisition of new customers and support of existing contacts around the use of our innovative product portfolio.
  • Implementation of targeted sales and marketing strategies as well as negotiations with surgical management, chief physicians, clinic administrations, medical technology and purchasing departments.
  • Advice and training of medical and nursing staff in the application of our products in the operating room as well as in workshops and test settings.
  • Development of demand-oriented customer solutions and creation of financing plans in close cooperation with the Mako team.
  • Participation in trade fairs, congresses and business meetings, identification of potential new customers and expansion of the Swiss healthcare network.

This sets you apart

Required

  • Initial sales experience, ideally 1–2 years in medical technology, in the clinic or operating environment or in a comparable consulting-intensive industry environment.
  • Enthusiasm for the distribution of a complex, innovative product portfolio with direct added value for patients.
  • Initial experience in building long-term customer relationships, developing existing customers and generating new business.
  • Communication strength, initiative, willingness to learn as well as an open and winning way of dealing with different stakeholders.
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, solution orientation, strategic foresight and a very good self-organization.
  • Possession of a valid car driving licence.
  • Flowing German and good English skills in word and writing.

Preferably

  • Completed studies or a comparable qualification, e.g. in medical technology, engineering, health management or in the medical-technical field.
  • knowledge in the field of orthopaedics or surgical experience, e.g. as surgical assistant (OTA), surgical management or in a similar function within the clinical or medical technology environment.
  • Good knowledge of French in word and writing is beneficial.

Diversity is important to us. We welcome applications from people regardless of their ethnic, national or social origin, gender, disability, age or sexual identity.

Additional information

  • Travel with company cars in the sales area can amount to up to 80%, including occasional external overnight stays.
  • Depending on the planning of the working week, the remaining time is in the home office.
