Original description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our Analytical Development Team in the Early Phase area, we are looking for a dedicated scientist (a) 100% with in-depth expertise in the method development of peptides.

Their tasks

  • As a committed and motivated personality, you are part of our agile QC Early Phase Development Group.
  • You can expect a wide range of tasks and questions in a dynamic and exciting environment.
  • In this varied function, you will independently develop (U)HPLC methods for early and late-phase projects in peptide analysis.
  • Another focus is the development of mass spectrometry methods to identify and quantify impurities for peptides.
  • You include substance-specific information in the method optimization.
  • Their robust methods form the basis for validations and approvals in the GMP area.
  • You represent your work and the group in interdisciplinary teams as well as external contacts.
  • In addition, you support us and your colleagues with your open-minded and friendly manner and are happy to actively contribute your expertise to the team.

Your profile

  • Completed studies in chemistry, pharmacy or a related scientific field
  • In-depth experience in instrumental analysis with a focus on chromatography
  • Hands-on experience in HR mass spectrometry
  • Expertise in (U)HPLC method development and validation
  • Expertise in data evaluation with Chromeleon or other CDS
  • You think innovatively and look for optimization potential when changing
  • Reliable personality with enthusiasm for responsibility and initiative
  • Quality-conscious and exact working method
  • Appreciative and team-oriented personality
  • Good knowledge of German and English
  • Hands-on experience in dealing with thermal systems
  • Knowledge in the analysis and/or synthesis of peptides / Biologics
  • Relevant professional experience in the chemical/pharma environment
