Original description

Bühler (China) Investment Co., Ltd. established in 2011 in Wuxi, Jiangsu Province, is the group headquarters of the Swiss Bühler Group in Greater China and the first integrated headquarters of multinational companies in Jiangsu Province. With our advanced technology, we lead the market in grain handling and processing, consumer food manufacturing, and die-casting, grinding and dispersion. With our vision of "Innovations for a better world", we are committed to developing more innovative solutions by leveraging the global resources of the Bühler Group and taking advantage of localization.

Aufgaben

  • Manage sourcing activities of indirect materials, including MICE、Capital Expense, MRO, facility management, IT and HR services
  • Responsible for sourcing and supplier management, continually optimize supply base to get competitive supplier base for indirect materials
  • Work with regional category manager in building and implementing sourcing and category strategy
  • Lead cross function team to complete potential supplier assessment and audit, and supplier performance improvement with corrective measures
  • Closely work with internal customers to specify procurement requirements including but not limited to cost target, delivery time, brand, technology, and services
  • Carry out RFI and RFQ process with competitive bidding process, and negotiate best prices and other commercial terms together with internal stakeholders
  • Implement supplier contract to mitigate all potential supply and legal risks
  • Complete subsequent purchasing activities timely including purchase order release, supplier delivery follow up and payment process
  • Ensure supply and service continuous improvement, for business needs as well as internal customer service
  • Establish the price database of indirect material, take lead on category annual cost saving initiatives and meet the objective.
