Hospitals in Luzern currently list around 102 open positions, spanning nursing, medical-technical, administrative and support functions. Candidates should expect recruitment based on Swiss-recognized qualifications, German-language requirements for most clinical roles, and registration with the relevant cantonal health authority. Luzern's central location and strong transport links make it accessible from across Switzerland, though international applicants typically need a valid work permit before starting employment.
