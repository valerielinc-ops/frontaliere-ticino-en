The Opportunity

As an intern, you are a valuable member of our global team and are encouraged to make a meaningful contribution. You will support the qualification and monitoring of suppliers across all sites, indications, and platforms—including small molecules, biologic, and medical devices. Depending on the team, your tasks may involve creating Direct Material Specifications, certifying our products, and/or supporting various projects managed by our team.

Support and get assignments for internal and global tasks such as:

  • Support on preparation of global material specifications and certificates
  • Support stakeholder related projects like reduced testing programme
  • Support the Certification Team by establishing product relevant certifications
  • Support the DMM Team in continuous improvement projects

Further take away opportunities that we offer for our interns:

  • Many learning and training opportunities that will help improve your personal development and can significantly influence your own career
  • Learn to move in a global company, to network and to collaborate with stakeholder groups
  • Interaction with global and local stakeholders
  • Be a member of our Interns Network and get to experience the culture of Basel in the heart of Europe

Who You are

  • To be eligible for an internship lasting 12 months, you must either be currently enrolled at a university preferably in the field of life sciences (chemistry, biology, pharmacy), data science or another degree, or you completed your studies within the last 12 months, prior to the start date.
  • You are looking for an interesting and versatile internship for 12 months in the pharmaceutical industry and interested to experience a modern working environment.
  • For Non-EU/EFTA citizens : due to regulations, you must provide a mandatory letter issued by your university stating that a 12 months internship is mandatory for your studies. Please upload this mandatory letter and your enrolment certificate.

Furthermore, You have/are

  • Interest in technical processes and stakeholder management
  • Excellent organizational skills and a flexible approach to coordinate demanding workloads, as well as the ability to adapt to changes
  • Ability to work effectively and independently within a diverse group of people
  • Very good Microsoft Office skills
  • Very good English skills required and good German skills desirable

Your application: Please upload your CV and indicate your preferred start date of the internship in your motivation letter.

Start : from November 2026 or upon agreement

The internship is not suitable for writing a Bachelor’s/ Master’s Thesis.
