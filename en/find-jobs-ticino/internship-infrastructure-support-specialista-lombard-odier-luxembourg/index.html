Internship - Infrastructure Support Specialist

Ready to kick-start your career in IT? Join our Infrastructure Support team and gain hands-on experience in a dynamic international environment. You will work alongside experienced IT professionals, solve real technical challenges, and contribute to the daily operations of a modern workplace.

YOUR ROLE

As an Infrastructure Support Specialist Intern, you will be at the heart of our IT operations, helping ensure a smooth and efficient experience for our employees.

You will

  • Investigate and resolve infrastructure-related incidents escalated by the Service Desk.
  • Support end users with issues related to:
  • Workstations and laptops
  • Microsoft Office 365
  • Telephony and collaboration tools
  • Email services
  • Mobile devices
  • Printing solutions
  • Interact directly with users to diagnose issues and provide technical support.
  • Collaborate closely with Service Desk and Level 3 Infrastructure teams to ensure timely issue resolution.
  • Coordinate with specialized IT teams including Security, Systems, Networks, and Software Packaging.
  • Contribute to troubleshooting, documentation, and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Gain exposure to enterprise-level IT infrastructure and support processes.

YOUR PROFILE

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
  • Passionate about technology, troubleshooting, and delivering excellent user support.
  • Curious, proactive, and eager to learn new technologies.
  • Organized, detail-oriented, and able to manage priorities effectively.
  • Comfortable working both independently and as part of a team.
  • Resilient under pressure and able to approach challenges with a solution-oriented mindset.
  • A confident communicator with strong interpersonal skills.
  • Familiarity with Windows 11, Microsoft 365 and Office applications.
  • Understanding of Active Directory, Exchange, and Citrix environments.
  • Knowledge of PowerShell scripting.
  • Knowledge of tools such as Splunk, Nexthink, SCCM, or App-V is considered a strong advantage.
  • Fluent in French and English, both written and spoken.

WHAT YOU WILL GAIN

This internship is an opportunity to develop valuable technical and professional skills in a real-world enterprise environment.

You will

  • Gain practical experience supporting a large-scale IT infrastructure.
  • Work on real incidents and technology challenges from day one.
  • Learn from experienced IT professionals and infrastructure experts.
  • Develop your troubleshooting, analytical, and communication skills.
  • Build knowledge of enterprise tools, processes, and best practices.
  • Expand your professional network within an international organization.
  • Strengthen your CV with hands-on experience highly valued by employers.

WHY JOIN US

  • Work in an international and collaborative environment in Luxembourg.
  • Be part of a supportive team that values learning and development.
