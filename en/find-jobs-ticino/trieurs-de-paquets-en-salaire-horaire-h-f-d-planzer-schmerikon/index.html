Original description

Brand of the Planzer Group: Planzer Paket.

Planzer Paket stands for reliable and fast parcel deliveries in Switzerland. As part of our team, you actively contribute to further developing our high quality standards and service. We offer you a varied job in a future-oriented company that focuses on sustainable solutions. Become part of our team and shape the future of the package world!

Tasks

  • You ensure that our packages run safely, quickly and efficiently through the sorting plant
  • With your eye for detail, each package is labeled correctly and prepared for the next tour.
  • Even when loading into the assigned vehicles, you actively participate with power and team spirit
  • Work is carried out in shift operation from approximately 02:00 to 08:00 and/or approx. 17:00 to 20:00 – perfect for everyone who wants to gas early or start again in the evening

Profile

  • You are fit and like to pack – packages up to 30 kg do not get you out of breath. Have you had any experience? Jackpot! If not, you learn quickly
  • Your reputation is impeccable, there is nothing wrong with you.
  • You can communicate well verbally and in writing in the German language
  • Saturday assignments? No problem, you are at the start!
  • Do you want more than just to collaborate? Perfect. Anyone who shows motivation, takes responsibility and fits into the team can take on additional tasks and develop step by step – Promotional opportunities included

Benefits

  • With us you start with 5 weeks of holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
