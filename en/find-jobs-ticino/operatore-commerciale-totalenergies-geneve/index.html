Role overview

Context & Environment: The Trading Operations team of Trading & Shipping (T&S) is essential to the trading organisation and operates in a fast-paced, real-time environment requiring reliable and committed profiles with a strong cooperative mindset.

The Job Holder is part of the Trading Operations team and works closely with traders and counterparties to coordinate all aspects of post-deal trading activities, from trade inception to completion, ensuring safe, compliant and cost-efficient execution of physical flows (petroleum products, crude and/or operational activities).

  • Trading Operator Responsibilities As a Trading Operator, the Job Holder will:
  • Coordinate logistics for all loading/discharging operations by sea and/or overland, anticipating, avoiding and mitigating any potentially hazardous or costly situation.
  • Follow up on vessels chartered by the company (spot, COA or T/C) and optimise rotations and loading plans.
  • Nominate essential service providers (independent inspectors, loss controllers, additive suppliers, etc.) and ensure the accuracy and consistency of the results obtained.
  • Diligently manage storage and process contracts (quantities, qualities, financial exposure, customs, etc.).
  • Identify the cause of any operational problems encountered and ensure resolution through coordination with all affected stakeholders, mitigating costs and consequences for the company.
