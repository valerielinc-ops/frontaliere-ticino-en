Role overview

Your mission

The energy transition is ongoing and the deployment of silicon-based photovoltaic capacity is growing fast.

The Photovoltaics and Thin Film Electronics Laboratory (PV-Lab) at EPFL is looking for a highly motivated Postdoctoral Researcher in Photovoltaic Recycling and Circularity to strengthen its activities in sustainable photovoltaic technologies.

  • Your mission
  • The energy transition is ongoing and the deployment of silicon-based photovoltaic capacity is growing fast.
  • Main duties and responsibilities
  • You will develop and optimize PV recycling technologies, including mechanical, thermo-mechanical, delamination and advanced separation processes for conventional and emerging PV modules.
  • A PhD in photovoltaics, materials science, polymer science, chemical engineering, mechanical engineering, recycling technologies, environmental engineering, or a closely related discipline.
  • A strong academic background in photovoltaic recycling, PV module materials, circular photovoltaics or end-of-life PV technologies.

Main responsibilities

  • Main duties and responsibilities
  • You will develop and optimize PV recycling technologies, including mechanical, thermo-mechanical, delamination and advanced separation processes for conventional and emerging PV modules.
  • You will investigate material recovery and circularity pathways for glass, polymers, encapsulants, backsheets, silicon cells, metals and composites, including their potential for reuse, remanufacturing and high-value recycling.
  • You will develop material characterization and process-evaluation methods for end-of-life PV modules, assessing recovery yield, purity, energy consumption, environmental impact, scalability and economic relevance.
  • You will plan and conduct experimental research, including experimental campaigns, data analysis and process optimization, while ensuring high standards of laboratory practice, documentation, scientific integrity and safety.
  • You will supervise and mentor PhD, Master’s and junior researchers, supporting research planning, experimental work, interpretation of results, scientific discussions and knowledge sharing within PV-Lab.
  • You will coordinate collaborative research projects, like Work-Package Leader in a European project, including scientific activities, partners, tasks, milestones, deliverables, resources, risks, meetings, reporting and technical communication.

Key requirements

  • A PhD in photovoltaics, materials science, polymer science, chemical engineering, mechanical engineering, recycling technologies, environmental engineering, or a closely related discipline.
  • A strong academic background in photovoltaic recycling, PV module materials, circular photovoltaics or end-of-life PV technologies.
  • Alternatively, a strong background in polymer or composite recycling with a clear interest in applying this expertise to photovoltaics.
  • Demonstrated experience in experimental research and laboratory work.
  • Good understanding of materials, interfaces and manufacturing processes relevant to PV modules.
  • Experience in material characterization and quantitative analysis of experimental data.
  • Ability to independently define, plan and execute research activities.
  • A strong scientific publication record appropriate to the candidate's career stage.
  • Experience or demonstrated interest in supervising students and junior researchers.

Application process

  • You will contribute to scientific dissemination and future research development through publications in high-impact scientific journals, international conferences, collaborative research proposals and funding applications.
