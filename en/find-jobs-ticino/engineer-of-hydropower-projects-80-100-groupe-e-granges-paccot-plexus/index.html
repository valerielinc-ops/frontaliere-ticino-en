Original description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2450 employees, including 245 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life. The Directorate of Electrical Energy operates hydraulic and thermal production infrastructure, develops production facilities from renewable energy sources and provides electricity supply on the best conditions. Mission: • Direct multidisciplinary projects for the renewal of hydropower production equipment (turbines, alternators, automation), in the phases of study, production and commissioning • Pilot project teams, composed of internal and external experts, for the improvement or renovation of hydropower developments operated by Group E • Establish and optimize project planning, taking into account the techno-economic challenges of production • Ensure that project portfolio objectives are met. Profile: • Degree in mechanical engineering or electrical engineering • Min. 7 years experience in the operation or construction of electrical production equipment. • In-depth knowledge of the operation of hydroelectric developments. • Experience in managing major multidisciplinary projects in hydraulic production or electrical energy. • A sense of responsibility and teamwork, conceptual spirit, good communication and good editorial capacity. Negotiating capacity in German and English. What we offer: • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance. • The social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development. • A strong spirit of cohesion and team: "proud to collaborate together, we are moving towards success".
