Original description

Do you want to gain practical experience in an SAP and digitalization project and make a visible contribution to improving quality? Then you are exactly right with us. In this exciting working student activity, you will work independently on a clearly defined digitalization project, gain direct insight into quality management and industrial production processes and gain valuable practical experience with SAP in an international industrial company. With your work, you make an important contribution to improving quality and process efficiency and at the same time benefit from flexible working hours that are optimally compatible with your studies.

Your tasks

  • Transfer of test features from technical drawings into SAP test plans
  • Planning and maintenance of test plans in SAP quality management
  • Support for standardization of test processes and quality data
  • Ensuring a consistent and traceable database in the SAP system
  • Close cooperation with quality management, purchasing and research & development
  • Support and optimization of quality processes

What you bring

  • Ongoing studies in mechanical engineering, industrial engineering, production engineering, quality management or comparable
  • Technical understanding
  • Independent, structured and careful working methods
  • Good MS-Office knowledge
  • Initial SAP knowledge is advantageous, but not mandatory
  • Good knowledge of German
