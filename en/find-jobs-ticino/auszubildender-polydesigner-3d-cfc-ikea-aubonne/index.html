Original description

Who you are

  • You show a lot of passion and curiosity for interior design and everyday life at home.
  • You distinguish yourself with a creative mind and you have an eye for aesthetics.
  • You see trends in furniture and are interested in different materials while having a sense of colours and shapes.

•You take responsibility, you commit and you are not afraid of change.

What you'll do every day

  • You design IKEA atmospheres according to certain rules while skillfully applying your knowledge in the field of layout and products.
  • You like to go beyond the expectations of our customers and find out their personal tastes.
  • You ensure that the products are well developed and thus facilitate the decision to purchase.
  • You simplify the purchasing process by means of appropriate communication measures so that our customers systematically and intuitively travel through the store.

Teamwork

Where others see furnishings, we see clever solutions to improve daily life. With a lot of passion, imagination and brainstorming, we transform colours, textiles and furniture into inspiring atmospheres that enthralle, attract and convince visitors to IKEA stores that they can reproduce the same thing at home.

Any questions or need help? Contact us!

Do you also want a job at IKEA? Find out on our website: www.ikea.ch/vocational training

Further information You want to grow professionally at IKEA? Then apply directly to our website.

The cover letter is not mandatory for us. The shortlisted candidates will be invited to spend an afternoon in an IKEA store to get to know and discover practical learning. It helps us and you see if we're meant to work together.

*The discovery day at IKEA Aubonne will take place on Tuesday, November 17, 2026 from 09:00 to 15:00 at the store.
