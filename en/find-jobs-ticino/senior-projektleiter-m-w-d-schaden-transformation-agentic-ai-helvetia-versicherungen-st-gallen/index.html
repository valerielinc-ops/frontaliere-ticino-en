Role overview

In damage transformation, we realize an ambitious project.

We are not looking for a classic project management that processes an IT rollout according to plan.

  • In damage transformation, we realize an ambitious project.
  • At the core of this next stage is the use of Agentic AI agents, who independently control a claim from the reporting to the cover check to the payout[RT2.1], working with people within clearly defined guardrails.

Main responsibilities

  • You lead the project as a co-lead for the introduction of Agentic AI in claims processing – implementation in iterative expansion steps.
  • You are responsible for the automation roadmap in the damage environment and drive the expansion of end-to-end dark processing in mass damage significantly
  • You work closely with claims specialists, IT architecture and technology partners to integrate AI agents with clear decision boundaries into redesigned processes
  • You make sure that people and agents work together in a meaningful way in the claims process (human-in-the-loop) and compliance and quality requirements are met at all times.
  • You prepare decision templates and represent the project in the Damage Transformation Steering Committee as well as the management.
  • You accompany the cultural change in the claims team – from classic case handling to modern damage management in cooperation with AI agents Requirements
  • Several years of experience in managing complex digitalization and/or IT projects with a transformational character, ideally in the property or insurance environment
  • Very good knowledge of classical and agile process models
  • Daily use of AI and basic working methods with AI support
  • In-depth understanding of Agentic AI architectures (orchestrator/sub-agent models, LLMs, APIs) and their use in operational processes
