Main responsibilities

  • Your responsibilities: Strategic Product Development
  • Support the definition and execution of the product roadmap in alignment with business strategy and evolving customer needs.
  • Architect complete dental treatment workflows spanning across the treatment pathway.
  • Collaborate on workflow innovation initiatives that integrate digital technologies such as CAD/CAM, 3D printing, imaging, and treatment planning.
  • Translate market trends and customer insights into compelling product requirements and clear value propositions. Cross-Functional Collaboration
  • Act as the voice of the customer across cross-functional teams, including R&D, Marketing, Regulatory, Digital Transformation, and Sales.
  • Contribute to project planning and execution across complex product initiatives, ensuring alignment between clinical, technical, and commercial perspectives.
  • Drive engagement with clinicians, labs, and internal stakeholders to validate product concepts and ensure usability and clinical relevance. Product Lifecycle Ownership
  • Manage the lifecycle of assigned workflow solutions from early-stage ideation to post-market optimization.
  • Collaborate on the creation of go-to-market strategies and support product marketing with impactful messaging, training content, and launch assets.

Key requirements

  • 3–5 years of experience in product management, preferably within dental, medical device, or healthcare technology sectors.
  • Proven ability to manage complex projects in cross-functional and international environments.
  • Strong understanding of dental workflows and clinical practices, experience in implantology or restorative dentistry is a strong plus.
  • Passion for innovation and digital transformation in healthcare.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder engagement skills.
  • Strategic, analytical mindset with a customer-first orientation.
  • Bachelor's degree in business, engineering, healthcare, or related field; advanced degree (e.g., MBA, MSc) is a plus.
  • Fluent in English; additional languages are a plus.
