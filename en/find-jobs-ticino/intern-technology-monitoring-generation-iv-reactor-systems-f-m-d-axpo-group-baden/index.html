Original description

Place: Baden | Workload: 100% | Start: start immediately or upon agreement | Duration: 6 months

This internship gives you the chance to work in a substantive, complex project environment alongside a specialist team, exploring the technologies that could shape the future of nuclear energy.

What you will do

  • Conduct a structured literature review on Gen IV reactor systems, covering technology concepts, safety approaches, fuel cycles, and international development status
  • Analyse selected Gen IV technologies and designs in terms of Technology Readiness Level, deploy-ability, safety and licensing feasibility, fuel and waste aspects, and supply chain
  • Develop a technology and design-based dashboard that enables systematic comparison of Gen IV technologies across key assessment criteria
  • Document data sources, assumptions, and uncertainties in a transparent and traceable way
  • Present interim results to the project team and integrate feedback from internal and external experts

What you bring & who you are

  • Ongoing or recently completed studies in nuclear engineering, physics, energy engineering, or a closely related technical field
  • Knowledge of nuclear engineering fundamentals, including reactor physics, thermohydraulics, nuclear materials, fuel cycle, and safety principles
  • Ideally some initial knowledge of Gen IV reactor systems (e.g. sodium-cooled fast reactors, gas-cooled fast reactors, molten salt reactors, lead-cooled reactors, supercritical water reactors) – or a strong interest and willingness to build this up quickly
  • Strong analytical skills, with the ability to structure complex technical topics and synthesise information from diverse sources
  • A precise, detail-oriented way of working, especially when it comes to documentation, referencing, and data handling
  • Good English skills, written and spoken
  • German is a plus but not required
  • You enjoy working independently and proactively, while collaborating closely with a multidisciplinary team.

About the team

You will work closely with experienced experts from nuclear engineering, strategy, and regulatory affairs on a clearly defined assignment with tangible outputs, in a professional and supportive environment at Axpo's headquarters
