About the role The Finance Operations Coordinator plays a key role in supporting the organization’s day-to-day financial activities. This position is responsible for validating operational financial data, ensuring policy compliance, and assisting with accurate and timely financial processing. The ideal candidate is detail-oriented, organized, and able to collaborate effectively across teams.

Key Responsibilities

  • Review and validate team timesheets and reported working hours to ensure accuracy and compliance.
  • Monitor employee expense submissions, verify supporting documentation, and ensure adherence to company policies.
  • Review contractor invoices prior to approval and payment, resolving discrepancies as needed.
  • Maintain accurate and up-to-date records related to financial operations, reimbursements, and payments.
  • Collaborate closely with the finance team to support timely and precise payment processing.
  • Assist in identifying and implementing process improvements to enhance financial reporting and expense control.
  • Support ad-hoc financial tasks, audits, and reporting as required.

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration, or related field (or

equivalent experience).

  • Experience in financial operations, accounts payable, or similar roles preferred.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to accuracy.
  • Proficiency with spreadsheets and financial software/tools.
  • Excellent organizational and communication skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines.
  • Location: EU timezone

About You

You thrive in a structured, process-driven environment and enjoy ensuring that financial data is correct, compliant, and well-organized. You’re proactive, resourceful, and a strong collaborator who brings reliability and clarity to financial operations.
