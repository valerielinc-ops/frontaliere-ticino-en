  • On the specialized inpatient therapy station Olvido, together with the therapeutic management in dual management, you assume the personnel, professional and organizational responsibility for the station.
  • Together with your interprofessional team, you will create a safe therapeutic framework in which adolescents between 14 and 17 years old with psychotic spectrum disorders can develop stability, find orientation and gain new perspectives for their further life.
  • You can expect a versatile working environment with great creative scope.
  • Together with your team and the therapeutic management, you will continuously develop the Olvido station and its therapeutic offerings and actively shape the future of child and adolescent psychiatry.
  • You take on a responsible leadership task with a high level of personal responsibility in an established model of dual leadership.

  • At the same time, you have the opportunity to actively shape development and change processes and to shape the professional development of the station in a sustainable way.
