Role overview

In preparation for the High-Luminosity LHC phase, the CMS collaboration is working on an ambitious upgrade of the first stage of its online selection system, the Level-1 (L1) trigger.

The upgraded system will use powerful FPGA processors connected through a high-bandwidth network, paving the way for more sophisticated online algorithms such as Particle Flow reconstruction.

By joining the team, you will contribute to the development of these algorithms, their implementation in firmware, and the evaluation of their physics performance. of CERN
