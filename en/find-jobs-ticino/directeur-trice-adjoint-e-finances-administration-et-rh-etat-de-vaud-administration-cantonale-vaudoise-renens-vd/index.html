Role overview

As Deputy Director of Finance, Administration and Human Resources at ECAL- Ecole cantonale d'art de Lausanne, you are the guarantor of its financial strategy: you draw up the School's budget, guide its implementation and advise the management on financial issues.

A member of management, you are actively involved in its strategic leadership and outreach.

You also direct human resources and supervise the administrative and technical services of the School.
