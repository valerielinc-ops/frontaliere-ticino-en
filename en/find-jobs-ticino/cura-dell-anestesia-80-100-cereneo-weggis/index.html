Role overview

Tasks

  • Varied activity in a combined function with an application of approximately 50% in the surgical/anaesthesia area and 50% in the Intermediate Care Unit (IMC)
  • Independent and competent care of patients in the context of anesthesia and at the IMC
  • Preparation, implementation and follow-up of anesthesia care in close cooperation with the medical anesthesia team
  • Monitoring and support of patients before, during and after surgical or interventional interventions
  • Professional monitoring and care of patients with increased medical and nursing needs at the IMC
  • Reliable detection of clinical changes as well as rapid and competent action in demanding situations
  • Close interprofessional cooperation with the medical service, nursing and other specialist areas

Requirements

  • For this versatile function, we are looking for an experienced anesthesia care expert who can safely move around in the anesthesia and surgical environment as well as in the care and monitoring of patients on an IMC.
  • Diploma as nursing specialist HF/FH with completed NDS HF anesthesia care or an equivalent qualification recognised in Switzerland
  • Several years of professional experience and high professional competence in anesthesia care
  • Experience in the care of IMC or intensive care patients is beneficial
  • Enjoyment of a combined activity with approximately 50% use in the operating room and 50% at the IMC
  • Strong clinical observation and assessment competence as well as high reliability of action
  • Self-employed, responsible and structured working methods
  • Flexibility and pleasure in working together in an interprofessional and dynamic environment
  • Willingness to actively shape the interface between anesthesia and IMC and to contribute to the further development of the corresponding structures and processes
