Role overview

The FM and Workplace Project Manager is responsible for the operational management of Real Estate, FM and Workplace related projects for the Straumann Campus in Basel (UPTOWN).

The position is also actively involved in Health, Safety & Employee Wellbeing and site security for Basel (later Arlesheim, Uptown).

The role establishes an attractive, sustainable, and compliant workplace environment while supporting business growth, employee experience and wellbeing, operational resilience, and cost optimization.
