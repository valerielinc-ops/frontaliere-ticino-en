Original description

Your tasks

You won't get bored with building insurance. We insure all buildings in the canton of St.Gallen against fire and elemental damage, are actively involved in damage prevention and are responsible for property estimates and fire protection in the canton. During your training, you work in various departments such as damage, firefighting, reception, estimation, customer center and accounting – in the middle of the action and with direct customer contact.

Your profile

  • You think along and like to join
  • You like working in a team
  • You are honest, open and respectful
  • You are interested in business and organization
  • You can speak German, orally and in writing
  • You bring good grades from secondary school (highest level)

Our offer

  • An open and collegial working environment
  • Exchange with our learners
  • Practical training with great support
  • Versatile tasks that you do independently
  • Exciting know-how about buildings, insurance and administration You work in the middle of St. Gallen, close to the train station. We pay for your mobile subscription and cost sharing for public transport, for your notebook, language stays and diplomas. Our atmosphere is open and familiar. You are constantly learning new things and can try what you have learned right away. Together we will make you fit for the future!
