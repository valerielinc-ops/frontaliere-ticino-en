Role overview

Carros MSEPENgler PW (a) Galliker Service AG prepares new cars for the Swiss market by various car manufacturers.

Furthermore, it carries out serial, conversion and various workshop work on behalf of importers, corporate customers, rental companies, leasing companies and dealers.

For the car brands concerned, Galliker Service AG is also responsible for the delivery and return of the car hire and leasing vehicles and performs all kinds of workshop, carrosserie and painting work in the service of customers.
