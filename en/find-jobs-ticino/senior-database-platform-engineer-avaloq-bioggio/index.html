Role overview

As a Senior Database Platform Engineer, you will help transform Oracle database operations into a fully automated, secure, and scalable platform.

Working within an agile delivery team, you will design and maintain automation across the entire database lifecycle, ensuring reliable, auditable, and resilient database services while minimizing manual intervention. Your key tasks

Automate the complete Oracle database lifecycle, including provisioning, cloning, refresh, reconfiguration, backup, restore, and decommissioning using Terraform, Ansible, and scripting.

Key requirements

  • Qualifiche
  • 5+ years of experience as an Oracle DBA or Database Engineer in a production, mission-critical environment.
  • University degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a comparable field.
  • Strong hands-on Oracle expertise, including installation, patching, upgrades, Oracle Home and parameter management, Data Guard, RMAN, backup and recovery, TDE, cloning, refreshes, and performance troubleshooting.
  • Proven experience in automation using Ansible, Terraform or OpenTofu, Git, GitOps, CI/CD pipelines, and scripting in Python and Shell.
  • You have successfully replaced manual operational processes with code.
  • Solid understanding of public cloud platforms, preferably OCI, as well as Linux, storage, networking, secrets management, and container technologies such as Kubernetes.
  • Experience working in regulated and security-conscious environments with strong knowledge of auditability, segregation of duties, vulnerability management, and patch management.
  • Experience within financial services is considered an advantage.
  • Strong engineering mindset focused on automation, infrastructure as code, reliability, and continuous improvement.
  • Effective communication skills in English and the ability to collaborate within international, agile, and distributed teams.
  • German and/or Italian are considered an asset.
