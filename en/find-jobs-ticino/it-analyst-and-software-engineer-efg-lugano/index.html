Role overview

We are looking for a Software Engineer with strong development skills to join the IT Trading & Treasury team of a leading private bank, supporting mission-critical front-office systems used daily by a trading room of around 50 traders.

You will own the technical delivery and operational support of our Order Management, Pricing and Position Keeping platforms across asset classes — securities, derivatives and structured products — as well as the in-house applications we build to close gaps around them.

This is a hands-on engineering role. You will design and write code, customise vendor platforms, integrate systems front-to-back, and keep production running. The environment is technically rich and highly integrated: order flow, market data, pricing, risk and core banking all connect through the platforms you will own.

<i>Build & Change</i>

  • Develop, customise and maintain components across the order management, pricing and position keeping platforms, to support new business requirements and platform evolution.
  • Design and build in-house applications for the trading desk — integration layers, reporting tools, automation and health-check utilities — where the vendor platforms do not cover the need.
  • Deliver enhancements and full project phases end-to-end: analysis, technical design, development, testing, deployment and documentation.
  • 3–8 years of experience in application development and/or application support within financial services IT.
  • Solid development skills in Java and/or Python — you write production code, not only scripts.
  • Very good knowledge of SQL and relational database systems (Oracle, MS SQL), including the ability to read and optimise queries; familiarity with application servers (Windows mainly, Linux).

Main responsibilities

  • Develop, customise and maintain components across the order management, pricing and position keeping platforms, to support new business requirements and platform evolution.
  • Design and build in-house applications for the trading desk — integration layers, reporting tools, automation and health-check utilities — where the vendor platforms do not cover the need.
  • Deliver enhancements and full project phases end-to-end: analysis, technical design, development, testing, deployment and documentation.
  • Work front-to-back across the technology stack, working across databases (Oracle, MS SQL), application servers (Windows, Linux) and front-end components.
  • Manage and troubleshoot FIX protocol connectivity towards brokers, exchanges and execution venues — session configuration, message flow analysis, troubleshooting.
  • Build and maintain integrations between trading platforms, market data providers, the core banking system and downstream risk and back-office systems.
  • Ensure data consistency across systems with different data models and formats, and design the mapping and reconciliation logic.
  • Provide L2/L3 application support across trading workflows, from intraday incidents to root-cause analysis and permanent fixes.
  • Act as technical escalation point of complex issues, coordinating with external vendors and other IT teams (infrastructure, database, network, core banking) to resolve them.
  • Manage incidents with the trading desk with clear communication on impact and expected resolution time — and write the post-mortem when it matters.

Key requirements

  • 3–8 years of experience in application development and/or application support within financial services IT.
  • Solid development skills in Java and/or Python — you write production code, not only scripts.
  • Very good knowledge of SQL and relational database systems (Oracle, MS SQL), including the ability to read and optimise queries; familiarity with application servers (Windows mainly, Linux).
  • Understandingof financial products, related workflows and pricing concepts: mark-to-market and valuation logic, volatility and market data inputs, P&L attribution — sufficient to reason about pricing behaviour and diagnose pricing issues
  • Ability to read and navigate an existing codebase, diagnose issues and deliver clean, maintainable solutions.
  • Fluent English; Italian is a strong advantage for the Lugano location; French is a plus.
  • University degree in computer science, engineering, finance or an equivalent background.
  • Knowledge of the FIX protocol — experience configuring or troubleshooting FIX sessions is a significant plus.
  • Exposure to securities trading workflows (equities, fixed income), derivatives or structured products.
  • Experience with vendor front-office platforms — FrontArena, Ullink, FinIQ or comparable OMS/pricing systems.
  • Familiarity with Powershell or shell scripting, CI/CD, version control, environment management (dev / UAT / production).
  • Composure under pressure: trading systems fail at the worst possible moment, and you keep a clear head when they do.
