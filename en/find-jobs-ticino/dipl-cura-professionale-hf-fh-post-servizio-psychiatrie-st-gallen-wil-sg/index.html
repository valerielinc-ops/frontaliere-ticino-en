Main responsibilities

  • Together with our interdisciplinary team, you will treat patients with subacute psychological and social problems in the daily clinical setting.
  • After a well-founded familiarization, you lead group therapies and accompany the patients through the structured therapy process.
  • As a nurse, you design the daily program, observe and analyze the group dynamics and document the course of treatment in our hospital information system.
  • In your varied daily work, you will encounter the entire spectrum of psychiatric diseases and work closely with our treatment team from medicine, psychology, care and therapy.
  • At our location in Uznach, you actively participate in rapports, case discussions and training courses and maintain a constant exchange with the other practitioners.
  • Together with your colleagues, you are involved in public relations work and represent the psychiatry of St.Gallen professionally and sympathetically. Requirements
  • You have completed training as a nurse HF/FH and ideally have already gained professional experience in psychiatry (SRC recognition or positive SRK PreCheck required for foreign diplomas).

Key requirements

  • You work independently, are open to new things and appreciate working together in a motivated and committed team.
  • With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatments and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations.
