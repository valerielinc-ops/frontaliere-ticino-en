Original description

Discover the cooperative area as a truck driver at the cooperative Migros Aare! Explore our beautiful region and help ensure that our products arrive safely and on time in the stores. This activity offers an ideal opportunity to pursue an enriching part-time job in addition to study, family or other commitments.

Mansioni

  • You drive our modern and versatile vehicle fleet (Scania, MAN, Renault Trucks) with trailer
  • You supply the sales outlets in the cantons of Aargau, Bern and Solothurn and ensure punctual deliveries
  • By securing and controlling the cargo, you ensure it remains intact during transport.
  • You carry out regular checks on the vehicle assigned to you to ensure maximum safety
  • By loading and unloading goods and returns, you ensured an efficient interface to goods logistics

Requirements

  • Professional experience: Ideally, several years of professional experience as a truck driver
  • With a CE driving licence, you have the necessary qualification to drive our vehicles safely.
  • As a team capable, resilient and flexible personality, you adapt to different situations
  • Your strong sense of responsibility and security ensures that you always keep the safety of our goods and our employees in mind
  • No education/training required
  • in German (negotiation certain)
