Original description

A locksmith or mechanic (100%) with the Territorial Unit III Service (SUTIII), in Charrat and Sierre. The Territorial Unit III Service is one of 11 units in Switzerland responsible for maintaining national roads. SUTIII employs around one hundred employees who ensure 24/7 safety and operation of national roads in Valais. This network extends over 186 kilometers, from the exit of the A9 motorway at Bex-nord to Gondo.
