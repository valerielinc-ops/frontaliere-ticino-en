  • Determination of individual learning objectives adapted to the level of training and skills to be developed
  • Promoting the development of self-employment, clinical judgment, professional appearance and interpersonal skills of students
  • Support for the implementation of theoretical knowledge into daily clinical practice
  • Conduct of interim and final assessments in accordance with the guidelines of training centres

  • Performing delegated medical acts, delegating acts according to one’s own role and taking responsibility for them
