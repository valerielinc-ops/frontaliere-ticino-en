Description

Join us and celebrate the beauty of human experience. Create for happier, healthier lives, with love for nature. Together, with passion and creativity, we deliver food innovations, craft inspired fragrances and develop beauty and wellbeing solutions. There’s much to learn and many to learn from, with more than 17,000 employees around the world to explore ideas and ambitions with. Every day, your talent, your innovative spirit, and your determination will shape our future, making a positive difference on billions of people. Every essence of you enriches our world. We are Givaudan. Human by nature.

As Senior Regulatory Data Analyst (reporting to the Registration & Compliance Lead, Regulatory Ingredients Compliance Strategy CoE), you will work within our Regulatory Ingredients Compliance Strategy CoE – Registration & compliance, you will provide subject matter expertise with chemical regulatory compliance, chemical management and be responsible for by carrying out compliance monitoring reviews to ensure the business is meeting its regulatory obligations and risks are mitigated. In this role, you will develop, implement and maintain processes, programs to ensure compliance with existing and emerging chemical control legislations.Sounds interesting? It could well be your perfect opportunity if, like us, you always aim to exceed customer needs, deliver value and are skilled at fostering strong relationships. In this exciting role you will: Perform compliance monitoring on all aspects of the CCL (Chemical Control Legislation) to assess compliance with applicable regulations, standards and outcomes.Collaborate with the RAPS team and business stakeholders to finalise reports for presentation and submission to relevant boards and sub-committees, in line with the requirements of the plan, including impact analysis for decision making.Provide analyses for large datasets necessary for decisions using actionable insightsAttending sub-committee’s sessions as required to present monitoring findings and recommendations.Support the review/update/development of the regulatory/compliance policies and procedures to ensure they remain appropriate, relevant and effective.Participate to the development of the solutions to ensure of the compliance globally, the testing and the training of the teams as neededDefine and deliver metrics, reporting platforms, dashboards for tracking and managing the complianceTracking of monitoring actions through to successful resolutionIdentify business challenges and initiate process improvement projectsFacilitate the computerization of analytics and data collection processes You? Are you someone who wants to grow and shape your own world? Who thrives being in a culture where you can express yourself in a creative team environment? Who wants to collaborate and learn together with teams who are as passionate as you are? Then come join us – and impact your world. Your professional profile includes: University / Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in a scientific fieldA minimum of 5 years relevant in a compliance role in chemical or related industryProficient knowledge of BI, SAP, or other business Intelligence tools. Must also demonstrate advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPointFamiliarity with programming languages (e.g: Python, Javascript, SQL) and AI is a plusStrong analytical skillsGood working knowledge of CCL regulatory requirements and legal guidelines is an advantageBusiness fluency in English AND French is a mustExperience in supporting cross functional groups and teamsAbility to work well in a fast-paced, deadline driven environment Our benefits: Healthcare supportPaid Time Off and family leaveRetirement planSport and leisure clubsCompany shopStaff restaurantCorporate Mobility Plan
