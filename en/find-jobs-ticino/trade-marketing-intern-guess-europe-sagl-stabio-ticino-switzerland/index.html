Role overview

The Trade Marketing Senior Specialist is responsible for the strategy of the trade marketing activities across wholesale channel and countries, in alignment with the corporate trade marketing and communication strategy.

They will also ensure customer satisfaction as a main priority, representing the link between Merchandising, Product Management and Sales teams and ensuring the achievement of sales and budget objectives.
