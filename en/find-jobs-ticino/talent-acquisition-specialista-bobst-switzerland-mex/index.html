Talent Acquisition Specialist BSA - Bobst Mex SA Apply now At BOBST, we shape the future of the packaging world through innovation, collaboration and operational excellence. With more than 130 years of history and a global footprint, we empower our teams to make a meaningful impact and contribute to sustainable progress across our industry. We are looking for a Talent Acquisition Specialist to play a key role in shaping the future of our organization by attracting, engaging, and hiring top talents across a diverse range of functions. Beyond managing the full recruitment lifecycle, you will act as a trusted advisor to hiring managers, helping them identify and secure the talent needed to support our business objectives. Reporting directly to HR Director Switzerland, you will be working closely with the Head of Talent Acquisition. If you are passionate about recruitment, enjoy building strong relationships, thrive in a fast-paced environment, and are motivated by the opportunity to make a tangible impact on business success, this role offers the perfect combination of operational excellence and professional growth. Position Summary The Talent Acquisition Specialist is part of the BOBST SA Talent Acquisition team. The role is responsible for managing end-to-end recruitment activities for the site, promoting the employer brand, developing partnerships with educational institutions, and contributing to Group-wide HR and Talent Acquisition initiatives. Main

  • Responsibilities Manage the full recruitment lifecycle, from validating hiring needs with the HR Business Partner and Hiring Manager through to candidate offer acceptance

Advise and challenge managers on recruitment strategies, labor market trends, and candidate selection practices

Define and implement sourcing strategies tailored to targeted talent profiles

Screen and shortlist applications

Conduct phone pre-screening and qualification interviews

Ensure a high-quality candidate experience throughout the recruitment process

Guarantee timely feedback and communication to all applicants

Collaborate with recruitment and temporary staffing agencies when required
