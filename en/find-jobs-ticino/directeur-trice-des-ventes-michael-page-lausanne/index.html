About Our Client

A historic player in Swiss real estate for more than 125 years, deRham has opened a new chapter in its development and wants to strengthen its organisation in order to support its ambitions for growth and transformation.

In this context, deRham recruits a Sales Director, who is responsible for managing all of the group's transactional activities (residential, plan sales, high-end sales and real estate performance), while reflecting prospectively on the evolution of the Swiss real estate market, both on its traditional and premium segments.
