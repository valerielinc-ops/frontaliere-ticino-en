Original description

Arxada is a global leader in innovative solutions for protecting our planet. Our cutting-edge technologies, extensive regulatory expertise, and experience in process development and production support our customers in efficiently safeguarding nutrition, health, and infrastructure through chemistry and biotechnology that promote sustainability.

  • We offer a broad portfolio of ingredients and services for various end markets, including health and nutrition, home and body care, professional hygiene, paints and coatings, and wood protection. With customers in over 100 countries, the company achieved a turnover of 2 billion CHF in 2024. Arxada’s headquarters is located in Basel, Switzerland. The company employs 3,200 people worldwide across 24 production sites and 14 R&D centers, all dedicated to the success of our customers. To strengthen our Process Technology team within the R&D department in Visp, Switzerland, we are currently seeking a (Senior) Chemical Engineer or Process Engineer. ## Key
  • Responsibilities
  • In this role, you will be part of interdisciplinary project teams alongside colleagues from R&D, Operations, Safety, Quality, and Product Marketing, representing and further developing your expertise in the field of chemical engineering.
  • Development of processes and scale-up of chemical processes from laboratory to production scale
  • Optimization of various chemical production processes in terms of yield, productivity, quality, safety, and ecology
  • Supporting production teams in problem-solving, e.g., through process data analysis, specialized analyses, in-depth process knowledge, and coordination with various stakeholders
  • Planning, supervising, and evaluating experimental work on laboratory and pilot scale
  • Advising on technical and commercial decisions and solving scientific problems
  • Leading, coaching, and developing laboratory technicians
  • Anticipating and implementing trends and innovations in your field of expertise ##
  • Requirements
  • MSc or PhD, preferably in Chemical Engineering or Process Engineering
  • Relevant experience, preferably in process development and production
  • Written and oral proficiency in German and English (C1 / B2)
  • Proactive approach to work and enjoyment of working in interdisciplinary teams
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills ## What We Offer Arxada provides top-tier solutions in two well-defined areas:
  • Microbial Control Solutions (MCS) focuses on the threats posed by microorganisms to humans and the environment. This area comprises five autonomous business units: professional hygiene, home and body care, wood protection, paints and coatings, and material protection.
  • Nutrition, Care & Environmental (NCE) supplies materials for the production of composites in electronics, aerospace, and other industries. The portfolio also includes vitamins and nutritional components, high-performance chemical intermediates, and raw materials for a wide range of demanding applications.
