Role overview

Introduction For our Production Division, we are looking for a Workshop Manager (H/F) whose main mission will be to manage the organization of the resources of an assembly workshop by putting in place the actions to ensure that the production objectives are met.

Responsibilities

  • To inform and analyse production indicators and the monitoring of the workshop's short-term objectives
  • Adapt daily the organisation of the workshop according to production hazards
  • Ensuring cross-border linkages and exchanges with production support services
  • Participate in or lead coordination sessions
  • Manage inventory organization
  • Conduct ad hoc and operational analyses to ensure the proper functioning of the service.

Profile

  • Post-compulsory secondary training, CFC or equivalent, in production management and/or logistics
  • Significant industrial experience in a production workshop management function
  • Mastery of IT tools (MS-Office, SAP and production monitoring) and knowledge of an ERP
  • Strong analytical and synthesis spirit
  • Rigourous and methodical, with a good team spirit
  • Available and equipped with a very good listening and communication capacity.

Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.
