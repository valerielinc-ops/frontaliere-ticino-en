Original description

Your tasks

  • Professional management of the disposal of laboratory waste of different categories
  • Cleaning, preparation and autoclaving of laboratory and production materials (e.g. glassware, equipment components, consumables)
  • Operation, monitoring and simple maintenance of the operational facilities of laboratories (especially autoclaves)
  • Execution according to valid SOPs, compliance and documentation of all process steps in accordance with GMP/GxP specifications
  • Cooperation in quality control
  • Support for inventory management and ordering of consumables

Your requirements

  • Training as a biology laboratory technician, chemical laboratory technician, pharma assistant, medical assistant (MTLA/BTA/MTA), medical device preparation specialist, laboratory manager or min. 3 years experience in laboratory services and activity under regulated conditions
  • Experience with the disposal of laboratory waste in particular. Autoclaves and general laboratory services
  • High quality and hygiene awareness, very careful and very reliable working method
  • Enjoy structured, recurring processes and exact documentation
  • Teamwork as well as professional and service-oriented appearance
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
