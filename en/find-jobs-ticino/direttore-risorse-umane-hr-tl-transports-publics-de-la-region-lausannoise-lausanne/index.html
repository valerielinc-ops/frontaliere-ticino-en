Role overview

As a reference player for mobility in French-speaking Switzerland, the Tl ss are committed to a simple, innovative and sustainable mobility at the service of the population.

With nearly 135 million travellers per year and more than 2,000 employees engaged on a daily basis, they have a mission of public utility essential to the economic, environmental and social dynamism of the canton of Vaud.

At the heart of the company's human stakes, Human Resources plays a key role in supporting managerial developments, social dialogue and anticipation of resource and skills needs.
