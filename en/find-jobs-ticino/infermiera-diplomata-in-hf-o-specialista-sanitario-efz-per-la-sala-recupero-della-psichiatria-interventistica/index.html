Role overview

In the Department of Interventional Psychiatry, electroconvulsion therapies (EKT) are performed in short anesthesia.

The preparation and follow-up of the ECT patients is carried out in the recovery room.

We also offer repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation (rTMS) and esketamine treatments.

  • In the Department of Interventional Psychiatry, electroconvulsion therapies (EKT) are performed in short anesthesia.
  • The preparation and follow-up of the ECT patients is carried out in the recovery room.
