Role overview

Privatklinik Wyss AG is a leading specialist clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy.

Special areas of expertise include the treatment of depression including burnout as well as anxiety and obsessive-compulsive disorders.

The clinic offers its outpatient, daily clinical and inpatient services in the regions of Bern, Münchenbuchsee and Biel and employs around 340 employees at all three locations.
