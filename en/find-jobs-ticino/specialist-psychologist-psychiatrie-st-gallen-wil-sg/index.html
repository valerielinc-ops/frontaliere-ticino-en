Main responsibilities

  • In the daily clinical setting, you take over the psychotherapeutic clarification, treatment and advice of patients and their relatives in individual and group setting.
  • Individual and multi-person meetings as well as the implementation of various psychotherapeutic group offers are among your versatile tasks in the field of activity.
  • For an individually tailored treatment, you coordinate the treatment processes, plan therapies and carry out test psychological assessments.
  • You are actively involved in rapports, case discussions and further training and maintain a regular professional exchange with the other practitioners.
  • With your ideas and your expertise, you contribute to the further development of a modern, evidence-based and versatile psychotherapeutic offer. Requirements
