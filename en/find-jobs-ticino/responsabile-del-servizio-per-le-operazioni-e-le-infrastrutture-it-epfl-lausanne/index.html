Role overview

Context and Mission

At the interface of state-of-the-art research excellence, teaching, and major challenges such as the advent of AI or the management of sensitive data, the service for Operations and IT Infrastructure (ITOP) of EPFL's Information Systems (DSI) field is piloting the operation, maintenance and evolution of EPFL's cross-cutting critical IT infrastructure.

As head of this service, you manage an average annual budget of about 8 MCHF and lead a team of 60 ETPs.
