Tasks

As a member of our dedicated kitchen team, you are jointly responsible for the culinary well-being of our residents and restaurant visitors. You ensure a professional preparation of healthy, tasteful and varied meals according to Domicil standards. Thanks to your organizational skills, you ensured a smooth process in day-to-day business. Careful and economical handling of food and equipment, as well as compliance with hygiene regulations are a matter of course for you.

You work without a room hour in the early or late service and have on average two weekends a month off. Your mission is in the Domicil Cluster North with Domicil Spitalackerpark and Domicil Wyler.

The contact with our guests, especially with elderly people, gives you pleasure and you maintain a courteous approach? Then you are exactly right with us! Take the opportunity and join our team.
