Original description

Welcome to Medbase Kriens Mattenhof

Our versatile, modern medical center is located directly at the Kriens Mattenhof S-Bahn stop. The Mikropole Mattenhof offers a wide range of sports, shopping and food options. Around 30 employees from the disciplines of general internal medicine, endocrinology and diabetology as well as ophthalmology with orthoptics, orthopaedics and allergology work together interprofessionally. We also run a public pharmacy integrated into the Medical Center. We maintain a close cooperation with our partners Oviva and with Medbase Lucerne Allmend. At Medbase Kriens Mattenhof, we ensure a balanced work-life balance.

You can move that

As an MPA, you are the first contact person for our patients and ensure a pleasant practical experience. Medbase offers you a dynamic team that values quality and collegiality.

  • Patient reception and administration
  • Termination and documentation, power acquisition by Axenita
  • Performing various laboratory tests
  • Administration of injections and infusions
  • Fully digital X-rays of the extremities and orthopaedic special images
  • 24-hour blood pressure measurements, lung function tests and derivations of rest ECG and application of long-term ECG
  • Assistance in small-surgical procedures, application of plaster splints, wound controls and dressing changes
  • Independent performance of ophthalmological technical tests
  • Various allergological diagnostic tests such as skin prick tests and nitrogen measurements

What you bring

  • Education as a Med. Practical assistant/EFZ
  • Readiness for further training in extended conventional X-rays or for Med. Practice coordinator clinical direction
  • Self-employed and precise working methods
  • Patient and service-oriented work attitude
  • Strong, open and humorous personality to complement our great existing team
