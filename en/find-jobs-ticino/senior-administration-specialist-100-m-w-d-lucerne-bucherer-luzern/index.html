Original description

Your role with us

  • Responsible for the administrative management of the Watch Service, with a focus on the Swiss market
  • Coordination and monitoring of internal and external repair schedules
  • Maintenance and assurance of high-quality customer, product, and order data within the repair management system
  • Ensuring, optimizing, and continuously developing existing processes
  • Acting as the escalation point for complex customer inquiries and concerns
  • Managing and monitoring service orders throughout the entire repair process
  • Further developing quality standards to ensure an excellent Watch Service customer journey
  • Contributing to the development and enhancement of Power BI reporting and the analysis of relevant KPIs
  • Participating in and taking partial responsibility for global projects
  • Deputizing for the Global Head of Watch Service Administration in administrative and coordination-related matters

What you bring to the role

Commercial apprenticeship or retail-specific vocational qualification (EFZ) supplemented by further education in business administration or project management (e.g., Federal Diploma of Higher Education, HF Diploma, or equivalent qualification)

  • Several years of professional experience in a demanding administrative role, ideally within the luxury goods industry
  • Experience in supporting, coordinating, or taking partial responsibility for projects
  • Strong ERP system knowledge, preferably Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations
  • Power BI knowledge is an advantage
  • Excellent written and spoken German and English skills (C1 level); French and additional languages are considered an asset
  • Strong analytical skills with the ability to think holistically and identify interconnected relationships
  • High level of resilience, ability to work under pressure, and confidence in decision-making, including in deputy and escalation situations
  • Independent, customer-oriented, and assertive personality with a strong entrepreneurial mindset and proactive approach to work
