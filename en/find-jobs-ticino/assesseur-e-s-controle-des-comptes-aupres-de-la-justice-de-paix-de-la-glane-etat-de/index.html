The assessors are non-professional judges who have decision-making competence in judicial matters (art. 4 LJ). An assessor shall be summoned according to the needs of the authority concerned. He/she shall receive meeting allowances in accordance with s. 79a RJ . The assessors shall be elected by the Grand Council, on notice of the Judicial Council, which shall contest the Grand Council with judicial functions (Art. 12 LJ).

  • The Judicial Council is looking for two people with experience and expertise in auditing
  • Candidates must be very available on a day-to-day basis and flexible as the sessions are generated in the short term due to cases handled by the Justices of the Peace
  • Citizenship active on the cantonal level. Persons of foreign nationality must hold an establishment permit and have been domiciled in the canton for at least five years. Candidates must not be the subject of an act of lack of property, not have been criminally convicted for acts incompatible with the function
  • Mastery of the French language with knowledge of the German language
