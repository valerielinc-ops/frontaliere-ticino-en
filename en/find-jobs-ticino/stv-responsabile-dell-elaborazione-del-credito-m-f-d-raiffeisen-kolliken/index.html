Role overview

For Raiffeisenbank Aarau-Lenzburg, a successful, cooperative and locally anchored bank with four offices, we are looking for a motivated personality who, with enthusiasm and curiosity, personally, modernly and regionally shapes the world of credit processing.

  • For Raiffeisenbank Aarau-Lenzburg, a successful, cooperative and locally anchored bank with four offices, we are looking for a motivated personality who, with enthusiasm and curiosity, personally, modernly and regionally shapes the world of credit processing.
  • You process credit transactions from opening to dissolution in the private and corporate customer sector and thus support our credit consulting team competently and reliably.
  • You manage the current credit positions and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

Main responsibilities

  • You process credit transactions from opening to dissolution in the private and corporate customer sector and thus support our credit consulting team competently and reliably.
  • You manage the current credit positions and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.
  • You create real estate assessments and financial analyses and thus make an important contribution to sound credit decisions.
  • You clarify open questions with offices, insurance companies and other external bodies and ensure a smooth handling of credit transactions.
  • You are actively involved in projects and process optimizations and take over the deputy team management credit processing. Requirements
  • You have completed banking education and experience in credit and corporate banking.
  • Ideally, you already have experience in credit processing and can use your knowledge in a targeted manner.
  • You are characterized by an independent, exact and responsible way of working.
