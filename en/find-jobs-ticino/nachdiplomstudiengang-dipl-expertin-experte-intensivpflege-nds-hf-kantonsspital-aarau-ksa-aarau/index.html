Role overview

Tasks

As a student, you care for and accompany our patients. Acquired under professional, competent guidance and guidance During your training period, you will acquire the necessary skills for the intensive care sector in accordance with the specifications of the framework curriculum for intensive care. In addition to your expertise, you bring in ideas and creativity and are ready to embark on something new. Learning in our field is very important to us and is therefore supported both by the entire team and by the existing, modern infrastructure. They work in teams of about 40 employees. Specialist staff, vocational trainers and the medical service are at your side in working and learning. Together with the educational provider of the Aargau School of Anesthesia, Intensive Care and Emergency Care (see afsain), which organizes theoretical education, we support and support our students in everyday life.

Requirements

You are a committed, open-minded personality, have a diploma in health and nursing HF/FH or an equivalent education and at least 6 months of professional experience 100% in the acute field. In the absence of professional experience, we enable you to acquire it in one of our intensive care units. You bring a corresponding understanding for patients in intensive care units, enjoy your profession, interdisciplinary cooperation and want to further develop your nursing competence. Dealing with rapidly changing, complex situations is a welcome challenge for you.
