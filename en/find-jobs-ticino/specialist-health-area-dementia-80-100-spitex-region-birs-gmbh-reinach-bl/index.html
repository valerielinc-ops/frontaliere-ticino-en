Original description

Future-oriented, competent and professional – that is our non-profit organization Spitex Region Birs GmbH. Are you ready for an exciting challenge in an exceptional and diverse working environment? Come join us in the team! With flat hierarchies and a respectful cooperation at eye level, we attach great importance to professional skills and strong social skills. To strengthen our dementia team, we are looking for you as Specialist Health Area Dementia (80-100%)

What to expect

  • Day service from Monday to Friday incl. Public holidays
  • Support our clients and their relatives in dealing with dementia diseases
  • Independent, competent and resource-oriented basic and treatment care of our clients with dementia in their usual living environment
  • Care of reference personnel in an individually adapted setting
  • Support of the Dipl. Nurse HF in case responsibility for a certain number of clients
  • Active participation in the care process
  • Promoting the independence and quality of life of our clients
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with relatives, doctors and other professionals

What you bring

  • Completed vocational training as a specialist in health EFZ
  • Professional experience working with people with dementia or gerontopsychiatry
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German
  • High empathy and empathy
  • An independent, responsible way of working
  • Flexibility and initiative
  • Willingness to travel by car

Our offer for you

  • When designing working hours, we take your personal needs into account
  • Co-creation opportunity in building our offer in the area of dementia
  • Space for personal initiative and professional development
  • A motivated team and an open, appreciative working atmosphere
  • A modern, rapidly growing and innovative organization

With us you will not only find a job, but a meaningful task with a perspective! Become part of our dynamic team and shape the future of healthcare with us. We look forward to your application!
