Appointing authority / department / institution

The Office for Forests and Nature (WNA) is responsible for the management and preservation of forests, biotopes, the landscape, the protection and promotion of flora and fauna, the prevention and protection against natural hazards and the training of forestry specialists. It has a territorial presence in all regions of the canton of Friborg. The various tasks are coordinated at the headquarters, and implementation takes place in the forestry districts and in the wildlife management areas.

Tasks

  • You lead the 2nd forestry district professionally, organizationally and strategically
  • You are committed to the protection, preservation and sustainable development of forests
  • They actively participate in the management of protective forests
  • You plan, coordinate and accompany measures to promote biodiversity
  • You support the further development of multifunctional and future-oriented forest management

Requirements

  • You preferably have a diploma as a forestry engineer from the ETH/FH or equivalent training
  • You have personnel management skills and experience in a management position
  • You have strong team and communication skills and are confident when dealing with forest stakeholders, authorities and partners
  • You have in-depth knowledge of protective forest management and forestry construction and are interested in biodiversity promotion and forest police issues
  • You are proficient in common IT tools, have experience in dealing with geographical information systems and have project management skills
  • You work independently, take on responsibility and are able to manage complex tasks in a structured manner and implement them successfully
  • You speak German and have a good knowledge of French
