Original description

The Bachem Group plans a new site in Sisslerfeld in Northwestern Switzerland as part of its long-term strategy to significantly expand production capacity. The new site will play a central role in supporting Bachem’s growth ambitions in peptide manufacturing.

To strengthen our Global Engineering organization, we are looking for a Senior Project Manager who will take full accountability for the successful delivery of large and complex CAPEX projects and build high-performing project teams from early concept through to operational readiness.

  • Overall project responsibility

Take full accountability for large-scale CAPEX projects (>100 MCHF) across all project phases – from feasibility and concept studies through execution, commissioning, qualification and handover to operations

  • Building and leading high-performing teams

Establish, onboard and lead multidisciplinary project teams; define clear roles, responsibilities and ways of working, and ensure effective collaboration across all project participants

  • Owner’s project leadership and governance

Act as the Owner’s representative ensuring that project design and execution meets defined objectives (scope, functionality, capacity, cost, schedule, quality, safety and sustainability) Drive governance processes including project charters, business cases, URS/URB inputs, and structured decision-making in alignment with Steering Committees

  • Stakeholder alignment and integration

Align and integrate all key stakeholders (User, QA, SHE, Manufacturing IT, Finance) into one high-performing project team and ensure transparent communication and collaboration

  • Execution strategy and partner management

Define and manage appropriate project delivery models (EPCm, EPC, AE&CM) from an Owner’s perspective and lead external engineering partners, contractors and suppliers

  • Project controls and performance management

Implement and ensure robust project controls (scope, schedule, cost, risk, change management) including structured reporting and forecasting (PMR) to senior management

  • Compliance and regulatory excellence

Ensure compliance with GMP requirements, corporate standards, local regulations and building codes across all project phases

  • Commissioning and operational readiness

Lead commissioning, qualification and start-up activities and ensure a smooth transition to operations including post-handover support (“hypercare”) to achieve stable and reliable performance

  • Leadership in decision-making and problem solving

Identify, challenge and evaluate alternatives, drive decision-making and proactively resolve critical project issues in close alignment with the Steering Committee

Your Profile

  • University degree (MSc / BSc) in Engineering, Construction or related discipline
  • Minimum 10 years of experience in managing large capital projects in an industrial, preferably GMP-regulated environment
  • Proven track record in leading complex CAPEX
