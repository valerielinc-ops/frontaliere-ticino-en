Product Purchasing Manager Outdoor, Personalcare, Coffee & Kitchen 80-100 %

Interdiscount — Jegenstorf, Bern, Svizzera

At Interdiscount, we offer our customers the best shopping experience in Switzerland. Engage in a dynamic and entrepreneurial working environment. Take responsibility, be proactive with us and develop further.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the holistic market and earnings development of the product areas Outdoor, Personalcare, Coffee & Kitchen and ensure the achievement of procurement, market share, sales, profitability and quality goals.
  • As a member of the management team, you actively shape the category strategy and business plan and drive their implementation.
  • Together with your Purchasing Managers, you will continuously develop the product ranges and ensure an optimal price-performance ratio with a focus on the needs of Swiss consumers.
  • You develop and implement a holistic promotional planning via the relevant sales channels (POS and online) in coordination with the category management.
  • You maintain national and international supplier relationships and negotiate to ensure attractive conditions and a competitive range.
  • You carry the business responsibility for your area, analyze relevant KPIs and derive targeted optimization measures.
  • You will observe trends and competitive developments and develop your Purchasing Managers in a goal- and result-oriented manner.

Requirements

  • You have a high affinity for home electronics / non-food and necessarily bring experience in FMCG, retail or e-commerce.
  • You have a commercial basic education with business administration training or a business administration degree.
  • You have at least five years of experience in a comparable position as well as leadership experience.
  • You have stylish German skills and good English skills
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage.
  • You have good MS Office knowledge as well as profound experience in dealing with SAP.
  • You think customer- and solution-oriented and convince through high commitment and willingness to learn.
  • You work independently, reliably, analytically and team-oriented.
