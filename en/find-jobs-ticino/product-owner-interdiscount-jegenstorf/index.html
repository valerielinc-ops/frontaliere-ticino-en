Product Owner

Interdiscount — Jegenstorf, Bern, Svizzera

At Interdiscount, we offer our customers the best shopping experience in Switzerland. Engage in a dynamic and entrepreneurial working environment. Take responsibility, be proactive with us and develop further.

Tasks

  • Control and prioritization of the e-commerce backlog within the SAFe framework
  • Participation in roadmap and program backlog planning
  • Translating business and customer goals into clear, actionable user stories
  • Ensuring the definition of ready/done as well as the quality and performance goals
  • Continuous conversion optimization through data-driven insights (KPIs)
  • Close coordination with UX, IT, Marketing and Analytics for holistic e-commerce solutions
  • End-to-end responsibility for the implementation, testing coordination and go-live support of e-commerce features and bug fixes
  • Co-responsibility for the requirements definition and implementation of technical interfaces and for the stability, performance and monitoring of the online shop

Requirements

  • Higher degree in the field of marketing, e-commerce, computer science, business administration (HF, FH, Uni, ETH) or comparable educational path
  • At least 3 years of experience as a product owner or in a similar function
  • Good knowledge of current technologies and systems (shop system, search, customer identity, etc.)
  • Target group-oriented and transparent communication
  • Very good organization and presentation skills as well as a quick understanding
  • Stylistic German is mandatory, good English skills and French is advantageous
