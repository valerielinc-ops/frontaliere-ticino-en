Staff Frontend Engineer (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. Staff Frontend Engineer (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, immediately or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. In the agile capacity control program area, we develop, operate and train central software products that keep over 10,000 train journeys safe and on time every day. You will work on rail-critical and highly available systems and take on a key role in the transition from a desktop front end based on Eclipse RCP to a modern web architecture with Canvas and WebGL. You can expect a cross-team environment with a lot of creative freedom, close collaboration with the business and visible impact in the operations centers. You will grow professionally and personally by taking on architectural work, empowering the team and shaping the path to a sustainable front-end platform. You can find benefits such as free GA and discounted travel abroad

  • also for the family
  • on our website under Benefits. You can move that.
  • As a staff frontend engineer, you will design the architecture and design of the future web UI of a highly available application for capacity control, thereby contributing to stability, performance, good usability and sustainable maintainability in rail operations.
  • You translate complex technical requirements into robust web views, canvas and WebGL-based user interfaces, clearly defined APIs and reusable web components.
  • You evaluate technical options transparently, make viable decisions and plan features realistically so that effort and benefit remain in balance.
  • You will provide in-depth advice to product owners as well as system architects and managers so that architectural decisions are made on a solid technical basis.
  • Across teams, you empower development teams through coaching, pairing, code and architecture reviews, and anchor common UI standards for consistent development.
  • You promote a sustainable engineering culture with clear best practices, templates and well-maintained documentation and consistently convert prototypes into production-ready software. This is what you bring with you.
  • Enjoy empowering others and effectively sharing knowledge in cross-functional teams.
  • Analytical and structured thinking in order to communicate complex issues in an understandable way and to coordinate confidently with many stakeholders.
  • High quality standards with pragmatism and acceptance of responsibility over the entire life cycle from the idea to operation.
  • Several years of experience in the design and implementation of modern web front-end architectures for business-critical applications. Confident in using TypeScript, Angular, Web Components, Canvas, WebGL and SVG as well as robust interfaces to backends.
  • Familiar with Java based cloud environments. Practice with Java, Spring, OpenShift and Solace Messaging. Experience in migrating desktop and rich client applications to web front ends as well as in the conscious use of AI-supported development.
  • German at level C2 and English at level C2.
