District Manager Dairy / Bread

Coop Cooperative — Münchenbuchsee, Bern, Svizzera

Responsibilities

  • With your commitment, you ensure that the products are always available in sales and make you want to buy.

  • With respect for your team, you ensure a pleasant team atmosphere.

  • Conditions of employment: Pension fund: 2/3 paid in by Coop, attractive employment conditions and certified equal pay, loyalty bonuses, savings plan with the pension fund, collective employment agreement (CLA), 18 weeks of paid maternity leave and three weeks of paid paternity leave

  • Further training: Over 600 internal training courses, talent management with a wide range of development opportunities, training and further education offers from the L-GAV and Coop
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people, animals and nature with over 400 actions
  • Communication: internal app for employees
