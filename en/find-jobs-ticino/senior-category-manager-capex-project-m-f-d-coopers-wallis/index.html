Original description

For our client from the Pharma manufacturing, we are looking for a Senior Category Manager, who will act as a Lead for the development and execution of category strategies for Civil and CSA packages within CAPEX projects

Your Tasks

  • Define and deploy category strategy for CSA (civil works, structural steel, buildings)
  • Lead sourcing, tendering and supplier selection for CSA packages
  • Drive commercial negotiations including contracts, frameworks and pricing structures
  • Partner with Engineering, Construction and Project teams to ensure early procurement involvement
  • Manage strategic supplier relationships and contractor performance
  • Drive cost optimization, benchmarking and value engineering across projects
  • Ensure risk management for construction activities (schedule, cost, HSE and supply risk)
  • Ensure compliance with GMP, EHS and construction standards
  • Translate category strategies into project-specific sourcing and execution plans
  • Monitor contractor performance and implement continuous improvement initiatives

Your Profile

  • Degree in Engineering (Civil, Structural, Construction) or Business. Technical background strongly preferred.
  • 10–15 years experience in Procurement, Category Management or Construction sourcing
  • Strong experience in pharmaceutical / life sciences CAPEX projects
  • Proven experience in CSA / construction sourcing
  • Strong track record in complex negotiations and contract management
  • Experience in large CAPEX projects (greenfield / brownfield)
  • Solid understanding of construction execution, engineering and project lifecycle
  • Strong stakeholder management across Engineering, Construction and Project teams
  • Fluent English and German
