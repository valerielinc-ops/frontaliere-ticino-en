Original description

At Alcon, we are driven by the meaningful work we do to help people see brilliantly. As a world leader in ophthalmology, we are committed to innovation and progress and act quickly. We reward you for your commitment and achievements and can drive your career forward as never before. Together, we go beyond ourselves to improve the lives of our patients and customers.

We promote a corporate culture that involves everyone and are looking for diverse, talented people who want to join Alcon.

As Professional Education & Development Manager (m/f/d) - Vision Care DACH, you are responsible for the development, implementation and continuous development of educational and training programs for opticians, optometrists and other specialist groups with a focus on Switzerland. You work closely with internal and external stakeholders and promote the transfer of knowledge to Alcon products, technologies and clinical topics. The office is located in the home office.

A typical working day in this function includes

  • Development, planning and implementation of education, training and training programmes for customers and partners.
  • Creation of training materials, presentations and digital learning content.
  • Organization and implementation of training and workshops.
  • Organization of participation in congresses.
  • Establish and maintain sustainable relationships with key-service leaders (COLs), universities and professional associations; actively participate in the association's work.
  • Technical and technical support for customers and internal teams.
  • presentation of scientific and clinical content at specialist events.
  • Evaluation and continuous optimization of training programs.
  • Management of projects in the field of Professional Education & Development.

WHAT YOU MITTING TO ALCON

  • Completed studies in the field of eye optics/optometry.
  • Perennial experience in eye optics/optometry.
  • Experience in planning and conducting training, workshops and events.
  • Extensive presentation and communication skills.
  • High level of ownership and strong project management and organisational competence.
  • Existing network within the Swiss eye optics and optometry industry is an advantage.
  • Very good knowledge of German, English and French.

HOW TO BE AFOLLOWING ALCON

  • Experience excellent onboarding from the first working day at one of the top employers 2026(*) and discover the many opportunities for further development within national and international departments.
  • Work independently, in a sales-oriented manner and discover the varied field of tasks with many design possibilities and space for your own ideas.
  • Work with an excellent team of colleagues to help you achieve your goals and succeed.
  • Get an extensive package of the available benefits in addition to an attractive remuneration.
