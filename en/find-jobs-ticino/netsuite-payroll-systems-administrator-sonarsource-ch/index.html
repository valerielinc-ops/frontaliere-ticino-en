Company and context

  • You are comfortable owning the administration of our core payroll and financial platforms — ADP and NetSuite — for a fast-growing, multi-national organization.
  • You ensure payroll processes are accurate and compliant, keep the ERP running smoothly, and support the data flows our Finance team depends on for planning and reporting. Payroll Systems Administration (ADP)
  • Own the end-to-end administration and configuration of ADP (Workforce Now preferred), ensuring timely, accurate, and compliant payroll processing across multiple entities.
  • Manage user access, roles, and governance in ADP; coordinate with HR and Finance on payroll cycles, headcount changes, and regulatory compliance requirements.
  • Maintain and improve data integrations between ADP and NetSuite — ensuring payroll journal entries, employee records, and cost allocations flow accurately and on schedule.
  • Troubleshoot payroll discrepancies, manage the relationship with ADP support, and coordinate system updates and releases. NetSuite ERP Administration
  • Administer and maintain the NetSuite instance: user management, roles and permissions, workflows, saved searches, and module configurations (P2P, Multi-book, Intercompany, and others).
  • Support month-end close activities and ensure NetSuite remains the trusted system of record for financial actuals.
  • Lead change management processes — documenting, testing, and deploying system changes with proper controls and impact assessments.
  • Support IT General Controls (ITGC) and SOX compliance requirements across financial systems. Financial Planning & Reporting Support
