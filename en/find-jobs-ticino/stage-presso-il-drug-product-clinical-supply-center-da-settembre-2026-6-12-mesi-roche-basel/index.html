The Position As part of this internship, you will receive a unique insight into the processes and processes of the Drug Product Clinical Supply Center (Parenterals), which deals with the production of clinical investigational preparations for parenteral application (syringes, vials for liquid & lyophilized products) using modern and highly automated filling systems. The business unit supplies phase I, II & III clinical trials worldwide based on the rich R&D portfolio of new, innovative and highly active drugs.

The possibility As an intern, you support the teams of the Science & Technology department in their activities in the fields of qualification & validation and single-use technology. which are connected with solution production and vial and finished syringe filling. Due to the interfaces to the support areas, such as engineering and quality assurance, you also have the opportunity to gain insights into adjacent areas.

  • Support for project implementation in the cGMP environment
  • Planning, coordination and implementation of studies at scale in cross-functional teams
  • Support for asset qualifications & process validations
  • Insights into the Contamination Control Strategy and Aseptic Assurance
  • Collaboration on concept paper in line with leading regulations
  • Preparation of standard work regulations and other documents based on good documentation practices
  • Assumption of organisational tasks
  • Ensuring quality requirements according to cGMP

Who you are

  • You are completing a master’s degree in science, engineering or pharmaceuticals or have completed it in one of these areas within the last 12 months.

  • You bring commitment, motivation and the willingness to work independently.
  • Good knowledge of the common office application as well as fluent German and good English skills are required.
