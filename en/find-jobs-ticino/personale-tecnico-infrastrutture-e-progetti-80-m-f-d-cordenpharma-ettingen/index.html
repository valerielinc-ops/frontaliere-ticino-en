Role overview

CordenPharma is one of the leading Contract Development and Manufacturing Organizations (CDMO) and develops and produces pharmaceutical active ingredients, pharmaceuticals and related packaging services on behalf of its customers as a full-service service provider. The group employs around 3,000 people. Our network in Europe and the USA offers flexible and specialized solutions for six technology platforms: Peptides, Lipids & Carbohydrates, Injectables, Highly Potent & Oncology, Small Molecules and Oligonucleotides. We strive for excellence in supporting this network and are committed to providing the highest quality products for the benefit of patients. Our People Vision: We strive for excellence. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a dedicated technical specialist in the areas of buildings, infrastructure and project management.

  • Responsibility for the maintenance and repair of buildings and infrastructure
  • Management and supervision of projects and sub-projects such as new installations, conversions and extensions of production or infrastructure facilities
  • Planning and procurement in compliance with regulations, budgets and deadlines
  • Conceptual and operational management of the projects in cooperation with internal and external stakeholders
  • Ensuring compliance with technical standards, GMP requirements and HSE guidelines
  • Active participation in qualifications in the GMP environment
  • Project management and project controlling of investments at the entire site
  • Regular reporting to senior management

To successfully master this role, we are looking for the following qualifications:

  • Basic technical training with further training in engineering, facility management, engineering or project management
  • Experience in supporting building and infrastructure facilities
  • Project management experience, ideally in a regulated environment
  • Knowledge of GMP, HSE and technical standards is beneficial
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Communication strength and pleasure in working with different stakeholders
  • Very good German - as well as good English skills in word and writing
  • On-call service
