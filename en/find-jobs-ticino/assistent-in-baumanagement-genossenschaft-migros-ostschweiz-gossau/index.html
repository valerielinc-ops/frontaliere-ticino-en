Original description

You are the organisational hub of our construction projects and support our project and construction managers in all administrative matters. With your own initiative, organizational talent and communication strength, you ensure smooth processes, keep an overview and successfully advance projects. Become part of our team!

Mansioni

  • You are the right hand of our project and construction managers and ensure that everything runs smoothly – from offers to contracts and payments.
  • You organize meetings like a professional, including logging (clear, right?).
  • You keep the wire to our planners and entrepreneurs and show external planners how the construction cost structure GMOS works.
  • You assume the responsibility that all data and information are entered correctly in the construction administration program (MesserliBAUAD) - independently and sovereignly.
  • You take care of everything that is involved in everyday project life: correspondence, reports, presentations – and also have the IT and the interface to the finance department under control.

Requirements

  • Professional experience: in a similar position ideally in an architecture firm, a general contractor or a construction company.
  • Completed basic or further education: in the commercial sector
  • You like to take action, think along and take responsibility – Initiative is your middle name.
  • With your winning appearance and your communication strength, you simply convince everyone.
  • You are a team player with assertiveness and always work precisely and structured.
  • Microsoft Office 365? No problem for you! Bonus points are available if you already have experience with the Messerli construction administration program.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • L-GAV: Attractive Migros Land collective employment contract
  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Cumulus Points: Multiple Cumulus Points on food and nonfood items
  • Mobile subscription: Highly discounted conditions for private mobile subscription also for family and friends
  • Sports and fitness offers: discount on subscriptions to leisure and fitness facilities
