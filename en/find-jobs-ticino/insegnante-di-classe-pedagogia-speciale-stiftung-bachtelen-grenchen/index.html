Role overview

In the canton of Solothurn, BACHTELEN is committed to the individual promotion and integration of children, young people and young adults.

In order to achieve our goals, we run daycare centers, day schools, housing offers, offers of individual support as well as therapeutic and advisory offers.

We are looking for the school year 2026/2027 for the day school Grenchen as of now Healing class teachers for cycle 1 and 2 (40 – 60 %) or a teacher with readiness, start training within the next two years
