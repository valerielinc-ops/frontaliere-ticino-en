We pay great attention to the diversity of life paths of our employees. Our positions are open to both women and men.

Located in the heart of Sion, the Valère Clinic is one of the most famous acute care clinics in the country. In collaboration with over 60 doctors, its 180 employees provide several thousand patients with the best care each year. In a relaxing environment and a warm atmosphere, the Clinic offers personalized medical care for all Valaisans and Valaisans, regardless of their insurance coverage (community, half-private and private).

Since 2013, the Valère Clinic has been part of the Swiss Medical Network, a network of clinics and medical centres throughout Switzerland. It is listed on the Hospitals list in the Canton of the Valais. The Valère Radiology Centre, with its team of radiologists, offers high quality services and expertise in the most advanced areas of imaging: neuroradiology, radiology-ORL, women's imaging, thoraco-abdominal radiology, urological, osteoarticular, vascular and interventional.
