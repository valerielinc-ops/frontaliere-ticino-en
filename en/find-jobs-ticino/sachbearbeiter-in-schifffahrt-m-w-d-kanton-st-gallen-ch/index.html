Role overview

The Road Transport and Shipping Office is an ISO-certified organization with 160 employees in various professional fields. The Maritime Office is responsible for the inspection and certification of watercraft and shipmasters as well as maritime police surveillance in the lakes. Together with a small team, you make a significant contribution to safety and environmental protection on our St.Gallian waters (Bodensee, Walensee and Upper Lake Zurich).

  • Certification of skippers and ships
  • Scheduling of exam dates for driver examinations and technical inspections of ships
  • Collection of watercraft taxes and fees
  • Examination and issue of authorisations for nautical events
  • Customer counter and back office services
  • Completed basic commercial training with several years of professional experience
  • Nautical knowledge is beneficial
  • Experience in direct customer contact
  • Readiness for the seasonally very high time burden with holiday and compensation options mainly in the months October to April
  • Resilient and team capable personality with high social skills and healthy assertiveness
  • Varied everyday work with a lot of self-responsibility and intensive customer contact
  • Working in an open, committed and competent team
  • Current employment conditions
  • Attractive workplace in Kornhaus Rorschach

Contact: Peter Neuweiler, Head of Shipping Department, 058 229 93 20
