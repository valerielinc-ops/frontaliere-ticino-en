More about this search for cross-border applicants

Mendrisio, nestled between Italy and Switzerland, offers a dynamic job market for professionals who commute across the border. Whether you’re in nursing, IT, or teaching, local demand and flexible work arrangements make Mendrisio a practical choice for career-focused expats.
