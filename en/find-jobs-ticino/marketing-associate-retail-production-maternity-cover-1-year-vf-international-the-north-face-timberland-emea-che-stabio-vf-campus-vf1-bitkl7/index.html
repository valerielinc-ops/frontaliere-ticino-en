Role overview

The North Face EMEA are looking for an outstanding Marketing Associate, Retail Production (Maternity Cover 1 year) to join our Marketing team of The North Face in our European Headquarter in Stabio, Switzerland. Let's talk about the role

The Marketing Associate, Retail Production is responsible for supporting all functions and aspects of the The North Face Go To Market process from season’s launch until the in store execution building on the consumer experience driven by Purpose and Creative Vision, across The North Face 4 walls environments.

The selected candidate will also take care of external communications as main point of contact for Retail Marketing campaign executions and bespoke retail projects(seasonal windows, store customizations, POP material, instore videos, store takeover), as well as coordinating external agencies and vendors in order to deliver all the seasonal campaigns on time and on budget with strong focus of retail marketing briefing to brand team, collecting input from operations, product merchandising and visual merchandising team, in collaboration with the Creative Environment Coordinator.
