Original description

Would you like to participate in the design and management of the holiday care of the elementary school Stadt Lucerne? Are you a leader with organizational talent, for whom the well-being of the children is of course at the center? Then you are welcome to contact us. Tasks: • You support the management of holiday care in various areas of responsibility and temporarily assume overall responsibility (personnel management, communication with internal and external stakeholders, budget, guidance/accompanying new employees and apprentices) • You plan and create the rough program for the activities • You run the operational operation at a holiday care location for a few weeks (use times in consultation with the overall management) Requirements • Social, socio-cultural or tertiary education (higher vocational training) • You bring experience in supplementary school care • You are resilient, agile and enjoy dealing with children in large, changing and heterogeneous groups of children • You want to take on responsibility and partly management tasks • The majority of you are available during the holiday care weeks and you work annually from January to the end of October
