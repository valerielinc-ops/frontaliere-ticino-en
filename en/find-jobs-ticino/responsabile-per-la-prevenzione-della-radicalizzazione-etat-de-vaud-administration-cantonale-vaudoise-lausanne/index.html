Role overview

As a task manager for the prevention of radicalisation, you ensure that individuals who are radicalised or are in the process of radicalisation are supported individually and act as an expert on all issues relating to radical ideologies.

You are involved in the development of the Unit for the Prevention of Radicalisations (UPRAD), attached to the SG-DJES, which perpetuates the mechanism for preventing radicalisation and violent extremism set up by the Council of State.

At the request of the UPRAD officials, you ensure on the ground the support of radicalised or radicalised people by offering them individualised support, and deploy strategies of disengagement in interaction with the family environment and the various partners.
