Original description

Discover the LIEBHERR world. As a successful family-owned company, Liebherr focuses on innovation, quality and reliability. Liebherr-Export AG in Nussbaumen near Baden is the international sales hub for the construction portfolio. They benefit from a modern workplace, a collegial team and a flexible working culture. In this position, you act as a central interface between sales, customers and internal and external partners and actively contribute to the joint success.

Tasks

  • Responsibility for end-to-end offer management for defence projects, from the qualification of business opportunity to submission of the offer.
  • Coordination and management of all offer-related activities with internal stakeholders (engineering, sales, law, compliance and finance) in a highly complex organizational environment.
  • Guarantee of the timely, regulatory and high-quality creation of RFIs (request for information), RFPs (request for proposal) and RFQs (request for quotation) in an international environment.
  • Structure, planning and active drive forward supply activities, ensuring a high degree of organization, transparency and traceability. - Support for the strategies of the company's activities, including coordination with customers, OEMs and partners in the phases prior to submission of the offer. - Control of supply schedules, risks, decision milestones and management approvals with a strong focus on effectiveness and efficiency.
  • Participation in the design, implementation and continuous optimization of bid and opportunity management processes in a growing defense environment. - Support for structured handover from the offer phase to the implementation of the contract and the early development of program management. - They help to establish scalable structures and working methods for a continuing development of defense business within the organization.

Qualification

  • A completed university degree in engineering, business administration or a similar subject area forms the professional basis.
  • Professional experience in Bid Management, management, project management or in a comparable function is ideally brought with you in the defence, aerospace industry or in a complex industrial environment.
  • A distinctive organizational talent as well as the ability to structure complex topics, processes and stakeholder landscapes are among the particular strengths.
  • Dealing with changing organizational structures is easy, coupled with the willingness to actively shape new processes and working methods. - A practical and implementation-oriented approach goes hand in hand with a high level of efficiency, quality and reliability. - Excellent skills in the areas of coordination, communication and stakeholder management enable successful cooperation across different departments and hierarchy levels.
  • Interest and motivation to develop your own role in the direction of the management of the programme in the future and early programme responsibility round off the profile. - Negotiable knowledge of German and English is required; additional language skills in Spanish or French are of benefit.
