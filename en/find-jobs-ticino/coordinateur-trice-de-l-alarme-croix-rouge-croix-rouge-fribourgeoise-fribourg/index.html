Original description

The Red Cross (CRF) is a cantonal association founded in 1909, a member of the Swiss Red Cross. With some 160 employees and the support of 500 volunteers, our institution is active in the fields of health, migration and social. In particular, we act in favour of all vulnerable persons, while respecting the fundamental principles of the Red Cross. For the next retirement of the current incumbent, we are looking for a Red Cross Alarm Coordinator As part of home maintenance services, you ensure the coordination and operational functioning of the Red Cross Alarm device, as well as the provision of electric beds. You guarantee a rapid, effective and structured mobilization of volunteers and stakeholders, in connection with beneficiaries, their relatives, the alarm centre and internal partners. In performing this function, you receive and process applications, organize interventions, manage technical and administrative monitoring of equipment and contribute to continuous service improvement. You are the reference point for logistical and technical questions related to alarms and electric beds. Main tasks:

  • Receive alarm installation requests and enter information in the software;
  • Plan facilities with volunteers and provide them with the necessary information;
  • Follow-up of interventions, liaison with the plant and management of technical emergencies or disputes;
  • Manage local stock, maintenance, recovery, rotation and inventory of equipment;
  • Organize the delivery of electric beds, monitor billing, debtors, volunteer fees and statistics:
  • Participate in coordination sessions with the various partners.
  • Profile required: - SWC business employee with at least 5 years of work experience or equivalent training; - Experience in the social field or in customer service
  • French or German mother tongue with excellent knowledge of the other language; bilingualism essential to the function;
  • Strong organizational skills, rigour and ability to manage priorities;
  • Aisance with common digital tools, including Office suite, spreadsheets, e-mail and file management;
  • Basic technical knowledge of electronic devices, tele-assistance, telephony and connections is an asset;
  • Very good relational qualities, a sense of listening and diplomacy;
  • Knowledge of the health and social network and experience with desired volunteers. Entry on duty
