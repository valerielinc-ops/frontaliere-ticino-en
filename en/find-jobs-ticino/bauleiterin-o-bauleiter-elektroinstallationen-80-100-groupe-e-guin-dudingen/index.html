Role overview

We operate in Western Switzerland and support our customers in implementing their projects by offering comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, gentle mobility, air conditioning or water treatment.

As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2,300 employees, including 210 apprentices, as well as the reconciliation of private and professional life.

Thanks to its expertise, Groupe E Tech is a customer-oriented and powerful partner for interdisciplinary projects in the areas of electrical installations, panels and electrical infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar systems, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, refrigeration, sanitation, water treatment, sales and installation of kitchens and household appliances as well as plant maintenance.
