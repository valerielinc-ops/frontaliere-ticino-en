Role overview

Who are we?

The cantonal institution of social insurance (ECAS) is an autonomous institution governed by public law which administers and coordinates the activities of the Caisse de compensation du canton de Fribourg, those of the cantonal office of disability insurance and the central services.

It thus constitutes a single competence centre in social insurance under the first pillar for insured persons and enterprises in the canton of Fribourg.
