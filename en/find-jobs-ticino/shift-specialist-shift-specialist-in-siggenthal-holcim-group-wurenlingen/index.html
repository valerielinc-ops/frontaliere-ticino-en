Original description

You also keep an overview in shift operations, enjoy working as a team and ensure a smooth production process with your technical understanding? Then become part of our team and get our production rolling reliably.

What to expect

  • Operation, monitoring, maintenance and cleaning of our production facilities in compliance with all safety, quality and environmental standards
  • Ensuring smooth shift transfer and documentation of events and disturbances
  • Control of installation parameters and manual sampling if necessary
  • Close cooperation with maintenance, production and quality management
  • Assistance in the event of downtime, revisions and other maintenance measures
  • Assumption of further operational tasks as required

What you bring

  • Completed technical vocational training and ideally experience in industrial shift operations
  • Technical understanding with basic knowledge in mechanics, electrics and IT
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • High security awareness as well as solution- and process-oriented thinking
  • Team spirit, communication strength and openness to new technologies
  • Good knowledge of German and willingness to work shifts
