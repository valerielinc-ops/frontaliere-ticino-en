Original description

Working independently. Carried in the team. Close to people. You like to work independently, appreciate calm and focused care and want to reconcile your assignments well with your everyday life? Then our regional night service could suit you exactly. The Spitex organizations Regio Liestal, Waldenburgertal, Thürnen-Diepflingen and Obers Homburgertal together ensure nursing care during the night. The regional night service is run by the Spitex Regio Liestal. Dipl. Nurse HF/FH – Regional Night Service This is what awaits you with us

  • Plannable flexibility: You work about 5-8 nights per month. Service planning takes place in a small team and is coordinated together.
  • Self-employment with support: You work independently and have a direct professional contact person during the night.
  • Time for good care: Our night tours allow you to work independently with little time pressure.
  • Start well accompanied : We will carefully introduce you to your tasks and the regional night service.
  • Attractive surcharges : You receive CHF 10 per hour night allowance and CHF 10 per hour for the pikett service. For bets on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays, an additional allowance of CHF 10 per hour applies.
  • Well compensated on the way: For journeys with your own car you receive CHF 0.70 per kilometer.
  • A small team: They coordinate easily with each other and support each other.

Your tasks at night They look after and care for clients competently in their home. Your tour usually lasts from about 21.30 to 04.00. Afterwards, you will perform piquetting service from home until 7 a.m. You take responsibility for the nursing situations during the night, support and advise relatives if necessary and are also on tour as a qualified nurse.

This is what you bring with you

  • Diploma as nurse HF/FH or equivalent education at tertiary level
  • Enjoyment of independent and self-responsible work
  • Safety and flexibility in dealing with different people and situations
  • Willingness to travel at night and to get along in different households
  • Category B driving licence and own car

Get to know us without obligation and get a taste of us! This gives you a realistic insight into the regional night service and can find out whether this special form of Spitex work suits you. We compensate for the trial. Do you have questions? Renate Fluri, Division Manager, Tel. 061 926 60 90, is happy to provide you with information. We look forward to your application via our job portal. Applications and contacts of personnel service providers cannot be considered.
