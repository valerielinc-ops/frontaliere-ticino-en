Original description

Within the Center for Interprofessional Practices (CPI), the specialized clinical dietitian provides her expertise in complex nutritional situations. She advises and supports IMAD professionals and contributes to improving nutrition and dietetics practices, in the context of home and community support. The specialized clinical dietitian constitutes a resource for teams in complex situations and contributes to the development of nutrition and dietetics practices through clinical expertise, leadership, implementation and continuous improvement. Your missions and responsibilities

Expertise in human nutrition and metabolism

  • Provide specialized clinical expertise in nutrition and dietetics in the analysis and management of complex nutritional situations at home.
  • Support dietitians in nutritional assessment, development of nutritional diagnosis and definition of intervention plans adapted to patient needs (PSN).
  • Contribute to the identification of nutritional risk factors and the definition of responses adapted to the home and community context.
  • Develop, update and promote evidence-based clinical recommendations, tools and resources.

Advanced practices and leadership in nutrition and dietetics

  • Assume a second-line reference role for clinical situations requiring advanced clinical expertise in nutrition and dietetics.
  • Support professionals in the analysis of complex situations and support clinical decision-making.
  • Promote the development of the skills of dietitians through practice analysis, advice and training.
  • Contribute to the development of clinical leadership and the recognition of expertise in nutrition and dietetics within the IMAD.
  • Contribute to the evolution of professional practices and the development of innovative and adapted responses to the challenges of home care.
  • Participate in the design, implementation and evaluation of clinical projects aimed at improving the quality and safety of nutrition and dietetic services.
  • Develop and update recommendations, clinical processes, etc. based on evidence.
  • Contribute to training and the integration of scientific knowledge into professional practices.

Nutritional epidemiology and implementation in clinical nutrition

  • Ensure scientific and professional monitoring in nutrition and dietetics in order to guarantee the updating of practices in accordance with the most recent knowledge.
  • Mobilize scientific data and nutritional epidemiology to support clinical decision-making.
  • Identify emerging issues likely to be the subject of evaluations, implementation projects or applied research.
  • Participate in institutional, evaluation or innovation projects related to customer needs and home care issues.
  • Contribute to the analysis, promotion and dissemination of results from projects and work carried out in order to support the development of services.

Interprofessional collaboration

  • Collaborate with all IMAD professionals,
  • Promote interprofessional collaborative practices in the management of complex situations.
  • Maintain collaborations with the health and social network and relevant partners.
  • HES-SO Bachelor in Nutrition and Dietetics, supplemented by a Master of Science in Health Sciences (MScSa), obtained or in progress.
  • Significant clinical experience in nutrition and dietetics,
  • Experience in supporting complex situations, training or developing practices.
  • Experience in project management and/or quality improvement.
  • Excellent analytical, synthesis, writing and scientific popularization skills.
  • Clinical leadership, autonomy, proactivity and excellent relational and teaching skills.
  • Ability to work interprofessionally and follow institutional guidelines.
  • Good knowledge of the context of home support and the Geneva health and social network: an asset.
  • Knowledge of English (read and spoken): desired.
