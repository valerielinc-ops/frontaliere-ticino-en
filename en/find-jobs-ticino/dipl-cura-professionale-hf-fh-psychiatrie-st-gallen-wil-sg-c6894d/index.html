Main responsibilities

  • On our dementia and delirium ward, you will strengthen the team as a committed and experienced nurse in the field of gerontopsychiatry.
  • As a caregiver, you assume the nursing responsibility for the individual care and care of our patients.
  • In the nursing and therapeutic process, you accompany our patients individually and master even complex situations safely.
  • Your versatile tasks include everyday life, administrative activities and the implementation of medical and technical prescriptions.
  • Your observations are carefully documented and reported in oral and written form. Requirements
  • Professional experience in long-term care or psychiatry is an advantage and ideally you already know the field of gerontopsychiatry or want to deepen in it.
  • Working in a multi-professional team and caring for people with a high need for support interest you and motivate you in everyday work.
  • With your resilient and open-minded personality as well as your good communication skills, you contribute to a constructive interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • Very good knowledge of German at least C1 level and good EDP user knowledge round off your profile and support you in your administrative tasks.
