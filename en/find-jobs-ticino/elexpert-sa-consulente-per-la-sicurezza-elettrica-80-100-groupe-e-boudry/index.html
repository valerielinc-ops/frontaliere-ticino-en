Main responsibilities

  • Your responsibilities:
  • Check the conformity of electrical installations and identify possible non-conformities.
  • Establish control reports, measurement protocols and safety reports.
  • Inform owners, operators and installers of the defects observed and the corrective measures to be implemented.
  • Ensure, in collaboration with the secretariat, the administrative monitoring of files until their closure.
  • Advise customers on questions relating to electrical safety and current regulations.
  • Participate in developing the quality of services and maintaining a high level of technical competence.
  • Maintain relationships of trust with various partners in the sector. Profile:
  • CFC of electrician installer, electrician planner or equivalent training, supplemented by a Federal Certificate of Electrical Safety Advisor.
  • Experience in the control of electrical installations and solid experience in the field of electrical installation.
