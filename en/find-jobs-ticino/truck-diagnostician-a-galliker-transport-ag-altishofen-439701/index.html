Truck Diagnostician (a) Our trucks are one of Galliker’s most important trademarks. Maintenance and repairs are therefore of great importance. Most tasks are performed in our own modern workshops. To complement our team, we are looking for you as a truck diagnostician (a) either immediately or by agreement. Task area • Creating vehicle diagnostics with brand-specific testers • Execution of repair orders and revisions on various brands • Installation of accessories • Procurement of the necessary spare parts in the on-site warehouse • Support to the workshop team leader • With the help of breakdown + towing service (Galliker Action Service) Requirement profile • Truck mechanic or related direction (agricultural/construction machinery/car mechanic) • Training as a truck diagnostician or equivalent • Cat C driving licence mandatory • Independent, proactive, resilient and flexible • Motivated, team capable and dutiful • Very good knowledge of German in writing and language Offer • Collegial and dedicated team • Varied work with the latest tools and devices • Always on the pulse of the latest technology and digitalization • Personal development opportunities in a versatile company • Brand-specific and personal training We offer you a varied and interesting job in a future-oriented team. Did we get your interest? We look forward to your application. Christoph Jeannerat, Human Resources, telephone: +41 62 748 82 03.
