Role overview

Context and Mission

The Vice-President for Operations manages all the activities that make campuses live on a daily basis — restoration, information systems, security, operation, library and scientific information — and plans to develop its infrastructure for the future.

The Vice-President for Operations manages an annual budget of CHF 200 million for a portfolio of approx. 200 projects and approximately 400 employees.

  • Main tasks and responsibilities Your tasks and responsibilities will be:
  • Articulation Projects-Services: Ensure consistency between new projects (constructions, renovations, SI, etc.) and their operational impact on the quality of services delivered to EPFL units.
  • Piloting the process of project entry into portfolios: Conduct the entire Demand Management process, manage its planning and conduct with key stakeholders.
