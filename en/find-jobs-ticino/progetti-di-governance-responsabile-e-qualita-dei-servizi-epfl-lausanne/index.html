Original description

Context and Mission The Vice-President for Operations manages all the activities that make campuses live on a daily basis — restoration, information systems, security, operation, library and scientific information — and plans to develop its infrastructure for the future. The Vice-President for Operations manages an annual budget of CHF 200 million for a portfolio of approx. 200 projects and approximately 400 employees. To strengthen its State-Major team and in a context of retirement, the Vice-President for Operations is looking for its future Head of Governance Projects and Quality of Services. At the crossroads between strategic leadership and user orientation, you guarantee the governance of the Vice-President's portfolios of projects while ensuring the quality and continuous optimization of the services offered. You act as a real bridge between the methodological rigour of projects (building infrastructure and digitalisation) and the concrete experience experienced by users on a daily basis. Main tasks and responsibilities Your tasks and responsibilities will be: • Articulation Projects-Services: Ensure consistency between new projects (constructions, renovations, SI, etc.) and their operational impact on the quality of services delivered to EPFL units. • Piloting the process of project entry into portfolios: Conduct the entire Demand Management process, manage its planning and conduct with key stakeholders. • Governance and methodological compliance: Ensure adherence to processes, milestones and methodological quality throughout the life cycle of the Vice-President's project and service portfolios. • Quality and satisfaction management: Develop and monitor performance indicators (KPIs) measuring the health of major projects and the perceived quality of service delivery by users. • Continuous improvement: pilot and lead cross-cutting initiatives and projects to continuously improve the quality, fluidity and efficiency of the Vice-President's services.
