Original description

The GBS St.Gallen is an important vocational and further education institution in Eastern Switzerland. In addition to commercial-industrial basic education with 4 500 learners in over 45 professions, we also train 600 students and course participants in the field of higher vocational training and further education (HBB/WB). Our mission statement of people-education-success is based on the principle of shared leadership responsibility, where cooperation and collaboration are at the heart of creating a dynamic learning and working environment based on diversity, innovation and continuous development. You like to make sure that everything runs smoothly in the background – and enjoy developing processes and making things even better? Then this task could be just right for you. From the production of certificates to the recording of our training in NESA to the cooperation with external partners: inside you can expect a versatile task with responsibility, scope for design and a lot of potential for process improvements.
