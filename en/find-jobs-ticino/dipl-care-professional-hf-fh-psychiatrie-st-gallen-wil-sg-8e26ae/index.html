Original description

Your tasks • In the daily clinical setting, you take over the individual, need and resource-oriented treatment and care of our patients. • You contribute your professional skills as part of an interdisciplinary team in a caregiver-oriented environment and actively shape the cooperation. • For expert care within the care standards, you coordinate the nursing therapeutic treatment planning with internal and external support groups. • An open and relationship-oriented working environment offers you the opportunity to use your skills in complex situations and to actively shape the therapeutic environment. • In close exchange with relatives and referees, you contribute to a trusting cooperation and an appreciative cooperation. Your profile • With your completed training as a nurse HF or FH and a required SRK recognition or a positive SRK PreCheck with a foreign diploma, you bring the necessary professional foundation. • Ideally, you have already gained experience in the psychiatric field and enjoy working with mentally ill people as well as working in a multi-professional team. • Your social skills, flexibility and resilience characterize you as well as an independent and self-responsible working method. • High commitment and creativity are among your strengths and are complemented by very good knowledge of German at at least C1 level. • You also bring good IT knowledge and can use it specifically to support you in administrative work safely and efficiently. With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatments and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations. We are on the road digitally and are committed to sustainability, so we look forward to your online application via our homepage. As soon as your application has been received online, you will receive a confirmation of receipt by e-mail. We are happy to get to know you
