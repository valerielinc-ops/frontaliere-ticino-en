Role overview

To allow family caregivers a period of rest, the UATRs offer AVS-age clients short stays and provide services in continuity with those provided at home.

Client care and support adapt to their physical and mental health.

The nurse works in interdisciplinarity from one of the 3 sites ( either: Mervelet, Petit-Saconnex or Villereuse Geneva 1207 or Twins, Thônex), respecting the mission and objectives of the institution and may be required to intervene occasionally also on the other two sites.

Main responsibilities

  • Your missions and responsibilities
  • Deliver comprehensive, preventive, curative, educational and palliative personalized nursing care to AVS-age clients independently and/or on medical delegation
  • Perform a situational referent role, on a daily basis, in order to adjust the care of the client according to his needs and acting in accordance with the principles of the professional code of ethics nurse
