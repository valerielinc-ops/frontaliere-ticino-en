Original description

Introduction For our Production Division , we are looking for a Quality Operations Technician (H/F) whose main mission will be to animate the steps relating to the compliance and adaptation of quality criteria within the sector, as well as to participate actively in the continuous improvement of the quality of our components. Responsibilities

  • Ensure compliance with quality criteria throughout the sector by liaising with the Experts.
  • Ensure the application of the control methodology and homogeneity of the reference.
  • Relay between the sector and the quality technician by providing information in case of incident or non-compliant products.
  • Ensure compliance with control methods and tools through post audits.
  • Participate in the validation of the aesthetic criteria of the parts produced.
  • Conduct weekly reviews and ensure a cross-sectional role of quality meetings.
  • Ensure that the level required for each controller is maintained by providing the necessary training and support.
  • Participate in the training, coaching and control of aesthetic criteria of all employees in the sector.
  • Actively contribute to continuous quality improvement. Profile
  • Diploma of ES Technician in Mechanical or Microtechnics
  • Confirmed experience in the field of quality, enriched with continuing training(s) certifying
  • Professional practice of 3 years minimum in quality control of watch products
  • Know-how in aesthetic control and mastery of basic quality tools
  • Good visual acuity and plan reading knowledge
  • Good control of computer tools
  • Good training, coaching and audit skills. Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits. Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
