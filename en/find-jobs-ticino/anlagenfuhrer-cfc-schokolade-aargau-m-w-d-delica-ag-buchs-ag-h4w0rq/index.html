Original description

Do you love technology and chocolate? With us you control large facilities and create pleasure that inspires. You work in a team with colleagues from many cultures. We celebrate successes together, are proud of our cult products and promote your further development in the Migros universe.

Mansioni

  • You set up production and filling plants yourself, operate them and ensure process stability.
  • You carry out planned production orders independently and control format changes efficiently.
  • You control and document quality and process parameters seamlessly.
  • You carry out cleaning cycles according to specifications and contribute to hygiene safety.
  • Recognize maintenance needs, fix disruptions (1st level) and actively bring ideas to the KVP/Shopfloor.

Requirements

  • 3 - 5 years of professional experience: in food production, ideally chocolate/cocoa, baked goods, snacks, drinks or dairy
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): As a plant manager, food technologist or comparable qualification (e.g. baker, brewer, miller)
  • German (very good knowledge): At least level B1
  • Readiness for 2- to 4-shift work (weekend if necessary) and corresponding mobility
  • High quality, safety and hygiene awareness
  • You work structured and clean.
  • Technical understanding, problem-solving skills in disruption and team and communication strength

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central place of work: Very good transport connections and free parking
  • Professional provision: Compared to the BVG, depending on age, 7% - 18% more is paid by Delica
  • Meals: Balanced and fine lunch menu/buffet in the staff restaurant for only CHF 8.-
  • Mobile subscriptions: Top prices on mobile subscriptions at Galaxus Mobile, Sunrise or Wingo – starting from CHF 10.- / month
  • Cumulus points: 2 times Cumulus points on food and 5 times on non-food items
  • Migros Bank: Migros grants higher interest rates on the private and investment account for its employees
  • Sports and fitness offers: Free use of the fitness studios integrated at the site in Buchs, Birsfelden and Meilen as well as CHF 200.- Refund on fitness annual subscriptions
  • Club School Migros: Up to 70% refund per course
  • Health insurance: Up to 25% discount at various insurance partner companies (CSS, Sanitas, AXA, Generali and many more)
