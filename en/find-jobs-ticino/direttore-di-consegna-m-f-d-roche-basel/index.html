Main responsibilities

  • Your Responsibilities Delivery Leadership & Coordination
  • Drive alignment, support decision-making and ensure the team delivers value effectively, on time, within budget and with the expected level of product quality.
  • Maintain the Delivery Plan as a self-updating single source of truth for product delivery, coordinating across cross-functional stakeholders to manage milestones, dependencies, clear execution paths and address blockers.
  • Proactively map cross-team dependencies, monitor technical/delivery risks and make impediments visible to resolve them early, leveraging AI-powered forecasting.
  • Oversee the release process, backlog prioritization, integration needs and end-to-end flow of value across the platform to enable predictable, sustainable and high-performing delivery.
  • Facilitate key ceremonies (PI Plannings, Demos, Inspect & Adapt, Refinements, Syncs and Retrospectives) ensuring the team maintains alignment and focus on clear priorities.
