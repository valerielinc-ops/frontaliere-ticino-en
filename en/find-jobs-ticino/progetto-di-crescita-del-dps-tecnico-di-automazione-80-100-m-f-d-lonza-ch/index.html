Relocation support: Available for eligible candidates and their families.

As an Automation Technician (m/f/d) at our sterile production site in Stein, Canton Aargau, you play a central role in ensuring the operational readiness, reliability and compliance of the HVAC and supply systems that support pharmaceutical production. In this position, you contribute to safe, efficient and compliant processes while ensuring high standards of quality and technical excellence.

Your tasks

  • Support for FATs and commissionings to ensure a smooth transition from aseptic fuel systems and automated systems
  • providing first-level support, maintenance and troubleshooting for assigned automation-related systems
  • Close cooperation with mechanics teams to solve technical problems
  • Implementation and documentation of maintenance, calibration and commissioning work according to safety, cGMP and regulatory requirements
  • Monitoring and controlling automated production and plant processes to ensure stable and reliable operation
  • Contribute to sustainable programs and tools that improve asset usability, system reliability and long-term performance
  • Preparation of systems for operational readiness, including planning operations, ensuring safe system operation, management of spare parts and tools, and organisation of necessary training
  • Training and induction of new employees in accordance with GxP and company guidelines

Your requirement profile

  • Completed technical training as Automation Technician EFZ or equivalent in automation engineering
  • Professional experience in plant maintenance, ideally in the pharmaceutical industry or another highly regulated environment
  • Knowledge of Siemens Step7, TIA Portal or Emerson DeltaV is an advantage
  • Solid MS Office skills (in particular Excel); SAP‐PM and CMX are a plus
  • Precise, reliable and structured workflow with strong technical understanding and strong problem-solving skills
  • Willingness to work flexible hours, shifts and participation in the piketting service
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • English skills are desired
