The Position

Location: Location Region Switzerland / Remote

Type of employment: [full-time/permanent]

We believe there is an urgent need to bring medical solutions directly to where they are most needed. Our ultimate goal (“North Star”) is to achieve better treatment outcomes for more patients in less time. To advance this vision, we are looking for a Patient Journey Partner (PJP). In this modern, hybrid role, you do not act as a classic salesman, but as a strategic partner, systems thinker and driving force to create real added value in the health ecosystem together with our customers.

As a patient journey partner, you are the primary contact for our stakeholders (specialist doctors, hospitals, health networks) in defined therapeutic areas. They think beyond silos and boundaries to holistically improve the patient journey from diagnosis to treatment.

Their tasks

  • Strategic Partnership: You are the central, trusted point of contact for stakeholders in the Swiss healthcare sector and represent our innovative products, tests and solutions.
  • Co-Creation & Systems Thinking: You understand the needs of all actors and together with HCPs (Health Care Professionals) develop tailor-made solutions to sustainably improve patient care.
  • Customer & Account Management: You are responsible for account planning (including budgeting, investments and projects), prioritize target customers and control interactive, omnichannel-based customer engagements (e.g. virtual meetings, eCamps).
  • Agile collaboration: You proactively bring external market insights to the company and work closely with interdisciplinary teams (medical affairs, digital experts, ecosystem managers) to implement agile goals (90-day priorities).
  • Product life cycle: They accompany our solutions through their life cycle, share medical-scientific findings in a balanced and well-founded manner and initiate interdisciplinary scientific exchange.

Your profile

  • Qualification: Completed university studies (natural sciences, medicine or economics), ideally complemented by a Master in Life Sciences / MBA. A valid SHQA certification is a requirement.
  • Experience: Several years of professional experience in customer engagement, key account management or medical affairs with proven success in building strategic partnerships.
  • Technical expertise: Deep understanding of medical strategies, the patient journey and current treatment options within the field of nephrology / rheumatology are a plus
  • Mindset: You are a system thinker, enjoy working in agile, complex network structures and are characterized by a high degree of initiative and strong digital competence.
  • Communication: Excellent presentation and communication skills. Fluent German and French skills as well as very good English skills for company-wide cooperation.
