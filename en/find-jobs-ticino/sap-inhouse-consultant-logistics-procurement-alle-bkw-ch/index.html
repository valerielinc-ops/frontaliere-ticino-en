Original description

Your field of activity

  • Together with the departments, you will analyze business processes and develop sustainable SAP solutions for logistics and procurement
  • You advise stakeholders at different levels and provide well-founded decision-making principles on processes, technologies, roadmaps and licenses
  • You implement changes, projects and further developments on your own responsibility or in a sub-project management and accompany their successful introduction
  • You ensure the stable operation of SAP solutions and are responsible for monitoring, incident and problem management as well as compliance requirements
  • Collaboration with process managers, cluster leads (professional topic responsibility) and other stakeholders is actively designed and ensures transparent communication
  • With your contribution, you continuously develop the SAP roadmap in the area of Procurement & Logistics and support the Cluster Lead in an international environment

You bring that with you

  • You have at least five years of experience in SAP consulting with a focus on logistics / warehouse and / or procurement
  • In-depth knowledge of SAP S/4HANA and a good understanding of operational processes enable you to develop practical solutions
  • A university degree or a comparable education in the field of business informatics, computer science or a related field forms your professional basis
  • You master German and English at a very good level and communicate appropriately with different stakeholders.
  • A solution-oriented way of working, perseverance and the ability to combine different interests set you apart
  • Experience in the energy industry, with SAP IS-U or additional language skills in French or Italian are a plus

We do not want requests from personnel service providers.

We look forward to your online application.

About us

BKW offers forward-looking overall solutions in the areas of energy, buildings and infrastructure – internationally and in partnership. With around 12,000 employees, we build modern networks, generate renewable energy and realize energy-efficient buildings. For this we are looking for people with passion, who together with us make a contribution to the society and environment of tomorrow. Become part of the BKW network and create living spaces with us!
