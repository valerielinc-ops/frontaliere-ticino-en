Original description

Delivery logistics manager Hub coop.ch

Coop Genossenschaft — Bussigny, Vaud, Svizzera

Mission

  • You lead the transport team in French-speaking Switzerland, composed of about 150 employees spread over several sites, and ensure optimal planning, development and training of staff
  • You develop and implement the delivery strategy for the Suisse Romande and plan different scenarios for the delivery areas
  • You manage the day-to-day activities and guarantee, in collaboration with the relevant stakeholders, full and timely delivery of customer orders
  • You are responsible for the costs, budget and management of the fleet while ensuring compliance with safety, quality and legal requirements.
  • You continuously optimize internal processes, follow market developments, develop new and more efficient delivery strategies, and participate in or manage projects related to delivery logistics

Qualifications

  • You have training in logistics and transport, supplemented by a higher education
  • You have at least 5 years of professional experience, including practical experience in a management function, and proven leadership skills in a modern logistics company
  • You are comfortable in exchanges with different people and you stand out for a strong customer orientation
  • You are a communicative, flexible, assertive personality with a global vision, with an excellent sense of organization and a real interest in optimizing processes
  • You master MS Office as well as various logistics tools such as SAP, are native French and have a minimum level of German B2. You are also willing to travel in the coop.ch delivery area in Suisse Romande (pool vehicle available at the workplace)
  • Discounts and discounts: 5 times more superpoints in supermarkets, up to 20% discount in all formats of the Coop group, mobile and prepaid subscriptions at a reduced price at Coop Mobile, discount on the Reka Card, discounted admissions to cultural events, discounted admissions to leisure activities
  • Conditions of employment: pension fund: 2/3 of contributions paid by Coop, attractive conditions of employment and guaranteed equal pay, collective labour agreement (CCT), pension savings plan, flexible and attractive RA scheme for early retirement from the age of 58
  • Working time: 6 weeks of vacation
  • Continuing training: Over 600 in-house training courses, support for external training and development courses, talent management with many and various development opportunities
  • Sustainable development: A commitment to man and animals and nature with more than 400 acts
  • Communication: internal app dedicated to staff
  • Health: Update-Fitness: Fr. 200.– discount on annual subscription, free participation in various company races, sports activities offered by the company, sports activities: volleyball tournaments and hikes
