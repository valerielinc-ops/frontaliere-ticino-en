Role overview

In the company development team you will assume the following responsibilities:

  • management of the main strategic issues of SSR and progress of transversal transformation projects;
  • analysis of complex challenges and development of future scenarios, operational recommendations and proposals for concrete solutions;
  • participation in the development of the strategic process of SSR and translation of strategic objectives in initiatives, measures and guidelines;
  • monitoring of market, technology and industry trends and the definition of operational proposals based on the Management Committee and the various sectors;
  • mediation between the different interests, promotion of intersectoral collaboration and definition of common decision bases;
  • accompaniment of the projects of change from the conception phase to the realization and guarantee of the respect of the commitments, even without a role of formal responsibility.

Requirements

  • University diploma or higher vocational training and experience in the field of corporate development, strategic development or organizational development.
  • Systemic and interdisciplinary approach and interest in dealing with complex and demanding issues.
  • Experience in accompanying the processes of change and solid knowledge of the methods of managing change.
  • Exciting communication and facilitation skills as well as experience in transversal management.
  • Ability to quickly grasp complex situations and present them adequately to the target audience.
  • Great mastery of German and French; the knowledge of Italian or Romance is a preferential title.

What we offer

  • A key role in the meeting point between strategy, transformation and business management.
  • Wide freedom of action within a motivated and newly established team.
  • Stimulating and varied projects, of great strategic importance for SSR.
