Role overview

Introduction

For our Production Division, we are looking for a Project Manager (H/F) whose main task will be to pilot the industrialisation of the assembly of new products, developing and deploying the necessary technical and industrial means, in close collaboration with the R&D Division.

You will also coordinate product changes and accompany the commissioning of new equipment.
