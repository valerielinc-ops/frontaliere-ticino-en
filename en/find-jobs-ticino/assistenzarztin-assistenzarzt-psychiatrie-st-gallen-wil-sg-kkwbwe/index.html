Main responsibilities

  • As a member of our interdisciplinary treatment team, you look after patients in the inpatient setting with dependency diseases and comorbid disorders and accompany them as part of their psychiatric treatment.
  • After in-depth training, you will take on case-leading tasks as well as individual psychotherapeutic work and group therapies with patients in close cooperation with our interdisciplinary team.
  • Your varied day-to-day work offers you insight into the entire spectrum of psychiatric diseases and thus enables you to gain diverse professional experience.
  • As an assistant psychologist, you coordinate the treatments, plan the appropriate therapies and carry out psychological evaluations.
