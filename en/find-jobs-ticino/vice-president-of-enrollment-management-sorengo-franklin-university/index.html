Role overview

Franklin Switzerland, founded in 1969, is one of the first institutions to bring liberal arts education to Europe and to be recognized by both U.S. and Swiss accreditation agencies.

A leader in international education, Franklin provides an experiential and multinational learning environment through small classes, strong student and faculty relationships, a residential campus, and hands-on Academic Travel experiences.

With roughly 300 students from more than 55 countries, set in the safe and striking landscape of Lugano, Franklin is distinctive: an intimate residential community, a uniquely dual U.S.–Swiss accreditation, and an Academic Travel program that turns the world into a classroom.

Main responsibilities

  • KEY RESPONSIBILITIES Enrollment Strategy & Planning
  • Support the development, implementation, and evaluation of a comprehensive enrollment management plan aligned with Franklin’s mission, academic priorities, financial realities, and strategic goals.
  • Translate enrollment goals into practical recruitment plans, territory strategies, communication flows, event calendars, staff priorities, funnel targets, and measurable outcomes.

Application process

  • Franklin is looking for a VPEM who is genuinely fluent across the enrollment cycle and who can hold three priorities in balance: Recruitment and conversion — building a disciplined, warm, and responsive admissions operation that supports students and families from first inquiry through application, admission, deposit, visa, arrival, and handoff to the student experience.
  • Data and systems — using Slate, funnel reports, dashboards, segmentation, and timely analysis to identify opportunities, address weaknesses, improve conversion, and support evidence-based enrollment decisions.
  • Collaboration and execution — working across departments to ensure that Franklin’s distinctive academic, residential, and international identity is translated into clear recruitment messages, effective campaigns, strong events, and a consistent prospective-student experience.
  • The ideal candidate understands that enrollment management is both strategic and practical.
  • Students and families make decisions over time, through many interactions, questions, doubts, comparisons, and moments of reassurance.
  • Franklin must be visible, compelling, responsive, credible, and operationally excellent throughout that journey.
  • Monitor enrollment trends, application behavior, deposit patterns, yield indicators, market conditions, and competitor activity, and recommend timely adjustments.
  • Work closely with senior leadership to support short- and long-term enrollment planning, including undergraduate, graduate, transfer, visiting, study abroad, and partnership enrollment.

Contacts

  • Work closely with Marketing and Communications to ensure that recruitment campaigns, landing pages, email flows, social content, events, and digital outreach are aligned with enrollment priorities and Franklin’s distinctive identity.
