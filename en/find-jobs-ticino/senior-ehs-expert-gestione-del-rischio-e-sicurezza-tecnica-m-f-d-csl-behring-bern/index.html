Senior EHS Expert – Risk Management & Technical Safety (m/f/d) Their mission

As a Senior EHS Expert – Risk Management & Technical Safety, you take on the professional responsibility for risk management and technical safety at the Bern site.

They act as Subject Matter Expert (SME) for Risk Management, Technical Safety and Incident Investigation and accompany technical projects throughout the entire life cycle.
