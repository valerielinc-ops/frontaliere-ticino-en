Main responsibilities

  • As a member of our interdisciplinary treatment team, you look after patients in the ambulatory.
  • After a thorough familiarization, you will take over the psychotherapeutic clarification, treatment and advice of patients and their relatives in individual and group setting.
  • Your varied day-to-day work covers the entire spectrum of psychiatric conditions, in which you conduct individual and multi-person discussions.
  • You coordinate treatments independently, plan therapies and carry out test psychological assessments.
  • You actively participate in rapports, case discussions and training courses and are in close contact with other practitioners.
  • With the support of our secretariat, you will handle case-related documentation, write reports and record services in the clinic information system. Requirements
  • You have successfully completed your psychology degree and have already gained experience in outpatient or inpatient adult psychiatry.
  • Your psychotherapy training is recognized in Switzerland and either completed or advanced.
  • You already have the Psychotherapy Professional Permit of the Canton of St.Gallen or are ready to acquire it before starting your job.
  • Interest in outpatient, interdisciplinary treatment distinguishes you, always taking into account the individual needs of patients and their relatives.

Key requirements

  • You have successfully completed your psychology degree and have already gained experience in outpatient or inpatient adult psychiatry.
  • Your psychotherapy training is recognized in Switzerland and either completed or advanced.
  • You already have the Psychotherapy Professional Permit of the Canton of St.Gallen or are ready to acquire it before starting your job.
  • Interest in outpatient, interdisciplinary treatment distinguishes you, always taking into account the individual needs of patients and their relatives.
  • You are motivated to actively contribute your expertise, develop your skills and help shape the future of psychiatry in St.Gallen.
  • You bring your own initiative, sense of responsibility and very good knowledge of German (compulsory C1).
  • With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations.
