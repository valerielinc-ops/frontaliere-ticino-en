Role overview

Mission

The EPFL Networked Systems Abstractions Lab (LASeR) developments programming languages and formal reasoning tools for networked systems.

Networks are the invisible infrastructure that connects the modern world, yet programming and reasoning about them are surprisingly difficult.

  • Mission

  • Main duties and responsibilities
  • Conducting research on network abstractions, spanning language design, implementation, and verification.

Main responsibilities

  • Main duties and responsibilities
  • Conducting research on network abstractions, spanning language design, implementation, and verification.
  • The precise focus will be discussed with the successful candidate depending on their background, expertise and affinities. Possible directions include:
  • Design and semantics of network programming languages in the NetKAT family, including quantitative and probabilistic extensions, and scalable verification via symbolic automata and related decision procedures
  • Routing algebras: composable, algebraic representations of distributed routing protocols, as a main basis for the compilation and verification of protocols such as BGP
  • Formal abstractions for packet scheduling: expressive specifications of schedule policies, and compilation to programmable hardware with provable guarantees
  • AI-based cybersecurity: applying learning and AI-assisted techniques to network security, e.g., automata learning from security logs, validation of protocol models, and verified defensive controllers
  • Build a strong network in the programming languages, verification, and networking communities, including collaboration with the labs academic and industrial partners
  • Contribute to the design, implementation, and open-source release of the labs tools and artifacts
  • Participate in the supervision of PhD and master students Profile
