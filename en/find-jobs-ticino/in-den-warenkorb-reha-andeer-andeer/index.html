Original description

Reha Andeer is looking for a dedicated personality for the position: nursing assistant.

Reha Andeer is a private rehabilitation clinic in Andeer (canton of Graubünden) and offers inpatient and outpatient treatments in a family environment in the middle of the Shams mountains.

The individual rehabilitation according to Mass Die reha andeer ag is a modern musculoskeletal rehabilitation clinic in the heart of Graubünden. After knee, hip, shoulder and spine operations, we accompany our patients on their way back to everyday life. The focus is on personal care, a family working atmosphere and a high quality of treatment. Nursing assistant / nursing assistant SRK (40-60%) In order to strengthen our care team, we are looking for a committed personality as a nursing assistant / nursing assistant SRK 40-60% of your tasks.

  • Recognize changes and forward them to the responsible nurse

  • Assistance in organizational and domestic work on the station
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with therapy, kitchen, service and administration for a smooth clinical everyday life. Your profile
