Role overview

Psychologist/assistant course (100%) at the Hospital Malcantonese OSCAM (Fondazione Giuseppe Rossi), Castelrotto (Malcantone, Canton Ticino).

The OSCAM is an acute care hospital based in Castelrotto serving the Malcantone region.

The foundation Giuseppe Rossi manages departments of internal medicine, surgery, psychiatry, obstetricia-ginecology and palliative care, as well as an emergency and outpatient services for the population of the Lugano-Malcantone district.
