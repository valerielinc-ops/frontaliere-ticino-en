Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society. About the role For the start of training 01. September 2027 we are looking for a Apprentice industrial mechanic (m/f/d). Your tasks: In the apprenticeship industrial mechanic (m/f/d), we prepare our trainees for a variety of activities in the company. The content includes, inter alia:

  • Processing and manufacturing of components of different materials
  • Assembly and testing of parts and finished products

The theoretical part of the training takes place at the Balthasar-Neumann School in Bruchsal. Your profile: We expect our future apprentices to have a good secondary or secondary school degree, especially in scientific or technical subjects.
