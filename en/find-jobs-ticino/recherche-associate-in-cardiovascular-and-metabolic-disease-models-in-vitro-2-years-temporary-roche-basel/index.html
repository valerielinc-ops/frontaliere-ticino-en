Company and context

  • In Roche’s Pharmaceutical Research and Early Development organization (pRED), we make transformative medicines for patients in order to tackle some of the world’s toughest unmet healthcare needs.
  • At pRED, we are united by our mission to transform science into medicines.

  • pRED Cardiovascular & Metabolism, Immunology (CMI), located in Basel, Switzerland, focuses on the discovery and early development of innovative treatments for specific diseases that affect the lives of millions.
  • In the cardiovascular and metabolism area, we are developing cutting-edge therapies to treat metabolic diseases.
  • We are seeking an experienced Research Associate with a strong industry background to join our Cardiovascular and Metabolic Diseases (CVM) in vitro department.
  • In this role, you will design and execute in-vitro experiments using complex cellular models to evaluate the efficacy and safety of novel therapeutic candidates for cardiovascular and metabolic diseases. The opportunity
  • Cultivation of primary cell lines from human and animal tissues, optimizing protocols for primary cell isolation, differentiation and maintenance.
  • Execution of functional cell based and enzymatic assays to study metabolic pathways and other relevant metabolic pathways related readouts e.g. lipolysis, glucose uptake, cAMP signaling
  • Experience in advanced cellular and biochemical techniques, such as cell painting, and metabolic flux analysis using Seahorse to evaluate cellular bioenergetics.
