Job Description Summary Director, Strategic Projects Lead #LI-Hybrid Location: Basel, Switzerland

This role is based in Basel, Switzerland. Novartis is unable to offer relocation support for this role. Please only apply if this location is accessible for you.

Transforming how strategy is executed can be just as powerful as the strategy itself. As Director, Strategic Projects Lead, you will be entrusted with driving some of the most critical transformation and innovation initiatives across Advanced Quantitative Sciences (AQS). Partnering with senior leaders across Development, you will translate strategic ambitions into enterprise-wide execution, ensuring transformational priorities are delivered, adopted, and embedded to create lasting impact. This role offers a unique opportunity to influence the future operating model of AQS, accelerate innovation at scale, and shape how a global organization delivers value in an increasingly complex and evolving environment. If you are energized by leading large-scale change, navigating complexity, and turning vision into measurable outcomes, we would love to hear from you.

Job Description

Key responsibilities

  • Lead end-to-end delivery of strategic transformation initiatives, ensuring measurable business impact across AQS.
  • Develop and maintain integrated transformation roadmaps aligned with strategic priorities and organizational goals.
  • Translate strategic objectives into executable plans with clear priorities, milestones, and dependencies.
  • Establish robust governance frameworks to monitor progress, risks, outcomes, and overall program performance.
  • Drive alignment and execution across cross-functional teams, functions, and Development stakeholders.
  • Influence and lead matrix teams to successfully deliver complex initiatives without direct authority.
  • Implement scalable operating models, processes, and ways of working that enhance organizational performance.
  • Champion change adoption through effective communication, capability building, and stakeholder engagement.
  • Provide data-driven insights and recommendations to optimize execution, prioritization, and transformation outcomes.
  • Act as a trusted advisor to senior leaders on transformation strategy, delivery, and execution excellence.

Essential Requirements

  • Degree in Life Sciences, Engineering, Business, or a related discipline.
  • Significant experience in pharmaceutical research and development, life sciences consulting, or transformation environments.
  • Proven success leading large-scale, cross-functional transformation or strategic initiatives.
  • Strong understanding of drug development and the pharmaceutical research and development operating environment.
  • Demonstrated ability to translate complex strategies into executable plans and measurable outcomes.
  • Expertise in program and project management, including planning, governance, execution, and delivery.
  • Proven ability to influence senior stakeholders and lead effectively within matrix organizations.
  • Excellent analytical, communication, and data-driven decision-making capabilities.
