Original description

The public prosecutor's office with around 250 employees consists of four regional investigative offices, a cantonal investigative office responsible for the entire cantonal territory, the staff services and the youth prosecutor's office. The Public Prosecutor's Office is an independent investigative and prosecuting authority of the Canton of St.Gallen, which is responsible for the equal enforcement of the state criminal claim and conducts criminal proceedings in the event of crimes, offences and transgressions. What can AI do for a public prosecutor? Where can digital solutions really make our work better? And how do we use new technologies when data protection, information security and confidentiality are particularly important? This is where you come in.

What to expect

  • Discover opportunities: You recognize digitalization potential, question existing processes and contribute ideas.
  • Put technology to the test: You will be faithful to new technologies and AI, test specific products and find out what creates real added value for us.
  • Bringing ideas to their goal: You lead digitalization, innovation and organizational projects – decisive and clever from the first idea to a pilot to introduction.
  • Networking: You connect specialist areas, IT, data protection and information security and develop viable, legally secure solutions together with all parties involved.
  • Organize knowledge smarter: You shape and prioritize our knowledge management and develop digital knowledge and collaboration solutions.

What distinguishes you

  • A university degree or a comparable qualification – for example in business informatics, digitalization, business administration, innovation, organizational development or information and knowledge management.
  • You have already implemented digitization, transformation, IT or organizational projects and bring experience in managing projects.
  • You understand the interaction of people, processes, organization and technology.
  • AI and new technologies arouse your curiosity – at the same time you can critically assess what actually works in practice.
  • Data protection, information security and the careful handling of sensitive information are part of this right from the start.
  • You work independently, structured and pragmatic and enjoy developing together with other solutions.
  • You bring your own ideas, can take other perspectives seriously and want to actually implement things. Experience in administration, justice or law enforcement? Great, but not a must. It is more important to us that you are curious and can quickly work your way into a new world.

What we offer

  • You get the opportunity to contribute your own ideas and to actively shape the digital development of our public prosecutor’s office.
  • Proximity to decisions: You work directly with the overall management and at the same time with departments, staff offices and external partners.
  • Technology with a real context: We are not concerned with digitalization as an end in itself, but with solutions that actually have to work in a demanding environment.
  • Cooperation at eye level: We are not looking for lone fighters. Good ideas come together with us.
  • Contemporary working: flexible working hours and models, home office, discounted East Wind company subscription
