Main responsibilities

  • Main Responsibilities
  • The Primary responsibility of the Assistant Relationship Manager (ARM) is the day-to-day management of the Bank’s relationships with Wealth Management clients.
  • The ARM will utilise their in-depth knowledge of the Bank’s products and services to understand the needs and requirements of the client.
  • The ARM should be the primary point of contact for the client ensuring a joined-up approach to client coverage with the RM.
  • The ARM must have the required skill set to be a delegate on behalf of the RM when they are unavailable.
  • The role requires a high level of client service and to act accordingly in the clients’ best interests, in a regulatory compliant and commercial manner. Role Duties
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with clients whilst handling day-to-day correspondence with them and their representatives
  • Multi-task and use effective time management skills to prioritise short terms tasks while simultaneously managing longer-term projects.
  • With the ability to work in a highly pressurised environment, whilst meeting both client and internal deadlines
  • Handling data requests from clients and stakeholders within the firm
