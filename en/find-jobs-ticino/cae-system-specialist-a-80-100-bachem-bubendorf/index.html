Role overview

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals.

To strengthen our team in Engineering Services, we are looking for a dedicated CAD / CAE system specialist (f/m/d), 80 – 100%.

In this function, you are responsible for ensuring smooth operation and the further development of our AVEVA authoring tools as a key user and administrator, as well as for the creation of plans within the framework of projects for conversion or refurbishment.
