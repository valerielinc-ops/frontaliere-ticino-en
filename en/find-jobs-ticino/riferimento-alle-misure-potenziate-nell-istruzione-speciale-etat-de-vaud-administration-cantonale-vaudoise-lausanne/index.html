Role overview

You are sensitive to the challenges of specialized pedagogy and want to contribute to the system of allocation of special education services for the Jura – Nord Vaudois region, join us to complete our team.

To exercise responsibility and regional coordination for the provision of special education and integration assistance, in collaboration with the head of the office and to ensure the development of the system in place, by including it in the lines defined by the Law of 1 September 2015 on specialised pedagogy (LPS), its implementing regulations and the 360° Concept.

Advise and inform school directors, teachers, parents and other partners on supervision, and notify school guidance decisions of students under strengthened or auxiliary measures of specialized pedagogy.
