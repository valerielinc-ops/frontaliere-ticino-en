Original description

When it comes to challenging questions about the performance basics, our team comes to work – and soon maybe you too. In this role, you are the central point of contact for complex performance issues. You arrange connections, find viable solutions and share your knowledge with others. At the same time, you have the opportunity to actively develop your department and make a difference with your ideas. You can expect a team with different backgrounds, a lot of expertise and open cooperation. So that there is enough space outside of work for what is important to you, we offer you flexible working conditions and an attractive employment package. Sounds exciting? Our team is looking forward to you!

This is what your everyday life looks like

  • At the heart of it: You are the first point of contact for answering complex performance questions in your assigned field (pharmaceuticals or outpatient services).
  • You are not only a professional contact person, but also a source of ideas! It gives you pleasure to accompany exciting projects and to advance the interests of our department with constructive solutions in specialist meetings and working groups.
  • The exchange with various stakeholders and other specialist departments, such as performance testing, service purchasing or performance control, enrich your everyday life.
  • Behind the scenes you also hold the strings in your hand: you care for and update our professional databases with the utmost care.
  • You carry out the quality assurance together with teammates with a lot of commitment.

You bring that with you

  • First things first: Your several years of professional experience in the area of health insurance serves as the foundation for this exciting function.
  • Do you have a (para)medical education or a basic commercial education with suitable further education? There will be open.
  • Do you bring further training in the social security sector or perhaps even explicitly in the health insurance sector or are currently in the corresponding training? That would definitely be a plus!
  • You express yourself well in German (verbal and written). Maybe you even know French or Italian?
  • For you, teamwork means not only cooperation, but real cooperation. At the same time, you appreciate it if you can act independently in your position.
  • Work Flexible: Adapt your work to your lifestyle annually with part-time options and other flexible models like home office and more.
  • Choose your holiday model: 5 or 6 weeks holiday? Your decision. Accordingly, you work 41 or 42 hours a week.
  • Stay fit: Ergonomic workplaces, promotion of mental well-being and many other offers around your health.
  • Engage Socially: Does Life Mean Good to You? Then let others share your happiness and expand your horizons alone or in a team.
  • Enjoy your family life: Make time for your offspring: 3 weeks for fathers, for mothers up to 20 weeks. There are also generous child and education allowances.
  • Celebrate with us: Focused work and celebrate. We love this combination and maintain it regularly.
