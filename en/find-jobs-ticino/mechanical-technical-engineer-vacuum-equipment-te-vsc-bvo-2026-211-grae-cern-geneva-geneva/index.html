Role overview

Join CERN's Vacuum, Surfaces and Coatings (VSC) Group and contribute to the upgrade of critical beam vacuum systems that are essential for the performance and reliability of the Large Hadron Collider (LHC).

Working alongside experienced engineers, technicians and physicists, you will support the installation, testing and commissioning of key equipment that will help prepare the LHC for future scientific discoveries.

No prior experience in particle accelerators is required.

Main responsibilities

  • Your responsibilities
  • Perform vacuum acceptance testing of baked components intended for installation in the HL-LHC collimator upgrade.
  • Test and validate vacuum equipment before installation, perform residual gas analysis (RGA) ensuring it meets performance and quality requirements.
  • Carry out leak tests, bake-out system installation and non-evaporable-getter (NEG) activation on vacuum components and assemblies.
  • Assemble and prepare mechanical and vacuum systems for installation and operation.
  • Support the installation, testing and commissioning of equipment for the LHC upgrade programme.
  • Prepare technical documentation, test reports and intervention records.
  • Coordinate with suppliers and support the procurement and delivery of technical components.
  • Participate in maintenance, upgrade and repair activities on equipment installed in the LHC tunnel.

Key requirements

  • Experience with helium leak detection methods and equipment.
  • Experience with on-site vacuum system work, including mechanical installation and in-situ testing.
  • Basic experience with outgassing phenomena in vacuum systems and with quadruple mass spectrometers (RGA) for residual gas analysis in UHV environments.
  • Practical familiarity with techniques for achieving and maintaining UHV conditions. Skills
  • Understanding of ultra-high vacuum (UHV) principles and fundamentals.
  • The ability to analyse data from ultra-high vacuum (UHV) gauges.
  • Basic knowledge on how to assemble and install UHV-compatible components and systems.
  • Knowledge of helium leak detector use and the ability to perform leak testing and interpret results.
  • The ability to prepare, update, and manage technical documentation.
  • Spoken and written English or French, with a commitment to learn the basics of the other language. Eligibility criteria:
  • You are a national of a CERN Member or Associate Member State .
