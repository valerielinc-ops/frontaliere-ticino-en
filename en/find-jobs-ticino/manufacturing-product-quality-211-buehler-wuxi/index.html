Original description

Aufgaben

  • Lead supplier quality management system audit, and new supplier on boarding process from sampling to ramping up
  • Have experience in manufacturing process and application of electronic and electric component
  • Manage supplier non-conformity cases to minimize the disturbance to internal manufacturing workshops and external customers
  • Drive supplier to conduct root cause analysis (like 8D principle) to take systematic improvement activities.
  • Drive Supplier quality performance improvement with clear KPIs in place, and analyze supplier quality indicators to ensure the efficiency of improvement actions
  • Plan and execute supplier development projects together with category managers, to build world class supplier base with requested supply capacity and quality performance
  • Work with category manager on supplier relationship management, to promote supplier partnership with Buhler
  • Be familiar with the process of PCB/PCBA, electric & electronic components, and supplier OEM etc.
  • Have a good understanding of the properties of various materials, mechanical, electrical principles.

Anforderungen

  • Bachelor or above degree in Mechanical Engineering, or ‌Electrical Engineering‌.
  • Minimum 5 years of work experience in multinational companies
  • Minimum 3 years of supplier quality management experience
  • At least 3 years working experience in mechanical field
  • Good English in written while Spoken is preferred.
  • Familiar with Microsoft Office and data analysis.
  • Solid quality management knowledge, especially 8D improvement skills.
  • Good skills in communication, coordination and cooperation
  • Convincing and influencing
  • Shaping and driving changes
  • Ability to take decisions, sense of urgency
  • Resilience and self-motivation
  • Process-oriented
