Tasks

Are you looking for a meaningful part-time job with scheduled working hours and do you like working with people? Then you are exactly right with us! Ideal also as a part-time job for students!

As an employee: in the residential group service, together with our team you ensure a pleasant and warm atmosphere in the dining room of our nursing home group. Your tasks include:

  • Warm and attentive care of our residents during meals
  • Detecting and preparing the dining room
  • Preparation and serving of food and drink
  • Cleaning work
  • Ensuring a well-groomed, clean and welcoming appearance

You work 2 to 3 evenings a week from 5 pm to 7:30 pm. In addition, you are ready to take about 1 to 2 weekends per month.

Requirements

  • Experience in gastronomy or care of advantage
  • Clean and correct working and appearance
  • Understanding and empathy for older people
