Role overview

The group “Cards & Payments” manages central applications in the field of digital payment solutions such as TWINT, card transactions and instant payment. Together with our suppliers and business, we ensure the further development and smooth operation of these applications.
To expand our team, we are looking for a reliable personality as Application Manager Cards & Payments (f/m/d) for our location in St. Gallen with the opportunity to work in the home office.

Tasks

  • Collaboration in strategic projects around payment transactions and the digital customer experience
  • Ensuring the stable operation and further development of our applications for payment transactions
  • Close cooperation with business, IT and software suppliers
  • Quality assurance of software releases through testing and the expansion of test automation
  • Analysis and resolution of incidents in 3rd-level support around payment methods and transactions

Requirements

  • Successfully completed study of computer science, business informatics or an equivalent education/qualification
  • Experience in the operation and further development of business-critical systems
  • Solution orientation for complex problems across various IT systems
  • Experience in payment transactions and/or card management is advantageous
  • Collaboration in strategic projects around payment transactions and the digital customer experience
