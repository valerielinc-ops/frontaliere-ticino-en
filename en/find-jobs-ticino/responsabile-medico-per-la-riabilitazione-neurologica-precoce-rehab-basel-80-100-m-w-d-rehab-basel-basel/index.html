About us Anything but everyday

At Station 1, early rehabilitation and wakeful coma, we treat patients after accidental and/or disease-related serious brain injuries with an impact on a wide range of areas such as breathing, communication, mobility and cognition.

The ward takes over patients from the Intermediate Care Unit (IMCU) or directly from the acute care hospital.
