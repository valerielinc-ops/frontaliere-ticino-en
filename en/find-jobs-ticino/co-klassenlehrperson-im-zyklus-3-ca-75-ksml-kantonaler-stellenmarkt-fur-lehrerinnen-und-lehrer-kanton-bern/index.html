Portrait At the Special Elementary School in Bern (BVS), children and young people between the ages of 4 and 18 with a cognitive impairment, on the autism spectrum or with another impairment are taught and supported according to remedial education principles. Currently around 90 children and young people are taught in 15 classes. 12 classes are held in the school building at Statthalterstrasse 80, two classes at Tscharnerstrasse 10 (Eigerplatz) and one at the Burgunder external location.
