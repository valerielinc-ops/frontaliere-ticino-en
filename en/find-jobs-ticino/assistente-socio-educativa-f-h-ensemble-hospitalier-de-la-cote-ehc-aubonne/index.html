Role overview

The park of Beausobre is nestled in the park of the same name in the city of Morges, and offers a personalized reception in the Temporary Reception Centre, and an EMS for 82 residents.

Designed to foster intergenerational ties, the EMS is surrounded by a sheltered housing residence, a Child Living Centre and two colleges.

The park of Beausobre also has a restaurant and cafeteria, open every 7 days/7.
