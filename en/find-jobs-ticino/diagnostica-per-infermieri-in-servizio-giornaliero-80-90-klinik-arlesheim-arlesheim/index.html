Role overview

Tasks

  • Professional assistance in endoscopic examinations: You support our medical team in diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopies (e.g. gastroscopy, colonoscopy) and take over the comprehensive preparation of the patients.
  • Care and supervision: After endoscopic procedures, you accompany our inpatient and outpatient patients: inside, monitor the sedation and take care of the follow-up care.
  • Ensuring equipment and hygiene quality: They ensure that our endoscopic devices comply with internal hygienic requirements at all times.
  • Organization and day planning: You support the efficient planning and organization of the daily process and thus contribute to smooth processes.
  • Quality and concept development: You implement innovative care and support concepts and contribute to the continuous improvement of care quality.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: You work closely with our medical team and the secretariat, as well as other employees of the clinic.
  • Partial cooperation in the diagnostics of pneumology and cardiology: You support the departments of pneumology and cardiology in diagnostic examinations, take over the professional preparation, monitoring and follow-up of the patients.

Requirements

  • Diploma as a nurse HF or equivalent with SRK recognition
  • Experience in endoscopy, gastroenterology and/or functional diagnostics
  • Efficient and clean working methods and enjoyment of contact with people
  • Motivation and willingness to actively participate in new processes
  • Joy and interest to learn and pass on what has been learned
  • Flexibility, reliability, resilience and independence
  • solution-oriented and communicative personality

We offer

A varied and responsible activity with a lot of room for manoeuvre and a well-founded introduction. You can expect a good working atmosphere and attractive working conditions in a value-oriented hospital, a good public transport connection and our TNW job ticket, free use of pick-e-bike and regular working hours. For further information, Simone Heller, Head of Medical Diagnostics, is at your disposal: Tel. +41 (0)61 705 70 33. Only direct applications will be considered. We look forward to meeting you.
