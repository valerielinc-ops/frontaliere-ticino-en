Application process

  • Are you passionate about transforming emerging polymer technologies into industrial applications?
  • In this globally focused expert role, you will combine chemical expertise with industrial practice.
  • You will work closely with engineering, production, and project teams worldwide and play a pivotal role in advancing technologies, manufacturing processes, and technical standards across the Endress+Hauser Group.
  • This role is ideal for someone who enjoys combining scientific expertise with practical industrial application.
  • You are comfortable moving between laboratory work, production environments and strategic technology discussions.
  • Which tasks will you perform? You will:
  • Define material and chemistry-related roadmap for polymers, adhesives, potting compounds and cleaning processes. Evaluate new polymer technologies
  • Ensure compliance with relevant industry standards and regulatory requirements (drinking water, hygiene, toxicology,..) and act as advisor for the Health and Safety responsible concerning chemical safety.

Company and context

  • We are looking for a Principal Expert Manufacturing Technology (f/m/d) to join our Group and play a key role in advancing manufacturing technologies across our organization.
  • As our expert for industrial manufacturing technology, you will play a key role in developing and qualifying and qualifying polymeric materials and chemistry related processes used in our flow measurement products.
  • Your expertise in polymers, adhesives, cleaning processes, surface treatments and material-related manufacturing challenges will help ensure product quality, regulatory compliance and long-term innovation.
  • What is the role about?
  • Lead cross-functional improvement initiatives to enhance quality, productivity, sustainability, and cost efficiency while fostering knowledge sharing across the organization.
  • What do we expect from you? You:
  • Hold a PhD in Polymer Chemistry, Macromolecular Chemistry, Materials Chemistry or a closely related discipline, alternatively a Master's degree combined with extensive industrial experience.
  • Bring at least 10 years of experience in industrial chemistry within a manufacturing environment.
  • Have hands-on experience with polymers, adhesives, potting materials, surface treatments and chemical analytical methods.
