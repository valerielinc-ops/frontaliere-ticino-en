Original description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. True to its cooperative nature and values, it encourages the creation of a healthy, inclusive and inspiring work environment, ideal for developing your talent. By joining the development team in charge of Sales and Distribution, you will be at the heart of business challenges and work closely with a Product Owner and Business Analysts, in a structured framework that promotes quick decisions and useful solutions to transform concrete needs into high-value application solutions. As part of the team strengthening, we are looking for a person capable of designing, developing and maintaining business applications based on the Power Platform, while also intervening on .NET solutions hosted on Azure, in a continuous and quality evolution logic. The missions that await you:

  • Design, develop and maintain Power Apps (Canvas and/or Model-driven) and .NET applications
  • Intervention on the Azure ecosystem, in particular: App Services and Azure Functions and autonomy on the infra as code part, with a real responsibility on the setting up and evolution of environments
  • Collaborate with business teams to understand needs and propose suitable solutions
  • Be a force for proposals on technical and functional developments
  • Good control of the Power Platform, especially Power Apps. Power Automation is a plus
  • .NET Development Experience (C#)
  • Very good knowledge of the Azure environment (App Services, Azure Service Bus, Key Vault, Storage, Logic Apps, DevOps Pipelines...), or strong motivation to deepen these topics in a demanding professional context
  • Good ability to work as a team in an agile and interdisciplinary environment
  • Analytical spirit, technical rigor and orientation
  • Autonomous, curious and pragmatic
  • At ease on low-code subjects as well as on more traditional development
  • Service sense and ability to interact with technical and non-technical profiles
  • Want to get involved in applications that are actually used daily
  • French (minimum level C1)
  • English (minimum B2 level) We offer you :
  • A healthy working environment in a mutualist company certified "Great Place to Work"
  • Multiple social benefits, including flexibility in work organisationA young, dynamic, caring and innovative team
  • Exploration Days: four times a year, an entire day dedicated to working on a topic you're passionate about
  • Active support for developing your skills and training Show us your personality! In your cover letter, share with us:
  • What you like in development
  • Your vision of agility
  • Your Personal Achievements
  • Your online contributions (GitHub, Stack Overflow, blog, etc.) We look forward to receiving your complete application digitally (CV, cover letter, work certificates and diplomas).
