Interpreter pool advisor and manager 80% (m/f/d)

An integral part of the French-speaking Swiss Migration and Integration Department, understanding each other is Caritas Switzerland's community interpretation service: professional, accessible and committed. We create the conditions for reliable and respectful communication between migrants and institutions. By ensuring equitable access to information and facilitating mutual understanding, we contribute every day to equity and social cohesion. We are active in the cantons of Fribourg, Jura and the French-speaking part of the canton of Bern. Our team consists of a coordination unit and more than 150 intercultural interpreters/mediators. We are looking for a dynamic person to lead the strategic development and ensure the operational and human follow-up of our pool of interpreters.

Your tasks

  • Coordinate and develop the interpreter pool according to the needs and directions of the service
  • Recruitment and integration of new interpreters
  • Be the reference point for interpreters and ensure the flow of information between the field and the service
  • Follow up the interpretation staff and accompany the various stages of their journey
  • Ensure quality of services and process claims
  • Conduct and coordinate strategic projects related to interpreter activity, conduct necessary analyses and make recommendations
  • Advanced training in human resources, management, labour psychology, social sciences, management or equivalent, ideally complemented by training in personnel management
  • Training or experience in conflict management
  • 3-5 years of experience in a similar function
  • Experience in recruitment and staff development
  • Autonomy, organizational sense, rigour and dynamism
  • Confirmed communication and project management skills
  • Mastery of French and German
  • Desired knowledge of migration issues
