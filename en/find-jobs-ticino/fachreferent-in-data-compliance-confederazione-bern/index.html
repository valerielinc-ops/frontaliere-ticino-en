Role overview

Operating, developing, anchoring and monitoring the effectiveness of the Data Compliance Management System (DCMS).

Further develop governance principles, guidelines, roles and processes and convert them into systems and operational workflows.

Identify and evaluate data compliance risks as well as accompany the implementation of appropriate measures to control them.

  • Operating, developing, anchoring and monitoring the effectiveness of the Data Compliance Management System (DCMS).
  • Further develop governance principles, guidelines, roles and processes and convert them into systems and operational workflows.

Company and context

  • Lead or accompany projects and change projects at the interface of law, organization and IT and advise directorates and departments on data compliance requirements.
  • Design and implement training, awareness-raising and communication measures.
  • university degree at master’s level or comparable education as well as in-depth knowledge of data protection and information law.
  • At least five years of practical experience in compliance and governance as well as in-depth experience in the development and implementation of governance structures, standards, roles and processes or compliance management systems.
  • In particular, the governance and development position seeks in-depth experience related to audit and internal control system (ICS);
  • Experience in risk management is beneficial.
  • Strong understanding of data, information systems and business processes as well as their legal and organizational relationships.
  • Experience in managing or accompaniing complex projects and change projects in the compliance and governance environment as well as the ability to quickly capture, structure and communicate complex issues in a user-friendly manner.
  • Good oral and written knowledge in two official languages as well as in English, each at least at level B2.
