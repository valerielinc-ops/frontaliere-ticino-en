Original description

Membership, meaningfulness and competence With an experienced team, we accompany patients through ups and downs and help them to find their way back to life or work after an accident or illness.

You can expect an exciting and versatile working environment in which you can contribute your expertise and actively shape it. Together with a committed interprofessional team, you accompany patients on their way back to a better quality of life. Look forward to a varied activity with a lot of personal responsibility, professional exchange and the opportunity to support people in their rehabilitation process. Become part of our team and design rehabilitation that moves with us. We look forward to your application.

Tasks

  • You carry out logopedic examinations independently, plan individual therapies and implement them in individual and group settings.
  • Together with doctors, physiotherapists and occupational therapists, you will work closely together in an interprofessional treatment team.
  • Regularly participate in reports, case discussions and supervision
  • When performing fiber endoscopic swallowing examinations (FEES), you actively participate
  • The coordination with pre-treatment bodies as well as the planning of further logopedic connection solutions are also part of your area of responsibility.
  • You document treatment courses carefully and prepare the corresponding reports
  • They will guide and advise you competently and empathetically.

Profile

  • You have completed training in speech therapy (BA, BSc or diploma with EDK recognition)
  • Ideally, you have already gained experience in rehabilitation or in the clinical environment, but this is not a requirement
  • Very good German skills in word and writing distinguish you, further language skills are advantageous
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation brings you joy and you convince through your communication strength
  • An independent, reliable and structured working method is a matter of course for you
  • Team spirit, openness to feedback and an appreciative approach to others are among your strengths.
  • You bring commitment, initiative and a sense of responsibility
  • Readiness for Saturday missions rounds off your profile

Pensum: 40-70 %
