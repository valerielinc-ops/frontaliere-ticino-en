Role overview

The gynaecology department of the Jura Hospital carries out hospital and outpatient activities at the Delémont site as a priority (+ from 3,300 visits per year / 450 births / 750 hospitalizations / 500 gynaecological operations).

The service offers a wide range of general and specialized consultations (senology, urinary incontinence, colposcopy, adolescent counselling, infertility and menopause) as well as emergency management.
