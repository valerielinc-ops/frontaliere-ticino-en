Your Mission

Design the technical solutions of the broadcast and IT infrastructure projects (studios, production and distribution authorities, technical premises, mobile units), in pairs with the project manager who carries the steering, budget and planning.

Develop design files: functional synoptics, principle diagrams, low current and strong current wiring plans, space layout plans and bay face views.
