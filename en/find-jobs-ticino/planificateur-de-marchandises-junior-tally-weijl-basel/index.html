Junior Merchandise Planner

We're here to make you feel sexy, empowered & UNSTOPPABLE.

Founded in 1984, TALLY WEiJL has been on a crazy journey to celebrate fashion, independence, fun, love, & community for close to 40 years.

Today we’re present in 35 markets with about 400 stores, online presence, and 3m+ fans across social channels.

About the Role

We are looking for a Junior Merchandise Planner to support the development and execution of merchandise financial plans and assortment strategies.

Working closely with Buyers and cross-functional teams, you will contribute to building the right assortment at the right time, in the right quantities and at the right margin, supporting business performance and decision-making.

Your

  • Responsibilities

· Support the development of assortment plans by analyzing past performance, market specificities and trend indications · Contribute to the preparation of financial plans including weekly takings, intake margin, markdown and gross profit · Calculate quantities for new products based on historical data and inputs from Buying teams · Support the management of Open To Buy (OTB) and stock levels · Analyze current business performance, historical results and market trends to identify risks and opportunities · Support the definition of ideal assortments in collaboration with Buyers · Contribute to financial planning updates and weekly budget tracking · Participate in promotional and clearance pricing activities to optimize sell-through · Prepare and analyze data for business reviews and presentations · Support business meetings by providing insights and analysis to drive performance

  • Profile

· Degree in Business, Finance or similar field · Strong analytical mindset and attention to detail · Good knowledge of Excel (SAP/BW is a plus) · Ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines · Proactive and team-oriented mindset · Strong communication skills · Fluent in English; additional languages are a plus

What We Offer

· A dynamic, international and fast-paced working environment · The opportunity to learn and grow within Merchandise Planning · Exposure to cross-functional teams and business decision-making · Competitive compensation and benefits package
