Role overview

The Urological Clinic of the Claraspital has an A2 training status and is a DKG-certified uro-oncology centre.

The urology team consists of eight specialists and offers the entire spectrum of urological diagnosis and therapy.

We have a versatile infrastructure (da Vinci Xi with two consoles, laparoscopy, digital flexible ureteroscopy, 180W XPS Greenlightlaser, holmium laser, mono-/bipolar resection, etc.) and two operating rooms in an integrated operating room.
