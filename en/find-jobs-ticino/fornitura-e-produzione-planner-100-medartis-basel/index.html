Role overview

As a Supply and Production Planner, you are responsible for the reliable and smooth supply of an assigned production portfolio on a global scale.

They translate customer needs into actionable production plans and ensure that the required materials, capacities and resources are available on time.

You play a key role in ensuring delivery capability and continuously develop planning processes and standards in line with the global supply chain strategy.

Main responsibilities

  • Your responsibilities
  • Responsibility in the area of Plan-to-Produce for the creation, maintenance and continuous optimization of a complete and feasible rolling production schedule in SAP S/4HANA, taking into account needs, material availability and capacities.
  • Preparation, moderation and follow-up of regular coordination meetings with internal production units and external contract manufacturers to hand over and implement the production plan.
  • Ensure material availability for the assigned product portfolio in close cooperation with production, quality, purchasing, engineering, product management and suppliers.
  • Implementation of monthly rough-cut capacity planning in SAP S/4HANA as well as coordination with the production areas for early identification of bottlenecks, development of scenarios and definition of suitable countermeasures.
  • Preparation and support of the monthly cross-site S Supply Review Meeting by providing meaningful analysis, reports and decision templates.
  • Analysis, monitoring and reporting of relevant supply chain indicators (e.g.
  • OTIF, capacity utilisation, lead times) and derivation and implementation of continuous improvement measures.
  • Maintenance and continuous optimization of production-relevant master data as well as ensuring high data quality.
  • Participation in the further development of planning processes, methods and systems to increase efficiency and transparency along the supply chain. Your qualifications
