Role overview

Spitex Gäu is a non-profit organization and performs on behalf of div. Resident communities provide professional and comprehensive care and care at home. We have been a training company for many years. Due to the increasing number of apprenticeship places, we are looking for a further Nurse HF with vocational training tasks for HF students
