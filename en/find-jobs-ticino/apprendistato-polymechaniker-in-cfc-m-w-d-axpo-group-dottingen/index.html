Original description

As a polymechanic you are a professional in the processing of various materials and/or the maintenance of machines. Start of training: 1 August 2027

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Manufacture and assembly of components and prototypes
  • Computer Controlled Machine Programming (CNC)
  • Maintenance and maintenance of the power plant
  • Start-up of new plants
  • Operating various measuring and testing equipment

Profile and skills

  • Completed elementary school, good performance in mathematics and physics
  • Enjoying accurate and careful working methods
  • Interest in technical relationships and mechanics
  • Craftsmanship, technical understanding and spatial imagination
  • Curiosity, perseverance and good concentration ability

About the team

As a polymechanic apprentice, you work with us in a well-established and future-oriented team. The basic training takes place in its own training workshop.

Start your apprenticeship with an eventful and practical introductory week together with many other learners. You can also expect 6 weeks of holidays, a contribution to your mobile subscription and a generous mobility contribution for your flexibility on the road. In addition, we reward good performance with great extras.

Look forward to a dynamic environment where you can get involved and grow beyond yourself.
