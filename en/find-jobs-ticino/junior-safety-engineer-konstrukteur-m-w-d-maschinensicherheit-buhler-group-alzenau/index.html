Original description

Become the security architect of our globally used high-tech facilities

At Bühler Leybold Optics, we develop high-precision thin-film coating systems for future markets such as architectural glass, smartphone displays, automotive headlights and medical technology. Our plants convince not only by technological excellence, but also by the highest safety standards.

In this key role, you accompany our plants from the first idea to commissioning and ensure that safety is considered right from the start. They identify risks at an early stage, develop practical solutions and ensure that our machines are economical, compliant and safe for people and the environment.
