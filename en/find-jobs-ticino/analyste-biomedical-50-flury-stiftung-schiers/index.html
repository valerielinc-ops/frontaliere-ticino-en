Original description

With more than 600 employees in the Schiers hospital, the three retirement homes Schiers, Jenaz and Klosters, Spitex, living with service, crèche, parental counselling and the Klosters Medical Center, the Flury Foundation ensures comprehensive and integrated health care in the Prättigau between Grüsch and Klosters. The Schiers Hospital is a modern acute care hospital in the canton of Graubünden with the clinics surgery/orthopedics, medicine, gynecology and obstetrics as well as medical surgical care in spinal surgery, vascular surgery, angiology, plastic surgery, ENT surgery and urology. It has a broad service mandate, which enables an extended basic service. The modern organization and infrastructure comprises an operation department with 3 halls (one of them hybrid), a certified IMC, a day clinic and a bed station for inpatient treatments with only single rooms. We are searching for our laboratory at 1. January 2027 a biomedical analyst 50%

Your responsibilities include

  • Blood sampling on the interdisciplinary ward and in the laboratory for outpatients
  • Receiving, processing and processing patient samples in the fields of clinical chemistry, haematology, clotting and immunohaematology
  • Validation of results and telephone information to internal and external bodies
  • Working hours from Monday to Sunday from 07.00 to 18.30. No night service

Your profile

  • Completed training BMA HF
  • Some years of professional experience as a BMA with laboratory activity
  • Quick comprehension, independent, careful and reliable working method
  • Friendly and empathetic personality, with high social competence
  • Good knowledge of current laboratory and office programs
