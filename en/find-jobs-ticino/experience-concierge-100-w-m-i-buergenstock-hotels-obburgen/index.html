Description

Experience Concierge 100% (f/m/i) - Bürgenstock Resort --> An icon of the Swiss hotel industry and a legend since its sensational opening in 1873. Located on the Bürgenberg, a 500-meter-high rocky outcrop in central Switzerland, the Bürgenstock Resort Lake Lucerne stands out for its bold design, groundbreaking sustainability, breathtaking landscapes and state-of-the-art leisure activities. Around 700 employees from 54 nations ensure excellent hospitality and provide the perfect setting for moments beyond imagination. Three exclusive hotels, the Bürgenstock Hotel & Alpine Spa (5Superior) with the two accommodation options The Contemporary and The Heritage, the Waldhotel by Bürgenstock (5 Superior) and the Tavern 1879 (3*) are available to guests. Each of these houses knows how to create a unique atmosphere. Twelve restaurants, lounges and bars are the showcase for culinary excellence

  • At the award-winning Bürgenstock Alpine Spa and Waldhotel Spa, guests will find the ultimate relaxation. The resort is not only a dream destination for travelers from all over the world, but also a workplace with an excellent corporate culture. For the third consecutive year, Bürgenstock Resort has been officially certified as a "Great Place to Work" in Switzerland - an award that fills the entire team with pride. To strengthen our team, we are looking for an Experience Concierge 100% (f/m/i) from December 2026 or by agreement They are primarily responsible for creating personalized guest experiences throughout the guest journey – from strategic pre-arrival planning to active in-house care and sustainable post-stay relationship work.

Individual guest advice on activities, transfers, special events and exclusive surprises as well as the sustainable increase in revenue per guest

Responsibility for the holistic care of VIP, Longstay and regular guests – from personal welcome to tailor-made Farewell
