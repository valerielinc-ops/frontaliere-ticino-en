Original description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, creative ideas in agile teams. Various activities are bundled in our Corporate Functions division. In addition to our finance and IT services, this area also includes global marketing and sales management as well as human resources. Every day fresh ideas are created here, which together we lead to success. Personal responsibility and networked work are particularly important to us. Do you also want to participate? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • You control the Group-wide budgeting and forecasting process and are responsible for the strategic five-year planning
  • You condense complex relationships into clear analyses, convincing presentations and well-founded decision-making principles. With your strategic recommendations, you support the management and other key stakeholders in important decisions
  • You analyze the performance of our national subsidiaries, examine business plans and forecasts with a critical eye and create transparency about opportunities and risks. In case of deviations, you initiate concrete countermeasures and accompany their consistent implementation
  • You will continue to develop our corporate management and actively promote the introduction of modern BI, planning and control instruments

You bring that with you

  • You have successfully completed a degree in economics with a focus on finance or controlling or bring a comparable qualification
  • You have in-depth management experience in corporate controlling or financial planning in an international corporate or matrix environment
  • You move confidently in complex financial models and master the integrated planning of income statement, balance sheet and cash flow. Modern BI and planning instruments you use specifically
  • You combine strong analytical skills with convincing management and C-level communication. As a leader, you provide orientation, set clear priorities and act in a structured, forward-looking and solution-oriented manner.
  • You communicate and negotiate stylishly in German and English

This is what we offer you

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The opportunity to bring in fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Chocolate in the workplace
  • Free parking
  • Discounts in Läderach Café and staff restaurant

Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh.

Sounds interesting? Then apply to us. Fabio Ferramosca, Talent Acquisition Partner, is at your disposal for any questions.
