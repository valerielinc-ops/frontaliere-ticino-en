Original description

Tasks

  • monitoring of the state of technical infrastructure, tracking, analysis and resolution of the technical problems encountered
  • responsible for the management and holding of the ticketing system
  • first level support to users in central traffic and contact point for external operators
  • assessment and implementation of the compensatory measures necessary in case of technical anomalies
  • operator of operating and safety systems in case of events on indication of the premises
  • administrative technical management of access to infrastructure (technical premises and computer network)
  • regular maintenance and updating of systems
  • systems management activities, such as data storage and video surveillance image processing
  • night and shiftwork outside office hours
  • in case of need, service performance picchetto

Requirements

  • Federal certificate of capacity (AFC) as computer/a
  • excellent knowledge of Windows operating systems
  • technical knowledge of hardware and computer systems
  • good knowledge of software applications based on SCADA
  • experience in cybersecurity (access management, antivirus systems)
  • System Management Experience with Active Directory
  • experience in the management of automation systems and systems
  • knowledge of road management systems and tunnels
  • aptitude for autonomous management, even in emergency cases
  • good organizational skills and flexibility at work
  • spirit of initiative and predisposition marked to work in team
  • good knowledge (conversation and editing) of Italian and knowledge (conversation) of German and English
  • license to conduct category B
  • excellent knowledge of the cantonal territory and its institutions

Special remarks

  • service office: Camorino
  • entry into service: to be agreed
  • candidates participating in the competition authorize the Office of Electromechanicals to process all the data necessary to verify the absence of precedents incompatible with the function, also with other authorities

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required further documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Conditions of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements
