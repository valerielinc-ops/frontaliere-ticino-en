Specialist in environment and sustainability 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Specialist in environment and sustainability Bern / Work Smart, January 1st, 2027 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. The Safety, Quality and Environment division has technical leadership for safety, risk, quality and environmental management at SBB Infrastructure. You can look forward to working with the environment and sustainability team in the Infrastructure Division to ensure SBB's environmental advantage. Bring your talent as an environmental and sustainability specialist and make a direct contribution to the sustainable development of Switzerland's transport infrastructure. You can find benefits such as free GA and discounted travel abroad

  • also for the family
  • on our website under Benefits. You can move that.
  • As a specialist in environment and sustainability, you drive forward topics such as contaminated sites, PFAS, accident prevention and noise. You optimize internal processes, initiate applied research and set priorities together with your partners.
  • SBB Infrastructure has identified 250 suspected areas for possible use of PFAS firefighting foam. You coordinate the historical and technical investigations in accordance with the contaminated sites regulations and promote the remediation of contaminated sites.
  • You coordinate the accident prevention as a whole, build up the technical leadership and ensure its implementation and regular review.
  • In your role, the line supports the legally compliant implementation of environmental issues and proactively addresses changes. You regularly inform management about the most important challenges and your proposed solutions.
  • You bring in sustainability topics such as sustainable procurement and the circular economy and of course anchor them in your work. This is what you bring with you.
  • You are passionate about the environment and sustainability and are committed to the topic with your know-how and passion. You work best in a team and want to develop your environment and yourself.
  • You inspire people and teams and communicate convincingly and skillfully. You can also convince critical voices with your winning demeanor and enthusiasm. Your good stakeholder management ensures the success of your work.
  • We require several years of experience in the field of environmental consulting (contaminated site processing, environmental construction support, accident prevention, water protection projects) as well as a scientific degree. Knowledge of environmental management rounds off your profile.
  • You also have experience in construction and/or railway infrastructure construction as well as project management. Your practical experience enables you to communicate with the project managers on an equal footing and to assume your role as a specialist manager.
  • You communicate confidently in the German language (at least B2). Good knowledge of Italian or French is required.
