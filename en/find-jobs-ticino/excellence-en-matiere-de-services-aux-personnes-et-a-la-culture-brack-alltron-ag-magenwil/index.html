Original description

Tasks

  • Overall responsibility for professional, timely HR operations, from payroll and contract administration to mutations, absences and social security
  • Professional and human leadership as well as further development of your team
  • Ensuring a high quality of service for employees, managers and internal stakeholders
  • Analysis, optimization and automation of existing P&C processes, including the use of AI-powered solutions to increase efficiency
  • Establishment of binding service levels and quality standards
  • Business ownership for our HR/IT systems, from HRIS to recruiting solutions to payroll systems, with a focus on the integration and further development of AI functionalities in close cooperation with the IT product lead P&C and external providers
  • Further development of policies and processes taking into account labour law, social insurance, GAV and data protection as well as early detection of compliance and process risks

Requirements

  • Leadership experience, ideally three to five years in an operational HR or service environment
  • Sound practice in HR operations as well as good knowledge of payroll and administrative employee lifecycle processes
  • Very good knowledge of Swiss labour law and social security system
  • Strong process competence as well as experience in digitization and automation of HR processes, ideally with initial experience in the use of AI tools in the HR environment
  • High IT affinity and experience as business owner: in HR/IT systems
  • Structured, pragmatic and solution-oriented personality with high quality awareness and pleasure in continuous improvement

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.
