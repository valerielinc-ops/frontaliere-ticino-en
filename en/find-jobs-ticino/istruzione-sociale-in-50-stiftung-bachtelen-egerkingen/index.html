Original description

The Bachtelen is committed to the individual support of children, teenagers and young adults in the canton of Solothurn. We are also committed to the integration of young people who are impaired in various ways: behavior, language, communication, interaction or cognition. In order to achieve our goals, we run daycare centers, day schools, residential offers, individual support offers as well as therapeutic and advisory offers. The TASI (Day School Individual) is run in Solothurn and Egerkingen. The aim is to integrate students into society, school and the professional world.
