Coop Cooperative — Allschwil, Allschwil, Svizzera

As a leading Swiss company in the convenience, filling station and energy sector, we operate a network of more than 320 Coop Pronto Shop with or without filling stations in Switzerland and Liechtenstein.

Company and context

  • You are responsible for the smooth organization of your Coop Pronto shop and guarantee the implementation of the high standards of Coop Pronto
  • You successfully carry out the financial tasks including controlling the business key figures of a Coop Pronto shop
  • They take care of the goods management (orders, goods incoming controls and goods maintenance)
  • They sell the products, advise customers: inside and maintain the gas station (if available)
  • They ensure compliance, enforcement and control of the legal provisions on quality assurance, youth protection and occupational safety
  • They lead the employees: inside: search, recruitment, training, payroll and social benefits as well as compliance with the labour law framework conditions (OR and labour law) Requirements
  • You have completed training in the retail trade (or comparable training in sales), preferably in the food retail trade
