Original description

Children are active and curious from birth.

They develop in their own personal way and at their own pace. They discover and understand the world with all their senses –

Attentively accompanied by us.

Under the roof of Bachtelen, institutions are united that are engaged in the canton of Solothurn with a wide range of offers for children, young people and young adults.

For the daycare center Villa Kunterbunt in Grenchen we are looking for a

Specialist child care, 80 – 100%

We want a person who

  • It is a pleasure to actively accompany the children in day-care
  • helps to shape the pedagogical development of the day-to-day nursery and actively implements it in everyday work
  • with her life experience and balance contributes to a family atmosphere in the kindergarten
  • is reliable, open and loyal and appreciates transparent communication in the team
  • remains calm and solution-oriented even in hectic situations

You bring with you

  • Training as a specialist (FaBeK)
  • 2 years of professional experience after graduation
  • High pedagogical competence in the promotion, care and accompaniment of children
  • Teamwork and openness as well as an independent and reliable working method
  • Creativity, flexibility and commitment in day-to-day daycare
  • Willingness to share responsibility for changes in day-to-day daycare

We offer

  • An established, motivated and creative team
  • Various employee events
  • one employee:internally friendly training concept
  • an attractive workplace in a centrally located daycare centre with a major changeover (easily accessible with public transport)
  • progressive employment conditions, generous pension benefits and other attractive benefits
  • Possibility to take on a group management function when further developing KITA
