Original description

The Michel Group AG comprises the psychiatric institution Privatklinik Meiringen, the rehabilitation clinic Hasliberg, the Helsenberg Foundation with dormitories and protected workplaces as well as the Haslital laundry and the Haslital daycare centre as well as services in other areas. It employs a total of around 800 people at its locations in Willigen, Meiringen, Hasliberg, Ringgenberg, Interlaken, Thun and Bern.

Haslital Laundry provides high-quality services to healthcare institutions, the hotel industry as well as commercial enterprises and private customers in the eastern Bernese Oberland. Every year, around 700 tonnes of flat, professional and private laundry are professionally processed. Reliability, teamwork and flexibility are our top priority.
