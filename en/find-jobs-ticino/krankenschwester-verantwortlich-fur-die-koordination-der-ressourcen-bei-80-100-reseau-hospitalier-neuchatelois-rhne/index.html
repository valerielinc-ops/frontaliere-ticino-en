Original description

The Department of Occupational Health (ORL) is responsible for the diagnostic and therapeutic management of patients with ENT pathologies, both in outpatient and in hospital. Our service works closely with the various medical and surgical specialties to provide multi-disciplinary, patient-centred care. It offers a stimulating environment for developing clinical and surgical skills and for participating actively in the training of doctors.

Head of clinic in ENT 100%

Your missions

  • Care for outpatients and hospitalized patients with ENT pathologies
  • Participation in diagnostic, therapeutic and operational activities
  • Participation in the organization of the service and on-call tours
  • Collaboration with different specialties in multidisciplinary management
  • Participation in the supervision of assistant doctors
  • Participation in pre- and post-graduate training

Your profile

  • Swiss or foreign doctor diploma recognized by MEBEKO
  • Title of specialist in oto-rhino-laryngology or advanced post-graduate training in ENT according to the level of function
  • Clinical and surgical experience in ENT
  • Diplomas and titles on the Register of Medical Professions (MedReg)
  • Language level in French B2, certified

Your skills

  • Responsibility and autonomy
  • Good clinical capacity and decision-making ability
  • Ability to work in interdisciplinarity
  • Organizational direction and priorities
  • Ability to work in teams and networks
  • Interest in teaching and mentoring doctors in training
