Role overview

Mission

As part of this function, attached to our Clothing Division, you will manage the team and all production activities of the UAP Boxes in Haute Horlogerie (HAH) workshop, which includes milling, turning, diamondmaking and polishing activities.

In close cooperation with another UAP HAH workshop and the other workshops of the Division, as well as support services, you will ensure the performance of your workshop by ensuring the waiting of production objectives and compliance with Patek Philippe standards, while promoting the collaborative experience.
