Description

Sandstrahler: at Stadler Rail in St. Margrethen. Stadler is a globally active Swiss manufacturer of rail vehicles with headquarters in Bussnang (Canton of Thurgau). Founded in 1942, the company develops and produces full, regional and S-Bahns, trams, locomotives and cog railways and employs several thousand people in Switzerland. Stadler offers a modern working environment, attractive employment conditions and a wide range of development opportunities in an innovative Swiss industrial company.
