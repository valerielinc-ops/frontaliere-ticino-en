Original description

In our Zollikerberg rehabilitation clinic, we treat patients especially in the area of musculoskeletal rehabilitation through modern and patient-centered care. Thanks to the interdisciplinary cooperation and the excellent technical expertise of the treatment teams, we adapt our efficient therapy programs individually to the needs of patients.

Your tasks

  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: You carry out findings and treat our inpatient and outpatient patients together with a team of doctors, nurses and other therapeutic disciplines
  • Rapporte: You participate in our interdisciplinary rapports and represent the field of therapy
  • Therapies: You perform individual and group therapies dry and in water
  • Patient-oriented care: You carry out assessments and documentation, which are personally tailored to each patient
  • Advice: You advise relatives and take care of the aid
  • Extended responsibility implies therapy coordination: in close cooperation with group management, specialists, care and the medical team. This includes:
  • Admission on the day of entry incl. Determination of rehabilitation goals in consultation with the patient
  • Presentation of new patients in the team
  • Monitoring and adjustment of rehabilitation goals in the course of therapy
  • Ensuring interdisciplinary coordinated treatment planning
  • Review of therapy development and goal achievement
  • Interface to therapy planning (including dispo times)
  • Assistance with exit management and the transfer of relevant information
  • Documentation and tracking of established therapeutic goals and measures
  • Performing Entry/Exit and Generic Educations

Your profile

  • Education and experience: Completed training as a physiotherapist or equivalent; in the case of foreign diploma with SRC recognition. You will bring several years of professional experience, ideally in the field of rehabilitation and interprofessional cooperation.
  • Professional, organizational and coordination competence: You have in-depth physiotherapeutic expertise and experience in jointly defining, reviewing and adjusting rehabilitation goals. You work in a structured, independent and reliable manner, can keep an eye on several patient processes at the same time and coordinate therapy processes in a targeted manner. You are safe with digital documentation and planning systems.
  • Communication and decision-making competence: You communicate clearly, appreciatively and solution-oriented and move safely in an interprofessional environment. You can combine different professional perspectives, structure conversations and votes and make decisions within your area of responsibility independently, comprehensibly and patient-centered.
  • Personality and responsibility: You enjoy the physiotherapeutic work and the direct contact with patients. At the same time, you are happy to take on additional responsibility, bring your own initiative and flexibility and are interested in actively shaping and developing therapy processes. You think networked, patient-centered and interprofessional and have a high level of social competence.

What we offer

  • Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collegial work climate: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers, pharmacies and our membership at Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits at the Wellnesstherme FORTYSEVEN
