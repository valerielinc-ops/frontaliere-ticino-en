Original description

Tasks

Are you looking for an activity in which meaningful and patient-centered medicine is the focus and teamwork is given priority?

The medical service of Domicil Bern AG was founded in the spring of 2024 and is still under construction - with room for co-creation and new ideas. If you want to live your passion for clinical work, are looking for an alternative to everyday hospital life or the classic family doctor's office is not right for you, this job could be the right next step for you!

As a doctor or doctor, together with an Advanced Practice Nurse (APN), you look after the residents at the assigned locations and regularly carry out visits there, while on the road. In addition, you are, alternately with your colleagues, responsible for the medical telephone background service, for which there is also the possibility for home office. As a medical contact person for nurses and APNs, you provide high-quality and patient-centered care for our residents and quickly make informed decisions in urgent situations.

Create the future of medical care with our young, dynamic team - we look forward to meeting you! Further information and an insight into the daily work of our doctors can be found on jobs.concara/medcara and domicilbern.ch/medical service

Requirements

  • Specialist/Specialist General Internal Medicine or Practical Doctor
  • Advantageous professional experience in geriatrics or palliative medicine
  • Category B driving licence required
  • Team-capable, dynamic and solution-oriented personality
  • Interest in shaping and building

We offer

  • 42-hour week at 100% and the opportunity to work half days
  • No late, night and weekend services
  • Flexible annual working hours, innovative working time models and possibility for home office
  • Pensum selectable between 30-100 %
  • Employer share Premiums for health day allowance insurance and pension fund higher than statutory regulations
  • Many design possibilities in an innovative and dynamic environment

Domicil offers senior citizens stationary services in care and care. With more than 20 locations and around 1,600 employees, Domicil is the number 1 in long-term care in the canton of Bern. Under the umbrella of Concara, we are committed to an integrated all-round supply together with Spitex Bern.
