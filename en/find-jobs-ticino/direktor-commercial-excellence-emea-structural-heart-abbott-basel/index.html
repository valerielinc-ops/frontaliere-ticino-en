Original description

The Director, Commercial Excellence for Europe, Middle East, Africa (EMEA) Structural Heart serves as a member of the EMEA Divisional Vice President’s senior staff. This leader helps to define and operationalize commercial strategy for the EMEA geography to achieve annual commercial objectives. The Director collaborates across functions and with the respective country General Managers to deliver on commercial execution tactics that drive business growth and market expansion. This role reports into Structural Heart’s Divisional VP for Global Commercial Excellence and will be based within an EMEA major market (preferably Abbott offices in Belgium, Germany or UK).
