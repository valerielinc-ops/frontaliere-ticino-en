Role overview

Eisberg AG is one of the leading manufacturers of ready-to-eat meals from the “4th range” range – i.e. salads, fruit and vegetables ready for cooking and consumption – in Switzerland.

True to our motto "Colour Your Life", our more than 800 employees produce up to 60 tonnes of fresh food every day with passion and commitment at the Dällikon, Dänikon and Essert-sous-Champvent locations.

Eisberg AG is proud to be part of the internationally renowned Eisberg Group, which is regarded as the market leader in the production of ready-to-eat salads in Central Europe.
