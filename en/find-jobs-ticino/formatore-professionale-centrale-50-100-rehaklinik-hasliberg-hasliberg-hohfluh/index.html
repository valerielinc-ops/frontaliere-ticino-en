Role overview

Hasliberg Rehabilitation Clinic is a renowned Swiss clinic in the field of psychosomatic, internistic-oncological and musculoskeletal rehabilitation.

The clinic has 83 beds and is on the hospital lists of the cantons of Bern, Lucerne, Nidwalden and Uri.

The ISO-certified company works process-oriented and interdisciplinary, based on the mission statement of “REHA competence” with the characteristics: respect, efficiency, warmth, attention and competence.
