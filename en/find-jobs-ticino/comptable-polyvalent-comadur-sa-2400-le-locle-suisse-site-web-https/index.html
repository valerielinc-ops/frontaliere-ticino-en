Multivalent Accountant - Swatch Group

Multivalent Accountant Introduction of society Comadur SA, World leader in the production of hard materials components Swiss Made. We are part of the Swatch Group, the world's leading watchmaker. Recognized as the preferred partner of group brands for the production of Ceramics, Sapphire Glaces and Clock Stones for the watch industry. We are always interested in meeting future colleagues wishing to contribute to the work of our teams with the help of their talents, ambitions and inspirations. As part of our growth and development, we offer, on our Col-des-Roches site,

  • Multipurpose Accountant (H/F) position.
  • Our profile
  • Your commitment will contribute to our growth as well as to your personal development through:
  • A modern and pleasant workspace
  • Social conditions of the highest order
  • A dynamic, demanding team listening and always looking for new challenges
  • A common will: to collaborate and grow together to become an indispensable partner.
  • Description of post
  • Your main missions will be to:
  • Participation in the monthly rounding according to the Group's guidelines
  • Support for all operational accounting processes
  • Preparation of the Reporting Comadur and Swatch Group
  • Monitoring the company's tax obligations (VAT, direct taxes and deferred taxes)
  • Treasury monitoring and planning
  • Preparation of financial statements with external auditors and Group
  • Implementation and monitoring of internal control procedures
  • Collaboration with other services: Controlling, Methods, Logistics, Sales

During periods of high activity (audits, loops) and to meet the deadlines, some overtime may be required. These periods should also be taken into account in the planning of holidays.

  • Qualifications
  • Flexibility and availability, to ensure monthly loops each beginning of the month
  • Rigor and organization
  • Relationship qualities: listening, understanding
  • Team spirit
  • Good analytical ability, critical mind, solution orientation
