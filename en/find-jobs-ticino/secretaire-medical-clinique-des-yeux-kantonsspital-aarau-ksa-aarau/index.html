Role overview

Tasks

  • Together with your colleagues, you support the medical team in organizational and administrative work.
  • They organize and plan office hours, coordinate appointments with internal and external bodies.
  • They do correspondence as well as general secretarial duties and write off medical reports.
  • In cooperation with your teammates, you are the competent contact person on the phone and at the reception, arranging and coordinating appointments at internal and external offices.
  • Depending on your needs, you can support the assistant doctors on the Urology bed station, in particular the station doctor in all administrative tasks.
  • Depending on your needs, you will participate in the weekly tumor conference and complete all administrative tasks in this regard.

Requirements

  • They have completed medical or commercial training with further training as medical secretary.
  • You bring pleasure in dealing with patients and organize your tasks efficiently.
  • They are well versed in dealing with IT and have very good knowledge of medical terminology.
  • You are a motivated, responsible, friendly and resilient personality and appreciate working together in a large team.
