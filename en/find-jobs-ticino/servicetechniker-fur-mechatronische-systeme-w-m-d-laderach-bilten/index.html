Original description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 3000 people from more than 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, innovative ideas in agile teams. In our production, we produce fresh couvertures from high-quality raw materials. Afterwards, we process and refine the liquid chocolates in our chocolate factory to form FrischSchoggi, pralinés, truffes, figures and much more. Do you also want to participate? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • You independently supervise special facilities and assume responsibility for maintenance in the area of mechanics and mechatronics
  • You are responsible for the provision of services in the area of technical service, in particular for repairs and maintenance work according to the order and objectives
  • You analyze faults and errors and take care of their sustainable correction through modifications or changes
  • You plan and carry out revisions and maintenance work on the plants
  • Ensure that all facilities, machinery, infrastructure and buildings in Ennenda and Bilten are operational and available at all times
  • You pay attention to compliance with operational safety and actively help to enforce it

You bring that with you

  • You have in-depth experience as a mechanic in the automotive industry and have received comprehensive training in the field of mechatronics
  • You have craftsmanship and an in-depth technical understanding of all equipment, both in buildings and on production machinery.
  • You have additional knowledge in areas such as pneumatics, hydraulics, electrics and PLC control
  • Welding experience (ideally TIG and also MIG/MAG) as well as knowledge of mechanical manufacturing techniques such as milling and turning are advantageous
  • You are physically resilient and have experienced PC skills
  • You are ready to work in the pikettdienst and have a driving license

This is what we offer you

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The opportunity to bring in fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Chocolate in the workplace
  • Free parking
  • Discounts in Läderach Café and staff restaurant

Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh.

Sounds interesting? Then apply to us. Juan Marfil, Head of Technical Service, tel. 055 645 77 71, is at your disposal for any questions.
