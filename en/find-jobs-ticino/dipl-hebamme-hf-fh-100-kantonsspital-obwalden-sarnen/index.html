Role overview

The Obwalden Cantonal Hospital is an innovative, versatile primary care hospital in central Switzerland with clinics for general, trauma and visceral surgery, orthopedics, internal medicine, ENT, urology, ophthalmology, spinal surgery, gynecology and obstetrics as well as anesthesia.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed and sensitive midwife on November 1st, 2026 or by appointment who will support expectant families with professional expertise, empathy and a high level of cooperation.
