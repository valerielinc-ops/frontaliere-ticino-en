Original description

In this position, you will report directly to the Head of Product Lifecycle. Your main mission is to manage and coordinate all watch and component lifecycle management processes to ensure a clear understanding of obsolescence causes and control of related data and costs. Mansioni

  • Manage the product end-of-life portfolio, ensuring alignment with the industrial and commercial plan
  • Ensure rigorous application of the product end-of-life process
  • Monitor and optimize inventory levels at the end of production
  • Propose adjustments to production and supply plans to limit surpluses
  • Conduct impact analyses related to production changes in S&OP cycles
  • Identify unnecessary inventory and analyze its causes through source processes
  • Define and recommend actions to optimize, reduce, or reuse inventory
  • Coordinate the implementation of validated reuse plans with stakeholders
  • Ensure consistency between product lifecycle, end-of-life, and associated data quality
  • Requisiti

  • You have a minimum of 5 years of confirmed experience in planning, Supply Chain, or product lifecycle management
  • You have worked in a demanding industrial environment, ideally in watchmaking
  • You are proficient in S&OP processes and production planning mechanisms
  • You are comfortable with product obsolescence processes and range transitions
  • You are recognized for your strong analytical skills and ability to assess operational impacts
  • You are proficient in ERP and Power BI tools at an advanced level
  • You are comfortable in a cross-functional environment and able to collaborate with various stakeholders
  • You are distinguished by your rigor, synthesis skills, and results-oriented approach
