Company vision At McDonald, every visit becomes a sunny experience. Our team loves to make guests smile and create a welcoming atmosphere in which everyone feels comfortable. If you like to be in contact with people and are looking for a job that is easy, dynamic and full of opportunities, you are exactly right for us! Main tasks - Greetings to guests and creating a cheerful atmosphere - Presentation of new offers and support for orders - Sharing of guest feedback to team and management - Support of the team with enthusiasm and team spirit - Ensuring a perfect restaurant experience for every guest Requirements - Sunny, open nature - Passion, making people happy - Team spirit and reliability - Attention for details and willingness to listen - Motivation to learn and develop - no experience necessary! Apply now and start your trip at McDonald,s. We are looking forward to seeing you!
