Original description

ELCO offers premium-grade heating solutions with first-class service, which is designed to meet the needs of our customers throughout the entire life cycle of the products and heating systems. The focus is on excellent consulting as well as convincing products and heating systems. ELCO has been a leader in burner, condensing and solar technology for decades and is represented throughout Europe with 1.7 million installed heating systems.

Since 1928, ELCO has been a leading European brand offering intelligent solutions and technologies tailored to the needs of private and commercial projects and ensuring maximum energy savings.

Your tasks

  • consulting and support of heating installers regarding ELCO heat generation plants
  • maintaining existing customer relationships and acquiring new customers
  • tracking of contract notices and offers
  • participation in exhibitions, events and customer training
  • you analyze customer needs, develop suitable solutions and coordinate your sales activities in cooperation with the development and production department
  • you work closely with sales management

Your profile

  • You are an entrepreneurial personality and have a flair for sales, preferably with an EDC in the area of heating or sanitary
  • your problem-solving orientation and your sense of responsibility make you a successful and persistent negotiating partner (m/w)
  • you have sales experience in the field of heating, ventilation, air conditioning technology and strive for a new challenge
  • Very good German knowledge is essential, French knowledge is advantageous

Your chance With us you will find an interesting, challenging and diverse activity in a dynamic and future-oriented company. Here you will meet modern employment conditions such as Home Office, business vehicle etc. and the competent support of the sales service.

Place of work: Oberwallis
