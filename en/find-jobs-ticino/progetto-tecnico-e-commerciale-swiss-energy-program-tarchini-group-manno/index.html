Role overview

GIG Europe SA, a company of the Tarchini Group, which deals with the planning, development and management of “green” energy plants, is looking for a technical-commercial specialist for the management of relationships and the negotiation of land agreements for the realization of BESS plants within the framework of the Swiss Energy Program with a view to expanding its own activities at national level in the field of energy storage through the realization of BESS plants.

The selected person will identify real estate, get in touch with the owners of the land, as well as with the operators of the power grids and conclude agreements on building rights for the installation of BESS facilities. Requirements Age between 30 and 40 years
