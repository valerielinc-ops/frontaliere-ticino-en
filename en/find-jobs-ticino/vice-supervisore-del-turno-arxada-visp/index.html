Original description

Deputy Shift Supervisor Arxada is a global specialty chemicals company committed to solving the world's toughest preservation challenges through better science. [With a proud history of innovation stretching back more than a century], we aim to help our customers develop more sustainable solutions that protect and maintain people's health and well-being and extend the life of vital infrastructure, while working to reduce our and our customers' environmental footprint. Sustainability is one of our top priorities. We are making significant strategic investments that not only reduce our own environmental footprint, but also help our customers adapt to long-term environmental and social change by developing cleaner and greener solutions. To strengthen our solids mono-plant operations at Arxada, Visp, Switzerland, we are looking for a deputy shift leader in chemical production. Main tasks and responsibilities:

  • Responsible for the training and further development of employees
  • Ensuring smooth shift operation and organization of all work orders as well as responsibility for shift handover
  • In the event of an incident, ensures contact with external departments (porter / on-call / fire brigade)
  • Leading the team with regard to orders, priorities, breaks and absences
  • Ensuring the implementation of the required work in the assigned work area in accordance with the applicable requirements (safety, environmental protection and quality)
  • Questioning the daily work in collaboration with the employees of the shift team and developing suggestions for improvement accordingly Qualifications and experience:
  • High standards to strengthen the safety culture
  • Residence in the Upper Valais to ensure readiness in the event of disruptions
  • Experience as an investment specialist in chemical production or service companies and in dealing with process control systems
  • Preferably completed CPT or CPP apprenticeship (formerly certified operator) or willingness to start it
  • You are interested and ready to learn new technologies and continually educate yourself
  • Dedicated, flexible and reliable personality
  • Motivation to work shifts (including night shifts and weekends) Arxada has a world-class offering in three different areas: Consumer Microbial Control (CMC) focuses on the threat that microorganisms pose to humans, with products for the food and beverage, professional hygiene, household and personal care, crop protection and wood protection sectors. Industrial Microbial Control (IMC) supplies antimicrobials and high-performance additives to the plastics, energy, textile and other industries, as well as dry film and wet preservatives for paints and coatings, providing protection and improving efficiency in construction and marine. Nutrition, Care & Environmental (NCE) supplies materials for the production of composites for the electronics, aerospace and other markets, as well as vitamins and nutritional supplements, chemicals and high-performance intermediates, and raw materials for a wide range of high-performance applications. CH15 Arxada is a global specialty chemicals company with major production facilities in Visp (Valais), Switzerland. The company develops innovative solutions for microbial control, nutrition, care, and environmental applications.
