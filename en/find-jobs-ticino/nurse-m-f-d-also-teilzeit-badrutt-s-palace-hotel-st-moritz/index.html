Original description

Your key responsibilites:

  • The role focuses on caring for and treating employees who are ill or have had an accident, and assessing whether further medical treatment is required.

  • This includes arranging appointments with our company doctor or, for emergencies, with a hospital.

  • Your job includes maintaining staff medical records with care while observing data-protection requirements.

  • Managing the staff medical room and ensuring that medical and first-aid supplies are complete, stored correctly and within their expiry dates is also a main task.

  • You will assist with accident reports and coordinate follow-up care with HR, doctors and hospitals.

  • Occasionally, support is also provided to our hotel guests to ensure their well-being.

• What you bring to the job

  • Completed nursing qualification, ideally with several years of professional experience

  • Good written and spoken English; knowledge of Italian is an advantage

  • Empathy, patience and a strong sense of responsibility

  • Excellent teamwork skills and the ability to remain resilient under pressure

  • Confident use of standard IT applications

Our offer

  • Fixed-term seasonal position from the beginning of December 2026 until the end of March 2027, with the possibility of returning for future seasons

  • Part-time employment possible

  • Five-day working week averaging 43.5 hours, adjusted according to the agreed workload

  • Five weeks of annual leave and six public holidays per year

  • 13th-month salary from the start of employment, paid pro rata

  • Accommodation in one of our attractive staff residences upon request

  • Meals available in our new, modern staff restaurant

If you are looking for an interesting, challenging and varied role in a dynamic environment and would like to join a well-established team, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
