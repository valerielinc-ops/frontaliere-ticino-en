Original description

The Legal Procedures Unit (UJUR-P) of the Directorate-General for Social Cohesion (DGCS) is responsible for handling appeals and monitoring legal procedures (administrative, criminal and civil) in the field of social assistance, as well as providing the necessary legal support for the activities of our Directorate. In order to strengthen this dynamic entity of some 20 collaborators, the DGCS is looking for a specialist case manager. • To ensure the functioning of the Registry of the First Instance Appeal Authority on Integration Income (RI) and the Secretariat of Jurists in charge of that body. • Provides secretariat (case management) for lawyers in charge of filing criminal complaints. • To act as the secretariat of the jurists responsible for carrying out the food action within the meaning of Articles 277 (2) and 328 CC. • Prepare statistics. • Sort and archive. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Mr Florian Fasel, Deputy Head of the Legal Unit Procedures (UJUR-P), at the email address: dgcs.recruitment@vd.ch • SWC or equivalent. • Professional experience of at least 3 years in the judicial field. • Knowledge of the legal field essential and ideally of the Cantonal Administration of Vaud. • Good editorial skills. • Easy to contact and manage. • A sense of responsibility, common sense of organization and public service ethics. • Adaptability, flexibility and self-help. Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
