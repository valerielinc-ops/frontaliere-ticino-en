Role overview

The civil engineering office of the Canton of St.Gallen, with around 33% of its employees, plans, builds and maintains a network of cantonal roads that is over 650 km long and provides a functioning infrastructure in the canton every day.

The department of mobility and planning deals with strategic planning, preliminary studies in the department of transport, basics and traffic technology as well as noise protection on cantonal roads.

They work closely with colleagues in mobility and planning and ensure efficient cooperation with the other departments of the Civil Engineering Office and in particular with the executing office for roads and art buildings. What you're expecting:
