Role overview

As an internal doctor, you have started your training course in internal medicine and now wish to move towards obtaining the title of specialist in infectious diseases.

In this context, you join the Infectious Diseases Department and participate in the activities of the various specialized consultations, particularly in general infectious diseases, with immunocompromised people, the elderly, in the field of septic orthopedics and HIV/AIDS, through rotations.

Under the supervision of senior doctors, you ensure the care of the patients entrusted to you, both on an outpatient basis and intrahospital, within the entire institution.
