Nurses in general care - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite Ticino hospital is present with its institutes on the whole cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, the EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing over 600,000 outpatients. The following position is addressed to candidates wishing to apply spontaneously as Infermiere/a in general treatment Profile The nursery service of the Ticino Cardiocentro Institute employs about 130 collaborators in the departments of cardiology and cardiac and chest surgery, in a context of report-based care. • Requirements Diploma of nurses (Bachelor SUPSI) or; Diploma of nurses (SSSCI) or; Diploma of nurses in other Swiss cantons or; Degree in nursing care (Italy or other countries) recognized by the Swiss Red Cross; Prepared for a patient-oriented quality service, for interdisciplinary collaboration within the team and for transparent communication; Great knowledge of the Italian language spoken and written and possibly of a second national language; Indispensable knowledge of the main computer means (Office package).
