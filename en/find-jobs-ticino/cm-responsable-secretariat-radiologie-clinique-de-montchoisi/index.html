Original description

  • Organize, coordinate and supervise all administrative activities of the Radiology Secretariat.
  • Provide guidance to the secretariat team, share tasks and ensure continuity of service.
  • Develop and manage team planning according to the activity of the department.
  • Ensure physical and telephone reception of patients and guarantee professional and personalized care.
  • Organize and optimize appointments for different imaging methods: MRI, scanner, conventional radiology, mammography, ultrasound and densitometry.
  • Coordinate agendas taking into account the availability of radiologists, equipment, examination times and possible emergencies.
  • Verify the administrative completeness of the records, medical prescriptions and information necessary for carrying out the examinations.
  • Ensure the link with prescribers and their secretariats for requests for examinations, additional information and transmission of documents.
  • Work closely with RMTs and radiologists to ensure the smoothness of the daily program.
  • Coordinate applications from the care units, operating room, day clinic and other clinical departments.
  • Supervise the preparation, sending and archiving of medical reports, images and documents.
  • Follow-up of requests for transmission of images and results to partner physicians and institutions.
  • Follow-up of insurance and other administrative partners requests.
  • Manage patient specific situations, urgent requests and first-level claims.
  • Ensure confidentiality, medical confidentiality and data protection.
  • Train and accompany new secretariat staff.
  • Participate in improving the administrative and organizational processes of the Department of Radiology.
  • Actively contribute to the quality of the patient journey and the general functioning of the radiology department.
  • Diploma of medical secretary
  • Minimum experience of 1 year in the necessary medical environment
  • Mandatory English
  • Perfect mastery of computer tools (Word, Excel, Outlook,...)
  • Perfect writing capacity and spelling knowledge, medical terminology
  • Versatility, organization and autonomy in the performance of tasks
  • Dynamics, responsiveness, proactivity and speed of execution
  • Meaning of contact
  • Very good stress management and ability to work as a team
  • Flexibility
  • Excellent presentation
  • The opportunity to join a renowned clinic at the cutting edge of development
  • Motivating activity in a pleasant work environment
  • A business culture oriented towards excellence and the quality of human relations

We pay great attention to the diversity of life paths of our collaborators. Our positions are open to both women and men.

Founded in 1932 and located in the heart of the city of Lausanne, in an exceptional setting facing Lake Geneva and the Alps, the Clinique de Montchoisi has always been one of the most famous establishments in the canton of Vaud. It brings together the best specialists of national and international renown, especially in its flagship specialties: ophthalmology, orthopaedics, ORL and radiology. The clinic makes every effort to offer excellent care and personalized, unique and warm care in a state-of-the-art environment. In 2003, the Montchoisi Clinic joined the Swiss Medical Network, one of Switzerland's two largest groups of private clinics.
