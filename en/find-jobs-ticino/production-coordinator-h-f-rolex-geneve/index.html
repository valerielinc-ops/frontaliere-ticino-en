Role overview

Introduction

For our Production Division, we are looking for a (M/F) Operations Technician whose main mission will be to coordinate and supervise the various activities of the production workshop - machining and finishing.

Combined with the entire production process, it is able to integrate a multidisciplinary team of specialists and collaborate with support services.
