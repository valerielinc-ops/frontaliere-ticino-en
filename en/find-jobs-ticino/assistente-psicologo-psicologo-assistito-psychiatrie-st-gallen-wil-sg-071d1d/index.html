Main responsibilities

  • As a member of our interdisciplinary treatment team, you support patients in the inpatient setting and actively participate in the design of the individual treatment process.
  • After a thorough induction, you will gradually take on case-leading tasks and carry out individual psychotherapeutic treatments in close cooperation with our interdisciplinary team.
  • Your varied day-to-day work covers the entire spectrum of psychiatric diseases and offers you the opportunity to get to know different clinical questions and develop your expertise.
  • As an assistant psychologist, you coordinate treatments, plan individual therapies and carry out test psychological assessments to support the diagnostic assessment.
  • Participation in rapports, interdisciplinary case discussions and further training as well as the regular professional exchange with the other practitioners are integral parts of your work. Requirements
  • You have completed a degree in psychology (recognition by the PsyReg is required for a foreign degree) and are already in an advanced phase of your federally recognized psychotherapy training.

  • An independent and responsible way of working as well as open-mindedness, empathy and joy in working together in a committed team distinguish you.
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing at at least level C1 enables you to secure and professional communication in clinical everyday life.
