CVA_Qualified dietician

  • • Part-time
  • Department: Clinique Valmont
  • Activity rate: 20 - 40%
  • Start date: Immediately or to be agreed Description of the company Anchored in the panorama of the Riviera since 1905, Clinique Valmont offers personalized rehabilitation stays for people with neurological, orthopedic and back problems, in an exceptional setting, facing Lake Geneva and the Alps. Today, it has become a flagship establishment in rehabilitation, thanks to its unrivaled therapeutic efficiency, its competent teams and its cutting-edge technical platform. Indeed, rehabilitation doctors collaborate with a team of multidisciplinary experts, made up of physiotherapists, occupational therapists, neuropsychologists, speech therapists, sports instructors and dietitians. In 2006, the clinic joined Swiss Medical Network, one of the two main Swiss groups of private clinics. Job description Your main missions will be as follows:
  • Carry out the nutritional assessment at patient entry within the required time frame
  • Ensure personalized dietary monitoring according to identified needs
  • Evaluate the nutritional status and make an appropriate diagnosis
  • Determine nutritional needs and adapt care
  • Implement nutritional interventions (oral, enteral or parenteral nutrition)
  • Adapt dietary recommendations according to the patient's pathology and preferences
  • Evaluate the effectiveness of nutritional care
  • Ensure traceability in patient files Qualifications
  • Holder of an ES or HES dietician diploma
  • Experience in individual consultation, ideally in a clinical or rehabilitation environment
  • Experience or marked interest in the nutritional care of the elderly (polypathologies, malnutrition)
  • Good command of French, both spoken and written, and ease with current IT tools
  • Pronounced taste for working in an interdisciplinary team
  • Sense of responsibility, of the organization and priorities
