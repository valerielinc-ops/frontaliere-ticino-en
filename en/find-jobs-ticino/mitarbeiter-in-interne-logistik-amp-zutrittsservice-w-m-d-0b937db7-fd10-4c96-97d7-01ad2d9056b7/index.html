Original description

Pensum: 80 %

With your organizational talent and your service orientation, you ensure that internal logistics, postal and access processes work reliably. Together we shape modern services and actively drive the digitalization of our processes.

Tasks & Responsibilities

You ensure smooth operation in the post office and support employees and external partners in matters relating to postal and service operations.You process digital and physical incoming mail, carry out security controls and coordinate on-time delivery.You organize the sending of letters and parcels as well as the internal and cross-location postal distribution.You organize the internal logistics and service processes, manage consumables and work flexibly in adjacent service areas.You process inquiries in the card center, create and personalize access badges and support with technical concerns in first-level support.You work with projects for the optimization and digitization of processes and support with the introduction of new services and processes.You manage keys: You issue and take them back, document all movements in the key management system and carry out regular inventory checks.

Profile & Requirements

You have a completed commercial or logistical basic training as well as experience in the areas of post, logistics, security or facility services.You work safely with M365 programs such as Outlook, Teams, Word and Excel and bring pleasure to digital processes.You have experience in digital post processing, with document management systems and ideally with SAP.Ideally, you are familiar with X-ray systems, badge personalization or key management.You work independently, structured and reliable and keep the overview even in hectic situations.You bring team spirit, service orientation and pleasure in cooperation with different stakeholders.You have very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English in word and writing.

About the team

Our team ensures a professional and reliable service operation every day. We work closely together, support each other and drive change and new digital solutions together.
