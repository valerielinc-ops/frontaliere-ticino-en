Role overview

As Head or Head of Clinic with the title of Specialist in Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation, you ensure the specialized outpatient management of patients with musculoskeletal pathologies, complex traumatic sequelae and functional limitations requiring rehabilitation expertise.

You contribute to the development of the clinical activities of the Physical Medicine and Orthopaedic Rehabilitation Unit (UMPRO) in close collaboration with the orthopaedic surgery, traumatology teams and the various specialists involved in the care journey.

In this function, you are involved or involved in the outpatient follow-up of polytrauma patients, for which you perform in-depth functional assessments, define rehabilitation objectives and coordinate the therapeutic interventions necessary to optimize their functional recovery and autonomy.

Company and context

  • Thanks to your organizational sense and ability to prioritize, you effectively ensure your clinical activities while responding to multiple demands (patients, telephones, correspondence, meetings).
  • You are evolving in a demanding environment, at the interface of care, teaching and research.
  • As such, you work closely with the entire medical team and ensure clear, fluid and respectful communication with your colleagues and patients, adapting your speech to their understanding.
  • Building on your team spirit, you show solidarity in the face of unforeseen and high workloads.
  • As head or head of clinic, you are actively involved in the supervision and training of in-house physicians by adopting a mentoring posture and sharing your expertise.
