Role overview

The Global Sales Analysis Manager leads global demand planning, sales intelligence and commercial forecasting activities across all Distribution Channels.

The role serves as a critical link between Commercial teams and Global Planning Operations, transforming market demand signals, product performance insights and customer trends into actionable forecasting inputs.

Through advanced analytics, Centric Planning and cross-functional collaboration, the position contributes to improving forecast accuracy at Style-Color-Size level, supporting more effective inventory, purchasing and production decisions on a global scale.
