Role overview

Role purpose

The Marketing Manager will lead the development of Rocca as a retail brand and luxury destination, translating its House of Brands model into a distinctive positioning, a compelling customer proposition and an integrated marketing calendar that drives awareness, traffic, engagement and commercial performance.

This is a hands-on leadership role for a marketer who combines strategic thinking and strong brand sensitivity with the ability to manage execution in detail.

Main responsibilities

  • Define and progressively strengthen Rocca's brand positioning, value proposition, narrative and distinctive identity as a luxury jewellery and watch retail destination.

  • Build the House of Brands concept into a clear consumer proposition that enhances both Rocca's equity and the desirability of its partner Maisons.
  • Translate strategic direction into practical creative briefs, communication platforms, campaigns and activation toolkits. Retail marketing and traffic generation
  • Develop an annual retail marketing calendar aligned with commercial priorities, store needs, launches, local opportunities and key client moments.

  • Adapt national marketing priorities into relevant local plans, while creating scalable frameworks for international markets as Rocca expands.
  • Partner closely with Retail, Sales, CRM and E-commerce to connect marketing activity with client development and measurable business outcomes. Integrated activation and content
  • Coordinate events, visual merchandising, digital marketing, CRM, social media, content production and in-store communication as one coherent 360-degree plan.
