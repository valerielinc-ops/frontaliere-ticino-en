Original description

At Alcon, we are driven by the meaningful work we do to help people see brilliantly. As a global leader in ophthalmology, we are committed to innovation and progress and act quickly. With us you will be rewarded for your commitment and achievements and can advance your career like never before. Together we go above and beyond to improve the lives of our patients and customers. We promote an inclusive culture and are looking for diverse, talented people to join Alcon. As a team leader (m/f/d) Production Environment Controlled Area (ECA), you are responsible for the timely and high-quality production of semi-finished and finished products. You lead a production team in two shifts, promote the professional development of employees and actively contribute to the continuous optimization of production processes. A typical working day in this function includes:

  • Ensuring the timely and quality-based production and testing of semi-finished and finished products
  • Complete and traceable documentation in the ERP system (SAP) and on the order papers
  • Carrying out Device History Record (DHR) reviews
  • Arranging maintenance and repair of the production facilities
  • Technical and disciplinary management of an assigned production team
  • Carrying out cross-training for the further development of employees (also cross-departmental)
  • Active participation in the performance assessment process
  • Implementation and tracking of CAPAs and change controls
  • Identification and implementation of improvement potential using LEAN methods
  • Participation in cross-departmental projects and problem solving
  • Ensuring the availability of orders, materials, tools and measuring equipment
  • Personnel deployment planning and cross-departmental resource support WHAT YOU BRING TO ALCON:
  • Completed craft or precision engineering training or many years of practical experience
  • In-depth knowledge of products and production processes
  • Confident use of Microsoft Office and ERP systems (SAP)
  • Technical understanding and quick comprehension of complex contexts
  • Experience in instructing, training and leading employees
  • High level of personal responsibility and organizational skills
  • Strong communication and conflict skills
  • Loyalty, confidentiality and role model function
  • Very good knowledge of German (written and spoken), knowledge of English is an advantage HOW YOU CAN BE SUCCESSFUL AT ALCON:
  • Work in a challenging environment with a motivated and quality-oriented team that will help you achieve your goals and be successful
  • Join a leading, innovative company in the field of medical technology that changes lives and of which you can be truly proud
  • Take on a responsible position with leadership tasks and actively shape the success of your team
  • Benefit from targeted support for your individual development - including cross-training and perspectives beyond your own area of responsibility
  • Contribute your ideas and work on the continuous improvement and optimization of processes with Take advantage of your opportunity and apply directly online here with your CV and a short letter of motivation – we look forward to receiving your application!
