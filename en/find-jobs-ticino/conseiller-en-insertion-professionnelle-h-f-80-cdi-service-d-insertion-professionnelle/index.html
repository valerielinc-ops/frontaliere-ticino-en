Your Mission

You are actively helping to meet the challenges of integration into the labour market and socio-occupational life of people receiving the benefits of the General Hospital.

In close cooperation with social workers, you promote the sustainable integration and autonomy of beneficiaries by relying on their needs, their resources, and by identifying the obstacles that may exist. Your main activities
