General information Code 2026-237 DESCRIPTION OF THE AUTHORITIES Title Specialist Section Head

  • Professional support and coordination of 3 employees
  • Ensuring smooth operation of the round polishing machines (slide grinding systems) and the washing system
  • Monitoring of compliance with quality rules and quality control of products
  • Initiation and implementation of appropriate intervention and corrective measures for quality deviations
  • Monitoring, analysis and continuous improvement of production processes in cooperation with production and department management and with suppliers
  • Training, coaching and promotion of employees
  • Preparation and maintenance of work instructions and process documentation
  • Documentation of processes according to ISO 9001
  • Ensuring cleanliness, order and safety at work in the area of responsibility
  • Active participation in day-to-day operations Profile

  • Strong hands-on mentality with pleasure in active participation in production
