Role overview

As a leading supplier of plastic piping systems for industry, supply and home technology, we offer high-quality and innovative products as well as application-oriented solutions. We have been setting standards for more than 200 years and are now represented at more than 30 locations worldwide.

Using state-of-the-art plastic injection molding machines and highly automated production cells with closing forces from 60 to 3,200 tons, we manufacture technical components up to over 100kg shot weight.

As we continue to grow and demand in the Microelectronics (ME) and Datacenter segments increases exorbitantly, we are looking for highly motivated and well-qualified specialists. In a demanding production environment, you shape the future of our production with your willingness to perform and your quality thinking. This position has a lot of development potential and offers you a great opportunity for development.

  • Self-configuration and start-up of injection molding machines including complex automation with robots and extensive peripherals
  • Performing plant and process optimizations
  • Autonomous maintenance and maintenance of equipment
  • Driving the Lean Management and Co-shaping of the "Smart Factory" concept (Industry 4.0)
  • Performing Picetting Service and Shift Work (2-shift)
  • Completed apprenticeship as a plastics technologist, mechatronics engineer or a completed apprenticeship in the mechanical field
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in plastics processing, preferably in injection molding
