Original description

In order to strengthen its teams, Banque Raiffeisen Alpes Riviera Chablais Vaudois is looking for a versatile, rigorous and service-oriented person to ensure the technical, operational and security management of its banking infrastructures.

Add

  • Ensure the maintenance and management of infrastructure, as well as the coordination of internal and external service providers.
  • Guarantee the availability of consumables necessary for the proper functioning of the sites.
  • Ensure management of computer equipment (software - hardware): PCs, screens, servers, keyboards, headsets, etc.
  • Ensure the management of security systems, in particular: employee access management, video surveillance, burglary detection and alarm systems, as well as fire safety.
  • Ensure management of ATMs: internal coordination, communication, orders, returns, management of matches, breakdowns and internal accounts linked to ATMs.
  • Participate in on-call duty on a rotating basis every other week.

Anforderungen

  • Driving license required.
  • Professional experience in ATM management desired.
  • Good knowledge of security systems.
  • Sense of service and excellent interpersonal skills.
  • Good technical knowledge in the fields of infrastructure and buildings.
  • Comfort in using IT tools.
  • Organizational skills and good administrative skills.
  • Autonomy, rigor and sense of responsibility.
  • Ability to work both independently and in a team.
  • Ability to effectively coordinate internal and external stakeholders.

Cooperative values are our pillars. Together. For each other. They shape the way we collaborate and behave towards each other. The culture of familiarity promotes personal proximity and exchanges on an equal footing at all job levels.
