Original description

Requirements

  • diploma or license or bachelor's + master's degree in psychology from a Swiss university or, if obtained from a foreign university, federal recognition of the diploma issued by the Commission of Psychological Professions PsiCo (according to Art. 2 and 3 of the Federal Law on Psychological Professions (LPPsi))
  • authorization in the canton of Ticino to practice as a psychologist active in the health care field (see details particular observation)
  • authorization in the canton of Ticino to practice as a psychotherapist (see details particular observation)
  • experience in the field of eating disorders (more than 3 years) may be an asset
  • ability to work in multidisciplinary teams, proactive attitude, adaptability empathy and interpersonal skills
  • possess adequate psycho-attudinal profile for the practice of the profession
  • knowledge of the territory, social services and the socio-health network of Canton Ticino
  • perfect command of the Italian language and knowledge of another official spoken and written language
  • possess adequate psycho-attudinal profile for the practice of the profession, specifically also adequate reception skills in serious crisis situations and psychic urgency Special remarks
  • candidates with psychologist title
  • applicants with a psychologist title with recognition issued by the Commission of Psychological Professions (PsiCo) in the case of foreign titles but not yet in possession of authorization in the Canton of Ticino to practice as an active psychologist in the health care sector, 6 months from the decision of appointment are granted for obtaining the same
  • applicants with a psychologist title awarded at a foreign university but not yet in possession of the recognition of the Commission of Psychological Professions (PsiCo), are granted 6 months from the decision of assignment for obtaining the same together with the authorization in the Canton of Ticino to practice the profession of psychologist active in health care
  • the actual start of the activity of the successful candidate is subject to obtaining the authorization in the Canton of Ticino to practice the profession of psychologist active in health care. Failure to obtain the requested requirements within 6 months of the decision of appointment will result in its automatic forfeiture
  • within 4 years after the start of activity, it is necessary to obtain authorization in the Canton of Ticino to practice as a psychotherapist. During this period, employment will be in the form of appointment (Art. 15 and 16 LORD) commutable to appointment upon obtaining authorization in the Canton of Ticino to practice as a psychotherapist. Failure to obtain will result in permanent termination of the employment relationship
  • candidates with psychotherapist title:
  • applicants with a psychotherapist title with recognition issued by the Commission of Psychological Professions (PsiCo) in the case of foreign titles but not yet in possession of the authorization in the Canton of Ticino to practice as a psychotherapist are granted 6 months from the decision of appointment to obtain the same
  • applicants with a psychotherapist title but not yet in possession of the recognition of the Commission of Psychological Professions (PsiCo), are granted 6 months from the decision of appointment for obtaining the same together with the authorization in the Canton of Ticino to practice as a psychotherapist
  • the actual start of the activity of the successful candidate is subject to obtaining the authorization in the Canton of Ticino to practice as a psychotherapist. Failure to obtain the requested requirements within 6 months of the decision of appointment will result in its automatic forfeiture
  • within 4 years of the start of activity, it is necessary to obtain authorization in the Canton of Ticino to practice as an active psychologist in the health care field. During this period, employment will take place in the form of an assignment (Art. 15 and 16 LORD) that can be transformed into an appointment upon obtaining authorization from the Canton of Ticino to practice as a psychologist/psychologist active in health care. Failure to obtain will result in the permanent termination of the employment relationship
  • degree of employment: 50%
  • availability on the part of the candidate to work irregular shifts
  • place of employment: Unità Di Cura Pedopsichiatrica (UCPP)
  • San Pietro di Stabio
  • start date: to be agreed upon Conditions of application
  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application will not be considered valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (cover letter and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of employment), additional documentation may be requested at a later date (criminal record with corresponding self-certification and self-certification on health status) Conditions of employment
  • appointment or assignment: are those provided for in the Law on the Organization of State Employees and Teachers of March 15, 1995 (LORD) and its Regulations. Any changes in the law regarding salary and organization remain explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100 percent employment ratio), flexible or according to the needs of the service Expiration date June 30, 2026 Further information can be obtained from Ms. Maria Chiara Ferrazzo Arcidiacono, Head of the OSC Clinical Psychology and Psychotherapy Service, tel. 091 815 21 11.
