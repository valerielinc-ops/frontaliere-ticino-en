Original description

In the area of Finance & Controlling, we are looking for reinforcement for our controlling team. You take responsibility for a company area and maintain an intensive exchange with it. In doing so, you ensure financial transparency, support executives in their decisions and play an important role in the further development of the area. Your role

  • Responsible for controlling a corporate division
  • Advising on all business management issues
  • Collaboration and partial responsibility in budget, forecast and rolling planning
  • Participation in projects for the further development of controlling processes and instruments
  • Preparation of periodic management and financial reporting, results analyses and ad hoc evaluations
  • Responsible for project controlling and maintenance of the project controlling system in the area
  • Audit of investment and overhead projects
  • Cooperation in the further development of the SAP system
  • Management of the BI tool DeltaMaster
  • Organisation and conduct of training

Your profile

  • Professional certificate in finance and accounting or specialist / university degree (with specialization in accounting / controlling) - at best in advanced bachelor's degree
  • Initial experience in accounting, in particular in cost and performance accounting
  • Experience in the SAP environment (modules FI/CO and PS), with BI tool DeltaMaster and with Microsoft Power BI
  • Safe appearance, pleasure in advising managers
  • Versed in the application MS-Office in particular Excel
  • Number flair, analytical thinking and recognition of complex relationships
  • Flowing knowledge of German in words and writing

Your Benefits

  • Up to 60% home office possible with a 100% pension
  • Holiday purchase (2 weeks per year)
  • 41h week with flexible working hours on a full-time workload
  • Mind. 25 days of holidays per year
  • Support for 100% financial or temporal training
  • Free SBB half-ax subscription for all employees
