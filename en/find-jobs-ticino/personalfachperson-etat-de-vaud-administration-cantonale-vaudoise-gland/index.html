Role overview

The Regional Employment Offices (ORP) in the canton of Vaud, attached to the Directorate-General for Employment and Labour Market (DGEM), are responsible for assisting clients in their job search and supporting employers in the recruitment of staff.

They play a key role in the implementation of employment policy and the fight against unemployment.

As a staff adviser, you work in support of the integration of persons seeking employment.
