Original description

As a Digital Supply Chain Manager, you drive the digital development of our supply chain – from the strategic roadmap and the future system and data landscape to the concrete implementation of analytics and automation solutions. At the interface between supply chain, IT and business intelligence, you translate operational requirements into scalable digital and data-based solutions. You combine analytical and conceptual thinking with a hands-on mentality, take responsibility for central digitization and analytics topics and help make our supply chain more transparent, data-based and efficient. Do you want to bring together data, systems and processes to help shape the digital future of our supply chain? We look forward to your application.

Digital Supply Chain Manager

Your responsibilities

  • You develop and control the end-to-end digitization roadmap for supply chain in close cooperation with supply chain, IT and business intelligence.
  • You lead digitalization projects from requirements reception and solution conception to implementation, coordinating internal stakeholders and external service providers.
  • You shape the future system and data architecture of the supply chain and bring the SCM perspective into relevant system and architecture decisions.
  • You are responsible for the KPI and reporting framework for supply chain and ensure uniform definitions and calculation logic as a reliable “Single Source of Truth”.
  • You will develop and optimize Power BI reports and data models based on SAP Datasphere data products and also work hands-on during implementation.
  • You take requirements from the supply chain teams and translate them into actionable digital, analytics and data solutions.
  • You identify potential for process automation, evaluate suitable use cases, create business cases and accompany the implementation.
  • You act as a technical interface to SAP S/4HANA and data-related SCM topics and contribute to analytics requirements as well as ensuring master data quality.

Your qualifications

  • You have a university degree in business informatics, industrial engineering, supply chain management, computer science or a comparable qualification.
  • You bring at least 3 years of relevant professional experience in digitization and/or supply chain projects and have already taken responsibility for projects.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of Power BI, especially data modeling and DAX, as well as experience with KPI reporting and data analysis.
  • You have experience with SAP S/4HANA and SAP Datasphere
  • Knowledge of SQL, data pipelines or process mining tools is an advantage.
  • You understand supply chain processes and can translate business requirements into structured system, data and reporting solutions.
  • You think analytically and conceptually, work in a structured, independent and solution-oriented way and at the same time like to participate.
  • You communicate convincingly and work confidently with different stakeholders from a wide variety of areas as well as with external partners.
  • You have very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing.

About Medartis

Medartis is one of the leading manufacturers of medical products for osteosynthesis in the area of the facial skull and extremities. For the benefit of the patient, Medartis is committed to providing surgeons and surgical staff with the most innovative titanium implants and instruments and providing the best service.

We offer you a responsible and varied activity with high quality requirements in a growing, international company with progressive social benefits.
